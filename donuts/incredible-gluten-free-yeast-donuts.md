# Incredible Gluten Free Yeast Donuts

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 24 donuts
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 3 minutes
- ❄️ **Chilling:** 8 hours
- ⏳ **Total:** 8 hours 33 minutes
- 🌡️ **Frying oil:** 320 to 330°F (160 to 166°C)
- 🌡️ **Air fryer:** 350°F (177°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Sweet dough

- 🌾 3½ cups (490 g) Kim's gluten free bread flour blend
- 🍬 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 🥄 1¾ teaspoons baking powder
- 🥄 2 tablespoons plus 1½ teaspoons (24 g) instant yeast
- 🌿 2 tablespoons (10 g) whole psyllium husks (or 1½ tablespoons psyllium husk powder), optional
- 🧂 1 teaspoon salt
- 🥛 1½ cups (360 ml) whole milk (or dairy-free alternative)
- 🧈 ¾ cup (169 g) butter, very soft or melted (or dairy-free butter)
- 🥚 2 large eggs, at room temperature

### Vanilla glaze

- 🍬 2 cups (250 g) powdered sugar
- 🧈 4 tablespoons (½ stick or 56 g) butter
- 🥛 4 tablespoons (60 ml) whole milk
- 🌼 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

### Chocolate glaze

- 🍫 ¼ recipe vanilla glaze (above; the source says to eyeball it)
- 🍫 ¼ cup (25 g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 🍯 1 tablespoon corn syrup
- 💧 Hot tap water by the tablespoon, or more powdered sugar, as needed for the right thickness

### Maple glaze

- 🍁 ¼ recipe vanilla glaze (above; eyeball it)
- 🍯 2 tablespoons real maple syrup
- 🌿 ¼ to ½ teaspoon maple extract (more or less to taste)

### Strawberry glaze

- 🍓 ¼ recipe vanilla glaze (above; eyeball it)
- 🍓 3 to 4 small strawberries, quartered
- 🍓 3 to 4 tablespoons freeze-dried strawberries (eyeball it)
- 💧 Hot water or powdered sugar as needed for the desired thickness

### Cream filling

- 🥛 ½ recipe Fluffy Filling (the source refers to this filling but does not include its recipe)

### Other

- 🫒 Oil for deep frying (canola, vegetable, or melted shortening)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Make the dough: in the bowl of a stand mixer, add the milk first, followed by all the other dough ingredients. Using the dough hook, knead for 5 minutes on medium speed. The dough will be very sticky. Scrape the dough into the center of the bowl and cover the bowl. Refrigerate overnight for a cold proof (bulk fermentation).
2. 🧻 Remove the dough from the refrigerator and knead it on a well-floured surface until smooth. Roll it out to about ½ inch thick. Cut the dough with a donut cutter dipped in flour, or a biscuit or cookie cutter, to make fillable donuts. Re-roll the scraps. Place the donuts and holes on parchment squares on a baking sheet. Cover with plastic wrap and let rise in a warm, draft-free area until a little less than doubled in size.
3. 🍳 Frying in oil: when the donuts are almost completely risen, heat the oil in a deep fryer or large heavy-duty Dutch oven to 320 to 330°F (160 to 166°C). Carefully lower the donuts, only a few at a time, into the oil, parchment and all (especially if they are sticking to the parchment). Use tongs to remove the parchment from the oil. Fry for 1 to 2 minutes on each side (longer for donuts without a hole in the center).
4. 🧻 Remove the donuts from the oil and place them on a wire rack set over a baking sheet. Glaze or garnish and fill as desired. Donuts are best eaten fresh, but will keep for a day, well covered, and can be heated very briefly in the microwave (about 15 seconds).
5. 🌬️ Air frying: preheat the air fryer to 350°F (177°C). Place as many donuts as fit in one layer in the air fryer, still on their parchment squares. "Fry" for 4 minutes (the time may need adjusting for your air fryer).
6. 🍽️ Remove the donuts from the air fryer. Glaze or garnish and fill as desired. To add dry toppings, such as sugar, powdered sugar, or cinnamon sugar, first dunk the donuts in melted butter so the toppings adhere.
7. 🍯 Vanilla glaze: in the microwave, melt the butter with the milk in a one-quart measuring cup or bowl. Add the powdered sugar and vanilla, whisking constantly until pourable. Dunk donuts in the glaze as desired.
8. 🍫 Chocolate glaze: add the cocoa powder and corn syrup to the vanilla glaze and whisk to combine fully. If too thick, add 1 tablespoon of hot tap water at a time until smooth. If too thin, add more powdered sugar to thicken.
9. 🍁 Maple glaze: add the maple syrup and maple extract to the vanilla glaze and stir or whisk to combine.
10. 🍓 Strawberry glaze: add the strawberries and freeze-dried strawberries to the vanilla glaze and use an immersion blender or regular blender to blend everything together. Adjust the thickness by adding a tablespoon at a time of hot water for a thinner glaze, or powdered sugar, a little at a time, for a thicker glaze.
11. 🥢 To fill: use a chopstick or something similar (the end of a wooden spoon, or a long, skinny decorating tip) to puncture a hole in the side of the donut. Wiggle and move it around to create enough space to fill the entire donut. Fill with jelly, fluffy filling, or anything else you'd like (see other suggestions in the source's post).

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: recipe by Kim at Let Them Eat Gluten Free Cake.
- 🌾 Psyllium: it is not absolutely necessary. Without it the dough is a little stickier. To omit it, reduce the milk to 1¼ cups (300 ml).
- 🍬 Sugar: the source says the total sugar for the whole recipe is one cup, and that the yeast needs this much sugar for the right texture. The source suggests using half the dough for donuts (1 dozen) and keeping the other half in the fridge for another use. You may also halve the recipe.
- 🧁 The donut holes in the source were rolled in granulated sugar (about ¼ cup), ½ teaspoon cinnamon, and ¼ teaspoon cardamom.
- 🍩 Reader tip (Lauren, per the source): the dough can be made without refrigerating, by piping it into a donut pan and baking at 350°F (177°C) for about 15 minutes. Or pipe it onto parchment, freeze for about 10 minutes, and fry as directed.
- ⚠️ The source refers to "½ recipe Fluffy Filling" but does not include that filling's recipe.
