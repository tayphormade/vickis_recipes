# Pumpkin Churro Doughnuts

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏱️ **Bake:** 15 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into a doughnut comes out clean

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Pumpkin doughnuts

- 🌾 1 cup + 1½ tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 🥄 ¾ teaspoon baking soda
- 🥄 ½ teaspoon baking powder
- 🍬 ½ cup granulated sugar
- 🍬 ½ cup dark brown sugar
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon pumpkin spice
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- 🎃 ¾ cup pumpkin puree [¾ cup water + 3/16 cup (3 tablespoons) pumpkin powder]
- 🥚 1 large egg (or 2 small ones)
- 🛢️ ⅓ cup oil
- 💧 ⅓ cup water

### Churro topping

- 🧈 ¼ cup (57 g) butter, melted
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🍂 1 tablespoon cinnamon

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌾 Pumpkin doughnuts: In a large bowl, combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, both sugars, salt, and spices.
2. 🥚 In another bowl, combine pumpkin puree, egg, oil, and water. Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients and mix until just combined (do not overmix).
3. 🍩 Spoon the batter into a greased non-stick doughnut baking tin. Bake for 15 minutes in the 350°F (175°C) preheated oven, or until a toothpick inserted into a doughnut comes out clean. Allow to cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then take out onto a wire rack to cool completely.
4. 🧈 Topping: In a small pan, melt ¼ cup butter. In a small bowl, mix sugar and cinnamon. Brush each doughnut with butter and sprinkle with sugar-cinnamon mixture.
