# Apple Fritters

- 🍽️ **Servings:** not stated in the source
- 🔥 **Frying temperature:** not stated in the source

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Glaze

- 🍬 1 cup powdered sugar
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 🥛 3 tablespoons milk

### Apples

- 🍎 2 medium apples (cored, peeled, and chopped)
- 🧈 3 tablespoons butter
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- 🍬 ⅓ cup sugar

### Dough

- 🌾 1 cup flour
- 🥄 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🍂 1 teaspoon cinnamon (optional)
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🥛 ⅓ cup milk

### Frying

- 🛢️ Oil for frying
- 🧈 ¼ cup butter

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍬 Mix the glaze ingredients well and set aside for glazing.
2. 🍎 Combine the apples, butter, and cinnamon. Cook on the stovetop or in the microwave until soft before adding to the dough batter.
3. 🌾 Mix the dough ingredients. Stir in the cooked apples.
4. 🛢️ Preheat a saucepan of oil for frying. Add ¼ cup butter to the oil before dropping in the batter.
5. 🥄 Drop the batter into the oil with a spoon. Brown on both sides.
6. 🧊 Take the fritters out and place them on a cooling rack with parchment underneath.
7. 🍯 Pour the glaze over the fritters and enjoy!
