# Basic Yeast Donuts

- 🍽️ **Yield:** Makes filled, plain, or glazed donuts (the source says the dough "can be cut in half")
- 🌡️ **Frying oil:** About 350°F (177°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Donut dough

- 🌾 5 cups all-purpose flour, plus a bit more if the dough is sticky
- 🥄 2 (7 g) envelopes yeast (the source adds "1 cup very active sourdough starter, may need to adjust flour or milk")
- 🍬 ⅓ cup granulated sugar
- 🥛 1½ cups whole milk, warmed to about 115°F (46°C)
- 🧈 ¼ cup unsalted butter, melted
- 🧈 ¼ cup shortening, melted
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🧂 ¾ teaspoon salt

### Chocolate glaze (ganache)

- 🥛 ¼ cup cream
- 🍫 ½ cup (4 oz) chocolate chips
- 🍯 1½ tablespoons corn syrup
- 🧂 Pinch of salt
- 🌼 ¼ teaspoon vanilla

### Custard filling

- 🥛 ¾ cup whole milk
- 🌾 1½ tablespoons all-purpose flour or cornstarch
- 🍬 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 🥚 1 egg yolk
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla paste, or 1 envelope vanillin
- 🧂 Small pinch of salt

### Glaze (for glazed donuts)

- 🍬 Powdered sugar (the source estimates about 1 cup)
- 🥛 Whole milk (the source estimates 2 to 3 tablespoons)
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥛 Heat the milk to 105°F (41°C). Add the sugar and yeast. Wait until it is visibly active.
2. 🧂 Mix the salt with the flour.
3. 🥣 In a mixing bowl, combine the butter, shortening, and eggs. Add the yeast mixture, then the flour and salt mixture.
4. 🥄 Using a dough hook, knead for about 5 minutes until the dough is nice and smooth and maybe a little tacky. Turn out into a lightly oiled bowl. Cover and let rise in a warm place for about an hour, until doubled in size.
5. 🧻 Place parchment paper on a lightly floured baking sheet. Lightly flour your kneading surface, turn out the dough, and roll it to about ½ inch thick. Cut with a 3-inch biscuit cutter or donut cutter dipped in flour, and place the donuts on the parchment paper.
6. ⏲️ Let them rise, covered, for ½ hour to 1 hour, until doubled in size.
7. 🍳 Heat a pan of oil or a deep fryer to about 350°F (177°C). (Trick from the source: dip the handle of a wooden spoon into the oil to the bottom. If bubbles come up from the bottom, but not ferociously, the oil is at temperature.)
8. 🥄 Using a spatula, knock off as much flour from the bottom of each donut, then add it to the oil. Add only a few at a time to keep the temperature up. Fry for about 2 minutes per side, or until golden brown.
9. 🧻 Remove to a rack or a baking sheet lined with paper towel to absorb excess oil.
10. 🍬 Jelly donuts: while the donuts are still warm, dip them in sugar to coat. To make filling easier, use a stick about the size of a lollipop stick to pop a hole in the side and wiggle it around to make space. This is not really necessary, but it allows for more filling. Pipe the jelly into a cooled donut using a pastry bag with a wide, round tip (a no. 10 is good). When the donuts are done, dip the tops in sugar again.
11. 🍫 Nutella-filled donuts: follow the jelly donut method, but fill the warm donuts with Nutella instead to soften the filling.
12. 🍫 Chocolate glaze: heat the cream, corn syrup, and salt to a simmer. Remove from the heat. Add the chocolate and vanilla and stir until melted.
13. 🍮 Custard filling: mix everything together in a saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it thickens like pudding, about 3 to 8 minutes. Pour into a bowl and let cool with plastic wrap placed directly on top of the custard so a skin won't form.
14. 🍩 Boston cream donuts with chocolate glaze: pipe the custard into cooled donuts with a pastry bag and tip, then dip the tops of the donuts in the chocolate glaze.
15. 🍩 Glazed donuts: cut donuts with a hole in the center and continue as above. When the donuts are fried, dip them in the glaze.

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: a home baker's basic yeast donut recipe (the source gives no author name or URL).
- 🥄 The source lists the yeast as "2 (7gr) Envelopes of Yeast (1 cup very active sourdough starter, may need to adjust flour or milk)." The parenthetical is kept as printed. The source does not say how much starter to use in place of the yeast.
- 🌡️ The source gives two milk temperatures: warm to about 115°F (46°C) in the ingredient list, and heat to 105°F (41°C) in the method. Both are kept as printed.
- 🍫 The chocolate glaze is labeled "ganache" in the source.
- 🍮 The custard is labeled in the source as good for donuts, éclairs, Boston cream pie, and fruit tarts.
- 🧊 Glaze amounts are the source's own estimate ("I guesstimate"): keep adding milk to the powdered sugar until the glaze is liquid or stiff.
