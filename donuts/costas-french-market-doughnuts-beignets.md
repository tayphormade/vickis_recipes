# Costas French Market Doughnuts (Beignets)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 30 minutes
- ⏳ **Ready in:** 1 hour 30 minutes
- 🌡️ **Frying oil:** 360°F (180°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 💧 ½ cup water (room temperature)
- 🥄 1 tablespoon yeast
- 🧈 ¼ cup shortening
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🧂 1 teaspoon salt
- 💧 1 cup boiling water
- 🥛 1 cup evaporated milk
- 🥚 2 eggs, beaten
- 🌾 7½ cups flour [1020g 6000ft]
- 🛢️ 1 quart vegetable oil for frying
- 🍬 1 cup confectioners' sugar for dusting

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 Pour ½ cup room-temperature water into a small bowl. Sprinkle the yeast over the water and let stand for about 5 minutes to dissolve.
2. 🥣 Combine the shortening, sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Pour the boiling water over the shortening mixture, then stir in the evaporated milk. Wait for the mixture to cool until it is lukewarm. Then add the yeast and water mixture and the beaten eggs.
3. 🌾 Slowly mix in the flour until the dough forms a ball. Cover the dough with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
4. 🔪 Working with a small portion (a little larger than a baseball) at a time, roll out the dough to ⅛ inch thick. Cut the rolled-out dough into strips 2 to 3 inches wide, then cut again in the opposite direction and at an angle, making diamond shapes.
5. 🍳 Heat the oil for frying in a deep, wide, heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat to 360°F (180°C).
6. 🍩 Slide the dough slowly into the oil to avoid splattering, and deep fry until puffed up and golden brown, 3 to 5 minutes. Carefully remove onto a rack with paper towels underneath, and let cool until you can handle them. Place them in a clean paper bag with confectioners' sugar and shake gently until generously covered, or use a sifter to dust the beignets with powdered sugar.
