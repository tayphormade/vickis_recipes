# Fluffiest Homemade Donuts

- 🍽️ **Yield:** Not given in the source
- 🌡️ **Frying oil:** 320 to 330°F (160°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Donuts

- 🌾 280 g all-purpose flour (2 cups + 3 tablespoons)
- 🍬 40 g sugar (3 tablespoons)
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🌰 ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🥛 120 g whole milk (½ cup); every flour absorbs liquid differently, so you may need an extra 2 to 3 tablespoons of milk
- 🧈 40 g melted butter (⅓ stick)
- 🥄 7 g dry yeast (1 packet)

### Glaze

- 🍬 250 g powdered sugar (2 cups)
- 🧈 75 g butter (⅔ stick)
- 🥛 3 to 5 tablespoons milk, depending on how thin you like the glaze
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Mix with a spoon or spatula until a smooth, sticky dough forms. Try not to add any flour (only 1 to 2 tablespoons if you must).
2. 🥐 Add ½ teaspoon of oil to your work surface so the dough doesn't stick, and knead for 10 to 15 minutes, using a dough scraper if the dough gets too sticky, until the dough is smooth and elastic. Form a ball and place it in an oiled bowl to rise.
3. ⏲️ When the dough has doubled in size, about an hour and a half later, lightly flour your work surface and the top of the dough. Roll it out to ⅓ to ½ inch thick and cut out the donuts.
4. 📄 Set the cut donuts on parchment paper, cutting the paper for each individual donut (this makes it easier to place them in hot oil), and let them rise for about 20 minutes.
5. 🍳 Fry in 320 to 330°F (160°C) oil until golden brown.
6. 🧻 Drain the donuts on a wire rack for a minute before dipping them in the glaze.
7. 🍩 Dip the donuts in the glaze and set them on the wire rack to drip and cool.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: a homemade glazed donut recipe that the source says tastes like Krispy Kreme donuts.
- ⚠️ The source includes a bracketed note from the recipe's author about frying: the first batch looked great and tasted good, but the fryer may have been too low, so the donuts absorbed too much oil and were heavier than light and fluffy. The author suggests the lower temperature is to lightly brown the donuts and keep the insides light and fluffy. The author also notes that mixing for 8 minutes in the mixer may have been too long.
- ⏱️ The source's reader comments say most people found the donuts light, fluffy, and melt-in-your-mouth.
- 🍬 The source does not say how to combine the glaze ingredients (for example, whether to melt the butter first).
- 🗄️ Tip from the source: donuts are best freshly made. To make them ahead, store cooled donuts in a Ziplock bag or an airtight container, and just before serving, microwave them for a few seconds.
