# Sourdough Donuts

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 17 donuts and holes
- 🌡️ **Frying oil:** 390°F (199°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥣 ½ cup sourdough starter
- 🧈 2 tablespoons shortening
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🥚 1 egg, or 2 egg yolks
- 🥛 ⅓ cup sour milk or buttermilk (a little vinegar in milk to curdle)
- 🌾 2 cups flour
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 🥄 ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🌰 ½ teaspoon nutmeg
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- 🍬 Granulated sugar, or a mixture of cinnamon and sugar in a bag, for dusting

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Cream together the shortening and sugar.
2. 🥚 Add the egg or egg yolks.
3. 🥛 Stir in the sour milk or buttermilk.
4. 🥣 Mix with the sourdough starter.
5. 🌾 Sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, nutmeg, and cinnamon.
6. 🥄 Sift the dry ingredients and stir them into the liquid. Roll out and cut. (Let rise if desired, or fry right away.)
7. 🍳 Heat some oil to 390°F (199°C) and fry.
8. 🍬 Dust with granulated sugar, or with a mixture of cinnamon and sugar shaken in a bag.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: "The Complete Sourdough Cookbook" by Don and Myrtle Holm.
- 🥛 The source's note for sour milk: add a little vinegar to milk to curdle it.
- 🥞 The source's file also includes a sourdough pancake recipe. It is not included here, since it is a breakfast pancake rather than a donut.
