# Beef and Bean Chili

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- ♨️ **Simmer:** 2–3 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥩 **Meat & beans**
  - 🥩 2 lb ground beef
  - 🫘 1 (29 oz) can kidney beans, with liquid
  - 🫘 1 (29 oz) can pinto beans, with liquid
- 🍅 **Tomatoes & vegetables**
  - 🍅 1 (29 oz) can tomato sauce
  - 🍅 3 medium tomatoes, chopped
  - 🧅 1 cup diced onion (1 medium)
  - 🫑 ½ cup diced green chili (2 chilies)
  - 🌱 ¼ cup diced celery (1 stalk)
- 🌶️ **Seasoning & liquid**
  - 🌶️ 3 tbsp chili powder
  - 🌿 2 tsp cumin powder
  - ⚫ 1½ tsp black pepper
  - 🧂 2 tsp salt
  - 💧 2 cups water

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥩 Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, then drain off the fat.
2. 🥄 Crumble the cooked beef into pea-size pieces with a fork.
3. 🍲 In a large pot, combine the beef with all the remaining ingredients.
4. 🔥 Bring to a simmer over low heat.
5. ⏲️ Cook 2–3 hours, stirring every 15 minutes.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🫘 Beans are added directly to the chili, unlike in some chili recipes.
