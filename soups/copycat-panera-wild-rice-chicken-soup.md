# Copycat Panera Wild Rice and Chicken Soup (Best)

- ⚡ **Instant Pot:** 10 minutes on SOUP (15 if the chicken is frozen), plus a 5-minute rest and thickening
- 🐢 **Slow cooker:** 5 hours on low, then 1 hour on high
- 🔥 **Stovetop:** 40 minutes simmer, then 15–20 minutes to finish

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥓 5 slices bacon, chopped (optional; not in Panera's soup)
- 🧈 4 tbsp butter (4 tbsp if using bacon; up to 12 tbsp without, per the version commenters rated spot on)
- 🧅 1 cup onion, chopped
- 🥕 1 cup carrot, diced (about 2 medium)
- 🌱 1 cup celery, diced (about 2 ribs)
- 🍄 4–8 oz sliced white or cremini mushrooms (optional; not in Panera's soup)
- 🍚 6 oz long grain and wild rice mix
- 🫙 2 tbsp Better than Bouillon Chicken, or 2 Not-Chicken bouillon cubes
- 🍗 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 🍲 6 cups chicken broth
- 🌾 ½ cup all-purpose flour
- ⚫ ½ tsp ground pepper
- 🌿 1 tsp thyme, or a fresh sprig (optional)
- 🥛 2–3 cups light cream, half-and-half, or milk

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

### ⚡ Instant Pot

1. 🥓 Set the Instant Pot to sauté, and when it displays HOT, cook the bacon.
2. 🧈 Add the butter and let it melt.
3. 🧅 Add the onions and sauté for about 5 minutes.
4. 🍲 Pour in the broth and scrape the bottom of the pot so nothing sticks.
5. 🥕 Add the carrots, celery, mushrooms, thyme, chicken, and rice with the bouillon.
6. 🔒 Cover and lock the lid with the valve set to sealing. Press SOUP and set to 10 minutes (15 minutes if the chicken is frozen).
7. ⏳ When the time is up, let the pot sit for 5 minutes, then move the valve to venting and remove the lid.
8. 🍗 Remove the chicken, chop or shred it, and return it to the pot.
9. 🌾 In a small bowl, mix the flour and pepper.
10. 🥛 In a large glass measuring cup, slowly whisk the flour mixture into the cream a tablespoon at a time until smooth.
11. 🍲 Add 1 cup of the hot broth to temper the dairy, then pour the whole mixture into the Instant Pot.
12. 🔥 Set to sauté and stir occasionally until the soup thickens.
13. ♨️ Turn off, then press Keep Warm/Cancel to keep warm (10:00 should appear).
14. 🧂 Season with a couple large pinches of kosher salt and as much fresh cracked pepper as you like.
15. 🥣 Ladle into bowls and enjoy.

### 🐢 Slow Cooker

1. 🥓 In a medium frying pan, fry the bacon and add it to the slow cooker.
2. 🧈 Melt the butter, then add the onions, carrots, celery, and mushrooms and sauté for about 5 minutes.
3. 🍲 Add the sautéed vegetables to the slow cooker with the thyme, chicken, rice with the bouillon, and broth.
4. 🐢 Cover and cook on low for 5 hours.
5. 🍗 Remove the chicken, chop or shred it, and return it to the slow cooker.
6. 🥛 In a large glass measuring cup, slowly whisk the flour into the cream a tablespoon at a time until smooth.
7. 🍲 Add 1 cup of the hot broth to temper the dairy, then add the whole mixture to the slow cooker.
8. 🔥 Cover and cook on high for 1 hour.
9. 🧂 Season with a couple large pinches of kosher salt and as much fresh cracked pepper as you like.
10. 🥣 Ladle into bowls and enjoy.

### 🔥 Stovetop

1. 🌾 In a small bowl, combine the pepper and flour, and set aside.
2. 🍲 In a large pot over medium heat, combine the broth and chicken.
3. 🔥 Bring to a boil, then stir in the rice.
4. ⏲️ Cover and cook for 40 minutes, then remove from heat.
5. 🥓 In a medium frying pan over medium heat, cook the bacon, if using, then remove it and set it aside.
6. 🧈 In the same pan, melt the butter (4 tbsp with bacon, 12 tbsp without), then add the carrots, celery, and onion and sauté for 5 minutes.
7. 🫙 Stir in the bouillon and thyme, if using, and cook until the vegetables soften, about 5 more minutes.
8. 🌾 Add the seasoned flour gradually, stirring constantly, to form a roux. Cook the roux for 3–4 minutes to remove the raw flour taste.
9. 🥛 Whisk in the cream a little at a time until smooth, then cook until thickened, about 5 minutes.
10. 🍚 Stir the cream mixture into the broth and rice, add the bacon back in, and cook over medium heat until heated through and the rice is done, 15–20 minutes.

- 🥓 Bacon and white/cremini mushrooms were added because they sounded good.
