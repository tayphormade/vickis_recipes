# Copycat Panera Wild Rice and Chicken Soup (Best)


This recipe combines two recipes: one that commenters said was spot on or better, and one with Instant Pot and slow cooker instructions. Bacon and white/cremini mushrooms were added because they sounded good.

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp butter (one version used up to 12 tbsp, which commenters said was spot on or better; use 4 tbsp if using bacon)
- 5 slices bacon, chopped (optional; not in Panera's soup)
- 1 cup onion, chopped
- 1 cup carrot, diced (about 2 medium)
- 1 cup celery, diced (about 2 ribs)
- 4–8 oz sliced white or cremini mushrooms (optional; not in Panera's soup)
- 6 oz long grain and wild rice mix
- 2 tbsp Better than Bouillon Chicken, or 2 Not-Chicken bouillon cubes
- 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 6 cups chicken broth
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp ground pepper
- 1 tsp thyme, or a fresh sprig (optional)
- 2–3 cups light cream, half-and-half, or milk

## Instructions

### Instant Pot

1. Set the Instant Pot to sauté. When it displays HOT, cook the bacon, then add the butter and let it melt. Add the onions and sauté for about 5 minutes. Pour in the broth and scrape the bottom of the pot so nothing sticks.
2. Add the carrots, celery, mushrooms, thyme, chicken, and rice with the bouillon.
3. Cover and lock the lid. Make sure the valve is set to sealing. Press SOUP and set to 10 minutes (15 minutes if the chicken is frozen). When the time is up, let the pot sit for 5 minutes, then move the valve to venting. Remove the lid.
4. Remove the chicken, chop or shred it, and return it to the pot.
5. In a small bowl, mix the flour and pepper. In a large glass measuring cup, add the cream. Slowly whisk in the flour mixture a tablespoon at a time until smooth. Add 1 cup of the hot broth to the cream mixture to temper the dairy. Pour the whole mixture into the Instant Pot.
6. Set to sauté and stir occasionally so it doesn't burn, until the soup thickens. Turn off, then press Keep Warm/Cancel to keep warm (10:00 should appear).
7. Season with a couple large pinches of kosher salt and as much fresh cracked pepper as you like.
8. Ladle into bowls and enjoy.

### Slow Cooker

1. In a medium frying pan, fry the bacon and add it to the slow cooker. Melt the butter, add the onions, carrots, celery, and mushrooms, and sauté for about 5 minutes. Add these to the slow cooker along with the thyme, chicken, rice with the bouillon, and broth.
2. Cover and cook on low for 5 hours.
3. Remove the chicken, chop or shred it, and return it to the slow cooker.
4. In a large glass measuring cup, add the cream. Slowly whisk in the flour a tablespoon at a time until smooth. Add 1 cup of the hot broth to temper the dairy. Add the whole mixture to the slow cooker. Cover and cook on high for 1 hour.
5. Season with a couple large pinches of kosher salt and as much fresh cracked pepper as you like.
6. Ladle into bowls and enjoy.

### Stovetop

1. In a small bowl, combine the pepper and flour. Set aside.
2. In a large pot over medium heat, combine the broth and chicken. Bring to a boil, then stir in the rice. Cover and cook for 40 minutes, then remove from heat.
3. In a medium frying pan over medium heat, cook the bacon, if using. Remove and set aside. In the same pan, melt the butter (4 tbsp with bacon, 12 tbsp without), then add the carrots, celery, and onion and sauté for 5 minutes. Stir in the bouillon and thyme, if using, and cook until the vegetables soften, about 5 more minutes. Add the seasoned flour gradually, stirring constantly, to form a roux. Cook the roux for 3–4 minutes to remove the raw flour taste.
4. Whisk in the cream a little at a time until smooth. Cook until thickened, about 5 minutes.
5. Stir the cream mixture into the broth and rice, add the bacon back in, and cook over medium heat until heated through and the rice is done, 15–20 minutes.
