# Chicken Soup in a Jar (Quart Jar)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 4
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 5 minutes pressure cooking on high

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🫙 In the jar (layer in this order)

- 🪴 **Spices**
  - 🔴 1 tbsp paprika
  - 🟤 2 tsp ground cumin
  - 🌿 1 tsp ground coriander
  - 🌶️ 1 tbsp chili powder
  - 🧂 1 tsp sea salt
- 🥦 **Dried vegetables**
  - 🍅 ¼ cup sun-dried tomatoes
  - 🧅 ¼ cup dried onion
  - 🌽 ½ cup dried corn
  - 🧄 1 tsp dried garlic
- 🫗 **Tortilla strips, broth & seasoning**
  - 🌮 1 cup tortilla strips
  - 🍲 ½ cup chicken bouillon broth
  - ⚫ Salt and pepper to taste

### 🍗 Chicken

- 🐔 1–2 cups dehydrated chicken (looks like about 2 cups)

### 🍽️ To finish

- 🥛 Sour cream
- 🌮 Tortilla strips

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🫙 Layer the dry ingredients in the jar in the order listed.
2. 🐔 Store the meat separately.
3. ⚡ To cook, pour all the ingredients into an Instant Pot.
4. ♨️ Pressure cook on high for 5 minutes.
5. 🥣 Finish with sour cream and tortilla strips.

---

## 📝 Notes

- ⚠️ The recipe says to store the meat separately, but the original's added note and photo put 1–2 cups of dehydrated chicken in the jar. Either way works for storage, so choose one.
- 💧 The recipe doesn't list how much water to add before pressure cooking.
- 🌮 Keep some of the tortilla strips in a separate bag to sprinkle on top of the finished soup.
