# Spicy Ramen Noodles

🔗 **Source:** [Chili Pepper Madness](https://www.chilipeppermadness.com/recipes/spicy-ramen-noodles/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 2
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- ♨️ **Cook:** 15 minutes
- 🔥 **Calories:** 698 per serving

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍲 **Broth & aromatics**
  - 🍲 5 cups chicken broth (or vegetable broth)
  - 🌶️ 2 serrano peppers, sliced (use jalapeño for milder)
  - 🧄 4 cloves garlic, smashed
  - 🫚 1 thumb-sized knob fresh ginger, peeled and sliced
- 🌶️ **Sauces & seasoning**
  - 🌶️ 3 tbsp gochujang (or to taste; the author usually uses ¼ cup)
  - 🌶️ 1 tbsp chili crisp (or hot chili oil)
  - 🧂 1 tbsp soy sauce, or more to taste
  - 🛢️ 1 tbsp sesame oil
  - 🌶️ 1 tsp sambal oelek (or chili-garlic sauce)
  - 🫙 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
  - 🌶️ Sriracha, to taste (or your favorite hot sauce)
- 🍜 **Noodles & protein**
  - 🍜 4 oz ramen noodles
  - 🦐 ½ lb shrimp, peeled and deveined (or other cooked proteins)
  - 🥚 2 soft-boiled eggs
- 🌿 **Garnish**
  - 🌿 Fresh chopped parsley
  - 🧅 Green onion
  - 🌶️ Chili flakes
  - ⚫ Sesame seeds

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍲 Add the chicken broth to a large pot and heat over medium heat.
2. 🌶️ Stir in the serrano peppers, garlic, ginger, gochujang, chili crisp, soy sauce, sesame oil, sambal oelek, vinegar, and sriracha until fully combined.
3. 🔥 Simmer 10 minutes to develop the flavors.
4. 🍜 Stir in the noodles and shrimp, and cook about 5 minutes, until the noodles soften and the shrimp is cooked through.
5. 🥣 Serve in bowls, and garnish with sliced eggs, parsley, green onion, chili flakes, and sesame seeds.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌶️ Use less gochujang for a milder soup, and use jalapeño instead of serrano for a milder heat.
- 🦐 You can use other cooked proteins in place of shrimp.
