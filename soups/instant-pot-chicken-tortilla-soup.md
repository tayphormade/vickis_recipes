# Instant Pot Chicken Tortilla Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Feasting At Home](https://www.feastingathome.com/instant-pot-chicken-tortilla-soup/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 30 minutes
- 📊 **Calories:** 392 per serving (1¾ cups)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥦 **Vegetables & aromatics**
  - 🫒 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  - 🧅 1 onion, diced
  - 🧄 5 garlic cloves, roughly chopped (or 1–2 tbsp granulated garlic)
  - 🥕 1–2 cups carrots, diced
  - 🌱 1 cup celery, diced
  - 🫑 1 small (4 oz) can mild green chilies (or 1 poblano, diced)
- 🍖 **Chicken & beans**
  - 🐔 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, left whole (or breasts; see notes)
  - 🫘 1 cup dry black beans, or 1–2 cans black beans, or corn (see notes)
- 🫗 **Tomatoes & liquid**
  - 🍅 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes with juices (fire-roasted preferred), or 1½ cups diced tomatoes with juices
  - 🍲 4 cups chicken stock or broth (or water with 4 chicken bouillon cubes)
  - 💧 2 cups water
- 🧺 **Seasoning**
  - 🧂 2 tsp salt, more to taste
  - 🟤 2 tsp cumin
  - 🟥 2 tsp chili powder
  - 🌿 1 tsp dried oregano
  - 🍈 Juice of 1–2 limes
  - 🟪 Chipotle, optional (adobo sauce from canned chipotles, or ground chipotle to taste)
- 🎀 **Garnish (optional)**
  - 🌮 Tortilla strips
  - 🧀 Grated cheese
  - 🍦 Sour cream
  - 🥑 Avocado
  - 🔘 Hot sauce
  - 🍀 Cilantro
  - 🟩 Fresh jalapeño
  - 🎋 Scallions

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧅 Set the Instant Pot to Sauté and cook the onion in the oil for 3–4 minutes while you prep the garlic, carrots, and celery.
2. 🧄 Add the garlic, carrots, and celery to the pot and sauté 3–4 minutes. If using granulated garlic, add it later with the spices.
3. 🍅 Add the tomatoes and their juices, the green chilies, chicken thighs, black beans, stock, water, salt, and all the spices, and stir well.
4. 🫘 Set to pressure cook on high: 25 minutes for dry black beans, or 14 minutes for canned beans. This is the same for fresh or frozen thighs.
5. 💨 Release the pressure manually, covering the steam spout with a towel, or let it release naturally.
6. 🐔 Shred the chicken thighs with two forks, either in the pot or on a cutting board.
7. 🍈 Squeeze in a generous amount of lime juice and taste for salt, adding more as needed. Dry beans usually need more salt.
8. 🔘 Divide among bowls and top as you like. Add hot sauce for more heat.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍲 Use one can of beans instead of two for a more brothy soup.
- 🫘 If your dry black beans are very old, soak them first or extend the pressure time to 30 minutes.
- 🐔 With whole chicken breasts, cook 8–12 minutes (8 for small, 12 for extra-large) and don't use dry beans, since they will overcook the breasts.
- 💡 For a bean-free version, use 3 cups of corn added after the pressure is released.
- 🍳 **Stovetop:** use a big pot or Dutch oven, sauté over medium heat, then simmer covered about 40 minutes, until the chicken is cooked and the beans are tender. Soaking the beans first shortens this.
- 🐢 **Slow cooker:** cook as above, then cover and cook on low 6–8 hours or on high 3–4 hours.
- 💡 Doubling the recipe won't fit in a 6-quart Instant Pot; use a big pot on the stove instead.
- 🌮 For tortilla strips, spray fresh tortillas with olive oil, sprinkle with salt, and bake at 300°F until crisp.
