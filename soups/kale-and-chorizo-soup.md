# Kale and Chorizo Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Emeril's](http://emerils.com/121368/kale-and-chorizo-soup)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥩 **Meat**
  - 🌭 3 cups chorizo or linguiça sausage, sliced in ½-inch rounds
- 🥦 **Vegetables & aromatics**
  - 🧅 ¾ cup chopped onions
  - 🧄 2 tbsp minced garlic
  - 🌿 ¼ cup coarsely chopped parsley
  - 🥔 3 cups peeled, diced potatoes (¼-inch dice, about 2 large)
  - 🥬 4 cups kale, rinsed, stemmed, and torn into pieces
- 🍲 **Broth & seasoning**
  - 🍲 3 quarts chicken stock
  - 🌿 2 bay leaves
  - 🌿 ¼ tsp dried thyme leaves
  - 🌶️ ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
  - 🧂 Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 🌿 **Finish**
  - 🌿 6 tbsp chopped fresh mint (optional)
- 🫒 **Pantry**
  - 🫒 2 tbsp olive oil

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🫒 Heat the oil in a large soup pot over high heat.
2. 🌭 When the oil is hot, add the sausage and onions and sauté, stirring once or twice, about 2 minutes.
3. 🧄 Add the garlic, parsley, and potatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, about 2 minutes.
4. 🍲 Add the stock and kale and bring to a boil.
5. 🌿 Stir in the bay leaves, thyme, and red pepper, and season to taste with salt and black pepper.
6. 🔥 Reduce heat to medium and simmer about 30 minutes, until the potatoes are fork-tender.
7. 🥄 Remove from heat and skim the fat from the top.
8. 🌿 Pour into bowls and stir ½ tsp mint into each, if using, letting it infuse for a minute or two.
9. 🥖 Serve with crusty Portuguese or French bread.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌿 The mint is a finishing touch and optional.
