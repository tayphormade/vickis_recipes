# Super-Delicious Zuppa Toscana

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/143069/super-delicious-zuppa-toscana/)

- ⏱️ **Prep:** 20 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 55 minutes
- 📊 **Calories:** 554 per serving

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Meat**
  - 🌭 1 lb bulk mild Italian sausage
  - 🥓 4 slices bacon, cut into ½-inch pieces
- 🧺 **Seasoning**
  - 🌶️ 1¼ tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  - 🧄 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🧅 1 large onion, diced
  - 🥔 6 medium potatoes, thinly sliced
  - 🥬 ¼ bunch fresh spinach, tough stems removed
- 🫗 **Broth & cream**
  - 🍲 5 (13.75 oz) cans chicken broth
  - 🍼 1 cup heavy cream

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌭 Cook the Italian sausage and red pepper flakes in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat for 10–15 minutes, until crumbly, browned, and no longer pink.
2. 🌭 Drain the sausage and set it aside.
3. 🥓 In the same Dutch oven, cook the bacon over medium heat until crisp, about 10 minutes.
4. 🥓 Drain the bacon, leaving a few tablespoons of drippings in the pot.
5. 🧅 Stir the onion and garlic into the pot and cook about 5 minutes, until soft and translucent.
6. 🍲 Stir in the chicken broth and bring to a boil over high heat.
7. 🥔 Add the potatoes and simmer about 20 minutes, until fork tender.
8. 🔥 Reduce the heat to medium.
9. 🍼 Stir in the cream, cooked sausage, and spinach.
10. 🥬 Cook and stir until the spinach wilts and the sausage is heated through, then serve.

---

- 🥬 The magazine version uses lacinato kale in place of the spinach and has different serving sizes.
