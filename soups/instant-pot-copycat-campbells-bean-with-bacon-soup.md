# Instant Pot Copycat Campbell's Bean with Bacon Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Comfort & Yum](http://www.comfortandyum.com/instant-pot-copycat-campbells-bean-with-bacon-soup/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 4
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 15 minutes with canned beans, 60 minutes with dried

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍱 **Beans**
  - 🫘 4 (15 oz) cans navy beans with liquid, or 2 cups (1 lb) dried navy beans
- 🥦 **Vegetables & ham**
  - 🥕 2–3 carrots, diced
  - 🧅 1 sweet onion, diced
  - 🐷 ½ cup diced ham
- 🧺 **Liquid & seasoning**
  - 🐖 1 quart pork or chicken broth, or water
  - 🫙 2 tsp ham broth base (such as Better Than Bouillon Ham or Orrington Farms)
  - 🧂 1 packet Goya ham seasoning (or smoked paprika, Goya adobo (optional), thyme, and garlic powder)
  - 🍬 1 tsp sugar
  - 🌫️ 1 tsp hickory liquid smoke
- 🎀 **Finish**
  - 🥫 2 tbsp tomato puree
  - 🔸 Salt and pepper, to taste

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🐷 Dice the ham, carrots, and onion.
2. 🐷 Add the beans, ham, carrots, and onion to the Instant Pot.
3. 🐷 Add the broth or water, the ham bouillon, and the Goya ham seasoning, and stir well.
4. 🥄 Make sure the liquid covers the ingredients by about 1 inch, adding more if needed.
5. ♨️ For canned beans, cook on high pressure for 15 minutes. For dried beans, cook on high pressure for 60 minutes or more.
6. 💨 Allow a natural pressure release.
7. 🥫 Open the pot, add the tomato puree, and stir well.
8. 🔸 Season with salt and pepper to taste.

---

## 📝 Notes

- ⚠️ The ingredient list includes 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp hickory liquid smoke, but the steps don't say when to add them.
- 💡 Dried beans need the longer cook time, so the total time is about 1 hour 15 minutes.
