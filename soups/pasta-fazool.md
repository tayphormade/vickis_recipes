# Pasta Fazool (Pasta e Fagioli)

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/255823/pasta-fazool-pasta-e-fagioli/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 2 large or 4 small
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- ♨️ **Cook:** 25 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌭 **Meat & pasta**
  - 🌭 12 oz sweet bulk Italian sausage
  - 🍝 ¾ cup dry elbow macaroni
- 🥦 **Vegetables & beans**
  - 🌱 1 stalk celery, diced
  - 🧅 ½ yellow onion, chopped
  - 🥬 3 cups chopped Swiss chard
  - 🫘 1 (15 oz) can cannellini (white kidney) beans, drained
- 🍅 **Broth & seasoning**
  - 🍅 ¼ cup tomato paste
  - 🍲 3 cups chicken broth, or more as needed, divided
  - 🌶️ ¼ tsp crushed red pepper flakes, or to taste
  - 🌿 ¼ tsp dried oregano
  - 🧂 Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 🫒 **Pantry & finish**
  - 🫒 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 🧀 ¼ cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, plus more for serving

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🫒 Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
2. 🌭 Cook and stir the sausage until browned and crumbly, about 5 minutes.
3. 🔥 Reduce the heat to medium, add the celery and onion, and cook until the onions are translucent, 4–5 minutes.
4. 🍝 Add the dry pasta and cook, stirring, for 2 minutes.
5. 🍅 Stir in the tomato paste until evenly distributed, 2–3 minutes.
6. 🍲 Pour in 3 cups of broth, increase the heat to high, and bring to a boil.
7. 🌶️ Stir in the red pepper flakes, oregano, salt, and pepper.
8. 🔥 Reduce heat to medium and simmer about 5 minutes, stirring often. Add more broth if needed.
9. 🥬 Rinse the chard in a bowl of cold water so the grit sinks, then drain it briefly.
10. 🥬 Add the chard to the soup and cook 2–3 minutes, stirring, until the leaves wilt.
11. 🫘 Stir in the beans and cook 4–5 minutes, stirring, until the pasta is tender.
12. 🧀 Remove from heat, stir in the grated cheese, and serve with more cheese on top.
