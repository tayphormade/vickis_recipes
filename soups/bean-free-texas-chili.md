# Bean-Free Texas Chili

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 7 cups
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- ♨️ **Cook:** about 3 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥩 **Meat**
  - 🐂 3 lb chuck roast, cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 🌶️ **Chiles**
  - 🌶️ 1 oz dried guajillo chile peppers
  - 🌶️ 1 oz dried ancho chile peppers
  - 🫑 1 cup chopped Anaheim green chilies (about 2 peppers)
- 🧅 **Aromatics**
  - 🧅 1½ cups chopped onion (about 1 medium onion)
  - 🧄 4 tsp minced garlic
- 🌿 **Spices**
  - 🌶️ 3 tbsp Gebhardt chili powder
  - 🌶️ 2 tbsp Mexene chili powder (or McCormick)
  - 🍬 2½ tbsp dark brown sugar
  - 🧂 1½ tsp salt
  - ⚫ 1½ tsp ground black pepper
  - 🌿 1 tsp ground cumin
  - 🌿 1½ tsp dried Mexican oregano
- 🥣 **Liquids & thickener**
  - 🛢️ 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  - 💧 5 cups water (2 for soaking the peppers, 1 for the bouillon, 2 for the masa)
  - 🥫 1 large Knorr beef bouillon cube (or 2 small beef bouillon cubes)
  - 🌽 ¼ cup corn masa
  - 🍲 4 cups chicken broth
  - 🍺 ½ cup lager beer (such as Budweiser)
  - 🍅 ⅓ cup El Pato tomato sauce
  - 🫙 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  - 🍋 1 tbsp lime juice
- 🧀 **Toppings**
  - 🧀 Shredded Cheddar cheese
  - 🧅 Chopped onion
  - 🌮 Tortilla chips (on the side)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. ✂️ Cut off the stem end of each pepper, slice up one side, and remove the seeds.
3. 🍳 Place the seeded peppers on a baking sheet and bake 3–4 minutes, until you can smell them. Don't let them get too dark.
4. 💧 Boil 2 cups of water and pour it over the peppers in a large bowl. Let them rehydrate for about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. 🥣 When the peppers are soft, puree them with the soaking water until smooth, adding half the liquid at a time if using a food processor. Set the puree aside.
6. 🔥 Heat a Dutch oven or large pot over high heat. Brown one-quarter of the chuck, then remove it to a bowl.
7. 🥩 Brown the remaining beef in 3 small batches, removing each batch to the bowl.
8. 🛢️ Don't rinse the pot. Add 2 tbsp oil. When hot, add the onion, green chilies, and garlic and cook 5 minutes, until the onions soften.
9. 🌶️ Return the beef to the pot. Stir in the chili powders, brown sugar, salt, black pepper, and cumin, and cook 3 minutes.
10. 💧 Heat 1 cup of water to boiling in the microwave or on the stove, and dissolve the bouillon cube in it.
11. 🥣 Whisk the corn masa into the remaining 2 cups of room-temperature water, and add it to the chili.
12. 🍲 Add the dissolved bouillon, chicken broth, beer, tomato sauce, vinegar, lime juice, oregano, and pureed chilies.
13. ♨️ Bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered 2½–3 hours, until thick and the beef is soft.
14. 💧 If the chili gets too thick before the beef is tender, add ½ cup of water.
15. 🧀 Serve topped with cheddar and chopped onion, with tortilla chips on the side.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌵 This is a bean-free, Texas-style chili with no tomato chunks.
- 🍺 The beer and tomato sauce add depth; the pepper puree gives the main chile flavor.
