# Pinto Bean and Rice Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Tasty Mediterraneo](https://www.tastymediterraneo.com/pinto-beans-rice-soup/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 4
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 10 minutes (plus soaking the beans overnight)
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 1 hour 15 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍱 **Beans & rice**
  - 🫘 360 g (1⅔ cups) dried pinto beans
  - 🍚 150 g (¾ cup) rice (brown rice ideally)
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🧅 1 onion, peeled and finely chopped
  - 🫑 1 red bell pepper, cored and diced
  - 🥔 1 potato, peeled and diced
  - 🧄 1 garlic clove, peeled and thinly chopped
  - 🍅 5 ripe tomatoes, finely chopped
- 🧺 **Liquid & seasoning**
  - 💧 1.5 L (6 cups) water
  - 🧂 ½ tbsp salt, or to taste
- 🥘 **Fat**
  - 🫒 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🫘 Soak the pinto beans in cold water for 10 hours, ideally overnight. Rinse well and drain.
2. 🥄 Wash and prepare all the vegetables as listed.
3. 🫒 Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan, add the garlic, and sauté 1 minute, until lightly golden.
4. 🧅 Add the onion, red pepper, and a pinch of salt, and sauté about 2 minutes, until lightly golden.
5. 🥔 Add the potato and sauté over medium heat for about 7 minutes.
6. 🍅 Reduce the heat to low and add the tomatoes and the drained pinto beans, and stir.
7. 💧 Cover with cold water and cook, covered, over medium-low heat for about 50 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the beans are almost done.
8. 🧂 Add salt and stir.
9. 🍚 Add the rice and cook until done, preferably al dente.
10. 🔥 Remove from the heat and serve hot.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 💡 Already-cooked beans work too. Add them in step 6 and cook only 10–15 minutes before the rest.
- 🍚 Brown rice is recommended.
- 👤 Recipe by Margarita, Tasty Mediterraneo.
