# Panera Bread Cream of Chicken and Wild Rice Soup (Top Secret Recipes Clone)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 7 (makes 10 cups)
- ⏱️ **Chicken brine:** 2 hours
- 🍲 **Simmer time:** about 1 hour (20 + 30 + 10 minutes)
- 🔪 **Prep time:** not listed in the source

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🍗 Chicken

- 💧 2 cups water
- 🧂 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp salt
- 🍗 1 (8 oz) chicken breast filet

### 🥣 Soup

- 🧈 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 🧅 1 cup sliced white onion (about half a medium onion)
- 🥕 1 cup sliced carrots (about 3 medium)
- 🌿 ¾ cup sliced celery (about 2 medium stalks)
- 🥬 ½ cup chopped green cabbage
- 🧄 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 🍲 6 cups chicken broth
- 💧 3 cups water
- 🥄 2 tsp Knorr large chicken bouillon powder (or 2 regular bouillon cubes)
- 🧅 ½ tsp onion powder
- ⚫ ½ tsp ground black pepper
- 🌿 ½ tsp dried parsley
- 🍬 ½ tsp granulated sugar
- 🧂 ¼ tsp salt
- 🌿 ¼ tsp dried thyme
- 🌿 ¼ tsp dried tarragon
- 🟡 ⅛ tsp ground turmeric
- 🥄 ⅓ cup cornstarch
- 🌾 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 🥛 1½ cups half-and-half
- 🍚 1 (4.3 oz) box Rice-A-Roni Long Grain & Wild Rice (flavor packet not used)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧂 **Brine the chicken.** Stir the water and salt in a medium bowl until the salt dissolves. Slice the chicken breast in half horizontally to make two thinner fillets. Add them to the brine and refrigerate for 2 hours. Remove, blot dry with paper towels, dice with a sharp knife, and refrigerate until needed.
2. 🔥 Melt the butter in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the onions turn translucent.
3. 🧄 Stir in the cabbage and garlic. Add the chicken broth, 2 cups of the water (save the last cup), the bouillon, onion powder, pepper, parsley, sugar, salt, thyme, tarragon, and turmeric. Raise the heat until the soup bubbles, then reduce and simmer for 20 minutes.
4. 🥣 Combine the cornstarch and flour with the remaining cup of water and whisk well. Add this slurry, the half-and-half, and the rice (not the flavor packet) to the soup and stir. Bring back to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer for 30 minutes. Add the diced chicken and simmer another 10 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the soup is thick.

---

## 📝 Notes

Other recipes that claim to clone this soup don't get the flavor right. The long grain and wild rice mix many of them use is a good way to get the right amount of rice, but don't use its flavor packet. This recipe makes a better seasoning for the soup. Panera's transparent ingredient policy revealed a couple of surprises, including cabbage.

**Tidbits**

- **Bouillon cubes:** You can substitute them for the powdered bouillon. Heat the reserved water in the microwave until boiling, then dissolve the cube(s) in it before adding it in step 3. Use 2 standard cubes, or 1 Knorr cube (double-size), in place of the 2 teaspoons of powder.
- **The leftover flavor packet:** Instead of tossing it, use it to season plain rice. Bring 1⅔ cups water and 1 tbsp butter to a boil in a medium saucepan over high heat. Stir in ⅔ cup white rice (jasmine works well) and the flavor packet. Reduce the heat and simmer 20–25 minutes, until the water is absorbed.
