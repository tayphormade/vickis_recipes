# Shrimp, Chicken, and Andouille Gumbo

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 4

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧺 **Seasoning mix**
  - 🧂 2 tsp salt
  - 🟧 2 tsp paprika
  - ⚪ 1½ tsp white pepper
  - 🔷 1½ tsp garlic powder
  - 🔶 1 tsp onion powder
  - 🔺 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  - ⚫ 1½ tsp black pepper
  - 🍃 1 tsp dried thyme
  - 🌿 1 tsp dried oregano
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🫑 1 green pepper, seeded and chopped
  - 🌱 2 celery stalks
  - 🧅 ½ onion, chopped
- 🥘 **Roux & frying fat**
  - 🧈 2 tbsp butter
  - 🌾 2 tbsp flour
  - 🛢️ 1 tbsp oil and 1 tbsp butter, for frying the chicken (not listed in the original ingredients)
  - 🌾 1 tsp flour, for dusting the chicken
- 🍖 **Meat & seafood**
  - 🐔 ¼ lb boneless chicken
  - 🍤 ½ lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  - 🌭 6 inches of andouille or kielbasa, cut into pieces
- 🫗 **Stock & tomatoes**
  - 🍅 1 (14.5 oz) can chopped tomatoes
  - 🍲 2 cups chicken stock
  - 🍂 1 bay leaf
  - 🧄 1 tsp garlic, minced
- 🍱 **Rice**
  - 🍚 1 cup rice
  - 🍲 2 cups chicken stock
  - 🔸 ½ tsp seasoning mix

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🐔 Trim the excess fat from the chicken.
2. 🐔 Coat the chicken with the seasoning mix, shake it in a bag with 1 tsp flour, and let it rest 15–30 minutes.
3. 🫑 Combine the green pepper, celery, and onion in a bowl and set aside.
4. 🛢️ Heat 1 tbsp oil and 1 tbsp butter in a heavy skillet, add the chicken, and fry until the crust is brown, 5–8 minutes per side.
5. 🐔 Remove the chicken and keep it warm.
6. 🌾 Add 2 tbsp flour, 2 tbsp butter or olive oil, and 1 tsp seasoning mix to the pan. Cook, whisking constantly, 3½–4 minutes, until dark red-brown. Be careful not to burn it.
7. 🔥 Remove the pan from the heat, add the vegetable mixture, and stir constantly until the roux stops darkening.
8. 🔥 Return the pan to low heat and cook, stirring and scraping the bottom, until the vegetables are soft, about 5 minutes.
9. 🍲 Bring the stock and tomatoes to a boil in a saucepan or Dutch oven.
10. 🍲 Add the roux mixture by spoonfuls to the boiling stock, stirring until it dissolves between each addition.
11. 🌭 Return to a boil, stirring and scraping often, then reduce to a simmer and stir in the andouille and garlic.
12. ♨️ Simmer uncovered about 45 minutes, stirring often toward the end.
13. 🐔 While the gumbo simmers, bone the cooked chicken and cut the meat into pieces.
14. 🍚 Combine the rice, 2 cups chicken stock, and ½ tsp seasoning mix, bring to a boil, cover, and cook 20 minutes.
15. 🐔 When the gumbo is done, stir in the chicken and shrimp and cook 3–4 minutes, until the shrimp is cooked.
16. 🧂 Adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper, and serve over the rice.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍅 The original notes that the tomatoes make this gumbo Creole instead of Cajun.
- ⚠️ The ingredient list says boneless chicken, but the steps say to bone it after cooking.
- 🛢️ The frying oil and butter, and the flour for dusting the chicken, appear only in the steps, not in the original ingredient list.
