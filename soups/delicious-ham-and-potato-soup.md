# Delicious Ham and Potato Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/56927/delicious-ham-and-potato-soup/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 20 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 20 minutes
- 📊 **Calories:** 195 per serving

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥦 **Potatoes, ham & vegetables**
  - 🥔 3½ cups peeled and diced potatoes
  - 🐷 ¾ cup diced cooked ham
  - 🌱 ⅓ cup diced celery
  - 🧅 ⅓ cup finely chopped onion
  - 💧 3¼ cups water
- 🧺 **Seasoning**
  - 🫙 2 tbsp chicken bouillon granules
  - ⚫ 1 tsp ground white or black pepper, or to taste
  - 🧂 ½ tsp salt, or to taste
- 🥘 **Milk sauce**
  - 🧈 5 tbsp butter
  - 🌾 5 tbsp all-purpose flour
  - 🥛 2 cups milk

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥔 Combine the potatoes, water, ham, celery, and onion in a stockpot.
2. 🥔 Bring to a boil, then simmer over medium heat 10–15 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
3. 🫙 Stir in the bouillon, pepper, and salt.
4. 🧈 Melt the butter in a separate saucepan over medium-low heat.
5. 🌾 Add the flour and cook and stir until thick, about 1 minute.
6. 🥛 Whisk in the milk and cook and stir until thick, 4–5 minutes.
7. 🥛 Pour the milk mixture into the stockpot and cook, stirring, until warmed through.
