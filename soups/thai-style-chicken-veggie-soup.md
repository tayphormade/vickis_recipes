# Thai-Style Chicken & Veggie Soup (Green Chef)

*The PDF has no text layer, so this was transcribed from page images.*

Inspired by Thai coconut chicken soup. Bell pepper, shiitake mushrooms, citrusy aromatics, a savory-creamy broth, and jasmine rice, ready in 30 minutes.

**Servings:** 2 (double for 4) · **Cook time:** 30 min · **Calories:** 630 per serving · Gluten-free

**Allergens:** Fish (anchovies in broth), tree nuts (coconut)

## Ingredients

- ½ cup jasmine rice
- 1 red bell pepper
- ⅛ oz dried shiitake mushrooms
- 1 whole scallion
- 10 oz chicken strips
- 2 oz yellow onions
- ⅛ oz fresh ginger
- ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (use sparingly or omit if heat-sensitive)
- 2¼ oz vegetable broth with citrus juice & fish sauce
- 1 chicken stock concentrate
- 5 oz coconut milk
- 1 tsp black sesame seeds

**Pantry:** cooking oil, salt, pepper, water

**You'll need:** small pot with lid, large pot, small bowl, measuring cups and spoons, thermometer

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the rice

1. Bring the jasmine rice, 1 cup water, and about ¼ tsp salt to a boil in a small pot. Stir, then reduce to medium-low, cover, and simmer 16–18 minutes, until tender and the water is absorbed.
2. Remove from heat. Let rest, covered, for 3 minutes. Fluff with a fork.

### 2. Prep

1. Medium-dice the red bell pepper into ½-inch pieces.
2. Roughly chop the dried shiitake mushrooms and place them in a small bowl. Cover with about ½ cup water and let soak at least 5 minutes, until softened. Reserve the soaking liquid.
3. Trim and thinly slice the scallion.

### 3. Start the soup

1. Pat the chicken strips dry. Cut any larger strips in half lengthwise.
2. Heat about 1½ tbsp cooking oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and season with salt and pepper. Cook 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown.
3. Add the yellow onions, ginger, and bell pepper. Season with the crushed red pepper flakes, about ¼ tsp salt, and a pinch of pepper. Cook 2–3 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften.

### 4. Finish the soup

1. Add the mushrooms with their soaking liquid, and the vegetable broth with citrus juice and fish sauce. Stir and cook 1–2 minutes, until warmed through.
2. Add about 1 cup water, the chicken stock concentrate, and the coconut milk. Stir to combine. Bring to a boil, then reduce to medium and season with ¼ tsp salt. Cook 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is fully cooked (165°F internal).
   - If the coconut milk has solidified in the fridge, spoon it into the soup.

### 5. Plate

1. Divide the rice between bowls. Ladle the soup over it. Garnish with scallion and black sesame seeds.
