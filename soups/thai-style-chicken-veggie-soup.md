# Thai-Style Chicken & Veggie Soup (Green Chef)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 2 (for 4, double the measurements where it makes sense)
- ⏱️ **Cook time:** 30 minutes
- 📊 **Calories:** 630 per serving
- ✅ **Gluten-free**

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🍚 Rice

- 🍚 ½ cup jasmine rice

### 🥣 Soup

- 🐔 10 oz chicken strips
- 🥦 **Vegetables & aromatics**
  - 🫑 1 red bell pepper
  - 🍄 ⅛ oz dried shiitake mushrooms
  - 🧅 2 oz yellow onions
  - 🫚 ⅛ oz fresh ginger
  - 🌶️ ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (use sparingly or omit if heat-sensitive)
- 🫗 **Broth & coconut milk**
  - 🫙 2¼ oz vegetable broth with citrus juice & fish sauce
  - 🍲 1 chicken stock concentrate
  - 🥥 5 oz coconut milk

### 🌟 Garnish

- 🌱 1 whole scallion
- 🌑 1 tsp black sesame seeds

### 🧺 Pantry

- 🫒 Cooking oil
- 🧂 Salt
- ⚫ Pepper
- 💧 Water

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍚 Bring the jasmine rice, 1 cup water, and about ¼ tsp salt to a boil in a small pot.
2. ⏲️ Stir, reduce to medium-low, cover, and simmer 16–18 minutes, until the rice is tender and the water is absorbed.
3. 🍴 Remove from heat, let rest covered for 3 minutes, and fluff with a fork.
4. 🫑 Medium-dice the red bell pepper into ½-inch pieces.
5. 🍄 Roughly chop the dried shiitake mushrooms and place them in a small bowl.
6. 💧 Cover the mushrooms with about ½ cup water and let soak at least 5 minutes, until softened. Reserve the soaking liquid.
7. 🌱 Trim and thinly slice the scallion.
8. 🐔 Pat the chicken strips dry with paper towels, and cut any larger strips in half lengthwise.
9. 🫒 Heat about 1½ tbsp cooking oil in a large pot over medium-high heat.
10. 🍗 Add the chicken, season with salt and pepper, and cook 3–4 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown.
11. 🧅 Add the yellow onions, ginger, and bell pepper.
12. 🌶️ Season with the crushed red pepper flakes (sparingly if heat-sensitive), about ¼ tsp salt, and a pinch of pepper, then cook 2–3 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften.
13. 🫙 Add the mushrooms with their soaking liquid, and the vegetable broth with citrus juice and fish sauce.
14. 🥄 Stir and cook 1–2 minutes, until warmed through.
15. 🥥 Add about 1 cup water, the chicken stock concentrate, and the coconut milk, and stir to combine.
16. 🔥 Bring to a boil, then reduce to medium and season with ¼ tsp salt.
17. 🌡️ Cook 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is fully cooked (165°F internal).
18. 🥣 Divide the rice between bowls and ladle the soup over it.
19. 🌑 Garnish with scallion and black sesame seeds, and enjoy.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🇹🇭 Inspired by Thai coconut chicken soup, with bell pepper, shiitake mushrooms, citrusy aromatics, a savory-creamy broth, and jasmine rice.
- 🐟 **Allergens:** fish (anchovies in the broth) and tree nuts (coconut).
- 🥥 If the coconut milk has solidified in the fridge, spoon it into the soup.
- 🍳 **Equipment:** small pot with lid, large pot, small bowl, measuring cups and spoons, thermometer.
