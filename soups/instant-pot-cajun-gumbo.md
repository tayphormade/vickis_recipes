# Instant Pot Cajun Gumbo

- 🔥 **Pot:** 6-quart Instant Pot

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍗 **Meat**
  - 🍗 5 boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into large bite-size pieces
  - 🌭 ½ lb smoked sausage, cut bite-size
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🧅 1 large onion, chopped
  - 🫑 1 bell pepper, chopped
  - 🌱 2 ribs celery, chopped
- 🥣 **Roux & seasoning**
  - 🥣 ¾-pint jar of roux (homemade or store-bought)
  - 🧂 Cajun seasoning, about 1 tsp or to taste (plus more later)
  - 🛢️ 1 tbsp oil
- 💧 **Liquid**
  - 💧 Water, up to the ½ mark on a 6-quart liner

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Set the Instant Pot to Sauté, and cook the seasoned chicken and sausage in the oil until they start to brown.
2. 🫑 Add the onions, bell pepper, and celery and cook until softened.
3. 🧂 Add another bit of Cajun seasoning.
4. 🥣 Add the roux and stir constantly until it melts. Be careful it doesn't stick and burn.
5. 💧 Add water just to the ½ mark on the 6-quart liner.
6. 🔒 Close the lid with the vent set to Sealing. Set to Manual for 10 minutes.
7. ⏳ Let the pressure release naturally for 35 minutes, then turn the knob to Quick Release if any pressure is left.
8. 🦐 To add shrimp, set to Sauté for 3–4 minutes until pink, then switch to Warm and serve. The shrimp may cook in the hot liquid without the extra Sauté.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍚 The author says the gumbo is quite liquidy and has no rice added.
- 🧂 The author used Slap Ya Mama Cajun seasoning, Season-All by McCormick, and sea salt.
- 🥣 **Homemade roux:** bake equal weights of oil and flour in a Dutch oven at 350°F for about 1½ hours, until it's the color you want. If you make a lot, stir every 30 minutes. A shallow layer can bake without stirring.
- 🥫 **Canning:** the original gives 90 minutes for quarts and 75 minutes for pints at 10 lb pressure from sea level to 1,000 ft, and its note for higher altitudes is unclear. Check a current USDA canning guide before canning.
