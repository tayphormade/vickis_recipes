# Birria Ramen

🔗 **Source:** [Chili Pepper Madness](https://www.chilipeppermadness.com/recipes/birria-ramen/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 6
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 3 hours 10 minutes (plus 2 hours marinating, overnight is better)
- 📊 **Calories:** 617 per serving

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Beef**
  - 🥩 2 lb boneless beef chuck
- 🏮 **Dried chiles**
  - 🟥 3 ancho peppers
  - 🌶️ 3 guajillo peppers
- 🥦 **Aromatics & tomatoes**
  - 🧅 1 small white onion, chopped
  - 🍅 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  - 🍅 1 large roasted tomato, chopped
  - 🧄 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 🧺 **Spices**
  - 🌿 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  - 🧂 1 tsp sea salt, or to taste
  - 🪵 ½ tsp cinnamon
  - 🟤 ½ tsp cumin
  - 🫚 ½ tsp ground ginger
  - ⚫ ½ tsp black pepper
- 🫗 **Liquids & fat**
  - 🍶 ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
  - 🍲 3 cups beef stock (1 cup for the sauce, 2 cups for cooking)
  - 🛢️ 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 🎀 **Ramen & toppings**
  - 🍜 12 oz ramen noodles (6 portions; other noodles work too)
  - 🟩 2 jalapeños, chopped or sliced
  - 🧅 1 small red onion, diced
  - 🔴 3 radishes, sliced
  - 🥚 3 soft-boiled eggs, halved
  - 🔸 Red chili flakes, for garnish
  - 🍀 Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Heat a large pan over medium heat and dry toast the dried peppers 1–2 minutes per side, until the skins darken.
2. 🔥 Remove the peppers from the heat, put them in a large bowl, cover with hot water, and steep 20 minutes, until softened.
3. 🛢️ Heat the oil in the same pan over medium heat.
4. 🧅 Add the onion and fresh tomatoes and cook 5 minutes, until softened.
5. 🧄 Add the garlic and cook 1 more minute, stirring.
6. 🧅 Put the cooked onion, tomatoes, and garlic into a food processor.
7. 🥄 Remove the softened peppers and add them to the food processor, reserving the soaking liquid.
8. 🍶 Add the seasonings, vinegar, and 1 cup beef stock to the food processor, and process until smooth. The sauce should be fairly thick. Strain if desired to remove any bits of skin.
9. 🥩 Cut the beef into large chunks and place them in a large bowl.
10. 🥩 Pour the birria sauce over the beef and rub it in. Cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight if possible.
11. 🍅 Add the meat with all of the marinade, the chopped roasted tomato, and the remaining 2 cups beef stock to a large pot.
12. 🔥 Cover and cook over medium heat for 3 hours, until fork tender. It should be fairly saucy.
13. 🍴 Shred the meat with forks to your preference.
14. 🍲 Reserve the birria broth (consommé).
15. 🍜 Boil the ramen noodles according to the package, drain, and divide them between 6 bowls.
16. 🍜 Top the noodles with shredded birria, then cover with the reserved broth.
17. 🥣 Add the toppings and garnish, then serve.

---

## 📝 Notes

- ⚠️ The method says to use olive oil in step 3, but the ingredient list says vegetable oil.
- ⚠️ The reserved chile soaking liquid is set aside in step 7, but the recipe never says to use it.
