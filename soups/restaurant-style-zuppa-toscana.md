# Restaurant-Style Zuppa Toscana

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/13183/restaurant-style-zuppa-toscana/)

- ⏱️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 50 minutes
- 📊 **Calories:** 380 per serving

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Meat**
  - 🌭 1 (16 oz) package smoked sausage
  - 🥓 6 slices bacon
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🧅 ¾ cup chopped onion
  - 🧄 1½ tsp minced garlic
  - 🥔 2 potatoes, cut into ¼-inch slices
  - 🥬 2 cups kale, washed, dried, and shredded
- 🫗 **Broth & cream**
  - 💧 1 quart water
  - 🐔 2 tbsp chicken soup base
  - 🍼 ⅓ cup heavy whipping cream

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 300°F (150°C).
2. 🌭 Place the sausage links on a sheet pan and bake 25 minutes, until done.
3. 🔪 Cut the links in half lengthwise, then cut them at an angle into ½-inch slices.
4. 🥓 Cook the bacon and onion in a large saucepan over medium heat until the onion is almost clear.
5. 🥓 Remove the bacon, crumble it, and set it aside.
6. 🧄 Add the garlic to the pan with the onion and cook about 1 minute.
7. 💧 Add the water, chicken soup base, and potatoes, and simmer 15 minutes.
8. 🥓 Add the crumbled bacon, sausage, kale, and cream.
9. ♨️ Simmer about 5 minutes, then serve.
