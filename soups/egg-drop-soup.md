# Egg Drop Soup

- 🍽️ **Servings:** not listed

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍲 **Broth & aromatics**
  - 🍲 4 cups chicken broth
  - 🍶 1 tbsp sherry or white wine
  - 🧂 1 tbsp soy sauce
  - 🫚 1 slice fresh ginger
  - 🧅 2 green onions, chopped (save some for garnish)
  - 🧄 1 clove garlic, minced
- 🌽 **Thickener & egg**
  - 🌽 3 tsp cornstarch
  - 🥚 2 eggs, beaten

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍲 Bring the broth to nearly boiling with the sherry, soy sauce, ginger, green onions, and garlic.
2. 🔥 Lower the heat to medium-high and cook a few minutes.
3. 🌽 Dissolve the cornstarch in a little broth, then add it to the soup.
4. 🥄 Stir constantly until the soup thickens and is smooth.
5. 🔥 Reduce the heat to low, stirring, and add the beaten eggs in a steady stream until they separate into ribbons.
6. 🫚 Turn off the heat, remove the ginger slice, and serve with a sprinkle of the reserved green onions.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🥚 Pour the eggs in a steady stream while stirring so they form ribbons instead of clumps.
