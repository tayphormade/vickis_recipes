# Potato Leek Soup

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥘 **Leeks & fat**
  - 🧈 ½ cup butter
  - 🎍 2 leeks, sliced
  - 🥓 1 slice bacon, chopped
  - 🧂 Salt and pepper, to taste
- 🫗 **Broth & potatoes**
  - 🍲 1 quart chicken broth
  - ⬜ 1 tbsp cornstarch
  - 🥔 4 cups peeled and diced potatoes (Yukon Gold preferred)
- 🧺 **Seasoning**
  - 🍃 ½ tsp thyme
  - 🔹 ½ tsp celery seed
  - 🔷 ¼ tsp garlic powder
- 🐄 **Dairy**
  - 🥛 1½ cups skim milk (or 2 cups, and leave out the cream)
  - 🍼 ½ cup heavy cream

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧈 In a large pot over medium heat, melt the butter.
2. 🎍 Cook the leeks and bacon in the butter with salt and pepper until tender, stirring frequently, about 15 minutes.
3. ⬜ Stir the cornstarch into the broth, then pour the broth into the pot.
4. 🥔 Add the potatoes and bring to a boil.
5. 🧂 Season with salt, pepper, thyme, celery seed, and garlic powder.
6. 🥔 Reduce the heat and simmer at least 30 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
7. 🥛 Pour in the milk and cream, and heat 5 more minutes, until hot.
8. 🥣 Adjust the seasoning and serve.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 💡 Variation: add a can of drained baby clams for clam chowder.
