# Cajun Chicken and Sausage Gumbo

🔗 **Source:** [Chili Pepper Madness](https://www.chilipeppermadness.com/chili-pepper-recipes/soups-salads-stews/cajun-gumbo/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 6
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 1 hour 39 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Meat**
  - 🐔 1 lb chicken thighs, chopped (chicken breast works too)
  - 🌭 12 oz andouille, sliced ¼ inch thick
  - 🧂 Salt and pepper, to taste
- 🥘 **Roux & fat**
  - 🫒 1 tsp olive oil
  - 🥜 ½ cup peanut oil or vegetable oil
  - 🌾 ½ cup flour
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🫑 1 medium bell pepper, chopped
  - 🧅 1 medium onion, chopped
  - 🌱 1 medium celery stalk, chopped
  - 🧄 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  - 🥒 1 cup okra (frozen is fine)
- 🧺 **Liquid & seasoning**
  - 🧡 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning, or more to taste
  - 🍲 6 cups chicken stock
  - 🍂 3 bay leaves
  - ☘️ 4 tbsp chopped parsley, plus more for serving
  - 🟫 1 tbsp filé powder, to taste (optional)
- 🎀 **To serve**
  - 🍚 Cooked white rice (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🫒 Heat a pan over medium heat and add 1 tsp olive oil.
2. 🐔 Season the chicken with salt and pepper, add it and the sliced andouille to the pan, and cook a couple of minutes per side, until browned. Set aside.
3. 🥜 Add ½ cup peanut oil to a large pot over medium heat, then stir in the flour.
4. ♨️ Cook the roux 20–30 minutes, stirring constantly, until it is the color of chocolate.
5. 🫑 Add the bell pepper, onion, celery, and garlic and cook about 5 minutes.
6. 🐔 Add the chicken and andouille and cook 1 minute.
7. 🥒 Add the okra, Cajun seasoning, and chicken stock, scraping up the brown bits from the bottom.
8. 🍂 Add the bay leaves and cook at medium-low for 1 hour to thicken. Simmer longer if you like.
9. ☘️ Stir in the parsley and cook 5 minutes.
10. 🟫 Remove from the heat and stir in the filé powder, if using.
11. 🍚 Serve in bowls, over rice if desired, and garnish with extra parsley.

---

## 📝 Notes

- ⚠️ The heat level is mild, but the flavor is bold from the Cajun seasoning. For more heat, add cayenne, hot chili flakes, or spicier peppers with the bell pepper.
- 🟫 Filé powder is optional and is stirred in off the heat.
