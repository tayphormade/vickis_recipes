# Panera Bread Cream of Chicken and Wild Rice Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Top Secret Recipes](https://topsecretrecipes.com/panera-bread-chicken-and-rice-soup-copycat-recipe.html)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 7 (makes 10 cups)
- ⏱️ **Chicken brine:** 2 hours
- ♨️ **Simmer time:** about 70 minutes (10 + 20 + 30 + 10 minutes)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🍗 Chicken

- 💧 2 cups water
- 🧂 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp salt
- 🐔 1 (8 oz) chicken breast filet

### 🥣 Soup

- 🥦 **Vegetables & aromatics**
  - 🧅 1 cup sliced white onion (about half a medium onion)
  - 🥕 1 cup sliced carrots (about 3 medium)
  - 🌱 ¾ cup sliced celery (about 2 medium stalks)
  - 🥬 ½ cup chopped green cabbage
  - 🧄 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 🫗 **Broth & water**
  - 🍲 6 cups chicken broth
  - 💧 3 cups water
  - 🫙 2 tsp Knorr large chicken bouillon powder (or 2 regular bouillon cubes)
- 🪴 **Herbs & spices**
  - 🟤 ½ tsp onion powder
  - ⚫ ½ tsp ground black pepper
  - 🌿 ½ tsp dried parsley
  - 🍬 ½ tsp granulated sugar
  - 🧂 ¼ tsp salt
  - 🍃 ¼ tsp dried thyme
  - ☘️ ¼ tsp dried tarragon
  - 🟡 ⅛ tsp ground turmeric
- 🥘 **Fat, thickeners & rice**
  - 🧈 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  - 🌽 ⅓ cup cornstarch
  - 🌾 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  - 🥛 1½ cups half-and-half
  - 🍚 1 (4.3 oz) box Rice-A-Roni Long Grain & Wild Rice (flavor packet not used)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Stir the water and salt in a medium bowl until the salt dissolves.
2. 🔪 Slice the chicken breast in half horizontally to make two thinner fillets.
3. 🧊 Add the chicken fillets to the brine and refrigerate for 2 hours.
4. 🧻 Remove the chicken from the brine, blot each fillet dry with paper towels, dice it with a sharp knife, and refrigerate until needed.
5. 🧈 Melt the butter in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat.
6. 🥕 Add the onion, carrots, and celery and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the onions turn translucent.
7. 🧄 Stir in the cabbage and garlic.
8. 🍲 Add the chicken broth, 2 cups of the water (save the last cup), the bouillon, onion powder, pepper, parsley, sugar, salt, thyme, tarragon, and turmeric.
9. 🔥 Raise the heat until the soup bubbles, then reduce and simmer for 20 minutes.
10. 🌽 Combine the cornstarch and flour with the remaining cup of water and whisk well.
11. 🥛 Add this slurry, the half-and-half, and the rice (not the flavor packet) to the soup and stir.
12. 🔥 Bring back to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer for 30 minutes.
13. 🐔 Add the diced chicken and simmer another 10 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the soup is thick.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍚 The Rice-A-Roni box gives you the right amount of rice in a good blend, but its flavor packet is not the right flavor for this soup.
- 🥬 Cabbage is one of the surprising ingredients found in Panera's published ingredient list.
- 🫙 Bouillon cubes: You can use them instead of the powder. Before adding the 2 cups of water in step 8, heat it in the microwave until boiling and let the cubes dissolve in it for a couple of minutes. Use 2 standard cubes, or 1 Knorr cube (double-size), in place of the 2 teaspoons of powder.
- 📦 Leftover flavor packet: Instead of tossing it, use it to season plain rice. Bring 1⅔ cups water and 1 tbsp butter to a boil in a medium saucepan over high heat. Stir in ⅔ cup white rice (jasmine works well) and the flavor packet. Reduce the heat and simmer 20–25 minutes, until the water is absorbed.
