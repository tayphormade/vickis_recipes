# Slow-Cooker Green Chicken Chili

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 6–8
- ⏱️ **Active time:** 15 minutes
- ♨️ **Total time:** 4 hours 15 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍗 **Chicken & potatoes**
  - 🍗 3 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  - 🍠 2 large sweet potatoes, unpeeled, cut into 1-inch cubes (about 2 lb)
- 🫙 **Salsa & broth**
  - 🫙 1 (16 oz) jar tomatillo salsa (salsa verde), about 2 cups
  - 🍲 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 🌿 **Seasoning**
  - 🌿 2 tsp ground cumin
  - 🧂 2 tsp (or more) kosher salt
  - ⚫ 1 tsp (or more) freshly ground black pepper
- 🫘 **Beans**
  - 🫘 2 cans low-sodium pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 🥑 **Serving (optional)**
  - 🥑 Sliced avocado
  - 🌿 Cilantro leaves
  - 🥛 Sour cream
  - 🧅 Finely chopped onion

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Mix the chicken, potatoes, salsa, broth, cumin, salt, and pepper in a 6-quart or larger slow cooker.
2. 🐢 Cover and cook until the chicken shreds easily with a fork and the sweet potatoes are tender, about 4 hours on high or 6 hours on low.
3. 🫘 Add the beans during the last 30 minutes of cooking.
4. 🍴 Shred the chicken in the slow cooker with 2 forks.
5. 🧂 Taste and season with more salt and pepper if needed.
6. 🥑 Serve topped with avocado, cilantro, sour cream, and/or onion, if using.
7. ❄️ The chili can be made 3 days ahead. Cover and chill.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍳 **Stovetop:** cook in a Dutch oven or large pot, covered, over medium-low heat, bringing it to a simmer first, until the chicken shreds and the sweet potatoes are tender, about 1½ hours. Add the beans during the last 30 minutes.
- 👩‍🍳 Recipe by Rhoda Boone.
