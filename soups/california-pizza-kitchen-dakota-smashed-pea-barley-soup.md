# California Pizza Kitchen Dakota Smashed Pea and Barley Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Top Secret Recipes](https://topsecretrecipes.com/california-pizza-kitchen-dakota-smashed-pea-and-barley-soup-copycat-recipe.html)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8 (the nutrition panel says 4 servings of 2 cups; the recipe header says 8)
- 📊 **Calories:** 450 per serving (2 cups)
- 📉 **Fat:** 3 g per serving

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🫛 2 cups split peas
- 🍲 4 cups vegetable broth
- 💧 6 cups water, for the peas
- 💧 6 cups water, for the barley
- 🌯 ½ cup barley
- 🥕 2 medium carrots, diced (about 1 cup)
- 🌱 ½ stalk celery, diced (about ¼ cup)
- 🧅 ⅓ cup minced onion
- 🧄 1 large clove garlic, minced
- 🍋 2 tsp lemon juice
- 🧂 1 tsp salt
- 🍬 1 tsp granulated sugar
- ☘️ ¼ tsp dried parsley
- 🍃 Pinch of dried thyme
- ⚪ ¼ tsp white pepper
- 🎋 Chopped green onion, for garnish

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🫛 Rinse and drain the split peas, then add them to a large pot with 6 cups water, the broth, onion, garlic, lemon juice, salt, sugar, parsley, pepper, and thyme.
2. 🔥 Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer 75 minutes, until the peas are soft.
3. 🌯 While the peas cook, combine the barley and 6 cups water in a saucepan.
4. 🌯 Bring the barley to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer 75 minutes, until soft and most of the water is absorbed.
5. 🥄 When the pea mixture is thick, puree it until smooth with a hand blender, or with a blender or food processor in batches. You can skip this step for a chunkier soup.
6. 🌯 Drain the barley in a sieve or colander and add it to the pea mixture.
7. 🥕 Add the carrots and celery and simmer 15–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the carrots are tender.
8. 🔥 Turn off the heat, cover the pot, and let the soup sit 10–15 minutes before serving.
9. 🎋 Garnish each serving with chopped green onion.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 💡 A hand blender is the easiest way to smash the peas. A standard blender or food processor also works.
- 🌯 The barley gives the soup a chunky texture and a nutty flavor.
