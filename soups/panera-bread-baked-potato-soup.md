# Panera Bread Baked Potato Soup

🔗 **Source:** [Top Secret Recipes](https://topsecretrecipes.com/panera-bread-baked-potato-soup-copycat-recipe.html)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 8 cups (the intro says 8 servings; the yield line says 5)
- ⏲️ **Cook:** about 1 hour

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🫗 **Potatoes & broth**
  - 🥔 4 cups peeled and diced russet potatoes (about 2 medium)
  - 🧂 2 tsp salt
  - 💧 8 cups water, divided (7 cups in the pot, 1 cup for the thickener)
- 🥦 **Aromatics**
  - 🧈 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  - 🧅 1 cup chopped white onion
- 🥘 **Thickeners**
  - 🍙 3 tbsp white rice flour
  - ⬜ 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 🐄 **Dairy & cheese**
  - 🍼 1¼ cups half-and-half
  - 🧀 4 oz Neufchâtel cheese, softened
  - 🟡 2 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese
  - 🥓 3 tbsp finely chopped cooked bacon (about 3 strips)
- 🧺 **Seasoning**
  - 🟨 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
  - 🟧 ½ tsp Tabasco pepper sauce
  - ⚪ ½ tsp ground white pepper
  - 🌫️ ¼ tsp liquid hickory smoke flavoring
  - 🔶 ¼ tsp onion powder
  - 🔹 ⅛ tsp celery seed
  - 🔸 Pinch of ground turmeric
- 🎀 **Finish**
  - 🎋 2 tbsp chopped green onion
  - 🟢 1 tbsp minced chives

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 Combine 7 cups water with the salt in a Dutch oven or large pot, and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Set the remaining cup of water aside.
2. 🥔 Add the potatoes and boil for 15 minutes.
3. 🥔 While the potatoes boil, melt the butter in a medium sauté pan over medium-low heat.
4. 🧅 Add the onion to the butter and cook 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until translucent.
5. 🥔 When the potatoes have boiled for 15 minutes, add the cooked onion to the pot.
6. 🍙 Whisk the rice flour and cornstarch into the reserved cup of water, and add it to the pot.
7. 🍼 Add the half-and-half, Neufchâtel, bacon, Parmesan, nutritional yeast, Tabasco, white pepper, hickory smoke, onion powder, celery seed, and turmeric.
8. 🔥 Bring back to a boil, then reduce the heat so the soup is just lightly bubbling. Simmer 40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
9. 🟢 Stir in the chives and green onion and cook 5 more minutes, until thick.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🟨 Nutritional yeast ("nooch") gives the savory flavor that chicken broth or bouillon would otherwise supply, so this version uses no broth.
- 🍙 The rice flour and cornstarch keep the soup gluten-free, like the original.
- 👤 From Todd Wilbur's copycat recipe for Panera Bread Baked Potato Soup.
