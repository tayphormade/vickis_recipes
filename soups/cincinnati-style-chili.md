# Cincinnati-Style Chili

🔗 **Source:** [Cooks.com](https://www.cooks.com/rec/doc/prt/0,1749,149162-241206,00.html)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 6 pints
- ♨️ **Simmer:** 2–3 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥩 **Meat & liquid**
  - 🥩 3 lb lean ground beef (not browned; chopped up)
  - 💧 8 cups water (2 quarts)
- 🧅 **Vegetables**
  - 🧅 2 large onions, chopped
- 🫘 **Beans & tomato**
  - 🫘 2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans, added afterward or served on top
  - 🍅 1 (28 oz) can tomato sauce
- 🌶️ **Spices**
  - 🌶️ 4 tbsp chili powder (or more)
  - 🌿 Cumin, amount not listed
  - 🍂 ½ tsp allspice
  - 🫚 ½ tsp ground ginger
  - 🥜 ½ tsp nutmeg
  - 🌶️ ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  - 🧄 ½ tsp garlic powder
  - 🧅 ½ tsp onion powder
  - 🌿 ½ tsp ground cloves
  - 🌿 Bay leaf, amount not listed
  - 🟤 2 tsp cinnamon
  - 🧂 1½ tsp salt
  - ⚫ 1 tsp pepper
- 🫙 **Finishing**
  - 🫙 2 tbsp vinegar (red wine or apple cider)
  - 🫙 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  - 🍫 ½ oz unsweetened baking chocolate
  - 🌶️ 4 drops Tabasco sauce (often added when served)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍳 In an 8-quart kettle, combine the hamburger, water, and onions, and simmer 30 minutes.
2. 🥄 Add the remaining ingredients.
3. ♨️ Simmer uncovered 2–3 hours.
4. 🍝 Serve over spaghetti with onions and cheese, or with steamed buns, hot dogs, onions, and cheese.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍝 Cincinnati ordering: "2-way" is spaghetti and chili, "3-way" adds cheddar, "4-way" adds onions or beans, and "5-way" has everything.
- 🥄 A reader's tip: mix the uncooked beef with the tomato sauce, spices, and vinegar and refrigerate for 2 days, then knead it by hand before adding the water. The reader says this gives a finer texture.
- 🧅 The recipe's comments say the onions were often replaced by garlic and onion powder.
- 🍫 A commenter says the Skyline employee's son claimed there's no chocolate in it, just nutmeg.
