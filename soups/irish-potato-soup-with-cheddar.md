# Irish Potato Soup with Cheddar

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8 (1 cup each)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 **Roux & dairy**
  - 🧈 ¼ cup unsalted butter
  - 🌾 ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  - 🥛 2 cups whole or 2% milk
  - 🧀 8 oz shredded mild or medium cheddar cheese
- 🍲 **Broth & vegetables**
  - 🍲 4 cups chicken broth
  - 🥔 5 cups unpeeled diced russet potatoes (about 2 large)
  - 🥕 ½ cup minced peeled carrot (about ½ large)
  - 🧅 ¾ cup chopped green onions (white and green parts)
- 🥛 **Finish**
  - 🥛 ½ cup heavy cream
  - 🌿 2 tsp minced parsley
  - 🧂 1¼ tsp salt
  - 🔥 ½ tsp hickory liquid smoke
  - 🥣 ¼ cup sour cream
- 🧀 **Garnish**
  - 🧀 Shredded medium cheddar cheese
  - 🥓 Crumbled cooked bacon

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧈 Melt the butter over medium-low heat in a large 3–4 quart saucepan or Dutch oven.
2. 🌾 Stir in the flour and cook until bubbling, about 1 minute.
3. 🥛 Whisk in the milk until no lumps of flour are visible, then turn the heat up to medium.
4. 🧀 Add the shredded cheese and whisk gently until melted and smooth, about 5 minutes.
5. 🍲 Gradually whisk in the chicken broth and turn the heat up to medium-high.
6. 🥔 Add the potatoes and carrot. When the soup begins to boil, reduce to a simmer and cook 30 minutes, uncovered, stirring occasionally.
7. 🧅 Add the green onions, cream, parsley, salt, and liquid smoke, and simmer 40–50 minutes, until the potatoes are soft and the soup has thickened. Stir often and use the spoon to break up some potatoes to help thicken it.
8. 🥣 Take the soup off the heat and stir in the sour cream.
9. 🧀 Top each bowl with shredded cheddar and crumbled bacon.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🧀 Use mild or medium cheddar. Older, sharper cheddar has less water and doesn't melt as smoothly.
- 🧀 Shred your own cheese. Pre-shredded cheese is often dusted with cornstarch and melts less well.
