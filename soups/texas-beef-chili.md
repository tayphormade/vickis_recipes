# Texas Beef Chili

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 6–8
- ⏱️ **Active time:** 1 hour 15 minutes
- ♨️ **Total time:** 4 hours 15 minutes (best made a day ahead)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌶️ **Dried chiles**
  - 🌶️ 4 oz dried ancho chiles (about 9)
  - 🌶️ 2 oz dried guajillo chiles (about 7)
  - 🌶️ 2 oz dried pasilla chiles (about 10)
  - 🌶️ 2 oz dried chipotle chiles (about 8)
  - 🌶️ 8 to 12 dried chiles de árbol (start with 4 for mild or 6 for medium)
- 🧄 **Garlic & aromatics**
  - 🧄 8 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  - 🧅 2 cups finely chopped yellow onion
- 🥩 **Meat & fat**
  - 🥩 6 lb boneless beef chuck, trimmed and cut into ½-inch cubes
  - 🛢️ 7 tbsp lard or vegetable oil
- 🌿 **Seasoning**
  - 🧂 1 tbsp plus 1¾ tsp kosher salt, divided
  - ⚫ 1¾ tsp freshly ground black pepper, divided
  - 🍯 1 tsp honey
  - 🌿 1 tbsp plus 1 tsp ground cumin
  - 🌿 2 tsp dried oregano
  - 🌶️ ½ tsp cayenne pepper, plus more to taste
  - 🍬 2 tbsp dark-brown sugar
  - 🫙 2 tbsp red-wine vinegar
- 🍲 **Liquid & thickener**
  - 🍲 4 cups low-sodium beef broth
  - 🌽 2 tbsp masa harina (recommended, optional)
  - 💧 2 cups water (for the masa)
- 🍋 **Serving**
  - 🍋 Lime wedges
  - 🥛 Sour cream
  - 🧅 Chopped red or white onion
  - 🌶️ Jalapeño
  - 🌿 Cilantro

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Heat a large dry skillet over high heat and toast the peppers in batches, 30–45 seconds per side, until browned in spots but not burned.
2. 🧄 Toast the garlic cloves in their skins in the same skillet, tossing, about 3 minutes, until browned in spots.
3. 🌶️ If the chiles are dusty, rub them gently with a damp paper towel.
4. 🔪 Cut the peppers in half lengthwise, and remove the stems and seeds.
5. 🥣 Place the chiles de árbol in a small heatproof bowl, and the other chiles in a large heatproof bowl.
6. 💧 Pour very hot water over all the chiles until they are submerged, and soak 45 minutes. Weigh them down with a plate if needed.
7. 🧄 Peel the garlic cloves and place them in a blender.
8. 🌶️ Add the soaked chiles (except the chiles de árbol), 4 cups of the soaking liquid, 1 tbsp salt, 1 tsp pepper, and the honey, and purée until smooth.
9. 🌶️ Taste the purée and add chiles de árbol to your preferred heat level.
10. 🥣 Strain the purée through a fine-mesh sieve into a large bowl, pressing it through with a rubber spatula. Set aside.
11. 🥩 In a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat, melt 2 tbsp lard. Add one-third of the beef, ½ tsp salt, and ¼ tsp pepper, and lightly brown 6–8 minutes.
12. 🥩 Transfer the browned beef to a bowl, and repeat with the remaining two batches, seasoning each with ½ tsp salt and ¼ tsp pepper.
13. 🛢️ Lower the heat to medium, add 1 tbsp lard, and cook the onion with ¼ tsp salt until soft, about 5 minutes.
14. 🌿 Add the cumin and cook 1 minute more.
15. 🌶️ Add the chile purée and bring to a simmer. Cook about 15 minutes, until it loses its raw chile flavor.
16. 🍲 Add the broth, oregano, and the beef.
17. 🥣 Combine the masa harina with 2 cups water and add it to the pot.
18. 🔥 Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook uncovered about 3 hours, until the meat is tender and the broth has thickened.
19. 🍬 Add the sugar and vinegar 1 tablespoon at a time, tasting after each addition.
20. 🌶️ Add cayenne, salt, and pepper to taste.
21. ⏲️ Let the chili sit at least 1 hour, and up to overnight, to let the flavors meld.
22. 🍋 Serve with lime wedges, sour cream, onion, jalapeño, and cilantro.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🛢️ The ingredient list calls for 7 tbsp of lard or oil, but the steps only mention 2 tbsp for the beef and 1 tbsp for the onion. The steps don't say to add more fat for the second and third batches of beef, so check that.
- 🌽 Masa harina is a ground corn flour used for tortillas, tamales, and arepas. It thickens the chili and adds an earthy richness.
- 💧 Don't reuse the water the chiles soaked in. One reviewer says it turns bitter.
- 🥄 Some reviewers suggest making the purée one day and the chili the next.
- 👩‍🍳 Recipe by Rhoda Boone.
