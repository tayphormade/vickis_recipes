# Loaded Baked Potato Soup

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8 (makes 8 cups)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 **Roux & broth**
  - 🧈 ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
  - 🧅 1 cup diced yellow onion (about ½ medium onion)
  - 🌾 ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  - 🍲 4 cups chicken broth
  - 💧 2 cups hot water
  - 🧂 1 Knorr extra-large chicken bouillon cube (or 2 regular-size cubes)
- 🥔 **Potatoes**
  - 🥔 6 cups peeled and diced russet potatoes (from about 3 medium potatoes)
  - 🥔 ½ cup dehydrated potato flakes
- 🌿 **Seasoning & finish**
  - 🧂 1½ tsp salt
  - ⚫ ½ tsp coarse ground black pepper
  - 🌿 ¼ tsp dried basil
  - 🥛 ½ cup heavy cream
- 🧀 **Garnish**
  - 🧀 Shredded cheddar/Jack cheese blend
  - 🥓 Crumbled cooked bacon
  - 🧅 Sliced green onions

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧈 Melt the butter in a Dutch oven or saucepan that holds at least 3 quarts over medium heat.
2. 🧅 Add the onion and cook it, without browning, for about 5 minutes, until it begins to turn translucent. Lower the heat if it cooks too fast.
3. 🌾 Stir in the flour and cook over medium-low heat until it begins to darken to tan, about 5 minutes.
4. 🍲 Add the chicken broth and turn the heat up to medium.
5. 🧂 Dissolve the bouillon cube in the hot water, then add it to the soup along with the diced potatoes.
6. 🔥 Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook 8–10 minutes, until the potatoes begin to soften.
7. 🥔 Stir in the potato flakes, salt, pepper, and basil, and simmer 10 more minutes, stirring occasionally.
8. 🥛 Stir in the cream.
9. 🧀 Serve hot, topped with shredded cheese, bacon, and green onions.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🥔 The raw potatoes cook right in the soup, so you don't need to bake them.
- 🥔 The potato flakes thicken the soup and add potato flavor. The recipe warns against using a lot of flour, which adds no flavor.
- 👤 From Todd Wilbur's copycat recipe for Outback Steakhouse Baked Potato Soup.
