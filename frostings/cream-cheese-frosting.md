# Cream Cheese Frosting

🔗 **Source:** [Southern Living](https://www.southernliving.com/recipes/cream-cheese-frosting-25)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 4 cups
- ⏲️ **Active time:** 10 minutes
- ⏳ **Total time:** 10 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 ½ cup butter or margarine, softened
- 🧀 11 ounces cream cheese, softened (about a block and a half)
- 🍬 1 (16-ounce) package powdered sugar
- 🌼 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Beat the butter and cream cheese at medium speed with an electric mixer until creamy.
2. 🍬 Add the powdered sugar and vanilla extract; beat at high speed for 10 seconds, or until smooth.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Southern Living Editors.
- 🎂 The source describes this as its best cream cheese frosting, suitable for layer cakes, cupcakes, and sheet cakes.
