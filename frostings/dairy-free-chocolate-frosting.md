# Dairy-Free Chocolate Frosting

🔗 **Source:** [MOMables](https://www.momables.com/dairy-free-chocolate-frosting/)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 12 servings (the source says it frosts a 2-layer, 9" round cake)
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 5 minutes
- 🏷️ **Diet:** Gluten free (per the source), dairy free, vegan
- 🔢 **Per serving (as listed by the source):** 212 calories; 9.7 g fat; 2.4 g saturated fat; 1.1 g trans fat; 0 mg cholesterol; 8.4 mg sodium; 32.8 g carbohydrates; 29.5 g sugar; 1.1 g fiber; 0.6 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 ½ cup vegetable shortening
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🍫 ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 🍬 3 cups powdered sugar
- 🥥 ½ cup unsweetened, canned coconut milk or non-dairy creamer

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 In a large bowl with an electric mixer, beat the vegetable shortening until light and fluffy.
2. 🍫 Add the vanilla, cocoa powder, and powdered sugar.
3. 🥛 While mixing on medium speed, add the coconut milk or creamer slowly until all ingredients are combined and the frosting is smooth.
4. 🔧 If the frosting is too thick, add more creamer 1 tablespoon at a time. If it's too runny, add more powdered sugar 1 tablespoon at a time.
5. 🎂 This frosting is ready to use once the cake has cooled completely.
