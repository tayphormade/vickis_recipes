# Buttermilk Glaze

🔗 **Source:** [Southern Living](https://www.southernliving.com/recipes/buttermilk-glaze-2)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 1½ cups

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🥄 1½ teaspoons baking soda
- 🥛 ½ cup buttermilk
- 🧈 ½ cup butter or margarine
- 🍯 1 tablespoon light corn syrup
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Bring the first 5 ingredients (sugar, baking soda, buttermilk, butter or margarine, and corn syrup) to a boil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
2. 🥄 Boil, stirring often, for 4 minutes.
3. 🌼 Remove from heat, and stir in the vanilla.
