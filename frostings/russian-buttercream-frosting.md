# Russian Buttercream Frosting (Vanilla Condensed Milk Buttercream)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 3 cups
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes (per the Sugarologie PDF)
- ⏳ **Total:** 15 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 1½ cups (337 g) unsalted butter, cool room temperature
- 🥫 1 cup (311 g) sweetened condensed milk
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt (Diamond Crystal)
- 🌼 ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Using the whisk attachment, beat the butter on medium-high speed for at least 5 minutes, until it has become slightly paler in color and a little fluffier.
2. 🥛 Add the sweetened condensed milk in thirds. Add the first third and whip on medium-high speed until thoroughly combined, then repeat with the rest. Once the milk has been added, continue to whisk for another minute. You should have a thick, creamy buttercream that balls up inside the whisk.
3. 🌡️ If the buttercream looks greasy, curdled, or broken at any time during this process, the butter was too cold. Just keep mixing; eventually the butter will warm as it mixes.
4. 🥄 Switch over to the paddle attachment. The whisk adds many air pockets, and the paddle smooths those out.
5. 👅 Taste it, and add more condensed milk if desired. Some bakers like the flavor of just sweetened condensed milk. The author always adds salt, which enhances the sweetness, and a splash of vanilla.
6. 🎨 The buttercream will be cream-colored from the condensed milk and butter. For a whiter frosting, dip the tip of a toothpick in purple gel food coloring and mix it in. This cancels out the orange and yellow hues. Start small, since you can always add more but cannot take it away if it turns out too purple.
7. 🥄 Mix the buttercream on low speed until it reaches a smooth, spreadable, and soft texture, about 2 to 3 minutes. Use the frosting immediately, or store it in the fridge for at least a few weeks. Let it come to room temperature, smooth it on the mixer, and it is ready to use.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Recipe and notes from Adriana (the notes below are from the author).
- 🧈 Butter: take the butter out of the fridge before using it to warm it up slightly. It should not be super soft, but when you press it with your finger, it should indent easily, like a ripe peach or avocado. This ensures the butter will whip to the right texture to emulsify the condensed milk.
- 🥫 Condensed milk: there are many brands of sweetened condensed milk with different flavors and colors. The author used Eagle Brand. The source links to a Serious Eats article about the differences.
- 📈 This buttercream has a high capacity for condensed milk. If it is not sweet enough, add a tad more in step 2. Start with just a tablespoon, mix, and taste again. The more condensed milk you add, the softer the frosting will be.
