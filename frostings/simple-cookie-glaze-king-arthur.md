# Simple Cookie Glaze

🔗 **Source:** [King Arthur Flour](http://www.kingarthurflour.com/shop/PrintRecipe?RID=176&radio=3)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** ⅔ cup
- ⏱️ **Hands-on time:** 15 to 20 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 2¼ cups confectioners' sugar, sifted
- 🍯 2 tablespoons light corn syrup
- 🥛 1½ to 2 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon milk
- 🎨 Food coloring (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 In a small bowl, whisk together the confectioners' sugar, corn syrup, and 1½ tablespoons of the milk until smooth.
2. 🎨 Add food coloring, if desired.
3. 🥄 Spread one cookie with glaze, using a table knife or offset spatula. If it doesn't smooth out after 1 minute, dribble in additional milk, ½ teaspoon at a time, until the glaze reaches the right consistency.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: King Arthur Flour, "Simple Cookie Glaze." Printed 12/22/2013.
- 💡 The source says the glaze dries hard and shiny, and is good for coating cookies before decorating them with food-safe pens or markers. It is thick and may not be perfectly smooth when applied, but it should settle into a smooth surface within half a minute or so.
- 💡 The source says to start with a glaze that's thicker than you think it should be, then add milk little by little, because it's easier to add more liquid than to stir in more sugar.
- ⚠️ The source says to measure accurately. Too little milk and the glaze won't spread well; too much and it will be thin, spotty, and develop splotches overnight.
- 🎨 Once the glaze has hardened, you can color it with food-safe markers, or pipe another color over the top with royal icing. You can sprinkle sugar on wet royal icing for a sparkly effect.
