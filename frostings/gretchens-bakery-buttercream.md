# Buttercream Recipe (Gretchen's Bakery)

🔗 **Source:** [Gretchen's Bakery](https://www.gretchensbakery.com/buttercream-recipe/)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 6 cups
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- 🕑 **Total time:** 15 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 1½ cups (3 sticks) (336g) unsalted butter
- 🥫 1½ cups (336g) Hi Ratio solid vegetable shortening
- ❄️ 6 cups (720g) confectioners sugar (Domino Brand 10X sugar), sifted
- 🥛 6 tablespoons (90ml) heavy cream
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🧴 1 teaspoon butter extract
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine the butter and shortening in the KitchenAid (or stand mixer) bowl with the paddle attachment.
2. 🔥 Whip on medium to high speed for about 3 minutes.
3. 🧂 Scrape the sides and bottom of the bowl to make sure it is evenly mixed, and add the salt. Mix well.
4. ❄️ Stop the mixer and add the sifted confectioners sugar all at once.
5. 🥣 Mix on low speed until incorporated, scrape the bottom and sides of the bowl, then mix on high speed for 3 minutes.
6. 🥛 Scrape the bowl again, then, while mixing on low speed, slowly drizzle in the heavy cream.
7. 🥣 Increase the speed to high and whip for another 5 minutes.
8. 🌼 Add the flavor extracts. You are done!
9. ☁️ The icing will gain volume almost to the top of a 6-quart KitchenAid bowl and get very white. If you are using a hand beater, the volume will not get as high, since hand beaters cannot incorporate air as efficiently as KitchenAid or stand mixers.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Gretchen's Bakery, "Buttercream Recipe." Printed 6/20/2018.
- 🎂 A comment added to the source says: for a 2-layer 9-inch cake with minimal decoration, use ⅔ of the recipe. "Very good - Light and fluffy." For chocolate frosting, add 3 tablespoons cocoa for ⅔ of the recipe.
- 🌱 Vegan version (per the source): use all shortening and almond milk instead of heavy cream, but only 3 Tbs, not 6.
- 🧊 Storage (per the source): keeps at room temperature in an airtight container for up to 3 days; refrigerate for up to 4 weeks; freeze for 6 months.
- 🌡️ Let the butter and shortening come to room temperature before mixing. The heavy cream can stay cold.
- ❄️ The source specifies Domino 10X confectioners sugar, which it says will not make the buttercream gritty. If you can't get it, sift other brands a couple of times before adding them. Confectioners sugar can go "off," so use fresh sugar.
- 🥫 The source specifies Sweetex HiRatio shortening, which it notes is hard to find. The source says Crisco and other solid vegetable shortenings can leave a greasy mouthfeel, and that another brand has been recommended.
