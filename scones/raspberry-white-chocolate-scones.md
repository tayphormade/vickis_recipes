# Raspberry-White Chocolate Scones

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 4 (yield: 4 large scones)
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 45 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 4 hours 25 minutes (includes the dried-raspberry step)
- ⏳ **Additional:** 8 hours 30 minutes (overnight drying of the raspberries)
- ⏳ **Total:** 13 hours 40 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 225°F (110°C) for drying the raspberries; 400°F (200°C) for baking the scones
- 🔢 **Per serving (as listed by the source):** 563 calories; 24.8 g fat; 95.5 mg cholesterol; 788.2 mg sodium; 75.1 g carbohydrates; 11.8 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Dried raspberries

- 🍇 2 (6 ounce) containers fresh raspberries

### Scones

- 🍫 2½ ounces white chocolate, or as needed
- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour, or more as needed
- 🍬 1 tablespoon white sugar, or more to taste
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon kosher salt
- 🥄 4 teaspoons baking powder
- 🧈 5 tablespoons ice cold unsalted butter
- 🥛 ¾ cup milk
- 🥛 2 tablespoons sour cream
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🥛 1 tablespoon milk

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 225°F (110°C).
2. 📄 Line a baking sheet with a silicone liner.
3. 🍇 Place fresh raspberries about 1 inch apart on the prepared baking sheet.
4. ⏲️ Bake in the preheated oven for 4 hours, then turn the oven off, leaving the berries inside for 8 hours, or overnight.
5. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with a silicone liner.
6. 🔪 Roughly chop the dried raspberries into smaller pieces and measure out ½ cup for this recipe. Reserve any extra for another use.
7. 🧀 Grate the white chocolate on the fine side of a grater until you have ½ cup.
8. 🥣 Whisk 2 cups flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder together in a mixing bowl. Grate the cold butter into the flour mixture, tossing every so often to coat it with flour so it doesn't start to clump. When all the butter has been added, work it into the flour with a pastry blender. Add the grated white chocolate and toss with a fork to incorporate.
9. 🥛 Whisk ¾ cup milk and the sour cream together in a liquid measure, then pour that into the flour mixture. Mix with a fork until everything just barely comes together to form a shaggy dough.
10. 🤲 Transfer the dough to a generously floured work surface, and without kneading, push and pack it together with your hands until it holds its shape, adding a little flour if it's too sticky. Press the dough into a rectangle about ¾ inch thick, and roughly twice as long as it is wide.
11. 🍇 Sprinkle the dried raspberries evenly over the dough. Spread the berries out almost to the edges and press them into the dough gently. Using a bench scraper, lift one third of the dough from one of the shorter edges and fold it in over the center third; press it down and square off the edges. Repeat with the other side so the scone dough is three layers thick.
12. 🔪 Cut the rectangle in half with the bench scraper, then cut each half into 2 triangles. Transfer the scones to the prepared baking sheet.
13. 🥚 Whisk the egg and 1 tablespoon milk together. Brush egg wash over each scone, and sprinkle with sugar.
14. 🔥 Bake in the upper center of the preheated oven until browned, about 25 minutes.
15. 🧊 Quickly and carefully transfer to a rack, and let cool completely before serving, about 30 minutes.

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Chef John, Allrecipes. The source's title is "Raspberry-White Chocolate Scones."
- 🍇 Chef's note: use any extra raspberries to top your cereal or to snack on. You can purchase dried raspberries instead of making your own.
- 🍫 Tip: if you don't have a bar of white chocolate to grate, use white chocolate chips and pulse them on and off in a food processor. For more white chocolate flavor, add some white chocolate chips with the raspberries in Step 11.
- 🧊 Tip: to get "ice cold" butter, put the butter in the freezer for 1 hour before grating it.
- 🍬 Tip: if you like your scones on the sweet side, add an extra tablespoon of sugar with the dry ingredients in Step 8.
- ⚠️ The source does not say which sugar or how much to use for the egg wash topping in Step 13.
