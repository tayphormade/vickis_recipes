# Classic Scones

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 12 servings (1 scone per serving)
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 425°F (218°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 17 minutes, or until golden
- 🔢 **Per serving (as listed by the source):** 149 calories; 30% of calories from fat; 5 g fat; 3 g saturated fat; 1.3 g monounsaturated fat; 0.3 g polyunsaturated fat; 3 g protein; 23.4 g carbohydrates; 0.8 g fiber; 13 mg cholesterol; 1.2 mg iron; 203 mg sodium; 56 mg calcium

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour (about 9 ounces)
- 🍬 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 🥄 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 5 tablespoons chilled butter, cut into small pieces
- 🥛 ½ cup fat-free milk
- 🌼 ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥚 1 large egg white
- 🍇 ⅓ cup dried currants
- 🥛 2 teaspoons fat-free milk
- 🍬 2 teaspoons turbinado or granulated sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C).
2. 🥄 Lightly spoon the flour into dry measuring cups; level with a knife. Combine the flour, 3 tablespoons granulated sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl; stir with a whisk. Cut in the butter with a pastry blender or 2 knives until the mixture resembles coarse meal.
3. 🥛 Combine ½ cup milk, vanilla, and egg white in a bowl. Add the milk mixture to the flour mixture, stirring just until moist (the dough will be soft). Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Sprinkle the surface of the dough with the dried currants. With floured hands, knead 4 times, or just until the currants are incorporated.
4. 🥄 Pat the dough into an 8-inch circle on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Cut the dough into 12 wedges, cutting into, but not through, the dough. Brush 2 teaspoons milk over the surface of the dough; sprinkle with 2 teaspoons turbinado sugar.
5. 🔥 Bake at 425°F (218°C) for 17 minutes, or until golden. Serve warm, or cool on a wire rack.
