# Tart Cherry-and-Vanilla Scones

- 🍽️ **Serves:** 8
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 25 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** 18 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 400°F (204°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍒 ¾ cup dried tart cherries
- 💧 ¼ cup boiling water
- 🌾 1¾ cups (8¾ oz./250 gm) all-purpose flour
- 🍬 ⅓ cup sugar
- 🌽 ¼ cup (1¼ oz./37 gm) yellow cornmeal
- 🥄 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 2 tablespoons chilled stick butter, cut into small pieces
- 🧈 2 tablespoons vegetable shortening
- 🥛 ⅓ cup plain fat-free yogurt
- 🥛 ¼ cup evaporated milk
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🌿 ¼ teaspoon almond extract
- 🥚 1 large egg white, slightly beaten
- 🍬 2 teaspoons sugar (for sprinkling on top)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍒 Combine the cherries and boiling water in a bowl; cover and let stand 10 minutes, or until softened. Drain; set aside.
2. 🔥 Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C).
3. 🥣 Combine the flour and the next four ingredients (through salt) in a large bowl; cut in the butter and shortening until the mixture resembles coarse meal.
4. 🥛 Combine the cherries, yogurt, milk, and extracts; add to the flour mixture, stirring just until moist (the dough will be sticky). Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface; knead lightly 4 times with floured hands.
5. 🥄 Pat the dough into an 8-inch circle on a greased baking sheet or on parchment paper. Brush the egg white over the dough, and sprinkle with the 2 teaspoons of sugar. Cut the dough into 8 wedges, cutting into but not through the dough.
6. 🔥 Bake at 400°F (204°C) for 18 minutes, or until golden. Serve warm.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍓 Variation (from the source): Cranberry-and-Vanilla Scones. Substitute ¾ cup dried cranberries for the tart cherries and omit the almond extract. Bake as directed, and serve warm.
