# Cranberry Orange Scones (Starbucks Copycat)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 6 small scones
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 400°F (204°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 12 to 15 minutes, or until lightly browned

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Scones

- 🌾 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
- 🍬 5 teaspoons sugar, divided
- 🍊 1 tablespoon grated orange peel
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🥄 ⅛ teaspoon baking soda
- 🧈 ½ cup cold butter
- 🍒 ½ cup dried cranberries
- 🍊 2 tablespoons orange juice
- 🍊 ½ teaspoon orange extract
- 🥛 2 tablespoons half-and-half cream
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🥛 1 tablespoon milk

### Filling (optional)

- 🍒 ¼ cup dried cranberries
- 🍊 ¼ teaspoon orange zest
- 💧 ½ tablespoon hot water
- 🍯 1 teaspoon corn syrup

### Glaze (optional)

- 🍬 ½ cup confectioners' sugar
- 🍊 1 tablespoon orange juice

### Orange butter

- 🧈 ½ cup butter, softened
- 🍊 2 to 3 tablespoons orange marmalade

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 In a large bowl, combine the flour, 4 teaspoons of the sugar, orange peel, baking powder, salt, and baking soda. Cut in the butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs; set aside.
2. 🥛 In a small bowl, combine the cranberries, orange juice, extract, cream, and egg. Add to the flour mixture and stir until a soft dough forms.
3. 🥄 On a floured surface, gently knead 6 to 8 times. Pat the dough into a circle about ¾" to 1" thick. Cut into 6 wedges. Separate the wedges and place them on an ungreased baking sheet.
4. 🍒 Filling (optional): In a small food processor or blender, mix the remaining ¼ cup dried cranberries, ¼ teaspoon orange zest, ½ tablespoon hot water, and 1 teaspoon maple syrup. Blend until a rough paste forms (you want some cranberry remnants). Using a sharp knife, slice the scones through the middle, beginning at the tip and stopping ¾ of the way back. Spread about a teaspoon of cranberry paste and sandwich it closed. Repeat with the remaining scones.
5. 🥛 Brush with milk, and sprinkle with the remaining sugar (1 teaspoon, from the 5 teaspoons divided).
6. 🔥 Bake at 400°F (204°C) for 12 to 15 minutes, or until lightly browned. Remove to a wire rack.
7. 🍬 Combine the glaze ingredients if desired, and drizzle over the scones. Combine the orange butter ingredients, and serve with the warm scones.
