# Gluten-Free Brownies

- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 20 to 30 minutes, until not wet in the middle

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 1 cup butter, melted
- 🍬 2 cups sugar
- 🍫 ½ cup cocoa powder
- 🥚 4 eggs
- 🌾 1 cup gluten-free flour
- 🍫 1 cup chocolate chips

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. 🧈 Grease a 9x13-inch pan.
3. 🧈 Melt the butter.
4. 🥄 In a bowl, mix the melted butter, sugar, and cocoa powder until well mixed.
5. 🥚 Add the eggs and gluten-free flour, and mix well.
6. 🍫 Stir in the chocolate chips.
7. 🥄 Pour the batter into the greased pan.
8. 🔥 Bake for 20 to 30 minutes, until not wet in the middle.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The author has used Better Batter brand gluten-free flour. A blend of brown rice flour and tapioca (without gums or psyllium) also worked.
- 🌟 Coconut oil can replace the butter. The brownies came out soft and oily at high altitude, so add 1 to 2 extra tablespoons of flour.
