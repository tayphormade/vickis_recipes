# Apple-Date Bars

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 16 (1 bar each)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 325°F (165°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 1 hour 5 minutes, until a wooden pick comes out clean
- 📊 **Per bar:** 279 calories, 9 g fat, 48.5 g carbohydrates, 2 g fiber, 3.3 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🌾 Dry ingredients

- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour (about 9 ounces)
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt

### 🧈 Batter

- 🍬 2 cups sugar
- 🧈 7 tablespoons butter, softened
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🥚 2 large egg whites
- 🍎 ¼ cup applesauce
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### 🍎 Fruit and nuts

- 🌴 1 cup chopped pitted dates
- 🌾 1 teaspoon all-purpose flour (to toss with the dates)
- 🍏 1½ cups chopped, peeled Granny Smith apple (about 1 large)
- 🍎 1½ cups chopped, peeled Red Delicious apple (about 1 large)
- 🍋 ½ teaspoon fresh lemon juice
- 🌰 ⅔ cup chopped pecans

### 🧴 For the pan

- 🧴 Cooking spray

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 325°F (165°C).
2. 🥄 Lightly spoon 2 cups of flour into dry measuring cups, and level with a knife.
3. 🥄 Combine the 2 cups flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl. Stir with a whisk. Set aside.
4. 🧈 Beat the sugar and butter at high speed for 2 minutes, until light and fluffy.
5. 🥚 Add the egg and egg whites, beating well after each addition.
6. 🍎 Stir in the applesauce and vanilla.
7. 🥄 Gradually add the flour mixture to the sugar mixture. Stir just until combined, to form a stiff batter.
8. 🌴 Toss the dates with 1 teaspoon flour.
9. 🍏 Toss the apples with the lemon juice.
10. 🌰 Add the dates, apples, and pecans to the batter. Stir just until combined.
11. 🥣 Pour the batter into a 13x9-inch baking dish coated with cooking spray.
12. 🔥 Bake for 1 hour 5 minutes, or until a wooden pick inserted in the center comes out clean.
13. 🧊 Cool completely on a wire rack.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Recipe source: Cooking Light, October 2006, by Lee Harrelson (MyRecipes).
- 🌟 The bars are a makeover of the original, which used 10 tablespoons of butter, 2 whole eggs, and ⅔ cup pecans.
- 🌟 You can use walnuts instead of pecans, or raisins instead of dates.
