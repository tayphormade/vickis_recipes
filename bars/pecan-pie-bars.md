# Pecan Pie Bars

🔗 **Source:** [The Salted Pepper](https://thesaltedpepper.com/pecan-pie-bars/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 40 bars
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 20 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 45 minutes
- 📊 **Per bar:** 158 calories

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🧈 Shortbread crust

- 🍬 ⅔ cup white sugar
- 🧈 1 cup salted butter, room temperature
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon fine sea salt
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour

### 🥧 Pecan pie filling

- 🍯 ⅔ cup light corn syrup
- 🍬 ¾ cup white sugar
- 🧈 6 tablespoons salted butter
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon fine sea salt
- 🍯 1 tablespoon blackstrap molasses
- 🥚 3 large eggs
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🌰 1 to 1½ cups pecan pieces

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧈 Cream the butter and sugar until fluffy. Add the vanilla and salt, and mix.
2. 🌾 Add the flour and mix until crumbly. Press the mixture into a ball.
3. 🧈 Coat a 9x13-inch pan with butter and line it with parchment. Press the shortbread dough evenly into the pan, starting with the edges.
4. ❄️ Chill the crust for about 30 minutes while you make the filling.
5. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Bake the crust for 10 minutes. Cool for 5 to 10 minutes.
6. 🍯 Heat the corn syrup, sugar, and butter in a pot on high, stirring until the sugar dissolves and the butter melts. Stir in the salt and molasses. Turn off the heat.
7. 🌡️ Let the sugar mixture cool slightly.
8. 🥚 Beat the eggs and vanilla in a bowl. Temper the eggs by whisking in about ¼ cup of the warm sugar mixture at a time, until you have about a cup. Then whisk in the rest of the filling.
9. 🥧 Pour the filling over the shortbread and scatter the pecans on top.
10. 🔥 Bake at 325°F (165°C) for 30 minutes.
11. 🧊 Cool at room temperature for 1 to 2 hours, then cut. For cleaner cuts, refrigerate for several hours or overnight first.
