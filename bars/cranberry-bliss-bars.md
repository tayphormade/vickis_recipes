# Cranberry Bliss Bars (Starbucks Copycat)

🔗 **Source:** [Food.com](https://www.food.com/recipe/cranberry-bliss-bars-starbucks-copycat-203229)

- 🍽️ **Serves:** 40
- 🕑 **Ready in:** 2 hours 40 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C); use 325°F (165°C) for a glass or dark pan
- 🧰 **Pan:** 10 x 15 inches (the source gives 26–28 minutes in a 9x13 pan instead)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Bars

- 🧈 1 cup butter (2 sticks, very soft)
- 🍬 1 cup brown sugar (some cooks reduce this to ⅔ cup)
- 🍬 ⅓ cup granulated sugar
- 🥚 3 large eggs
- 🍊 2 teaspoons orange extract or 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🌾 2 cups flour
- 🫧 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- 🫚 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 🍒 ¾ cup craisins (dried cranberries)
- 🤍 ¾ cup white chocolate chips

### Frosting

- 🧀 3 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 🧈 2 tablespoons butter, softened
- ❄️ 3 cups confectioners' sugar
- 🍊 1 teaspoon orange extract (or vanilla)

### Topping

- 🍒 ⅓ cup craisins, chopped
- 🍊 1–2 tablespoon grated orange rind (as printed in the source)
- 🤍 ⅓ cup white chocolate chips
- 🌻 ½ teaspoon canola oil

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C); use 325°F (165°C) for a glass or dark pan. Prepare a 10 x 15 pan by lining it with parchment paper or using a nonstick spray.
2. 🥣 Bars: with an electric mixer, beat the softened butter and sugars for 3–5 minutes, until light. Gently blend in the eggs and orange extract (don't overbeat the eggs). Add the flour, baking powder, and ginger, and beat briefly. Add the cranberries and chips, stirring just until incorporated.
3. 🥄 Spread the thick batter in the prepared pan. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20–24 minutes, until light brown at the edges and a skewer tests clean. Don't overbake, or the bars will be dry. Let cool completely. If you use a 9x13 pan, the bars will be thicker and take 26–28 minutes.
4. 🧀 Frosting: blend the cream cheese and butter until fluffy. Add the orange extract and confectioners' sugar, and beat until the frosting is fluffy and spreadable (adding 1 teaspoon milk if needed). Spread evenly over the COOLED bars.
5. 🍊 Garnish: use a zester to remove the rind from an orange, and sprinkle the zest over the frosted bars. Chop ⅓ cup craisins coarsely and sprinkle over the frosted bars.
6. 🤍 Drizzle: mix the white chocolate chips and oil in a glass measuring cup. Microwave for 1 minute, stirring every 15 seconds until melted. Use a fork or whisk to drizzle the white chocolate diagonally across the bars.
7. ⏳ Allow one hour for the white chocolate to set before cutting.
8. 🔪 For signature Starbucks triangles, cut into 20 large squares (5 cuts by 4 cuts). Then cut each square in half diagonally to create triangles.
