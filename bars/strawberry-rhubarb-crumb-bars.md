# Strawberry Rhubarb Crumb Bars

🔗 **Source:** [House of Nash Eats](https://houseofnasheats.com/wprm_print/14681)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12 to 16 bars
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 45 minutes
- 🕑 **Total time:** 55 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- 🧰 **Pan:** 9 x 9-inch baking dish, lined with parchment paper
- 📊 **Calories:** 233 kcal per bar (as listed by the source)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crumb layer

- 🌾 1½ cups all-purpose flour (141g)
- 🌾 1½ cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 🧪 ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🍬 ½ cup packed light brown sugar
- 🍬 ½ cup granulated sugar (100g)
- 🌰 ¾ cup chopped walnuts (optional)
- 🧈 ¾ cup salted butter, melted
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

### Filling

- 🍓 2 cups strawberries, chopped
- 🌿 1½ cups rhubarb, chopped into ½-inch pieces
- 🍋 1 Tablespoon lemon juice
- 🍬 ⅓ cup granulated sugar
- 🌽 1 Tablespoon cornstarch

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Prepare a square 9x9-inch baking dish by lining it with parchment paper, so the bars will be easy to remove and cut once they have cooked and cooled.
2. 🥣 In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, oats, baking soda, salt, sugars, and walnuts, if using. Add the melted butter and vanilla to the oat mixture and stir to incorporate. When the crumb mixture is evenly moist, press ⅔ of it into the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Set aside the remaining ⅓ of the crumb mixture for the top layer.
3. 🥣 In a separate mixing bowl, combine the chopped strawberries and rhubarb. Toss with the lemon juice, then add the sugar and cornstarch and stir to coat the fruit. Spread the strawberry rhubarb mixture evenly over the bottom crumb layer, then sprinkle with the remaining ⅓ of the crumb mixture.
4. ⏲️ Bake in the preheated oven for 45–50 minutes, until the top is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
5. 🧊 Remove from the oven and allow to cool completely before cutting into bars.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: House of Nash Eats, "Strawberry Rhubarb Crumb Bars" by Amy Nash. Printed 2/26/2024.
- 📦 Storage (per the source): keep at room temperature in an airtight container for about 3–4 days, or in the fridge for up to 1 week.
- ❄️ Freezing (per the source): freezes well for 2–3 months. Thaw completely on the counter for a few hours. Individual bars can be microwaved for a few seconds to enjoy them warm.
- 🔁 Variations (per the source): replace all of the rhubarb or all of the strawberries with the other for strawberry or rhubarb crumb bars, or replace either fruit with cherries, raspberries, apricots, or peaches.
