# Small Batch Lemon Bars

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 4 servings
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 30 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- 🧰 **Pan:** 9 x 5 loaf pan, lined with parchment paper

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 ¼ cup butter (cold)
- 🌾 ½ cup all-purpose flour
- ❄️ ⅛ cup powdered sugar
- 🍬 ½ cup granulated sugar
- 🌾 1 Tbsp flour
- 🫧 ¼ tsp baking powder
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🍋 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- 🍋 Zest of one lemon
- 🍋 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- ❄️ ½ cup powdered sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Blend together the butter, ½ cup flour, and the powdered sugar until crumbly, with pea-size pieces of butter.
2. 🧻 Line a 9 x 5 loaf pan with parchment paper.
3. 🥧 Press the flour mixture into the bottom of the pan in an even layer.
4. 🔥 Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12 minutes, until lightly browned around the edges.
5. 🥣 Mix together the granulated sugar, 1 Tbsp flour, egg, and baking powder.
6. 🍋 Add the 1 Tbsp lemon juice and the zest, and stir to combine.
7. 🥧 After the shortbread crust has baked, remove it from the oven and pour the lemon curd mixture over the top.
8. 🔥 Return to the oven and bake for an additional 15–20 minutes, or until light brown around the edges.
9. 🧊 Allow to cool for 5–10 minutes.
10. 🫗 Drizzle with glaze.
11. 🔪 Cool and cut into bars.
12. ❄️ Combine the powdered sugar and enough lemon juice to make a nice drizzling consistency.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Peachie (peachie.recipes), "Small Batch Lemon Bars," created by Leigh Anne Wilkes. The recipe is sold on the site as a printed recipe card.
- 💬 The source describes these as "the perfect amount for 1-2 people," but lists 4 servings.
- ⚠️ The source lists the glaze step (step 12) after the drizzle step (step 10). The order is kept as printed.
- ⚠️ The ingredient list includes two separate lines for 1 Tbsp lemon juice, as printed in the source.
- ⚠️ The source does not name a separate glaze ingredient list, so the glaze uses the ½ cup powdered sugar and lemon juice from the list above.
