# Lemon Poppy Seed Muffins

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 24 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 34 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (177°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 24 minutes (start checking at about 20 minutes with a toothpick)
- 🔢 **Nutrition (as listed by the source):** 342 kcal; 53 g carbohydrates; 5 g protein; 12 g fat; 6 g saturated fat; 81 mg cholesterol; 202 mg sodium; 158 mg potassium; 0 g fiber; 36 g sugar; 400 IU vitamin A; 0.4 mg vitamin C; 103 mg calcium; 1.5 mg iron

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Muffins

- 🍬 1½ cups sugar
- 🧈 ½ cup salted butter, room temperature
- 🍋 1 tablespoon lemon extract
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥚 4 eggs, room temperature
- 🍋 2 teaspoons lemon zest
- 🥛 ½ cup sour cream, room temperature
- 🌱 ¼ cup poppy seeds
- 🌾 2 cups flour
- 🥄 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🥛 ¾ cup milk

### Glaze

- 🍬 1 cup powdered sugar
- 🥛 5 teaspoons milk
- 🍋 ¼ teaspoon lemon extract
- 🍋 ½ teaspoon lemon zest

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C).
2. 🧁 Grease a muffin tin, and use muffin papers if desired. Set the muffin tin aside.
3. 🧈 With a mixer, cream the sugar and the butter together. When light and fluffy, add the lemon extract, vanilla extract, and eggs. Beat together until well blended.
4. 🍋 Add the lemon zest, sour cream, and poppy seeds. Mix until all ingredients are just blended in.
5. 🥣 In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt. Stir until the mixture is uniform.
6. 🥄 To combine the dry ingredients with the creamed butter, add a third of the flour into the butter, and then add about a third of the milk. Stir gently, then add the next third of the flour and milk, and stir gently. Scrape the sides of the bowl to make sure all ingredients are well incorporated. Add the remaining flour, and then the remaining milk. Stir until just blended.
7. 🧁 Fill each muffin tin 2/3 full. Bake the muffins for 24 minutes. You may want to start checking the muffins at about 20 minutes by inserting a toothpick to see if they are done. When the toothpick comes out clean, the muffins are done. Remove from the oven.
8. 🍋 Lemon glaze: In a small bowl, combine the powdered sugar, milk, lemon extract, and lemon zest. Stir until well blended. When the muffins have cooled to room temperature, drizzle the glaze over the muffins.
