# Blueberry Pumpkin Muffins

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 18 standard muffins (or 12 larger muffins, per the source's note)
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (177°C), "moderate oven"
- 🔥 **Bake:** 20 minutes (the source also says "40 minutes per recipe") or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Muffins

- 🌾 1⅔ cups flour (227 g)
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🥄 ½ teaspoon baking powder
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🍂 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon allspice
- 🎃 1 cup canned pumpkin (1 cup water to ¼ cup pumpkin powder)
- 🥛 ¼ cup evaporated milk [15 g powdered milk + ¼ cup water]
- 🧈 ⅓ cup shortening
- 🍬 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🫐 1 cup fresh blueberries
- 🌾 1 tablespoon flour (for tossing the blueberries)

### Streusel

- 🌾 2 tablespoons flour
- 🍬 2 tablespoons sugar
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- 🧈 1 tablespoon butter

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C).
2. 🥣 Combine the first six ingredients (flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, and allspice).
3. 🎃 Combine the pumpkin and evaporated milk until blended.
4. 🧈 Cream the shortening and sugar in a large mixer bowl. Add the egg and beat until the mixture is fluffy.
5. 🥄 Add the flour mixture alternately with the pumpkin mixture, beating after each addition.
6. 🫐 Combine the blueberries and 1 tablespoon flour. Gently stir them into the batter.
7. 🧁 Fill 18 paper-lined muffin tins ¾ full.
8. 🥣 Make the streusel: combine 2 tablespoons flour, 2 tablespoons sugar, and ¼ teaspoon cinnamon. Cut in 1 tablespoon butter until the mixture is crumbly.
9. 🍂 Sprinkle the streusel over the tops of the muffins.
10. 🔥 Bake in a moderate oven (350°F, 177°C) for 20 minutes (40 minutes per recipe), or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Libby's Canned Pumpkin recipe, November 1988.
- 🧁 For larger muffins, use 12 paper-lined muffin tins (per the source's note).
- 🎃 The source says to add canned pumpkin, not pumpkin pie filling.
- 🔁 The source includes a second short method for the same batter: "Mix flour, baking soda, baking powder, and cinnamon. Just give it a good stir! Add canned pumpkin (not pumpkin pie filling), and milk and stir well until well mixed."
