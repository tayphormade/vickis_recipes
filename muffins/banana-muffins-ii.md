# Banana Muffins II

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](http://allrecipes.com/Recipe-Tools/Print/Recipe.aspx?recipeID=42719&origin=detail&servings=12&metric=false)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 25 minutes
- ⏳ **Ready in:** 35 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** Mini muffins 10 to 15 minutes; large muffins 25 to 30 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🍌 3 large bananas, mashed
- 🍬 ¾ cup white sugar
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🧈 ⅓ cup butter, melted

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Coat muffin pans with non-stick spray, or use paper liners. Sift together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt; set aside.
2. 🥣 Combine the bananas, sugar, egg, and melted butter in a large bowl. Fold in the flour mixture, and mix until smooth. Scoop into muffin pans.
3. 🔥 Bake in the preheated oven. Bake mini muffins for 10 to 15 minutes, and large muffins for 25 to 30 minutes. The muffins will spring back when lightly tapped.
