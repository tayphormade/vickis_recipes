# Morning Glory Muffins

🔗 **Source:** [Epicurious](https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/morning-glory-muffins)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** Makes 12
- ⏳ **Total time:** 1 hour
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (177°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 25 to 30 minutes (rotate the pan after 15 minutes)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🛢️ ¾ cup neutral oil (such as grapeseed or canola), plus more for the muffin pan
- 🌾 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
- 🌾 1 cup plus 2 Tbsp. (145 g) whole wheat flour
- 🍂 2 tsp. ground cinnamon
- 🥄 1 tsp. baking powder
- 🥄 1 tsp. baking soda
- 🧂 ½ tsp. kosher salt
- 🥚 2 large eggs
- 🍬 ½ cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 🍬 ½ cup (100 g) light brown sugar
- 🌼 2 tsp. vanilla extract
- 🍍 1 16-oz. can crushed pineapple, drained and squeezed dry
- 🥕 1 cup shredded peeled carrot (from about 2 medium or 1 large carrot)
- 🍏 1 cup shredded peeled Granny Smith apple (from about 1 apple)
- 🍇 ½ cup golden raisins
- 🥥 ½ cup sweetened coconut flakes
- 🌰 ½ cup toasted pecans, coarsely chopped

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C). Line a standard 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners. Lightly coat the flat top of the muffin pan with neutral oil to prevent sticking.
2. 🥣 Combine the all-purpose flour, whole wheat flour, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, and kosher salt in a medium bowl.
3. 🥚 Whisk the eggs, granulated sugar, and light brown sugar in a large bowl until smooth. Whisk in the oil and vanilla extract. Mix in the drained pineapple. Fold in the carrot, apple, golden raisins, coconut flakes, and pecans.
4. 🥄 Fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined and no flour streaks remain. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups, filling them to the brim.
5. 🔥 Bake the muffins, rotating the pan after 15 minutes, until the top springs back to the touch or a tester inserted into the center comes out clean, 25 to 30 minutes. Let cool in the pan slightly, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely. Muffins are best the day they are made and even better if they are still warm.
