# Lemon-Blueberry Muffins

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 1 dozen (1 muffin per serving)
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 400°F (204°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 20 minutes, or until the muffins spring back when lightly touched
- 🔢 **Per serving (as listed by the source):** 187 calories; 23% of calories from fat; 4.8 g fat; 2.7 g saturated fat; 1.4 g monounsaturated fat; 0.3 g polyunsaturated fat; 3.7 g protein; 32.6 g carbohydrates; 1 g fiber; 30 mg cholesterol; 1.1 mg iron; 264 mg sodium; 59 mg calcium

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Muffins

- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 🥄 ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🍂 ⅛ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 🧈 ¼ cup butter
- 🥛 1¼ cups low-fat buttermilk
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🍋 1 tablespoon grated lemon rind
- 🫐 1 cup blueberries
- 🛢️ Cooking spray

### Glaze

- 🍋 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 🍬 ½ cup powdered sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C).
2. 🥣 Lightly spoon the flour into dry measuring cups; level with a knife. Combine the flour and the next 5 ingredients (through nutmeg) in a medium bowl. Cut in the butter with a pastry blender or 2 knives until the mixture resembles coarse meal.
3. 🥛 Combine the buttermilk, egg, and lemon rind; stir well with a whisk. Add to the flour mixture; stir just until moist. Gently fold in the blueberries.
4. 🧁 Spoon the batter into 12 muffin cups coated with cooking spray. Bake at 400°F (204°C) for 20 minutes, or until the muffins spring back when lightly touched. Remove the muffins from the pans immediately, and place them on a wire rack to cool.
5. 🍋 Combine the lemon juice and powdered sugar in a small bowl. Drizzle the glaze evenly over the cooled muffins.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Cooking Light Magazine, April 2003, by Jean Kressy, page 117.
- 🌟 The source describes these as lemon-blueberry muffins with a sour-sweet glaze and a hint of nutmeg, and says they are perfect any time of day.
- ⏲️ Make-ahead (per the source): make the muffins up to 2 days ahead and glaze them the morning of the brunch.
