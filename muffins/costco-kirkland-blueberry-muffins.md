# Costco (Kirkland) Blueberry Muffins

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 5 jumbo muffins, or 10 regular-size muffins
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 375°F (191°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 35 to 40 minutes for jumbo muffins (25 to 30 minutes for standard-size), until dark brown on top and a toothpick comes out clean

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 ¾ cup plus 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🥚 1 large egg white
- 🛢️ ¼ cup vegetable oil
- 🥄 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 🌼 1¼ teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🌾 10 ounces (2 cups) all-purpose flour
- 🥛 ½ cup whole milk
- 🫐 1⅓ cups frozen blueberries
- 🧈 ½ cup margarine, softened

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 375°F (191°C).
2. 🥣 Combine the margarine and sugar in a large mixing bowl with an electric mixer on high speed.
3. 🥚 Add the egg and egg white and beat well until fluffy, then mix in the oil.
4. 🥄 Mix in the baking powder, vanilla, and salt.
5. 🌾 Mix in half of the flour, then add half of the milk and mix. Add the remaining flour and the rest of the milk and mix until combined.
6. 🫐 Rinse the blueberries in a strainer until the water runs clear, with no more blue in it. Blot the berries on paper towels to remove any excess water, set 20 blueberries aside, then stir the remainder into the batter by hand. Stir just enough to evenly distribute the blueberries in the batter, and no more than that. Over-stirring will make the batter blue.
7. 🧁 Line a jumbo or "Texas-size" muffin pan with 5 paper muffin cups, then evenly distribute the batter into the 5 cups. Fill the sixth cup up about halfway with water. Place four of the leftover blueberries onto the top of each muffin, pressing them into the batter. Make the placement of the berries look random. This will ensure that all the muffins bake with visible blueberries on top, like those at Costco. If making smaller, standard-size muffins, divide the batter among 10 muffin cups and use 2 blueberries per muffin to top. Fill the 11th and 12th cups up about halfway with water.
8. 🔥 Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, or until dark brown on top and a toothpick pushed into the center of a muffin comes out clean. If you use a standard-size muffin pan, bake the muffins for 25 to 30 minutes, or until dark brown on top.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Todd Wilbur (the source says "BY TODD WILBUR"). The source is a copycat recipe for Costco's Kirkland blueberry muffins.
- 🧁 The source says you need a jumbo or "Texas-size" muffin pan to get muffins the same size as the originals. Standard-size muffins also work with this batter.
- 🛢️ The source uses oil for moistness, because the author found butter made the muffins taste more like butter cake or pound cake. The copycat's fat is part margarine (for a mild butter flavor and thicker batter) and part vegetable oil. The author notes that the Kirkland package ingredients list no butter.
- 🫐 Rinse the frozen blueberries well before adding them, since the juice frozen on the outside of the berries will streak the batter blue.
