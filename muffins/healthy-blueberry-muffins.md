# Healthy Blueberry Muffins

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12 muffins
- ⏲️ **Active time:** 20 minutes
- ⏳ **Total time:** 50 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 375°F (191°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 18 to 20 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean
- 🏷️ **Labels (from the source):** Low-calorie, vegetarian, low sodium, soy-free
- 🔢 **Per serving (1 muffin, as listed by the source):** 148.4 calories; 4.9 g protein; 19.3 g carbohydrates; 2 g dietary fiber; 8.9 g sugars; 6 g added sugar; 6.3 g fat; 3 g saturated fat; 28 mg cholesterol; 159.5 mg sodium; 68.6 mg calcium; 1.5 mg iron; 92.8 mg potassium; 16.8 mg magnesium; 8.2 mcg folate; 219.1 IU vitamin A; 2 mg vitamin C

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 ¾ cup old-fashioned rolled oats
- 🌾 ¾ cup white whole-wheat flour
- 🌰 ¼ cup almond flour
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 🥄 ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🫐 1½ cups blueberries
- 🥛 1 cup reduced-fat plain Greek yogurt
- 🍬 ⅓ cup packed brown sugar
- 🥛 ¼ cup reduced-fat milk
- 🧈 ¼ cup butter, melted and cooled
- 🥚 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 375°F (191°C). Lightly coat a muffin tin with cooking spray.
2. 🥣 Stir the oats, white whole-wheat flour, almond flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt together in a large bowl. Add the blueberries and stir to coat.
3. 🥛 Whisk the yogurt, brown sugar, milk, butter, egg, and vanilla together in a medium bowl until blended. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Divide the batter among the prepared muffin cups, using a generous ¼ cup of batter per muffin cup.
4. 🔥 Bake until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, 18 to 20 minutes.
5. ⏲️ Let cool in the pan for 10 minutes before removing to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: EatingWell.com, October 2018, recipe by Carolyn Casner.
- 🌟 The source says it reduced the sugar by 50% to make a lower-sugar, whole-grain muffin, and uses almond flour, rolled oats, and Greek yogurt to boost protein and fiber.
- 🗄️ To make ahead: store individually wrapped at room temperature for up to 2 days, or in the freezer for up to 1 month.
- 🔥 To reheat: remove the plastic wrap, wrap the muffin in a paper towel, and microwave on High for 30 to 45 seconds.
- ⚠️ The source's ingredient list has the note "-CHANGE TO 1/3 CUP" printed beside the Greek yogurt. The source does not explain this note, so the recipe keeps the amount as printed (1 cup).
- 🔁 Exchanges (per the source): 1 fat, ½ fruit, ½ other carbohydrate, ½ starch.
