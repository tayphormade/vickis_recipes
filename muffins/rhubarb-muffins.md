# Rhubarb Muffins

🔗 **Source:** [Dance Around the Kitchen](https://dancearoundthekitchen.com/wprm_print/rhubarb-muffins)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 24 muffins
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 20 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 20 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 40 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (177°C)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 18 to 22 minutes
- 🔧 **Equipment:** Oven, muffin tin
- 🔢 **Per serving (as listed by the source):** 170 kcal; 28 g carbohydrates; 2.2 g protein; 5.8 g fat; 1.2 g saturated fat; 2 g polyunsaturated fat; 2.3 g monounsaturated fat; 9.2 mg cholesterol; 49 mg sodium; 83.5 mg potassium; 0.7 g fiber; 16.2 g sugar; 17 IU vitamin A; 1.2 mg vitamin C; 33.2 mg calcium; 0.3 mg iron

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Muffins

- 🍬 ¾ cup sugar
- 🍬 ¾ cup brown sugar
- 🛢️ ½ cup vegetable oil
- 🥛 1 cup buttermilk (or milk + 1 T vinegar)
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 🌾 2¾ cups flour
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🌿 3 cups diced rhubarb

### Topping

- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon cinnamon
- 🧈 1 tablespoon butter, softened

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C) and add muffin liners to 24 muffin cups.
2. 🥣 In a large bowl, stir together the sugar, brown sugar, oil, buttermilk, egg, and vanilla.
3. 🥄 Next, add the flour and baking soda and stir until just combined.
4. 🌿 Add the rhubarb and stir until well dispersed throughout the batter; set aside.
5. 🥣 Add the topping ingredients in a small bowl and use a fork to mix until combined.
6. 🧁 Fill each muffin cup ⅔ full, and top each muffin with about 1 teaspoon of topping.
7. 🔥 Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, and remove from the muffin tin to cool.
