# Chili's Original Chili

🔗 **Source:** [Top Secret Recipes](https://topsecretrecipes.com/chilis-original-chili-copycat-recipe.html)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 4 (makes 6½ cups)
- ♨️ **Simmer:** 1½ hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Meat**
  - 🥩 2 lb 80/20 ground beef
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🧅 1½ cups diced white onion
  - 🫑 ½ cup diced green chile pepper (Anaheim or Hatch)
- 🫗 **Base & liquid**
  - 🥫 ¾ cup (one 6 oz can) tomato paste
  - 🍲 4 cups chicken broth
  - 💧 1½ cups water
  - ⬜ 3 tbsp cornstarch
- 🧺 **Spices**
  - 🟥 ¼ cup plus 3 tbsp chili powder (Mexene)
  - 🟤 2 tsp ground cumin
  - 🧂 1¾ tsp salt
  - 🟧 1½ tsp paprika
  - ⚫ 1 tsp ground black pepper
  - 🔷 ½ tsp garlic powder
  - 🍶 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 🎀 **Toppings**
  - 🧀 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  - 🟨 1 cup shredded pepper Jack cheese
  - 🌮 Crispy sliced tortilla strips or crumbled tortilla chips

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥩 Brown the ground beef in a large pot over medium heat, crumbling it as it cooks, about 5 minutes, until no pink remains.
2. 🥩 Pour the fat into a small bowl, then transfer the beef to a medium bowl.
3. 🔥 Return the pan to medium heat and measure 1/3 cup of the fat back into the pan.
4. 🧅 When the fat is hot, add the onion and green chile, and cook 5–7 minutes, until the onions are translucent.
5. 🥩 Stir in the beef and tomato paste, until the paste is broken up and coats the beef.
6. 🍲 Add the chicken broth.
7. ⬜ Dissolve the cornstarch in the water, and add the slurry to the pot.
8. 🍶 Stir in the remaining spices and vinegar, and bring to a simmer.
9. 🥩 Cook uncovered 1½ hours, until thick and the beef is tender. Reduce the heat if it bubbles rapidly.
10. 🧀 Combine the cheeses, and serve the chili topped with cheese and tortilla strips or chips.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🥫 This is a Texas-style con carne chili: no beans and no tomato chunks. The tomato paste adds a tomato base.
- 👤 Copycat of the chili served at Chili's, from Todd Wilbur's Top Secret Recipes.
