# Chile Verde con Cerdo (Green Chili with Pork)

🔗 **Source:** [Food.com](https://www.food.com/recipe/chile-verde-con-cerdo-green-chili-with-pork-20574)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 15
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 1 hour
- ⏲️ **Total:** 9 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Pork**
  - 🐖 2–3 lb pork roast (pre-diced pork or cube steaks work too)
- 🥦 **Fat & aromatics**
  - 🛢️ 2 tbsp cooking oil, lard, or bacon grease
  - 🧅 1 large onion, chopped (optional; not traditional)
  - 🧄 1 head garlic, minced
- 🥘 **Thickener**
  - 🌾 6 tbsp flour (use 2–3 tbsp ClearJel CookType if canning)
- 🏮 **Chiles & tomatoes**
  - 🫑 2 cups diced green chilies (Big Jim's, roasted, peeled, and frozen)
  - 🍅 1 (15 oz) can tomatoes, drained
  - 🍏 3 large tomatillos, husks removed and chopped (optional)
  - 🟩 2–4 tsp jalapeños (optional)
- 🧺 **Liquid & seasoning**
  - 💧 5 cups water, or 5 cups chicken broth (broth isn't traditional)
  - 🟤 2 tbsp ground cumin, or to taste
  - 🟥 2 tbsp chili powder, or to taste (optional)
  - 🌿 2 tsp oregano (bracketed in the source)
  - 🧂 1 tsp salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🐖 Simmer the pork roast in a large pan until it is tender and comes easily off the bone. You can also use diced pork or cube steaks, browned in the pot with the onion and garlic before adding the rest of the ingredients.
2. ⏳ Let the meat cool enough to handle.
3. 🐖 Cube the cooked pork into bite-size pieces.
4. 🫑 Process half of the green chilies until smooth.
5. 🛢️ In the same large pan, melt the lard or bacon grease, or heat the oil.
6. 🧅 Add the onions and garlic and sauté until tender but not brown.
7. 🌾 Stir in the flour until it absorbs the fat.
8. 💧 Add the broth or water and cook, stirring, until it boils and is slightly thickened.
9. 🐖 Add the cubed pork, drained tomatoes, tomatillos, all of the green chilies, and the jalapeños if desired. Taste first.
10. 🔥 Add the spices a little at a time, bringing to a simmer before each addition, until it tastes right to you.
11. ♨️ Simmer at least 1 hour, or longer if you can, stirring occasionally so it doesn't stick.
12. 🥣 For a stew-style chili, add cubed potatoes 20 minutes before serving, and serve with warm tortillas.
13. 🍅 Otherwise, serve over burritos and garnish with cheese, lettuce, tomatoes, and sour cream.

---

## 📝 Notes

- ⚠️ The source doesn't say where the blended green chilies go. They are presumably added with the rest in step 9.
- 🫑 For a vegetarian green chili sauce, leave out the pork.
- 🌾 Use ClearJel in place of flour if you plan to can the chili.
- 💡 The author suggests it over bean and beef or breakfast burritos and chimichangas, or as a stew with warm tortillas.
- 💡 It can be moved to a crock pot for all-day simmering on low.
- 🍏 Short on time? The author suggests a large can of green enchilada sauce instead of tomatillos.
