# Skyline Chili (Cincinnati Chili)

🔗 **Source:** [Cooks.com](https://www.cooks.com/rec/doc/prt/0,1749,149162-241206,00.html)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 6 pints
- ♨️ **Simmer:** 2–3 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Meat & liquid**
  - 🥩 3 lb lean ground beef (not browned; chopped up)
  - 💧 8 cups water (2 quarts)
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🧅 2 large onions, chopped
- 🍱 **Beans & tomato**
  - 🫘 2 (15 oz) cans kidney beans, added afterward or served on top
  - 🥫 1 (28 oz) can tomato sauce
- 🧺 **Spices**
  - 🟥 4 tbsp chili powder (or more)
  - 🟤 Cumin, amount not listed
  - 🟫 ½ tsp allspice
  - 🫚 ½ tsp ground ginger
  - 🌰 ½ tsp nutmeg
  - 🌶️ ½ tsp crushed red pepper
  - 🔷 ½ tsp garlic powder
  - 🔶 ½ tsp onion powder
  - 🔸 ½ tsp ground cloves
  - 🍂 Bay leaf, amount not listed
  - 🪵 2 tsp cinnamon
  - 🧂 1½ tsp salt
  - ⚫ 1 tsp pepper
- 🎀 **Finishing**
  - 🍶 2 tbsp vinegar (red wine or apple cider)
  - 🟣 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  - 🍫 ½ oz unsweetened baking chocolate
  - 🟧 4 drops Tabasco sauce (often added when served)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥩 In an 8-quart kettle, combine the hamburger, water, and onions, and simmer 30 minutes.
2. 🥄 Add the remaining ingredients.
3. ♨️ Simmer uncovered 2–3 hours.
4. 🧅 Serve over spaghetti with onions and cheese, or with steamed buns, hot dogs, onions, and cheese.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 💡 Cincinnati ordering: "2-way" is spaghetti and chili, "3-way" adds cheddar, "4-way" adds onions or beans, and "5-way" has everything.
- 🥩 One reader kneads the room-temperature beef by hand with tomato paste and a little water, adding the rest of the water while kneading, for an ultra-fine texture.
- ❄️ Another reader mixes the uncooked beef with the tomato sauce, spices, vinegar, and Worcestershire and refrigerates it for 2 days so the flavors mix.
- 💧 One comment suggests boiling the meat in water and refrigerating it overnight to remove excess fat.
- 🧅 Many comments say no onions are cooked in the chili. They go on top afterward, and garlic and onion powder are used instead.
- 🍫 A comment says the owner's son said there's no chocolate in it, just nutmeg.
