# Soup Nazi's Mexican Chicken Chili

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 6
- ♨️ **Simmer:** about 2 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍖 **Chicken & oil**
  - 🐔 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  - 🫒 1 tbsp olive oil
- 🫗 **Liquid**
  - 💧 10 cups water
  - 🍲 2 cups chicken stock
  - 🥫 ½ cup tomato sauce
  - 🍅 1 cup canned diced tomatoes
- 🥦 **Vegetables**
  - 🥔 1 potato, peeled and chopped
  - 🧅 1 small onion, diced
  - 🌽 1 cup frozen yellow corn
  - 🥕 ½ carrot, sliced
  - 🌱 1 celery stalk, diced
  - 🔴 ¼ cup diced canned pimento
  - 🟩 1 jalapeño, diced
  - 🧄 1 clove garlic, minced
- 🍱 **Beans**
  - 🫘 1 (15 oz) can red kidney beans, plus liquid
- 🧺 **Seasoning & garnish**
  - 🟥 1½ tsp chili powder
  - 🟤 1 tsp cumin
  - 🧂 ¼ tsp salt
  - 🔺 Dash of cayenne
  - 💚 Dash of basil
  - 🌿 Dash of oregano
  - ☘️ ¼ cup chopped Italian parsley, plus more for serving
  - 🍦 Sour cream, for serving

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🐔 Sauté the chicken breasts in the olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat, 7–10 minutes per side, until cooked through.
2. 🐔 Let the chicken cool until it can be handled. Don't rinse the pot.
3. 🐔 Shred the chicken by hand into bite-size pieces and return it to the pot.
4. 🔥 Add the remaining ingredients and turn the heat to high.
5. 🐔 Bring to a boil, then reduce and simmer 2 hours, stirring often so the chicken shreds into smaller bits.
6. 🔥 Keep simmering until the chili reduces, thickens, and darkens (less orange, more brown).
7. ☘️ Combine some chopped parsley with sour cream and serve it on the side as a topping.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 💧 The chili should reduce substantially during the 2-hour simmer, which accounts for the 10 cups of water.
- 🐔 The original says you can substitute turkey breast for the chicken to make turkey chili.
- 👤 A clone of the Mexican Chicken Chili from Al "The Soup Nazi" Yeganeh's International Soup Kitchen in New York.
