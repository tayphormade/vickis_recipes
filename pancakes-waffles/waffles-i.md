# Waffles I

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](http://allrecipes.com/Recipe-Tools/Print/Recipe.aspx?RecipeID=22180&origin=detail&&Servings=6)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 6
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cook:** 15 minutes
- ⌛ **Ready in:** 20 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 🥛 1¾ cups milk
- 🛢️ ½ cup vegetable oil
- 🍬 1 tablespoon white sugar
- 🥄 4 teaspoons baking powder
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🌼 ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧇 Preheat the waffle iron. Beat the eggs in a large bowl with a hand beater until fluffy. Beat in the flour, milk, vegetable oil, sugar, baking powder, salt, and vanilla, just until smooth.
2. 🥄 Spray the preheated waffle iron with non-stick cooking spray. Pour the mix onto the hot waffle iron. Cook until golden brown. Serve hot.
