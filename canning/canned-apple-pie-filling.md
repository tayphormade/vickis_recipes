# Canned Apple Pie Filling

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 1 serving (7 quarts), as printed in the source
- 🔥 **Process:** 25 minutes in boiling water

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌿 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 🌰 ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 🧂 1 teaspoon salt
- 🥄 1 cup clear jel (or cornstarch)
- 🍬 4½ cups organic sugar
- 💧 9½ cups water
- 🍋 3 tablespoons lemon juice (optional)
- 🍎 35 apples (medium, sliced)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Mix the spices, clear jel, and sugar in a large saucepan.
2. 💧 Add the water and lemon juice and cook until thickened, stirring constantly.
3. 🍎 Place apple slices directly into jars.
4. 🫙 Add layers of syrup as you fill each jar to help prevent air pockets.
5. 📏 Leave at least 1 inch headspace.
6. 🔒 Tighten the lids and process for 25 minutes in boiling water.
7. 🥧 For baking: fill an unbaked crust with filling, place dabs of butter on top, and add a top crust.
8. 🔥 Bake until the crust is lightly browned.
