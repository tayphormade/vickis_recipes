# Strawberry Pie Filling

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 4 quarts
- ♨️ **Process:** 30 minutes in a water bath (40 minutes at high altitude)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍓 4 quarts (6 pounds) halved strawberries
- 💧 3½ cups water
- 🍬 3 cups sugar
- 🍋 3/8 cup lemon juice
- 🥄 1¼ cups Clear Jel
- 🔴 Red food coloring (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Mix the sugar and Clear Jel together in a large pot.
2. 💧 Add the water and mix thoroughly.
3. 🔥 Heat over medium-high heat until it starts to thicken.
4. 🍋 Add the lemon juice and coloring, if using. Cook for 1 minute.
5. 🍓 Add the strawberries and mix.
6. 🫙 Fill the jars to 1¼ inches (up to 2 inches) of headspace.
7. 🧴 Clean the rims with vinegar, then add lids and rings, finger-tight.
8. ♨️ Water bath can for 30 minutes (40 minutes at high altitude).

---
