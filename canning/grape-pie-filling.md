# Canning Grape Pie Filling

🔗 **Source:** [SchneiderPeeps](https://www.schneiderpeeps.com/canning-grape-pie-filling/)

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** about 1 hour
- ♨️ **Process:** 25 minutes in a water bath canner

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍇 12 cups grape mash (about 3 quarts; unprocessed mash, not puree)
- 🍬 6 cups sugar (many recipes need only 3 cups, so taste first)
- 🍋 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 🥄 6 tablespoons Clear Jel (not optional, per the source)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Combine the grape mash, sugar, lemon juice, and Clear Jel in a large stockpot. Add more sugar a little at a time, if you like it sweeter.
2. 🔥 Bring to a boil over medium heat. Stir often, because the filling is thick and can scorch.
3. 🫙 While it simmers, sterilize the jars in boiling water with a splash of white vinegar.
4. 🧼 Wash and rinse the lids while the jars sterilize.
5. 🫙 Fill the hot, sterilized jars with the hot filling, leaving ¼ inch of headspace.
6. 🧴 Wipe the rims with a clean, damp cloth, then add lids and bands.
7. ♨️ Process in a water bath canner for 25 minutes.
8. ❄️ Let the jars cool. You'll hear them pop as they seal. Remove the bands, and check the seals.
9. 🥫 Store sealed jars in the pantry. Refrigerate any unsealed jar and use it first.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 To freeze instead, fill clean jars with 1 inch of headspace (wide-mouth jars work best). Loosely cover, refrigerate overnight, then move to the freezer.
- 🌟 Without Clear Jel, don't add any thickener.
- 🌟 Honey or maple syrup can replace some of the sugar, but they change the flavor.
