# Canned Concord Grape Pie Filling

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 4 quarts
- 📊 **Calories:** 1,889 kcal for the batch

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍇 22 cups Concord grapes, washed (up to 24 cups preferred; or 16 cups, or 4 quarts, of processed puree or mash)
- 🍬 4 cups granulated sugar
- 🍋 ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
- 🥄 1 cup Clear Jel

*Source note: for the full recipe, use 24 cups grapes, or 10 pounds grapes with stems. The source notes that 6 cups of grapes equals about 2½ pounds, stems on.*

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍇 Use two large saucepans. Separate the skins into the larger pan and the pulp into the other.
2. 🔥 Heat the pulp over medium heat. Stir and boil for about 15 minutes. Remove from heat.
3. 🥄 Place a strainer over the pan with the skins. Push the pulp through to catch the seeds. Discard the seeds.
4. 🍋 Add the lemon juice to the pulp and skins.
5. 🥄 In a separate bowl, whisk the sugar and Clear Jel together.
6. 🥄 Add 2 cups of the grape pulp and skins to the sugar mixture and keep whisking, to prevent lumps.
7. 🔥 Start heating the water canner.
8. 🥄 Pour the sugar mixture into the grape pot. Heat over medium, stirring occasionally, until it boils. The color changes from green to pink to light purple to deep purple.
9. 🔥 Boil for about 45 minutes, until very thick and bubbly. If you used processed puree, cook only until very thick and bubbly.
10. 🫙 Sterilize the jars and lids in the canner. Pour the hot filling into the hot jars, leaving 1½ inches of headspace.
11. 🧴 Wipe the rims with a damp towel. Add hot lids and rings, and process in the water bath canner.
