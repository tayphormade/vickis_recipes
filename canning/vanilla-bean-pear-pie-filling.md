# Vanilla Bean Pear Pie Filling

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 6 quarts (or 4 quarts with the smaller amounts in parentheses)
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 25 minutes
- ♨️ **Process:** 25 minutes in a water bath (plus altitude adjustment)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍐 12 cups peeled, cored, sliced pears (8 cups for 4 quarts), treated to prevent browning
- 🍬 2¾ cups granulated sugar (1⅚ cups)
- 🥄 ¾ cup Clear Gel (½ cup)
- 🍂 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg (⅓ teaspoon)
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon ground ginger (⅛ teaspoon)
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground cloves (⅓ teaspoon)
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon cardamom (⅓ teaspoon)
- 💧 1¼ cups cold water (¾ cup + 1 tablespoon + 1 teaspoon)
- 🍎 2½ cups unsweetened apple juice (1⅔ cups)
- 🍋 ½ cup bottled lemon juice (⅓ cup)
- 🌼 3 vanilla beans, split lengthwise (2 for 4 quarts)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍐 Wash, peel, core, and slice the pears. Treat them in a mixture of about 1 cup lemon juice to 4 cups water until ready to use.
2. 🥄 In a large stockpot, mix the sugar, Clear Gel, spices, water, and apple juice (everything except the lemon juice, vanilla bean, and pears). Bring to a gentle boil.
3. 🔥 Reduce the heat and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened.
4. 🍋 Add the lemon juice and mix well. Add the pears and remove from the heat.
5. 🌼 Place half a split vanilla bean in each jar before filling. Keep as many seeds in the jar as you can.
6. 🫙 Ladle the hot filling into jars, leaving a generous 1-inch headspace.
7. 🥄 Pack the mixture down in each jar, wipe the rim, and add lids and rings.
8. ♨️ Process in a water bath for 25 minutes. Turn off the heat and let the canner rest for 15 minutes before removing the jars.

---

## 🥧 To bake as a pie

- 🥧 Add the filling to a blind-baked pie crust.
- 🔥 Bake at 350°F (175°C) until bubbly, 30 to 40 minutes.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Altitude adjustment for processing time: 1,000 feet or less, 25 minutes. 1,001 to 3,000 feet, add 5 minutes. 3,001 to 6,000 feet, add 10 minutes. Above 6,000 feet, add 15 minutes.
- 🌟 For a Christmas version, swap the vanilla and some spices for cranberries.
