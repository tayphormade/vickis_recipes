# Cherry Pie Filling (National Center for Home Food Preservation)

🔗 **Source:** [National Center for Home Food Preservation](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/can_02/can_pie/cherry_filling.html)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 1 quart (the source also gives 7 quarts)
- 🔥 **Process:** boiling-water canner, hot pack, pints or quarts. 30 minutes at 0 to 1,000 ft; 35 minutes at 1,001 to 3,000 ft; 40 minutes at 3,001 to 6,000 ft; 45 minutes above 6,000 ft

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍒 3⅓ cups fresh or thawed sour cherries
- 🍬 1 cup granulated sugar
- 🥄 ¼ cup + 1 tbsp Clear Jel®
- 💧 1⅓ cups cold water
- 🍋 1 tbsp + 1 tsp bottled lemon juice
- 🌿 ⅛ tsp cinnamon (optional)
- 🌰 ¼ tsp almond extract (optional)
- 🔴 6 drops red food coloring (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍒 Rinse and pit fresh cherries, and hold them in cold water. To prevent stem-end browning, use an ascorbic acid solution.
2. 🍒 For fresh fruit, place 6 cups at a time in 1 gallon of boiling water. Boil each batch 1 minute after the water returns to a boil.
3. 🫙 Drain, but keep the heated fruit in a covered bowl or pot.
4. 🥣 Combine sugar and Clear Jel® in a large saucepan and add water.
5. 🌿 If desired, add cinnamon, almond extract, and food coloring.
6. 🔥 Stir the mixture and cook over medium-high heat until it thickens and begins to bubble.
7. 🍋 Add lemon juice and boil 1 minute, stirring constantly.
8. 🍒 Fold in the drained cherries immediately, and fill jars with the mixture without delay, leaving 1 inch headspace.
9. 🫙 Adjust lids and process immediately using the times above for a boiling-water canner.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: National Center for Home Food Preservation, "How Do I? Can Fruits: Pie Fillings." The source says it was adapted from the USDA "Complete Guide to Home Canning" (Agriculture Information Bulletin No. 539, revised 2015) and was reviewed February 2018.
- 🍒 Quality (per the source): select fresh, very ripe, and firm cherries. Unsweetened frozen cherries may be used. If sugar has been added, rinse it off while the fruit is still frozen.
- 🧰 The source asks you to read "Using Boiling Water Canners" before beginning, and "Principles of Home Canning" if this is your first time canning.
- 🧾 The source's 7-quart column lists 6 quarts sour cherries, 7 cups sugar, 1¾ cups Clear Jel®, 9⅓ cups cold water, ½ cup bottled lemon juice, 1 tsp cinnamon, 2 tsp almond extract, and ¼ tsp red food coloring. These are not repeated in the steps above.
- ⚠️ The source's processing table gives times only for the hot pack.
