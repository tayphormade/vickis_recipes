# Fruit Pie Filling (to Can)

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 4 quarts
- ♨️ **Process:** 35 minutes in a boiling-water bath canner (45 minutes at 5,900 feet and above)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 4 cups sugar
- 🥄 1 cup Clear Jel
- 💧 8 cups water or fruit juice
- 🧂 2 teaspoons salt (optional; ¼ teaspoon was used)
- 🍋 ½ cup lemon juice
- 🍒 9 cups fruit, drained

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Mix the sugar and Clear Jel in a large pan.
2. 💧 Add the water or juice and the salt, if using.
3. 🔥 Bring to a boil, stirring constantly, until thickened.
4. 🍋 Add the lemon juice and boil for 1 minute. The mixture should be thick.
5. 🍒 Turn off the heat and stir in the fruit.
6. 🫙 Ladle into sterilized jars, wipe the rims, and add lids and rings, tightened finger-tight.
7. ♨️ Process in a water bath canner, with water 2 inches above the jars, for 35 minutes (45 minutes at 5,900 feet and above).

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The original recipe says pressure canning for 10 minutes, but that is not a tested method. It came from an Amish cookbook (cold pack).
- 🌟 For cherry pie filling, use about 8 cups of cherry juice and 8 cups of drained cherries from a bucket of frozen sour cherries.
- 🌟 Source: *Favorite Recipes of the Amish of Wisconsin*.
