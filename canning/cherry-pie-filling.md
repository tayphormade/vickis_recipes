# Homemade Cherry Pie Filling

- 🍽️ **Makes:** enough filling for one pie (scale up for canning)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍒 4 cups pitted tart cherries
- 🥄 3 to 4 tablespoons cornstarch (or Clear Jel)
- 🍒 ¾ to 1 cup cherry juice
- 🌼 ½ teaspoon almond extract (optional)
- 🍬 1 cup sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. ❄️ If using frozen cherries, heat them in a saucepan until the juice flows as they thaw. Drain them before they get warm.
2. 🍬 If the cherries are sugared, reduce the sugar in the recipe.
3. 🥄 Combine the cherries, cornstarch or Clear Jel, juice, almond extract, and sugar, and cook until thickened.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Clear Jel is recommended if you plan to can the filling.
- 🌟 The source is a short homemade recipe, not a tested canning procedure. For canning, follow a tested method such as the National Center for Home Food Preservation's cherry pie filling directions.
