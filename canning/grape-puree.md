# Concord Grape Puree

- 🍽️ **Makes:** about 1 quart per quart freezer container
- 🧊 **Storage:** freezer

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍇 Concord grapes

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍇 Remove the skins from the grapes by pinching each one at the end opposite the stem. Set the skins aside.
2. 🔥 Put the pulp into a heavy pan, bring to a boil, and boil for about 5 minutes.
3. 🥄 Put the pulp through a food mill to remove the seeds.
4. 🟣 Pour the hot pulp over the skins and let them sit. Let it sit for at least a couple of hours if using right away. This colors the pulp and adds flavor and texture.
5. 🍇 Chop the skins a little when adding them back, if you like.
6. 🧊 Freeze in quart containers.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The source suggests freezing in quarts, which is about the right size for pies or jams.
- 🌟 Freeze in containers, not loose in zip-top bags. Bags can burst when carried.
- 🌟 Source: Krislady, September 21, 2010.
