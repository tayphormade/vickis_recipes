# Ice Cream/Sorbet for the Ninja Creami

- 🍨 **Equipment:** Ninja Creami (an ice cream maker can be used instead, as the source notes in each recipe)
- 🧊 **Yields:** strawberry ice cream 1 pint, lemon ice cream (FroYo) 1 pint, honeydew sorbet 2 pints
- ⏳ **Freeze:** 24 hours for the Ninja Creami methods

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Best Strawberry Ninja Creami Recipe

- 🍓 1 cup chopped or sliced strawberries
- 🍬 2 tablespoons monkfruit sugar
- 🥛 ⅓ cup whole fat Greek yogurt
- 🥛 ⅓ cup whole milk or cream
- 🥛 2 tablespoons sour cream (optional)

### Best Lemon Ice Cream (FroYo)

- 🍬 4 tablespoons monkfruit sugar
- 🍋 Zest of one lemon
- 🍋 Juice of one lemon
- 🥛 1 cup Greek yogurt
- 🥛 1 cup whole milk

### Honeydew Sorbet

- 🍈 1 whole honeydew, cut into pieces without the peel
- 🍬 2 tablespoons monkfruit sugar
- 🧂 1 healthy pinch of salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍓 Strawberry: Mix the monkfruit with the strawberries and let set for 30 to 40 minutes, until it has a soupy mixture.
2. 🥛 Strawberry: Add the Greek yogurt and whole milk and mix. Add the sour cream if you want a more creamy texture.
3. ❄️ Strawberry: Freeze for 24 hours. Take the container out for 15 minutes, then process in the Ninja Creami on light ice cream. OR place the mixture in the refrigerator overnight, or until thoroughly chilled, and add it to an ice cream maker. Makes 1 pint.
4. 🍋 Lemon: Mix the monkfruit with the lemon zest really well until the zest releases its oils.
5. 🥛 Lemon: Add the lemon juice, yogurt, and milk, mixing well (some small chunks are OK).
6. ❄️ Lemon: Freeze for 24 hours. Take the container out for 15 minutes, then process in the Ninja Creami on light ice cream. OR place the mixture in the refrigerator overnight, or until thoroughly chilled, and add it to an ice cream maker. Makes 1 pint.
7. 🍈 Honeydew sorbet: In a blender, combine the chopped honeydew pieces, monkfruit, and salt. Blend until liquidy.
8. ❄️ Honeydew sorbet: Pour into 2 Ninja Creami containers and freeze for 24 hours. Take the container out for 15 minutes, then process in the Ninja Creami on sorbet. Keep re-spinning until you get the texture you like. OR place the mixture in the refrigerator overnight, or until thoroughly chilled, and add it to an ice cream maker. Makes 2 pints.
