# Concord Grape Sorbet

🔗 **Source:** [Cookie Dough and Oven Mitt](https://www.cookiedoughandovenmitt.com/concord-grape-sorbet/)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 3 cups sorbet
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 15 minutes
- 🕑 **Total time:** 25 minutes
- 📊 **Calories:** 475 kcal (as listed by the source; the serving size is not specified)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍇 4 cups Concord grapes, washed and removed from the vine
- 💧 ½ cup water
- 🍬 1 cup granulated sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Place the grapes, water, and sugar in a medium saucepan. Turn the heat to medium and heat for 12–15 minutes.
2. 🥄 Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh strainer to remove the seeds and skins.
3. 🧊 Place the mixture in the fridge until completely cooled, or overnight.
4. 🍦 Once completely cooled, place it in the ice cream maker for the recommended time for your machine. The author's machine recommends no longer than 20 minutes.
5. 🧊 Place in a freezer-safe container and freeze for 2 hours, or until very firm. Scoop and serve!
