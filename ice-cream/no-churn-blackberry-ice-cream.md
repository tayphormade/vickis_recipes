# No-Churn Blackberry Ice Cream

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cook:** 20 minutes
- 🧊 **Chill:** 1 hour
- ❄️ **Freeze:** 4 hours (at least) and up to overnight
- ⌛ **Total:** 5 hours 35 minutes
- 📊 **Per serving:** 499 calories; total fat 28g; saturated fat 17g; cholesterol 90mg; sodium 102mg; total carbohydrate 57g; dietary fiber 6g; total sugars 52g; protein 9g; vitamin C 26mg; calcium 262mg; iron 1mg; potassium 490mg (serving size not stated in the source)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🫐 2 pounds fresh blackberries, rinsed and drained, divided
- 🍬 ⅓ cup white sugar
- 🍋 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- 🥛 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥛 2 cups heavy cream

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🫐 Add 1½ pounds of the blackberries (about 5 cups), the sugar, and the lemon juice to a saucepan, and bring to a simmer over medium heat.
2. 🥄 Use a potato masher to crush the berries, and continue to simmer until the mixture thickens and reduces by half, about 20 minutes. The heat can be turned up to reduce faster, but this will require more careful watching to be sure the blackberries do not scorch.
3. 🥣 Strain the blackberry mixture through a fine-mesh strainer into a bowl, making sure to get every drop of juice; discard the fiber and seeds. Stir the sweetened condensed milk and vanilla into the bowl. Refrigerate the mixture until completely cold, about 1 hour.
4. 🥛 Beat the cream in a chilled glass or metal bowl with an electric mixer until medium-stiff peaks form. Add half the whipped cream to the chilled blackberry mixture and fold in with a whisk until almost all the cream is incorporated. Add the remaining whipped cream and use a spatula to gently fold it in. Pour the mixture into a container, cover, and freeze until solid, at least 4 hours and up to overnight.
5. 🍨 To serve, add as many of the fresh berries as you want to the top, and use an ice cream scoop to mix and smash them into the ice cream as you scoop. Add a few remaining berries on top of each serving.
