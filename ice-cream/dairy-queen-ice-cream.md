# Dairy Queen Ice Cream

- 🧊 **Chill:** 5 to 6 hours
- 🍨 **Equipment:** 4- to 6-quart ice cream machine

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥄 2 envelopes Knox gelatin
- 💧 ½ cup cold water
- 🥛 4 cups whole milk
- 🍬 2 cups granulated sugar
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🥛 3 cups cream

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 Soak the Knox gelatin in the cold water.
2. 🔥 Heat the milk, but do not boil.
3. 🥄 Remove from heat and add the gelatin, sugar, vanilla extract, and salt.
4. 🧊 Cool, and add the cream.
5. ❄️ Chill for 5 to 6 hours.
6. 🍨 Pour into a 4- to 6-quart ice cream machine and process as per the manufacturer's instructions.
