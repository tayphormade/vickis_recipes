# Poor Man's Ice Cream

- 🍽️ **Servings:** not stated in the source
- 🧊 **Chill:** overnight, the can and bowl in the refrigerator
- ❄️ **Freeze:** about 1 hour, then whip again

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥛 1 can evaporated milk (can size not stated)
- ❄️ 1½ cups powdered sugar
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧊 Place the can of evaporated milk and a glass bowl in the refrigerator overnight.
2. 🥣 Pour the can of evaporated milk into the cold bowl. Add the powdered sugar and vanilla.
3. 🥄 Whip the ingredients with a mixer until foamy, and place in the freezer for about an hour.
4. 🥄 Remove from the freezer and whip again. The ice cream is ready to serve, or return it to the freezer until set.
5. 🍨 Enjoy!

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: "Our Favorite Recipes," St. John's Guild Church Spiral Cookbook, West Bend, Wisconsin, 1949.
- 🌟 The source does not give the size of the can of evaporated milk, a yield, or a freezing time beyond "about an hour."
