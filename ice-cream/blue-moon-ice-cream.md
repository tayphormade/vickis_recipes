# Blue Moon Ice Cream

🔗 **Source:** [Food.com](http://www.food.com/123818)

By babpd on May 27, 2005. The source says this tastes like the Blue Moon ice cream sold at Kilwins in northern Michigan, and has no pineapple or alcohol.

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 6
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- ⌛ **Total:** 30 minutes
- 📊 **Per serving** (serving size 1, 117 g): 310 calories; total fat 25.0 g; saturated fat 15.6 g; cholesterol 91.8 mg; sodium 137.9 mg; total carbohydrate 20.1 g; dietary fiber 0.0 g; sugars 16.7 g; protein 2.4 g

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥛 ½ cup milk
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🥛 ½ cup half-and-half
- 🌼 ½ teaspoon vanilla extract or ½ teaspoon vanilla oil
- 🥛 1½ cups whipping cream
- 🔵 ⅛ teaspoon blue food coloring
- 🍮 3 teaspoons vanilla pudding mix
- 🍇 1 teaspoon raspberry flavored oil or 1 cup frozen raspberries, pureed
- 🍋 1 teaspoon lemon flavored oil or 1 teaspoon lemon extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Scald the ½ cup of milk in a pan.
2. 🍬 Add ½ cup of sugar and ¼ teaspoon of salt. Stir until dissolved.
3. 🥛 Add ½ cup of half-and-half.
4. 🥛 Add 1½ cups of whipping cream.
5. 🌼 Add the vanilla, raspberry, and lemon flavorings.
6. 🔵 Add the blue food coloring.
7. 🧊 Chill in the refrigerator until very cold.
8. 🍨 Put in an ice cream machine.

- 🌟 The source's flavoring line lists either the oil or the frozen raspberries, and either the oil or the lemon extract.
