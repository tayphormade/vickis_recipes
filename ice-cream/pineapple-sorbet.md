# Pineapple Sorbet

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 9 servings (1/2 cup each)
- 🧊 **Freeze:** 1 hour or until firm, after churning
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 116 calories; fat 0.2g; carbohydrates 30g; protein 0.2g; fiber 0.5g; sodium 1mg

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍍 1 small pineapple, peeled and cored
- 🍋 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 🍬 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons sugar
- 🌿 Mint sprigs (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔪 Cut pineapple into 2-inch pieces.
2. 🥣 Place pineapple and lemon juice in a food processor; process until smooth.
3. 🍬 Add sugar; process 1 minute or until sugar dissolves.
4. 🍨 Pour mixture into the freezer can of an ice-cream freezer; freeze according to manufacturer's instructions.
5. 🥄 Spoon sorbet into a freezer-safe container. Cover and freeze 1 hour or until firm.
6. 🌿 Garnish with mint sprigs, if desired.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🧊 No ice-cream freezer: freeze the mixture in a covered metal bowl for 3 hours, or until hard on the outside but slushy in the middle. Remove from the freezer, beat with a whisk until smooth, and return to the freezer, covered, for 4 hours until firm.
