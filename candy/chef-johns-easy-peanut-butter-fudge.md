# Chef John's Easy Peanut Butter Fudge

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 64 small cubes
- 🧰 **Pan:** 8 x 8-inch square pan, buttered and plastic-wrapped

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 1¼ cups unsalted butter
- 🥜 1½ cups creamy peanut butter (natural peanut butter with oil)
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon kosher salt (or ¼ teaspoon fine salt)
- 🌼 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract
- ❄️ 1 pound powdered sugar, sifted (about 3½ to 4 cups unsifted)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 In a saucepan, combine the butter and peanut butter, and heat until bubbles appear around the edge. You don't need to get it to a full boil.
2. 🥣 Add the salt, vanilla extract, and powdered sugar, and stir together until a mass forms.
3. 🧈 Press the fudge into a plastic-wrapped, buttered 8 x 8-inch square pan, and place it in the refrigerator to firm up a little.
4. 🔪 Remove from the refrigerator and cut into pieces before it gets too hard. If it is too cold and crumbly to cut, let it warm up.
