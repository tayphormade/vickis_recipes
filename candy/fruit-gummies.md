# Fruit Gummies

- 🍽️ **Yield:** not stated in the source
- 🧊 **Storage:** up to 2 weeks in an airtight container in the fridge

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍒 500 g fruits with stone
- 🍋 80 ml lemon juice or orange juice
- 🧪 3 tbsp vegan gelatin
- 🍯 2 tbsp liquid sweetener, like honey, maple syrup, or simple syrup

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Blend the fruit and juice until smooth.
2. 🥄 Strain, and use the remaining pulp for fruit leather.
3. 🥣 To the strained fruit paste, add the maple syrup and gelatin, and mix until combined.
4. ⏳ Let it sit for 2 minutes, then add it to a saucepan.
5. 🔥 Warm it on low-medium heat for 5 minutes.
6. 🔎 Check that your gelatin is suitable for heating, since some gelatin types are not suitable for boiling.
7. 🧁 Add the mixture to silicone molds, then store in the fridge until set and cooled.
8. 🔪 Cut into the size you prefer.
9. 🍬 Optional: roll them in granulated sugar once they are set and cooled.
10. 🧊 Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
