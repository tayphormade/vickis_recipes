# Almond Paste

- 🍽️ **Yield:** the metric example makes 8.625 ounces (245 g)
- 🔥 **Heat, if too runny:** 220°F (104°C) until it forms a thick paste

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌰 1 part blanched almonds (whole, by volume)
- 🍬 1 part powdered sugar
- 🥚 Egg white (1 egg white for each cup of almonds)
- 🌿 Flavoring, almond and/or vanilla

### Example (US measures)

- 🌰 8 oz. blanched almonds
- 🍬 8 oz. powdered sugar
- 🥚 1 egg white
- 🌿 ½ tsp. flavoring: almond, vanilla, or rose water

### Example (metric)

- 🌰 110 g almond flour
- 🍬 110 g powdered sugar
- 🥚 ½ egg white
- 🌿 ¼ tsp flavoring

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌰 Mix the almonds and ½ of the sugar, and blend in a blender or food processor.
2. 🍬 Add the remainder of the sugar and blend.
3. 🥚 Add the egg white and flavoring, if using, and blend until a ball forms in a food processor.
4. 🔥 If the paste is too runny, heat it up to 220°F (104°C) until it forms a thick paste.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: a typed family recipe titled "Almond Paste." The heating step is credited to the Odense Almond Paste process.
- 📏 Measurements given in the source: 0.25 cups of almonds = 1 ounce = 28 grams = 20–25 almonds. The source also says "cup of sugar = 30 grams of sugar," as printed.
- 📏 Volume to weight, as printed: 1 cup whole almonds = 143 g (5 oz); 1 cup ground almonds = 112 g; 1 cup slivered almonds = 115 g (4 oz); 1 cup sliced almonds = 86 g (3 oz).
