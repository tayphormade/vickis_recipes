# Maple Syrup Candy

- 🍽️ **Yield:** not stated in the source
- 🌡️ **Hard crack stage:** 244–246°F (118–119°C) at sea level, per the source (cook to 32–34°F above the boiling point)
- 🧰 **Equipment:** candy thermometer, large heavy-bottomed saucepan, silicone or lightly greased metal molds

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍁 750 ml / 25 oz maple syrup (up to 4 cups)
- 🧈 Butter (optional)
- 🫗 4–5 drops vegetable oil (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Pour the maple syrup into a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan. The syrup should fill no more than 25% of the pan's volume.
2. 🧈 Butter the inside rim of the pot. The butterfat helps keep the bubbling syrup from rising too high in the pot.
3. 🫗 Add 4–5 drops of vegetable oil to the syrup, which also helps keep it from boiling over.
4. 🔥 Using a candy thermometer, bring the syrup to a boil over medium heat, and cook it to the hard crack stage, 32°F to 34°F above the boiling point. The source gives 244°F to 246°F (118°C to 119°C) for sea level, since water boils at 212°F (100°C) there; high altitude is lower.
5. ⏳ Remove from the heat carefully, without jostling the pot, and let it sit for 2–3 minutes.
6. 🥣 Remove the thermometer. With a wooden spoon, stir slowly for the first minute, then stir rapidly and continuously until the syrup begins to lose its gloss and you can see a distinct color change. This takes about 2 to 5 minutes.
7. 🧁 Pour into silicone or lightly greased metal molds immediately. Work quickly, as the syrup begins to harden almost instantly.
8. 🧊 Let cool and harden in the molds for at least 10 minutes. The candies will turn a whitish color when they are set, and should pop right out of the molds with ease.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: "Maple Syrup Candy," a typed recipe in a Word file. No author is named.
- 💡 The source suggests scraping any excess maple sugar from the bottom of the pot and saving it to put in coffee.
- ⚠️ The source says to boil water first to find your boiling point, since high altitude lowers the temperature for hard crack.
