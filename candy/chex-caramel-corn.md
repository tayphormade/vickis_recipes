# Chex Caramel Corn

🔗 **Source:** [Key Ingredient](http://www.keyingredient.com/recipes/877205063/chex-caramel-corn/)

- 🔥 **Oven:** 250°F (121°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 45 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes
- 🗄️ **Storage:** Airtight container, up to a week (per the source description)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥣 7 cups Chex cereal (corn, rice, or a combination)
- 🥣 2 cups Cheerios cereal
- 🍿 6 cups popped corn (about ⅓ cup popcorn kernels)
- 🍜 3 cups Chinese crispy noodles
- 🥜 1 to 2 cups peanuts, cocktail style (the source says "we love peanuts")
- 🧈 1 cup butter (don't use margarine or tub spread products)
- 🍯 6 tablespoons light corn syrup
- 🍬 1⅛ cups brown sugar, packed
- 🌼 ¾ teaspoon vanilla

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Heat oven to 250°F (121°C).
2. 🍿 Remove and discard unpopped kernels from the popped popcorn.
3. 🥣 Mix the cereals, popcorn, Chinese noodles, and peanuts in a large roasting pan; set aside.
4. 🧈 Heat the butter, sugar, and corn syrup to boiling in a 2-quart saucepan over medium heat, stirring frequently. (The source says you can heat them in a microwave if you prefer.)
5. 🌼 Add the vanilla. (The source prefers to add it once the caramel is hot, to keep the vanilla flavor more intense.)
6. 🥄 Pour the caramel over the cereal mixture, stirring until evenly coated.
7. 🔥 Bake 45 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes.
8. 🍽️ Spread on waxed or parchment paper to cool, stirring occasionally to break up.
9. 🗄️ Store in an airtight container.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Key Ingredient, adapted from Chex.com and Betty Crocker.com. The source says the author increased the peanuts and popcorn.
- 🥜 Tasting note from the source: substituting pecans or cashews for the peanuts would probably be good too.
- 🎁 The source says this makes a great Halloween or Christmas gift.
