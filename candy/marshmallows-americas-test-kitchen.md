# Marshmallows (America's Test Kitchen)

- 🧰 **Pan:** 13 x 9-inch pan, lined with foil with overhanging ends (a sling), sprayed with vegetable oil spray
- 🌡️ **Syrup:** cook to 240°F (116°C)
- ⏳ **Set:** overnight
- 🍽️ **Yield:** not stated in the source

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Marshmallow

- 🧪 2½ tbsp gelatin
- 💧 ½ cup cold water (for the gelatin)
- 💧 ½ cup water
- 🍯 ⅔ cup corn syrup (the source notes it used ⅔ cup, but the original said ⅓ cup)
- 🍬 2 cups granulated sugar
- 🧂 ¼ tsp salt
- 🌼 2 tsp vanilla extract (or other flavor, if desired)

### Dusting mixture

- 🌽 ½ cup cornstarch
- ❄️ ½ cup confectioners' sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧴 Line a 13 x 9-inch pan with foil, with the ends hanging over to form a sling to help remove the marshmallows later. Spray the foil with vegetable oil spray to prevent sticking.
2. 🥣 Sprinkle the gelatin over the cold water and let it set for about 15 minutes in a large mixing bowl on a stand mixer.
3. 🔥 Pour the corn syrup into a saucepan. Pour the sugar into the center of the pan, avoiding getting any crystals on the side of the pan to prevent crystallization, and add the salt.
4. 🔥 Bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat, swirling gently from time to time without stirring. Cook 6–8 minutes, until it reaches 240°F (116°C).
5. 🥣 Start the mixer with the bowl of gelatin on low to break it up. While the mixer is still running, slowly and carefully pour the hot sugar mixture into the bowl, trying not to hit the side of the bowl or the whisk. Gradually increase the speed to high. Mix for 10–12 minutes, until the mixture is opaque white and just barely warm. Don't let it cool down too much, or it will firm up in the bowl.
6. 🌼 Near the end of mixing, add the vanilla extract.
7. 🧴 Quickly scrape the marshmallow, using a spatula sprayed with cooking spray, into the foil-lined pan. Smooth the top flat as quickly as possible. Set aside.
8. ❄️ In a bowl, mix the cornstarch and confectioners' sugar. Sift a couple of tablespoons of the mixture over the marshmallow, saving the rest for dusting later. Cover the marshmallow and let it set overnight.
9. 🔪 The next day, sift the cornstarch and sugar mixture over a cutting board, and spray a chef's knife with vegetable cooking spray. Pull the marshmallow out of the pan using the foil sling, and turn it out onto the cutting board. Sprinkle the bottom side with a couple of tablespoons of the cornstarch and sugar mixture. Cut the marshmallow into 1-inch strips, then into cubes, spraying the knife as needed. Coat each marshmallow in the cornstarch and sugar mixture, then toss into a strainer and knock off the excess.
10. 📦 Store in a zip-top bag or airtight container for up to 2 weeks.
