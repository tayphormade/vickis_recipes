# Cream Cheese Mints

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/9441/cream-cheese-mints/)

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- ❄️ **Additional time:** 2 hours (drying)
- ⏳ **Total:** 2 hours 30 minutes
- 🔢 **Per serving (as listed by the source):** 112 calories; 3 g total fat; 2 g saturated fat; 8 mg cholesterol; 21 mg sodium; 23 g total carbohydrate; 22 g total sugars; 0 g protein; 5 mg calcium; 0 mg iron; 7 mg potassium. The source does not give the number of servings.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧀 1 (3 ounce) package cream cheese, softened
- 🧈 1 tablespoon butter, softened
- 🍬 3 cups confectioners' sugar
- 🌿 2 drops peppermint oil
- 🎨 1 drop any color food coloring paste

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 In a large bowl, combine the cream cheese, butter, and confectioners' sugar. Mix in the peppermint oil. Color as desired with food coloring paste, or leave white.
2. 🥄 Roll the mixture into small balls, and place them on waxed paper. Flatten with a fork dipped in confectioners' sugar. Let dry for about 2 hours on waxed paper, then freeze or refrigerate.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Allrecipes, recipe by Diane. The source describes these as everyone's favorite holiday candy recipe.
- 🗄️ Storage: if stored in an airtight container, the mints can be frozen for a couple of months (per the source).
- 🎨 Shapes: the source says the mints can also be made into pretty shapes by rolling them in granulated sugar, pressing them into candy molds, and dropping them out.
