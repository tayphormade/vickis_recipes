# Salted Caramel Sauce

🔗 **Source:** [Bake. Eat. Repeat.](https://bake-eat-repeat.com/salted-caramel-sauce-recipe/)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 1 cup (16 servings of 1 tablespoon, per the source's nutrition panel)
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 10 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 15 minutes
- 🔢 **Per serving (1 tablespoon, estimated by the source):** 112 calories; 7 g total fat; 4 g saturated fat; 0 g trans fat; 2 g unsaturated fat; 20 mg cholesterol; 201 mg sodium; 13 g carbohydrates; 0 g fiber; 13 g sugar; 0 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 1 cup granulated sugar
- 🧈 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut up into 6 to 8 pieces
- 🥛 ½ cup heavy cream
- 🧂 1½ teaspoons kosher salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Place the granulated sugar in a large saucepan over medium heat.
2. 🥄 Stirring constantly with a rubber spatula, allow the sugar to heat. It will clump together as it starts to melt and then will start turning to liquid.
3. 👀 Keep stirring so it doesn't burn, until it is completely melted into an amber-colored liquid.
4. 🧈 Once the sugar is melted, immediately add the pieces of butter.
5. 🫧 The liquid will bubble up when you add the butter.
6. 🥄 Stir the caramel until the butter is completely melted, about 2 minutes.
7. 🥛 Slowly drizzle in the heavy cream while still stirring. Be careful, as the caramel will bubble up a lot when you add the cream.
8. ⏲️ Allow this mixture to boil for 1 minute, stirring constantly, and then remove from the heat.
9. 🧂 Stir in the salt and allow it to cool down a bit before using it, or pouring it into another container, as it will be extremely hot.
10. 🗄️ Cover it tightly and store it in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.
11. 🔥 You can warm it up in the microwave before using it. The source's author scoops out the amount to be used into another small bowl to heat, since microwaving it multiple times can keep it from melting as nicely.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Bake. Eat. Repeat.
- 🧂 The source says this makes quite a salty salted caramel sauce. You can decrease the salt to 1 teaspoon if you want the salted flavor to be less pronounced.
- ⚠️ Use a large enough pan, as the caramel does bubble up quite a bit (per the source).
- 📋 The source lists the category as Dessert.
