# Easiest Peanut Butter Fudge

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/22724/easiest-peanut-butter-fudge/)

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** 5 minutes
- ❄️ **Chilling:** About 1 hour
- ⏳ **Total:** 1 hour 20 minutes
- 🧰 **Pan:** 8 x 8-inch dish
- 🔢 **Per serving (as listed by the source):** 168 calories; 6 g total fat; 3 g saturated fat; 8 mg cholesterol; 54 mg sodium; 28 g total carbohydrate; 0 g dietary fiber; 2 g protein; 20 mg calcium; 0 mg iron; 65 mg potassium. The source does not give the number of servings.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 ½ cup butter
- 🍬 1 (16 ounce) package brown sugar
- 🥛 ½ cup milk
- 🥜 ¾ cup peanut butter
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🍬 3½ cups confectioners' sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧈 Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat; stir in the brown sugar and milk. Bring to a boil and boil for 2 minutes, stirring frequently.
2. 🥜 Remove the pot from heat; stir in the peanut butter and vanilla.
3. 🥣 Place the confectioners' sugar in a large mixing bowl. Pour in the peanut butter mixture and beat with an electric mixer until smooth.
4. 🥄 Pour the peanut butter mixture into an 8 x 8-inch dish.
5. ❄️ Chill until firm before slicing, about 1 hour.
