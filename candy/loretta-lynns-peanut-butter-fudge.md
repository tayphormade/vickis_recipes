# Loretta Lynn's Peanut Butter Fudge

- 🌡️ **Soft-ball stage:** 234°F (112°C) or so (less for high altitude)
- 🍽️ **Yield:** not stated in this version

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 3 cups sugar
- 🍫 1 tsp cocoa
- 💧 ¾ cup water
- 🧈 1 stick butter
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla
- 🥜 1 cup smooth peanut butter

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 In a heavy pan, add ¾ cup of water and bring it to a boil over medium-high heat.
2. 🥣 Mix the 3 cups sugar and 1 tsp cocoa in a bowl. When the water comes to a boil, mix the sugar and cocoa into the water with a wooden spoon.
3. 🌡️ Using a thermometer, bring the mixture up to the soft-ball stage (234°F / 112°C or so; less for high altitude), stirring every so often.
4. 🧈 When it reaches the soft-ball stage, add the butter and vanilla, stirring until the butter melts.
5. 🔥 Turn off the heat and add the peanut butter. Stir continuously until the peanut butter is smooth, then let the mixture cool down a bit before you start beating the fudge until it loses its shine.
6. 🫓 Pour it out onto a buttered plate or pan, and set in a cool place until it is set.
7. 🔪 Cut into pieces.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: "Loretta Lynn's Peanut Butter Fudge," a typed recipe in a Word file.
- 🔁 A different version of this recipe appears in the source document for Grandma's Old Fashion Fudge. That version cooks the cocoa mixture in boiling water over medium heat, stirs in the peanut butter off the heat without beating, pours into a greased 9 x 9 x 2-inch pan, and adds "Makes 24 squares" and says to "score 1-inch squares while warm" and let cool about 20 minutes before cutting when firm. Those details are not in this version.
