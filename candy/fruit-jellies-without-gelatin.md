# Fruit Jellies Without Gelatin

- 🍽️ **Yield:** not stated in the source
- ⏲️ **Chill:** about 2 hours until firm

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧃 200 g fruit juice
- 🍬 20 g sugar
- 🌽 6 g cornstarch

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Mix the juice, sugar, and cornstarch together in a saucepan.
2. 🔥 Bring to a boil over low heat for about 10 minutes, or until reduced to 1/3 and thick.
3. 🧊 Let cool a bit.
4. 🧁 Put the mixture in a pastry bag and fill lightly oiled molds (candy-bit size).
5. ❄️ Cover with plastic wrap and place in the refrigerator until firm, about 2 hours.
6. 🍬 Turn out and coat with sugar. Coconut dust would work well too.
7. 😋 Enjoy.
