# See's Candies Cinnamon Lollypop

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 14 lollipops
- 🍳 **Cooking:** 20 to 30 minutes to reach 290°F (143°C)
- ❄️ **Cooling:** At least 2 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥛 ¼ cup heavy cream
- 🧈 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 🍬 1 cup granulated sugar
- 🍬 3 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 🍯 ¼ cup corn syrup
- 💧 ¼ cup water
- 🎨 25 drops (scant ¼ teaspoon) red food coloring
- 🌿 ½ teaspoon LorAnn cinnamon oil candy flavoring

### You will also need

- 🥢 14 lollipop sticks
- 🧊 1 plastic ice cube tray (small cubes with a silicone bottom is best)
- 🩹 Masking tape
- 🔧 Metal skewer
- 🌡️ Candy thermometer
- 🫒 Vegetable oil or coconut oil (for the mold)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔧 Prepare the ice cube tray by making dots on the side of the tray in the approximate middle of the side of each cube mold. Heat up the tip of a metal skewer over your stovetop until it is hot enough to melt the plastic. Push the tip of the skewer through each dot to make a hole for the lollipop stick, then glide the skewer up to elongate the hole into a slit. Don't melt the plastic all the way up; stop just below the top of the ice tray. The elongated hole allows for an easy release of the lollipops later.
2. 🩹 Once you have melted holes into all the cube molds, apply masking tape over all the holes. Rub some vegetable oil or coconut oil inside each of the ice molds with your finger. Use the tip of the metal skewer (cooled now) to poke holes through the tape at the BOTTOM of each hole, then poke a lollipop stick through each of the holes.
3. 📏 Place some long objects on the counter under the sticks on both sides to keep them level. (The source used pencils.)
4. 🥣 Combine the cream, butter, both sugars, corn syrup, and water in the order listed in a medium saucepan (preferably one with a spout) over medium-low heat. Stir as the mixture heats, then add a candy thermometer.
5. 🌡️ Turn off the heat when the candy reaches 290°F (143°C), which takes 20 to 30 minutes, then add the food coloring.
6. 🌡️ When the temperature reaches 285°F (141°C), remove the thermometer and stir in the cinnamon flavoring.
7. 🥄 Pour the candy into the prepared ice cube tray, filling the molds nearly to the top.
8. ❄️ Cool for at least 2 hours, then remove the masking tape. Twist the ice cube tray to help release the lollipops, as you would for ice. Pull up on the stick for each lollipop to remove it from the mold, then angle it up and pull it out.
