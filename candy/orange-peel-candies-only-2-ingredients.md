# Orange Peel Candies (Only 2 Ingredients)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 25 minutes
- 🫙 **Storage:** a sealed jar at room temperature, for three weeks

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 400 g sugar
- 🍊 300 g orange peel (about six large oranges)
- 🧃 ½ cup fresh orange juice (from 1 large orange)
- 🌼 ½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- 🍬 Enough sugar to coat the orange peel

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧃 Freshly juice one orange; you should get about ½ cup. Depending on the type of citrus, you can change the juice. If you use lime or lemon, which are extra sour, you only need 3 tbsp instead of ½ cup.
2. 🔪 Cut the orange peel into small pieces. Use a utility knife to remove the white layer from the peel. Don't remove it completely; leave a little pith for better texture.
3. 🍲 Boil the orange peel for 10 minutes, then change the water and soak overnight to remove the bitter taste.
4. 🥣 Drain and transfer the peel into a pan. Add sugar, orange juice, and, optionally, vanilla.
5. 🔥 Stir and simmer over medium-low heat until the syrup thickens, about 25 minutes.
6. 🧊 Let the candies dry on a rack for about 1 hour, and coat with more sugar to prevent sticking.
