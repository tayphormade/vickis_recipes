# Haribo Gold-Bears Gummy Candy Hack

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 50 pieces
- ⏱️ **Hustle:** 20 minutes
- 🕑 **Hang:** 3 days
- 🕑 **Total time:** 3 days 20 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍓 1 3-oz box Jell-O Gelatin Dessert powder (raspberry, lemon, orange, pineapple, lime, or strawberry)
- 🧪 1 pkg. Knox unflavored gelatin
- 🍋 ¼ teaspoon citric acid
- 🍯 ¼ cup corn syrup
- 💧 ⅓ cup water (room temperature)

### You will also need

- 🧸 1-inch silicone bear candy molds
- 🧴 Oil cooking spray

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine the Jell-O, unflavored gelatin, and citric acid in a medium microwave-safe bowl, and stir to combine.
2. 🥄 Add the corn syrup and water, and stir gently with a soup spoon until all of the ingredients are blended together. Stirring too fast may add air bubbles, so keep it slow and sweeping. Let the mixture sit for 5 minutes so the gelatin can bloom.
3. 🔥 Stir the candy syrup gently again, then microwave it on high for 30 seconds. Stir, then zap it for another 15 seconds. Repeat heating it for 15 seconds 3 more times, for a total heating time of 1½ minutes. If air bubbles begin to form on the surface, stop heating and stir gently. You don't want any bubbles in the syrup if you can help it. Let the mixture sit for 10 minutes. Give it a gentle stir every couple of minutes, and you should see it become clearer over time.
4. 🧴 Spray the bear molds lightly with oil spray.
5. 🫗 Pour the candy syrup into a measuring cup with a spout, then carefully pour the liquid into the bear molds. Don't fill each bear all the way to the top. Leave a little room in each mold.
6. ⏳ Let the candy sit for 30 minutes, then put the molds into the refrigerator for another 30 minutes.
7. 🧸 When the candy is firm, push each piece out of the molds and arrange them on a baking sheet or in a large storage container, standing upright. Let them sit this way for 48 hours, then lay them on their backs for 24 hours. This dehydrates the bears so they become chewy like the originals. Depending on your climate, the gummy bears may have shrunk enough after 72 hours. If not, leave them out for another day or two, until they are chewy. Toss them around every so often as they dry. When they are chewy enough for you, seal them in an airtight covered container or zip-top bag. Stored sealed, they should last a couple of weeks.

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Todd Wilbur, The Food Hacker, "Haribo Gold-Bears Gummy Candy copycat recipe" (© 2017). The source describes the candy as a nearly 100-year-old German recipe hacked with Jell-O.
- 🔁 The recipe makes one flavor. Repeat it for every flavor you want in the finished batch.
- 💡 Tidbit from the source: if the gummies begin to stick together, toss them with a pinch of cornstarch. Real gummies have traces of cornstarch from manufacturing; the molds are made of pressed cornstarch in a machine called a "mogul."
