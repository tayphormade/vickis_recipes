# Vegan Caramel

🔗 **Source:** [Nora Cooks](https://www.noracooks.com/wprm_print/vegan-caramel)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12 servings
- 🍳 **Cook:** 10 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 10 minutes
- 🔢 **Per serving (1 serving as listed):** 121 kcal; 15 g carbohydrates; 1 g protein; 7 g fat; 6 g saturated fat; 5 mg sodium; 83 mg potassium; 1 g fiber; 13 g sugar; 1 mg vitamin C; 14 mg calcium; 1 mg iron

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥥 1 cup coconut cream
- 🍬 ¾ cup brown sugar
- 🧂 Optional: pinch of salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 In a small pan or pot, add the coconut cream and brown sugar. Heat over medium-high heat, stirring constantly while the coconut cream melts. The mixture will get bubbly and almost foamy. Lower the heat to a simmer and stir frequently for about 5 to 10 minutes. If you want salted caramel, add a pinch of salt, or more to taste.
2. 🗄️ Remove from heat. The caramel can be used immediately, or let it cool, and it will thicken considerably. Each time you go to use it, give it a good stir. Store leftover caramel in a covered glass container in the refrigerator for about a week.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Nora Taylor, Nora Cooks. The source lists the course as dessert and the cuisine as American.
- 🥥 Canned coconut cream is preferred. If you can't find it, you can use full-fat coconut milk: scoop out the thick white part and leave the liquid in the can. You might need a few cans of full-fat coconut milk to get enough cream.
- 🍬 The source says coconut sugar may be substituted for the brown sugar, but the author prefers brown sugar.
- 🔥 The caramel will be quite thick after refrigerating. Microwave it, or heat it in a pan and stir until it thins a bit, as desired.
