# Easy Old-Fashioned Homemade Marshmallows

- 🍽️ **Yield:** About 70 homemade marshmallows
- ⏲️ **Setting time:** About 4 hours (or less in the fridge), or up to overnight
- 🌬️ **Air drying:** 3 to 4 hours before storing
- 📏 **Pan:** 9 x 13" (23 x 33 cm)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 💧 1 cup (240 ml) water, divided
- 🟨 2 packages gelatin (7 grams each) or 4½ teaspoons loose gelatin powder
- 🍬 2 cups (420 g) sugar
- 🌼 1½ teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 🧂 ⅛ teaspoon salt
- ❄️ Icing sugar to dust the pan
- 🥥 Icing sugar, toasted coconut (about 1¾ cups), or finely chopped toasted pecans (about 1½ cups), to roll the marshmallows in (see Notes)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 Sprinkle the gelatin on top of ½ cup (120 ml) of the cold water in a small bowl or measuring cup. Stir lightly with a fork. Let it "bloom" for at least 5 minutes, until it is all moistened and softened. It will be very thick.
2. 🍬 Meanwhile, heat the sugar and the remaining ½ cup of water in a medium saucepan until the sugar is dissolved. Add the dissolved gelatin to the sugar syrup, using a spatula to get every bit of gelatin out of the bowl.
3. 🔥 Bring the mixture to a full rolling boil. (It will get foamy and you can't stir down the bubbles.)
4. ⏲️ Remove the saucepan from the heat. Let sit until lukewarm (about 20 to 25 minutes).
5. 🥄 Pour the syrup into the bowl of a mixer. Add the vanilla and salt. Beat with a stand mixer (using the whisk attachment) on high speed until thick and tripled in volume, about 7 to 8 minutes, or with a rotary hand mixer, which will take a bit longer, maybe 10 minutes.
6. 🧈 While it is beating, prepare a 9 x 13" (23 x 33 cm) pan by lightly greasing it and dusting it with icing sugar. Tip the pan over and knock out any excess icing sugar.
7. 🥄 When the marshmallow mixture has finished whipping, scrape it into the prepared pan, and use a wet spatula to smooth the top.
8. ❄️ Allow to set until cold, about 4 hours (or less if placed in the fridge) or up to overnight.
9. 🔪 Cut into squares with a knife dipped in water between each cut. If the top of the marshmallow mixture has dried out too much to stick well to the coconut, wet your fingers and smooth a thin slick of water over the top to moisten it again.
10. 🥥 Roll the marshmallow cubes in either the icing sugar, toasted coconut, or chopped nuts. Shake off excess if using icing sugar. Press down firmly into the coconut or nuts if using them, so they stick to the marshmallows.
11. 🌬️ Place on a rack and allow to air dry for 3 to 4 hours, then store in an airtight container at cool room temperature for up to 2 weeks.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Mabel Johnson.
- 🥥 Toasting coconut (for the "toasted coconut" option): heat it in a skillet over medium heat. Stir constantly once it starts to brown, since it can burn easily. Toast to the depth of brown you'd like.
- 🌰 Toasting pecans: place them on a cookie sheet in a 350°F (177°C) oven for 10 minutes, until they are fragrant and golden. Chop very finely so they stick to the marshmallows.
- 🍯 Easy Honey Marshmallows (variation from the same source): make the recipe as above, replacing the sugar with 1 cup (240 ml) liquid honey. Use 3 packages gelatin (7 grams each) or 2 tablespoons plus ¾ teaspoon loose gelatin powder. Keep them refined-sugar-free by rolling them in potato starch or other starch, toasted coconut, or finely chopped nuts, and use starch to dust the pan. The honey version keeps the same water, vanilla, and salt amounts as the main recipe.
