# Homemade Altoids

🔗 **Source:** [Instructables](http://www.instructables.com/id/Homemade-Altoids-Recipe/)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** not stated in the source
- 🕑 **Drying time:** several days in the air

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧁 100 g gum paste
- 🌿 1 dram flavored oil (the author used LorAnn brand; see Notes)
- 🍬 Powdered confectioners' sugar (a sprinkling, to keep the dough from sticking)
- 🍋 Citric acid (optional, to make flavors sour; the author recommends the powdered form)

### You will also need

- 🧰 Bubble tea straw (or small round cutter)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧰 Gather the gum paste, powdered confectioners' sugar, flavored oils, citric acid (to make the flavors sour), and a bubble tea straw (or small round cutter).
2. ⚖️ Measure the ingredients using the author's ratio: 100 g gum paste to 1 dram flavoring. The powdered sugar is used to keep the dough from getting sticky, so you really only need a sprinkling.
3. 💧 Add the oil to the paste gradually, using an eyedropper. If you add too much oil at once, you'll have a sloppy mess on your hands. Keep tasting the dough so you can adjust it. Do the same if you are using citric acid to adjust how sour the candies are.
4. 👐 Knead the oil into the paste with your hands. You can add some powdered sugar if it gets too sticky, but the more powdered sugar you add, the more diluted your flavor will become. Use the powdered sugar mainly to keep the paste from sticking to the counter and the rolling pin. Roll the dough out to about 1/8 inch thick. If you don't have a rolling pin, a round bottle will do.
5. 🔪 Cut out shapes with the bubble tea straw. You can re-roll the leftover dough and cut more.
6. 🌬️ Toss the cut candies with a bit of powdered sugar to keep them from sticking together. Let them air-dry for several days to achieve the proper texture.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Instructables, "Homemade Altoids Recipe" by scoochmaroo (February 11, 2011). Printed 3/10/2013.
- 💡 The author started with peppermint, cinnamon, and sour apple flavors. The author suggests trying chocolate, chocolate-mint, and sour cherry.
- 🌿 In a reply in the comments, the author says "1 dram" means the full amount of oil as commonly sold.
- 🧴 The author says a liquid citric acid did not work well with the apple flavor, and recommends the powdered form.
- 🛒 The author found the gum paste at Sur La Table, and says any store with cake decorating supplies should have it.
- 🧰 The source also says the flavored oils came from LorAnn brand, bought online.
