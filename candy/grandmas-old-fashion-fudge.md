# Grandma's Old Fashion Fudge

- 🧰 **Pan:** 9 x 9-inch pan, buttered or foil-lined
- 🌡️ **Soft-ball stage:** 234°F (112°C), about 10 minutes of cooking
- 🍽️ **Yield:** not stated in the source

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 3 cups sugar
- 🍫 ⅔ cup Hershey cocoa
- 🧂 ⅛ tsp salt
- 🥛 1½ cups whole or evaporated milk
- 🧈 ¼ cup butter
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla
- 🌰 ¾ cup nuts (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 In a heavy 2¾-quart saucepan, mix the sugar, cocoa, and salt. Add the milk, and stir with a wooden spoon over medium-high heat, stirring constantly.
2. 🔥 Bring to a boil, stop stirring, and cook to the soft-ball stage (234°F / 112°C), or about 10 minutes.
3. 🧈 When it reaches the soft-ball temperature, turn off the heat and move the pan off the burner a bit. Add the butter and vanilla without stirring, and let it cool to about 110°F (43°C).
4. 🥄 Then start stirring until the fudge loses its glossiness, adding nuts if desired.
5. 🫓 When it thickens, pour or spoon it into a buttered or foil-lined 9 x 9-inch pan. Let it cool, but don't put it in the refrigerator. Cut into pieces.
