# Homemade Marshmallows

🔗 **Source:** [King Arthur Flour](https://www.kingarthurflour.com/recipes/PrintRecipe?RID=6287&radio=1)

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 25 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 3 hours 25 minutes (including setting)
- 🍽️ **Yield:** About 4 dozen 1½" squares

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🟨 3 packages (¼ ounce each) unflavored gelatin (the King Arthur page lists 3 packages, 21 g total)
- 💧 1 cup cool water, divided (the King Arthur page lists 227 g)
- 🍬 1½ cups granulated sugar (298 g)
- 🍯 1 cup light corn syrup (312 g)
- 🧂 ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 🌼 1 tablespoon vanilla extract (14 g)
- 🍬 Confectioners' sugar, to sprinkle on top

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine the gelatin and ½ cup cool water in the bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the whisk attachment. Mix briefly to combine; set aside.
2. 🥄 Combine the sugar, corn syrup, salt, and ½ cup cool water in a small, deep saucepan. Cook the mixture over medium heat, stirring, until the sugar dissolves.
3. 🔥 Raise the heat to high and cook, without stirring, until the syrup reaches 240°F (116°C) on a candy thermometer. Remove from the heat.
4. 🥄 With the mixer set on low speed, slowly pour the sugar syrup into the softened gelatin. Increase the speed to high, and whip until the mixture is very thick and fluffy and has cooled to lukewarm, 3 to 10 minutes (depending on the mixer and attachment you use; a stand mixer using the whisk attachment will work more quickly than a hand mixer equipped with beaters). The mixture should be cool enough that you can spread it into the pan without burning your fingers, about 95°F (35°C). Add the vanilla toward the end of the mixing time.
5. 🧈 Spread the marshmallow mixture into a greased 9" x 13" pan (glass or ceramic is best). Use your wet hands to smooth and flatten the marshmallows.
6. ⏲️ Sprinkle confectioners' sugar over the top, and let sit for several hours (or overnight) before cutting. Use a greased knife or cookie cutters to make squares or other shapes. Dipping the knife or cutters in cold water before cutting helps reduce any stickiness.
7. 🎁 For gift-giving, wrap marshmallows individually in twists of waxed paper, and store at cool room temperature. For storage, place in a closed container, with the layers separated by waxed paper or parchment paper.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: King Arthur Flour, "Homemade Marshmallows." The source describes the marshmallows as unbelievably rich compared to store-bought, and suggests that kids can help spread and cut them.
- 🌈 Flavor ideas from the King Arthur bakers: crushed peppermint candies (stir them into the mixture, leaving some swirls of plain white; they tint the marshmallow pink), a few drops of peppermint oil (omit the vanilla), or ½ teaspoon espresso powder.
- 🥣 For softer, "creamier" marshmallows that are still stiff enough to cut, reduce the gelatin to 2 packets.
- 🔎 The metric amounts in parentheses are from the King Arthur web page version of this recipe. The text version of the printed recipe lists only US measurements.
