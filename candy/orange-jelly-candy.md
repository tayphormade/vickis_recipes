# Orange Jelly Candy

🔗 **Source:** [Kraft Recipes](http://www.kraftrecipes.com/recipes/orange-jelly-candy-63267.aspx?pf=true)

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 30 minutes, plus 3 hours or overnight to set
- 🍽️ **Yield:** About 5 dozen candies, or 30 servings (2 candies each)
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 70 calories; 0 g total fat; 0 g saturated fat; 0 mg cholesterol; 30 mg sodium; 18 g carbohydrate; 0 g dietary fiber; 15 g sugars; 0 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧪 1 box SURE-JELL Fruit Pectin
- 💧 ¾ cup water
- 🥄 ½ teaspoon baking soda
- 🍬 1¼ cups sugar, divided
- 🍯 1 cup light corn syrup
- 🍊 2 teaspoons orange extract
- 🎨 5 drops each red and yellow food coloring

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 📦 Spray a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan with cooking spray; set aside. Mix the pectin, water, and baking soda in a medium saucepan. (The mixture will be foamy.) Combine 1 cup of the sugar and the corn syrup in a large saucepan.
2. 🔥 Cook both mixtures on high heat for 4 minutes, or until the foam has thinned from the pectin mixture and the sugar mixture comes to a full rolling boil (a boil that doesn't stop bubbling when stirred), stirring both saucepans frequently.
3. 🥄 Gradually add the pectin mixture to the boiling sugar mixture, stirring constantly. Boil 1 minute, stirring constantly. Remove from heat.
4. 🍊 Stir in the extract and food coloring. Pour immediately into the prepared pan. Let stand at room temperature for 3 hours or overnight, until the candy is cool and firm.
5. 🍬 Sprinkle the remaining sugar onto a large sheet of waxed paper. Invert the candy onto the waxed paper.
6. 🔪 Cut the candy into ¾-inch squares, stopping occasionally to dip the knife in warm water. Roll the candy in sugar until evenly coated.
7. 🌬️ Place in a single layer on wire racks. Let stand, uncovered, at room temperature overnight.
8. 🗄️ Store in an airtight container at room temperature.
