# Alton Brown's Microwave Peanut Butter Fudge

- 🧰 **Pan:** 8-inch square pan, greased, with a parchment sling
- 🔥 **Microwave:** 2 minutes on high, stir, then 2 more minutes on high

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 16 tablespoons (2 sticks) unsalted butter, plus more for greasing the pan (not Kerry Gold, wrong moisture content)
- 🥜 1 cup smooth peanut butter (Jiff, Skippy, or Peter Pan; not natural with the oil)
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- ❄️ 3¾ cups powdered sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧈 Grease an 8-inch square pan with butter. Trim a piece of parchment paper to 8 inches wide, and line the pan with the parchment sling, leaving enough overhang on two sides to cover the top of the fudge later.
2. 🥣 Combine the butter and peanut butter in a 4-quart microwave-safe bowl, and cover with plastic wrap. Poke a few holes in the plastic wrap to vent the steam, then microwave for 2 minutes on high.
3. 🔥 Stir, then microwave on high for 2 more minutes. Use caution when removing this mixture from the microwave, as it will be very hot.
4. 🥄 Add the vanilla and powdered sugar to the peanut butter mixture, and stir to combine with a wooden spoon. The mixture will become hard to stir and will lose its sheen.
5. 🧻 Spread the mixture into the prepared pan. Fold the excess parchment over the surface of the fudge, and refrigerate until cool, about 2 hours.
6. 🔪 Cut into 1-inch pieces, and store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to a week.
