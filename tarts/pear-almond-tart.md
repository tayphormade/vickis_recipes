# Pear Almond Tart

- 🔥 **Oven:** 375°F (190°C) for the crust, 350°F (175°C) for the tart
- ⏲️ **Bake:** about 55 minutes for the tart

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🥧 Crust

- 🍬 ½ cup powdered sugar
- 🌰 ¼ cup (55 g) blanched almonds
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 9 tablespoons (120 g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 🥚 1 large egg yolk
- 🌾 1¼ cups all-purpose flour

### 🌰 Almond filling

- 🌰 ⅔ cup (95 g) blanched almonds
- 🌾 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 🍬 7 tablespoons sugar
- 🧈 6 tablespoons (85 g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🍬 Powdered sugar, optional

### 🍐 Pears

- 💧 4 cups water
- 🍬 1¼ cups sugar
- 🍋 1½ tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 🍐 3 to 4 medium firm but ripe pears, peeled

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌰 Make the crust: process the powdered sugar, almonds, and salt until finely ground. Add the butter and blend until smooth. Mix in the egg yolk, then the flour, pulsing until the dough clumps together.
2. 🥧 Gather the dough into a ball, flatten into a disk, wrap, and chill at least 1 hour. (It can be made 2 days ahead.)
3. 🌰 Make the almond filling: finely grind the almonds and flour. Add 7 tablespoons sugar, then the butter, and blend until smooth. Mix in the egg. Chill at least 1 hour.
4. 🍐 Make the pears: bring the water, 1¼ cups sugar, and lemon juice to a boil in a large saucepan, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Add the pears, reduce to medium, and simmer about 20 minutes, turning occasionally, until very tender. Cool the pears in the syrup. (Canned pears can be used instead.)
5. 🔥 Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
6. 🥧 Roll the chilled dough on floured parchment to a 12-inch round. Transfer it to a 9-inch tart pan with a removable bottom, then trim the overhang to ½ inch. Fold the overhang in to make a double-thick edge. Prick the crust all over with a fork and freeze for 10 minutes.
7. ⚪ Line the crust with parchment and fill with dried beans or pie weights. Bake for about 20 minutes, until the sides are set. Remove the paper and weights. Bake about 10 minutes more, until the sides are golden and the bottom is set. Cool in the pan.
8. 🔥 Lower the oven to 350°F (175°C).
9. 🌰 Spread the almond filling evenly in the crust.
10. 🍐 Halve each pear lengthwise and remove the core. Slice each half crosswise, then fan the slices slightly. Arrange the pear halves over the filling, like spokes of a wheel, with the narrow ends in the center.
11. 🔥 Bake for about 55 minutes, until golden and a tester in the center comes out clean.
12. 🧊 Cool in the pan, then push the bottom up to release. Cut into wedges and dust with powdered sugar.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Ingredient weights: 1 cup whole almonds is 143 g (5 oz). 1 cup ground almonds is 95 g. 1 cup slivered almonds is 115 g (4 oz). 1 cup sliced almonds is 86 g (3 oz).
- 🌟 The tart can be made 8 hours ahead and left at room temperature.
