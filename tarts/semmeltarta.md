# Semmeltårta

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 8-12 pieces
- ⏳ **Rise:** dough 50-60 minutes; then ferment in the bakduk about 30 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** 20-25 minutes at 230°C (446°F)
- ❄️ **Cool:** completely in the pan

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Dough

- 🍞 25 g yeast
- 🥛 1 1/4 cup milk, finger hot
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🍬 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 🧂 1 pinch of salt
- 🌿 1/2 tsp kardemummakärnor, mortlade (ground cardamom(?))
- 🧈 50 g butter, room warm
- 🌾 4-5 dl wheat flour

### Brushing

- 🥚 1 egg

### Filling

- 🌰 about 150 g almond paste
- 🥛 2-3 tbsp milk
- 🍦 4-5 dl whipped cream

### Garnish

- ✨ icing sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍞 Crumble the yeast in a bowl. Add the milk and stir until the yeast is dissolved. Add the egg, granulated sugar, salt, cardamom, butter, and flour, a little at a time. Mix it all together into a smooth dough and knead vigorously for a few minutes. Let the dough rise in the bakduk for 50-60 minutes.
2. 🧻 Grease the edge of a springform pan, approx. 24 cm in diameter. Attach parchment paper to the bottom and cut off the edges that stick out. Snap the form together again.
3. 🥖 Knead the dough and shape it into a ball. Press out the ball at the bottom, on the parchment paper. Cut the dough into 8 or 12 wedges, depending on the size of the rolls you want. Let the dough ferment in the bakduk for about 30 minutes. Preheat the oven to 230°C (446°F).
4. 🥚 Brush the dough with beaten egg. Bake in the middle of the oven for 20-25 minutes. Let it cool completely in the pan.
5. 🔪 Cut the cake apart at its joints and cut off a lock on each piece. Scoop out a hole in the lower part of each piece.
6. 🥄 Mix the almond paste and milk to a sticky pulp and add a dab to each hole.
7. 🍦 Whisk the cream and spread it over the bottom. Put on the lid. Dust with the icing sugar before serving.
