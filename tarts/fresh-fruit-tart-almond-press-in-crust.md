# Fresh Fruit Tart With Almond Press-In Crust

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 8 servings
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- 🧰 **Pan:** 9-inch springform pan or tart pan with removable bottom

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🥚 2 large egg yolks
- 🌾 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 🌰 ½ cup almond flour (such as Bob's Red Mill Super-Fine Natural Almond Flour), or ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 🍬 3 Tbsp. sugar
- 🧂 1 tsp. kosher salt
- 🧈 ½ cup (1 stick) chilled unsalted butter, cut into pieces

### Custard and assembly

- 🥛 2 cups whole milk
- 🌼 1 tsp. vanilla paste or 1½ tsp. vanilla extract
- 🥚 4 large egg yolks
- 🍬 ⅓ cup sugar
- 🌽 ¼ cup cornstarch
- 🧂 ¼ tsp. kosher salt
- 🧈 3 Tbsp. unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 🫐 12 oz. fresh blueberries
- 🍇 6 oz. fresh blackberries, halved if very large

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Place a rack in the middle of the oven and preheat to 350°F (175°C). Whisk the egg yolks and 1 Tbsp. cold water in a small bowl to combine.
2. 🥣 Whisk the all-purpose flour, almond flour, sugar, and salt in a medium bowl. Add the butter and rub and smash it into the dry ingredients with your fingers to create large, shaggy pieces (you want some pieces that are flat and thin and some that are large and irregular).
3. 🥣 Drizzle the egg yolk mixture over the flour and, using a fork or your fingers, toss it into the dough to incorporate. Knead the dough in the bowl until it starts to come together. It will still look a little dry; if it looks too dry and doesn't come together, add 1–2 Tbsp. more water, but only if needed.
4. 🥧 Turn the dough out onto a clean work surface and knead 1 or 2 more times. Divide the dough in half, stack the pieces, and press down firmly to smash them together. Repeat until the dough is well combined and becomes slightly sticky, 2–3 more times. Press the dough into the bottom and 1 inch up the sides of the pan, and prick all over with a fork. Freeze until very firm, 20–30 minutes.
5. 🔥 Bake the crust until deep golden brown all over, 35–40 minutes. Let cool.
6. 📦 The crust can be baked 2 days ahead. Store tightly wrapped at room temperature.
7. 🥛 Bring the milk and vanilla paste to a simmer in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Remove from heat.
8. 🥣 Meanwhile, whisk the egg yolks, sugar, cornstarch, and salt in a medium bowl, starting slowly (it won't want to combine easily at first), then vigorously whisk until the mixture is very pale yellow and very thick, about 1 minute. The sugar should be nearly dissolved at this point. Whisking constantly, gradually add the hot milk mixture. Return the custard base to the saucepan and bring it to a simmer over medium heat, whisking constantly. Simmer, still whisking constantly, until the custard is thickened and has no cornstarch taste, about 3 minutes.
9. 🧈 Remove the custard from heat and add the butter a couple of pieces at a time, whisking until fully melted before adding more, until all of the butter is incorporated. Transfer to a medium bowl, cover with plastic wrap pressed directly against the surface, and let cool.
10. 🥣 Vigorously whisk the custard until smooth and slightly loosened. Scrape it into the crust and smooth the surface. Top with the berries.
11. 🧊 The custard can be made 4 days ahead. Keep it chilled.
