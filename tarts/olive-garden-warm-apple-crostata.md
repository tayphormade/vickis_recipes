# Olive Garden Warm Apple Crostata

- 🍽️ **Serves:** 4 to 8 (makes 4 crostatas)
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 30 to 35 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Apple filling

- 🍎 2 cups Northern Spy (or Granny Smith) apples, sliced and roughly chopped (2 small apples)
- 🍋 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons lemon juice (divided)
- 🧈 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 💧 ¾ cup water
- 🍬 ⅓ cup granulated sugar
- 🍬 2 tablespoons dark brown sugar
- 🌽 1½ tablespoons cornstarch
- 🌿 ¼ teaspoon plus ⅛ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt

### Pastry crust

- 🌾 10 ounces (2 cups) all-purpose flour
- 🍬 3 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon granulated sugar, divided
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 ⅔ cup (1½ sticks) unsalted butter
- 💧 ¼ cup ice water
- 🥚 1 large egg, beaten
- 💧 1 teaspoon room-temperature water
- ✨ 2 tablespoons coarse sugar (white sparkling sugar)

### On top

- 🍨 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 🍯 Caramel sauce (in a squirt bottle)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍋 To make the apple filling, toss the apples with 1 tablespoon of lemon juice in a medium bowl and set aside.
2. 🔥 Melt the butter in a sauté pan over medium heat. Add the apples and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until they begin to soften and a little browning is visible. Turn off the heat.
3. 🥣 In a medium saucepan, combine the water, sugars, cornstarch, cinnamon, and salt. Whisk over medium heat until thickened, 2 to 3 minutes. Add the sautéed apples and stir to coat evenly. Turn off the heat, cover, and let the filling cool while you make the dough.
4. 🥣 To make the pastry crust, mix together the flour, 3 tablespoons of the sugar, and the salt in a large bowl.
5. 🧈 Cut the butter into cubes and mix it into the dry ingredients with a pastry blender. Continue mixing until the chunks of butter are no bigger than peas. Mix in the water using your hands and form the dough into a ball. You can also use a food processor by combining the dry ingredients with the butter and pulsing until the butter becomes pea-size chunks. Slowly add the water while mixing until it comes together, then form it into a ball with your hands. Divide the dough into two portions. Shape each portion into a thick disk, like a thick hamburger patty, cover with plastic wrap, and chill for 30 minutes.
6. 🌡️ Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
7. 🥐 Working with one portion of dough at a time, roll it out on a floured surface until it is between ⅛ inch and ¼ inch thick. Using an inverted bowl that is 7 inches across, cut out a circle and place it on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Repeat with the rest of the dough, re-rolling as necessary, to get 4 circles.
8. 🥚 Combine the beaten egg with the remaining 1 teaspoon granulated sugar and 1 teaspoon of water.
9. 🥟 Spoon a heaping ⅓ cup of the filling into the center of one dough circle and smooth it out, leaving a margin for folding. Fold an edge of the dough circle down about 1 inch over the filling and brush it with the egg mixture. Repeat the folding all the way around in six folds, coating the top of each fold with egg and folding a bit of each fold over the previous one so the dough is pleated.
10. ✨ Sprinkle the pastry lightly with coarse sugar while the egg is still wet so the sugar sticks. Repeat for the remaining 3 crostatas.
11. ⏲️ Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, or until nicely browned. Serve warm with a scoop of ice cream on top, drizzled with caramel sauce.

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Top Secret Recipes, "Olive Garden Warm Apple Crostata" by Todd Wilbur. The source's menu description: "Northern Spy apples baked in a pastry crust topped with vanilla ice cream and a caramel drizzle."
- 🍎 The source says Northern Spy apples are hard to find; Granny Smith apples work fine.
- 🍎 The source says the apple pieces should be sliced, then coarsely chopped, so they come out in a mix of small and large pieces.
- 🧊 Leftovers can be stored in a covered container for a couple of days, then reheated under a broiler until hot just before serving.
- ❄️ Per the source's tidbits, leftover crostatas can be frozen and later reheated under a preheated broiler for 1 to 2 minutes, or until warm.
- ⚠️ The ingredient list includes 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons of lemon juice, but the steps use only 1 tablespoon. The remaining 2 teaspoons are not mentioned in the steps.
- 🧰 Equipment: a 7-inch inverted bowl (for cutting circles) and a parchment-lined baking sheet.
