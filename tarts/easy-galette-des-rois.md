# Easy Galette des Rois (French King Cake)

- 🔥 **Oven:** 400°F (200°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** about 30 minutes, until golden

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌰 1 recipe almond paste (about 1¼ cups; see below)
- 🥐 2 sheets puff pastry dough
- 🥚 Egg wash: 1 egg mixed with 1 tablespoon water
- 🎉 1 plastic baby, almond, or raisin

### 🌰 Almond paste

- 🌰 1 cup almond flour
- 🍬 ⅓ cup sugar
- 🧈 ⅓ cup butter, softened
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🧂 1 pinch salt
- 🥄 1 pinch baking powder
- 🌼 A few drops almond extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌰 Make the almond paste: mix all of its ingredients together until well combined.
2. 🥐 Cut one sheet of puff pastry into a 9 to 10-inch circle and place it on parchment paper.
3. 🌰 Spread the almond paste in the center, leaving a 1 to 2-inch edge.
4. 🎉 Hide the baby, almond, or raisin somewhere in the paste.
5. 🥚 Brush the edge with egg wash.
6. 🥐 Place the second sheet of puff pastry over the top.
7. ✂️ Press the edges to seal, then trim. For a pretty edge, cut diagonally along it.
8. 🥚 Brush the top with egg wash.
9. 🔪 Score lines and diagonals across the top, and poke a few holes where the lines cross.
10. 🔥 Bake at 400°F (200°C) for about 30 minutes, until golden.
11. 🧊 Cool, then slice.
