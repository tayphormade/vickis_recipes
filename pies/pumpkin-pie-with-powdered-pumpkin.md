# Pumpkin Pie with Powdered Pumpkin

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 45 minutes (plus 2 hours dehydrating the pumpkin, if making your own powder)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 425°F (220°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 15 minutes at 425°F, then lower the temperature to finish (the source does not give the second temperature)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🎃 ½ cup dehydrated pumpkin powder
- 🥚 3 large eggs
- 🥄 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🌰 ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
- 💧 2 cups boiling water
- 🥛 1⅔ cups unsweetened vanilla almond milk
- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ginger
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon cloves
- 🍂 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 🥥 1 container whipped cream, for topping
- 🥧 1 unbaked pie shell

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🎃 Place the pumpkin powder in a large bowl and pour the 2 cups boiling water over it.
2. 🥄 Stir well and let it stand for 20 minutes, until cooled and rehydrated.
3. 🥛 Add the almond milk, eggs, sugar, and spices to a blender, then add the rehydrated pumpkin.
4. 🌀 Blend for 1 to 2 minutes.
5. 🥧 Pour the mixture into the pie shell.
6. 🔥 Bake at 425°F (220°C) for 15 minutes, then reduce the heat and bake until set. (The source does not give the lower temperature or the full bake time.)
7. 🥥 Top with whipped cream before serving.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Excalibur Dehydrator recipes.
