# Pumpkin Pie Crumble

🔗 **Source:** [The Salted Pepper](https://thesaltedpepper.com/pumpkin-pie-crumble/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 48 (12 × 2 inch squares)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 450°F (220°C) for the crust, 325°F (165°C) for the filling
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 2 hours 20 minutes
- ❄️ **Cooling:** 4 hours
- 📊 **Per serving:** 149 calories

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🧈 Shortbread crust

- 🧈 1 cup salted butter, room temperature
- 🍬 ⅔ cup white sugar
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract or vanilla paste
- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour

### 🎃 Pumpkin pie filling

- 🥚 6 large eggs
- 🎃 30 ounces pure pumpkin puree
- 🍬 1 cup brown sugar
- 🍬 ¼ cup white sugar
- 🥛 12 ounces evaporated milk
- 🌼 1 tablespoon vanilla extract or vanilla paste
- 🍂 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 🍂 1½ teaspoons ground nutmeg
- 🍂 1 teaspoon ground allspice
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground ginger
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground cloves
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon fine sea salt or kosher salt

### 🍂 Crumble topping

- 🌾 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 🍂 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🍬 ½ cup brown sugar
- 🍬 ¼ cup white sugar
- 🧈 ½ cup salted butter, melted and slightly cooled

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Line a 9x13-inch pan with parchment. Preheat the oven to 450°F (220°C). Melt the butter for the topping.
2. 🧈 For the crust, cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add the vanilla.
3. 🌾 Mix in the flour one-third at a time, scraping the bowl as needed. The mixture will be crumbly.
4. ✋ Press the crumbly dough together into a ball.
5. 🥧 Press the dough into the bottom of the pan, about ¼ to ½ inch thick. Don't go up the sides.
6. 🔥 Bake the crust at 450°F (220°C) for 10 minutes. Cool on a rack while you make the filling.
7. 🔥 Lower the oven to 325°F (165°C).
8. 🥚 Whisk the eggs until the yolks and whites are combined.
9. 🎃 Add the remaining filling ingredients and mix until well combined.
10. 🥧 Pour the filling over the crust.
11. 🍂 For the topping, mix the flour, cinnamon, and brown sugar in a shallow container. Pour the melted butter over the dry mixture and stir with a fork into a thick paste.
12. ❄️ Spread the paste over the bottom of the container and chill for about 30 minutes, until firm.
13. 🥄 Sprinkle the chilled crumble topping over the pumpkin filling.
14. 🔥 Bake according to the source page (the final bake time was not captured here). Cool completely and chill for 4 hours before cutting.
