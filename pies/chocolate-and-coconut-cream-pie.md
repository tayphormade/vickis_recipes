# Chocolate and Coconut Cream Pie

🔗 **Source:** [Bon Appétit](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/chocolate-and-coconut-cream-pie)

- 🍽️ **Serves:** 6 to 8
- 🕑 **Total time:** 1 hour, plus 7½ hours or more of chill time
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🍪 9 graham cracker sheets (140 g)
- 🍬 1 Tbsp. granulated sugar
- 🧂 1 tsp. Diamond Crystal or ½ tsp. Morton kosher salt
- 🧈 5 Tbsp. unsalted butter, melted, slightly cooled
- 🌼 1 tsp. vanilla bean paste or vanilla extract

### Filling and assembly

- 🍦 ½ cup heavy cream
- 🍮 1 Tbsp. vanilla instant pudding mix
- 🍬 ¼ cup plus ⅓ cup (117 g) granulated sugar
- 🧂 2 tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1¼ tsp. Morton kosher salt, divided
- 🥚 3 large eggs
- 🌾 ⅓ cup plus 1 Tbsp. (50 g) all-purpose flour
- 🥥 2 13.5-oz. cans unsweetened coconut milk (preferably Whole Foods 365 or Thai Kitchen)
- 🧈 6 Tbsp. unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 🌼 2 tsp. vanilla bean paste or vanilla extract
- 🥥 1 cup (loosely packed; 75 g) sweetened shredded coconut
- 🥥 2 tsp. coconut extract
- 🍫 ¾ cup bittersweet chocolate chips (preferably Guittard 63%), or bittersweet or semisweet coarsely chopped chocolate (preferably Guittard 64%)
- 🥥 Toasted unsweetened coconut flakes (for serving)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Place a rack in the lower third of the oven and preheat to 350°F (175°C). Crumble 9 graham cracker sheets (140 g) into a food processor. Add 1 Tbsp. granulated sugar and 1 tsp. Diamond Crystal or ½ tsp. Morton kosher salt, and process until the crackers are very finely ground. Add 5 Tbsp. unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled, and 1 tsp. vanilla bean paste or vanilla extract, and pulse until the texture of wet sand.
2. 🥣 Remove the lid and blade from the processor and mix the crumbs with your hand or a rubber spatula to make sure there are no unevenly mixed crumbs.
3. 🥧 Transfer the crumb mixture to a standard 9-inch-diameter metal or glass pie dish. Press firmly and evenly up the sides and across the bottom with a flat-bottomed measuring cup. Build the sides first, then smooth the bottom, for a more even crust. Chill the crust in the refrigerator 20 minutes (or, if you are short on time, in the freezer 10 minutes).
4. 🔥 Place the pie pan on a rimmed baking sheet if desired. Bake the crust until a deeper golden brown, 15–18 minutes. Let cool. Do ahead: the crust can be baked 2 days ahead. Store tightly wrapped at room temperature.
5. 🍦 Whisk ½ cup heavy cream, 1 Tbsp. vanilla instant pudding mix, ¼ cup (50 g) granulated sugar, and ½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¼ tsp. Morton kosher salt in a medium bowl until thick and mousse-like in texture (some of the sugar may remain undissolved). Cover with a lid or plate and chill until ready to use.
6. 🥣 Vigorously whisk 3 large eggs, ⅓ cup plus 1 Tbsp. (50 g) all-purpose flour, the remaining ⅓ cup (67 g) granulated sugar, and the remaining 1½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1 tsp. Morton salt in a medium saucepan until smooth (no lumps of flour should remain). Add two 13.5-oz. cans unsweetened coconut milk and whisk to combine. Place over medium heat and bring to a simmer, whisking often and making sure to get into the corners of the pan, until bubbling and thickened, 8–10 minutes. Remove the pudding from heat; add 6 Tbsp. unsalted butter, cut into pieces, and 2 tsp. vanilla bean paste or vanilla extract, and whisk until combined.
7. 🥥 Measure out 3 cups (570 g) of the pudding into a large bowl. Stir in 1 cup (loosely packed; 75 g) sweetened shredded coconut and 2 tsp. coconut extract. Cover with plastic wrap, pressing it directly onto the surface, and chill until cool, 30–45 minutes.
8. 🍫 Add ¾ cup bittersweet chocolate chips, or bittersweet or semisweet coarsely chopped chocolate, to the remaining pudding in the pan, and stir until the chocolate is melted and the pudding is smooth. Scrape into the crust and smooth the surface with a small offset spatula. Chill, uncovered, until the pudding is cool and set, 30–45 minutes.
9. 🥣 Using a whisk, fold the chilled cream mixture into the coconut pudding, then scrape it into the crust over the chocolate pudding. Smooth the surface, mounding it slightly in the center with an offset spatula. Cover the pie loosely with plastic wrap and chill until the filling is cold and set, at least 6 hours (a full day is ideal).
10. 🥥 Scatter toasted unsweetened coconut flakes over the coconut filling just before serving. Do ahead: the pie (without coconut flakes) can be assembled 2 days ahead. Keep chilled.
