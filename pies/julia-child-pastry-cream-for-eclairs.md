# Julia Child Pastry Cream for Eclairs

- ❄️ **Chill:** 3 hours before filling
- 🧊 **Keeps:** 1 to 2 days refrigerated, or frozen until ready to use

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥛 1 cup milk
- 🥚 3 egg yolks
- 🍬 ¼ cup sugar
- 🌾 ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 🧈 1½ tbs butter
- 🍦 1 tbs vanilla extract, or rum, cognac, or kirsch liquor
- 🧂 a pinch of salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Heat the milk.
2. 🥣 Meanwhile, beat the egg yolks in a heavy stainless pan. Gradually pour in the sugar, beating well until thick and pale yellow.
3. 🥣 Beat in the flour and mix thoroughly.
4. 💧 By dribbles, beat in some of the hot milk, reserving ¼ of the milk.
5. 🔥 Stir custard mixture over moderately high heat with mixer. Lumps will occur as the boil is about to come.
6. 🥣 Beat vigorously to smooth, but make sure it is very thick. Use the remaining milk to thin it if it becomes too thick.
7. 🔥 Lower heat and stir for 3 more minutes.
8. 🧈 Remove from heat and beat in the butter, flavoring, and salt.
9. 🧊 Cover with plastic wrap on top and chill for 3 hours.
10. 🥐 When ready to fill eclairs, first crisp them in a 400°F (200°C) oven for 5 minutes.
11. 🔪 Cut slits in the bottom of the eclairs after they have cooled. Reheating helps give them a bit of crunch.
12. 💉 Using a small Ateco piping tube with your pastry bag, inject the cream into the underside of the eclairs.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Rachel Ruff, Denver Cooking Examiner, "Julia Child: Part 2, Eclair pastry cream recipe" (Examiner.com, August 20, 2009). The source says this is a condensed version of Julia Child's recipe from *From Julia Child's Kitchen*.
- 💡 The author uses this cream because the flour-based sauce protects the eggs from curdling.
- 🍨 The source says that if you don't have time, the eclairs can be filled with whipped cream or crème fraîche instead.
- 🔗 The source says a chocolate sauce recipe for the eclairs would come in a later post. It is not included here.
- ⚠️ The source does not give a yield; it says the recipe was condensed to make enough to fill the eclairs from Part 1.
