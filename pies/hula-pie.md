# Hula Pie

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 1 pie
- ❄️ **Freeze:** until ready to serve

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍪 1 9-inch chocolate cookie piecrust
- 🍨 1 half gallon macadamia nut ice cream (or vanilla ice cream with chopped macadamia nuts mixed in)
- 🍫 4 ounces chocolate fudge topping
- ☕ 1 shot espresso or strong coffee
- 🌰 6 ounces macadamia nuts
- 🥛 Whipped cream

### 🍪 Homemade crust (optional)

- 🍪 About 1½ cups chocolate wafer crumbs
- 🍬 A little sugar
- 🧈 ¼ cup melted butter

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍫 Bring the fudge topping to room temperature.
2. 🍨 Scoop the ice cream into the prepared pie shell, smoothing it into a rough dome, as high as you like.
3. ☕ Warm the espresso or coffee and stir it into the fudge topping. Spread the topping evenly over the ice cream dome with a warmed knife.
4. ❄️ Freeze until ready to serve.
5. 🥛 Before serving, top with whipped cream and chopped macadamia nuts.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The author prefers softened vanilla ice cream with chopped macadamia nuts mixed in, because the nuts stay firm.
- 🌟 For a homemade crust, mix the crumbs, a little sugar, and the melted butter, then press into a pie pan.
- 🌟 Source: Hawaii Magazine, by Chris Bailey, about Duke's Hula Pie.
