# Easy Julia Child Eclair Icing

- 🧊 **Keeps:** 1 day at room temperature, or 3 days refrigerated (warm before using)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍫 2 cups chocolate pistoles (the author used Ghirardelli semi-sweet morsels)
- 🥛 1 cup whole milk or cream
- 🌿 1 tbs Madagascar vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥛 Heat milk in a saucepan until hot to the touch and steaming.
2. 🍫 In a large stainless steel bowl, pour in 2 cups of chocolate chips.
3. 🥛 Pour hot milk over the chocolate. Let steep for 2 minutes to melt the chocolate.
4. 🥣 With a wire whisk, stir the milk and chocolate until you see a shiny, smooth consistency.
5. 🌿 Add in the vanilla.
6. 🖌️ Using a frosting palette or knife, spread the ganache across the tops of the éclairs in a vertical direction, from the top to the bottom of the éclair.
7. 🍴 Serve immediately.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Rachel Ruff, Denver Cooking Examiner, "Julia Child: Part 3, Eclair icing recipe" (Examiner.com, August 24, 2009).
- 🍫 The source calls this ganache a classic 2:1 ratio of chocolate to milk. Add more milk for a runnier icing for a cake.
- 🎂 The source says the chocolate and milk combination can also be used for frostings, glazes, cake fillings, and donut icings.
- 🔥 To use leftover frosting, the source says to warm it on low power in the microwave for 30 seconds.
- 🔗 The source is part of a series that begins with the eclair pastry and pastry cream recipes.
