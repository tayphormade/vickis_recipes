# Lemon Icebox Pie

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- 🔥 **Oven:** none (no bake)
- ❄️ **Chill:** until set

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🥧 Crust

- 🍪 2 cups cookie crumbs, such as graham cracker crumbs
- 🧈 2 tablespoons butter, melted
- 🥛 3 to 6 tablespoons milk

### 🍋 Filling

- 🍮 3 teaspoons powdered gelatin
- 🍮 2 cups prepared vanilla pudding
- 🥛 2 cups low-fat firm yogurt, such as Greek yogurt
- 🍬 10 tablespoons powdered sugar
- 🍋 ¾ cup lemon juice
- 🍋 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 🍦 1 cup low-fat whipped topping

### 🍦 Topping

- 🍦 1 cup low-fat whipped topping
- 🍬 1 tablespoon powdered sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍪 For the crust, process the crumbs, melted butter, and 3 tablespoons milk in a food processor until the crumbs hold together. Add more milk only if needed; the mixture should not be soggy.
2. 🥧 Press the crumbs into the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie dish.
3. 🍮 For the filling, mix the gelatin with ¼ cup cold water. Let stand 5 to 10 minutes to bloom.
4. 🍋 Mix the pudding, yogurt, powdered sugar, lemon juice, lemon zest, and 1 cup whipped topping.
5. 🔥 Gently heat the gelatin mixture, stirring until dissolved. Stir it into the lemon filling.
6. 🥧 Pour the filling into the crust and refrigerate until set.
7. 🍦 Before serving, top with the 1 cup whipped topping mixed with 1 tablespoon powdered sugar.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 A lower-fat, eggless pie. Source: Kitchen Nostalgia.
