# Apple Crumble Pie

- 🌡️ **Oven:** 375°F (190°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 30 minutes with the edges covered with foil, then 25 to 30 minutes uncovered
- 🥧 **Pan:** 9-inch pie plate

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Filling

- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🌾 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 🍋 1 tsp finely shredded lemon peel
- 🍎 2 pounds apples, peeled, cored, and thinly sliced (5½ cups)
- 🍋 3 tbsp lemon juice

### Pastry

- 🥧 Pastry for a single-crust pie (unbaked); the source gives no amounts

### Crumb topping

- 🌾 ½ cup all-purpose flour
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🫚 ½ tsp ground ginger
- 🍂 ½ tsp cinnamon
- 🌰 ¼ tsp ground mace
- 🧈 ¼ cup butter or margarine

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Stir together the ½ cup sugar, the 2 tablespoons flour, and the lemon peel. Set aside.
2. 🍋 Sprinkle apple slices with lemon juice.
3. 🥣 Toss apples with the sugar mixture.
4. 🥧 Prepare pastry for a single-crust pie. Fit it into a 9-inch pie plate; do not prick.
5. 🍎 Fill with apples.
6. 🥣 Mix the ½ cup flour, ½ cup sugar, ginger, cinnamon, and mace.
7. 🧈 Cut in the butter or margarine until crumbly, then sprinkle atop the apples.
8. 🛡️ Cover the edges of the pie with foil.
9. 🔥 Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 minutes.
10. 🔥 Remove foil and bake 25 to 30 minutes longer.
11. 🧀 Serve warm with cheese slices, if desired.
