# Cheaters Lemonade Pie

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 2 pies
- ❄️ **Chill:** until firm

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥧 2 premade graham cracker crusts
- 🥛 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
- 🍋 1 can frozen lemonade concentrate, thawed
- 🍦 1 tub Cool Whip

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥛 Combine the condensed milk and thawed lemonade until smooth.
2. 🍦 Fold in the Cool Whip.
3. 🥧 Pour into the prepared crusts.
4. ❄️ Refrigerate until firm.
