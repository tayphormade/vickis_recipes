# Marie Callender's Double Cream Blueberry Pie Copycat

- 🍽️ **Serves:** 6 to 8
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 400°F (200°C) for the crust

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🥧 1 baked 9-inch pie shell (Marie Callender's brand is best, of course), or crust from scratch:
- 🌾 6¼ ounces (1¼ cups) all-purpose flour
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 ¼ cup unsalted butter, cold
- 🥥 ¼ cup shortening, cold
- 💧 2 tablespoons ice water

### Blueberry filling

- 🧈 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 🍎 3 ounces (⅔ cup) petite diced red apple
- 🍬 ½ cup plus 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 🌽 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 🧂 Pinch of salt
- 💧 ⅔ cup water
- 🍋 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 🫐 6 ounces (1⅓ cups) frozen blueberries

### Custard filling

- 🍬 ½ cup plus 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 🌽 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 🧪 1 teaspoon gelatin powder
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🥛 1¾ cups whole milk
- 🥚 2 large egg yolks
- 🌼 1½ teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🍦 ¾ cup sour cream

### Whipped cream

- ☁️ 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🍬 ¼ cup powdered sugar
- 🧪 ¾ teaspoon gelatin powder
- 💧 1 tablespoon water

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 If making the crust from scratch, whisk together the flour and salt in a medium bowl.
2. 🥧 Using a pastry knife or large fork, cut the chilled shortening and butter into the flour until it has a crumbly, sand-like texture. Mix the ice water into the dough with a mixing spoon, then form the dough into a disk with your hands. Be careful not to work the dough too much or it could lose its flakiness. Seal the dough disk with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 30 minutes to rest.
3. 🔥 When the dough has chilled, preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
4. 🥧 Roll the dough out on a generously floured surface until it is a large circle. When you invert a 9-inch pie dish or pan onto the dough, it should have about a 1-inch margin all the way around. Fold the dough in half and lift it into the pie pan, then unfold it. Mold the crust so that it has a lip at the top, and slice off any excess. Use a skewer or toothpick to make several holes in the crust about ½ inch apart. These holes prevent the crust from bubbling without pie weights. Bake for 18 to 22 minutes, or until the crust has puffed up and is just starting to brown around the top edge. Make sure there are no spots that look uncooked. Cool.
5. 🧈 Make the blueberry filling by melting the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat, then adding the diced apple. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring often, until the apple is soft but not browned. Cool.
6. 🥣 Whisk together the sugar, cornstarch, and salt in a medium saucepan, then whisk in the water and lemon juice. Add the blueberries and cooked apple, and place the pan over medium heat. When the mixture begins to bubble, cook for 5 to 7 minutes, or until thicker, stirring often. Then cover and cool. When the filling is just warm but not hot, reserve 5 blueberries (to use later as a garnish), then pour the rest into the baked pie shell and chill it in the refrigerator.
7. 🍮 While the blueberry filling sets, make the custard: whisk together the sugar, cornstarch, gelatin, and salt in a medium saucepan, then whisk in the milk. Set the pan over medium heat, and when it begins to bubble, reduce the heat to a simmer and cook the custard for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring often, or until it is as thick as cream. Then turn off the heat.
8. 🥚 Beat the egg yolks in a small bowl or spouted measuring cup, then add several tablespoons of the hot milk mixture, one tablespoon at a time, stirring after each addition. This slowly heats the egg yolks without cooking them. When you've added around ½ cup of the milk mixture to the yolks, stir the hot yolk mixture into the saucepan. Bring the heat back up and simmer for 2 minutes, stirring often, until the mixture is much thicker. Turn off the heat, stir in the vanilla, then gently whisk in the sour cream and cool the custard, uncovered. When the custard has cooled for about 20 minutes, so that it is warm and no longer hot, pour it over the blueberry filling, then cool for at least 3 hours, or until firm.
9. ☁️ Make the whipped cream by combining the cream and vanilla in a large bowl. Add the powdered sugar and mix on high speed until just before the cream makes soft peaks. Stir the gelatin into the water in a small bowl, then heat the gelatin on high for 10 seconds in the microwave, or until the liquid gets hot and the gelatin dissolves. Drizzle the gelatin liquid into the whipped cream while mixing, then continue mixing on high speed until the whipped cream is smooth and forms stiff peaks. Don't overmix, or the cream will begin to become grainy and break.
10. 🥧 Spoon the whipped cream into a piping bag with a fancy tip. Apply whipped cream around the inside edge of the pie where the filling meets the crust, then add 5 dollops of whipped cream to the middle. Use tweezer tongs or steady fingers to place a reserved blueberry on each dollop. Cut into 6 or 8 slices to serve.

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Top Secret Recipes, "Marie Callender's Double Cream Blueberry Pie Copycat Recipe" by Todd Wilbur.
- 💡 The source says to dice the apple very small so the pieces soften and work well with the frozen blueberries.
- ⚠️ The source's introduction says an unbaked 9-inch pie shell from the frozen food aisle can be used ("any brand will do"), but the ingredient list says a baked pie shell. Both are kept as printed.
- 🧪 The source says the gelatin stabilizes both the custard and the whipped cream.
