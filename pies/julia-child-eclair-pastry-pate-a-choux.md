# Julia Child Eclair Pastry (Pate a Choux)

- 🌡️ **Oven:** 400°F (200°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** about 15 minutes, until golden brown

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 💧 1 cup water
- 🧈 ½ cup butter
- 🌾 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 🥚 4 large eggs

### Egg wash

- 🥚 1 egg white
- 💧 1 tsp water

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Heat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. 🔥 In a 2½ quart saucepan, heat water and butter to a rolling boil.
3. 🌾 Stir in flour, reducing heat to low.
4. 🥣 Stir vigorously over low heat about 1 minute, or until the mixture forms a ball; remove from heat.
5. 🥚 Beat in eggs, all at once; continue beating until smooth.
6. 🖌️ Brush with egg wash.
7. 🥐 Pipe as shown in the source's slideshow.
8. ⏲️ Cook for about 15 minutes or until golden brown. Do NOT open oven door during puff process!
