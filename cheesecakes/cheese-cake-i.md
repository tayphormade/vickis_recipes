# Cheese Cake I

- 🍽️ **Size:** very large cake
- 🧰 **Pan:** 10-inch springform pan
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 325°F (163°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 50 minutes, then 5 more minutes after the topping is added

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🍪 1 graham cracker pie crust (see the crust recipe below)

### Cake

- 🥚 5 eggs
- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🧀 3 8-oz cream cheese, softened
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla

### Topping

- 🍦 1½ pints sour cream
- 🍬 6 tsp sugar
- 🌼 1½ tsp vanilla

### Graham cracker or chocolate wafer crust

- 🍬 ⅓ cup sugar
- 🌾 1 tsp flour
- 🧈 ⅓ cup margarine or butter, softened
- 🍪 12–14 (about 1½ cups) graham crackers, crumbled, or 1½ cups chocolate wafers, crushed

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 To make the crust, cream together the sugar and butter. Add the flour and graham cracker crumbs. Press into a large pie plate.
2. 🔥 Bake the crust in a 350°F (175°C) oven for 8 to 10 minutes. Fill.
3. 🥣 For the cake, cream the cream cheese and sugar, then add the eggs and vanilla.
4. 🥧 Pour into the graham cracker crust.
5. 🔥 Bake in a 325°F (163°C) oven for 50 minutes.
6. 🍦 For the topping, mix the sour cream, sugar, and vanilla. Spread it on top of the cake, and return to the oven for 5 more minutes.
