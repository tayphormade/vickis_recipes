# Cheesecake Factory Pumpkin Pecan Cheesecake

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 1 cheesecake in a 10-inch springform pan
- 🔥 **Oven:** 400°F, then 300°F for the pecan layer, then 350°F for the cheesecake
- ❄️ **Chill:** 4 hours or overnight

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🥧 Crust

- 🌾 9 oz (1¾ cups) all-purpose flour
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 ⅓ cup cold unsalted butter
- 🧈 ⅓ cup shortening
- 💧 ¼ cup ice-cold water

### 🌰 Pecan filling

- 🧈 ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
- 🍬 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons dark brown sugar
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🥚 5 large eggs
- 🍯 1⅓ cups dark corn syrup
- 🌼 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 🌰 1¾ cups chopped pecans

### 🎃 Pumpkin cheesecake filling

- 🧀 2 (8-ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
- 🍬 ¾ cup granulated sugar
- 🎃 1 cup canned pumpkin puree
- 🥚 2 large eggs
- 🥛 ¼ cup sour cream
- 🌼 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon ground ginger
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon ground allspice
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg

### 🍯 Caramel nut topping (the best way)

- 🧈 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 🍬 ½ cup light brown sugar
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🥛 ⅓ cup half-and-half
- 🌰 ⅓ cup chopped walnuts

### 🍯 Caramel nut topping (the easy way)

- 🍯 ⅓ cup caramel topping
- 🌰 ¼ cup chopped walnuts

### 🍨 Garnish

- 🍨 Canned whipped cream

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌾 Make the crust: whisk the flour and salt. Cut in the butter and shortening with a pastry blender or fork, until no chunks are bigger than a pea.
2. 💧 Add the water and mix with your hands into a ball. Flatten into a disk, wrap, and chill for 30 minutes.
3. 🧈 Grease the bottom of a 10-inch springform pan, and line the bottom with parchment.
4. 🥖 Roll the dough into a circle about 14 inches across. Fold it in half, lift it into the pan, and press it into the bottom and up the sides, as close to the top as possible.
5. ✂️ Trim any excess dough, patch any holes, and even out the top edge so it can hold the filling. Chill for 30 minutes.
6. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
7. ⚪ Cover the crust with foil or parchment, fill it with pie weights or 1 pound of dry beans, and bake for 15 minutes. Remove the weights and foil, and bake 8 more minutes, until light brown. Don't poke holes in the crust. Cool.
8. 🥄 Make the pecan filling: beat the melted butter, sugar, and salt on medium speed. Mix in the eggs, then the corn syrup and vanilla.
9. 🍯 Cook the syrup mixture in a medium saucepan over medium-low heat for about 20 minutes, until thickened, stirring often. Stir in the pecans and cool for 20 minutes.
10. 🔥 Lower the oven to 300°F (150°C).
11. 🥧 Pour the pecan filling into the crust and bake for 30 to 40 minutes, until darker and firm around the edges. Cool for 45 minutes.
12. 🔥 Raise the oven to 350°F (175°C).
13. 🧀 Make the pumpkin filling: beat the cream cheese and sugar, then add the pumpkin, eggs, sour cream, vanilla, and spices until smooth.
14. 🎃 Pour the pumpkin filling over the pecan layer, up to the top edge of the crust.
15. 🔥 Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, until the top just begins to brown. Cool, then chill until firm, about 4 hours or overnight.
16. 🍯 Make the caramel nut topping. For the best way, melt the butter, stir in the sugar, salt, and half-and-half, and boil. Reduce to a simmer until thick and foamy, then remove from heat and stir in the walnuts. For the easy way, mix the caramel topping with the walnuts.
17. 🍯 Spread the topping in a circle in the center of the cheesecake, leaving room around the edge.
18. 🍨 Serve each slice topped with whipped cream.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Cook the pecan filling before it goes into the pan. Otherwise it soaks through the crust.
- 🌟 Recipe by Todd Wilbur, a copycat of The Cheesecake Factory dessert.
