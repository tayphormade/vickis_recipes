# Microwave Cheesecake for One

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 1
- ⏲️ **Cook:** about 1 minute in the microwave, in 15-20 second intervals
- ❄️ **Chill:** at least 1 hour in the fridge, after cooling to room temperature

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍪 4 tbsp crushed plain cookies (the source calls them "biscuits")
- 🧈 1 tbsp melted butter
- 🧀 4 tbsp cream cheese, softened
- 🥣 2 tbsp sour cream or yogurt
- 🍬 2 1/2 tbsp sugar
- 🥚 1 egg white
- 🌼 1/4 tsp vanilla
- 🌾 1 tsp all-purpose flour

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Combine the crushed biscuits and butter, and with a spoon press them onto the bottom of a microwave-safe mug or dish.
2. 🥣 Stir the cream cheese, sour cream, egg white, sugar, vanilla, and flour with a fork until fully incorporated. Pour over the crumbs.
3. ⏲️ Microwave for around 1 minute (in 15-20 second intervals). Make sure it doesn't bubble over the sides.
4. ❄️ Cool to room temperature before placing in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
5. 🍓 Top with your cheesecake topping of choice, and enjoy!

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📖 The source gives no author or URL. It describes the cheesecake as tasting the same as a traditional baked cheesecake.
