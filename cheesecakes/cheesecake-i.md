# Cheesecake I

- 🛠️ **Pan:** 10-inch spring-form pan (the source says this is a very large cake)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 325°F (163°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 50 minutes, then 5 more minutes after the topping is spread

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Cake

- 🥚 5 eggs
- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🧀 3 8-oz cream cheese, softened
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla

### Topping

- 🥣 1 1/2 pints sour cream
- 🍬 6 tsp sugar
- 🌼 1 1/2 tsp vanilla

### Crust

- 🥧 1 graham cracker pie crust (see the crust steps below)
- 🍬 1/3 cup sugar
- 🌾 1 tsp flour
- 🧈 1/3 cup margarine or butter, softened
- 🍪 12-14 (about 1 1/2 cups) graham crackers, crumbled, or 1 1/2 cups chocolate wafers, crushed

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥧 Crust: cream together the sugar and butter. Add the flour and graham cracker crumbs. Press into a large pie plate. Bake in a 350°F (177°C) oven for 8 to 10 minutes. Fill.
2. 🥣 Cream the cream cheese and sugar; add the eggs and vanilla. Pour into the graham cracker crust.
3. 🔥 Bake in the oven at 325°F (163°C) for 50 minutes.
4. 🥣 For the topping, mix the sour cream, sugar, and vanilla.
5. 🥄 Spread the topping on top of the cake and return to the oven for 5 more minutes.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🛠️ The crust steps say to press it into a large pie plate, while the cake itself is baked in a 10-inch spring-form pan.
