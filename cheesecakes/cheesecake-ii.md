# Cheesecake II

- 🍽️ **Size:** a much smaller cheesecake; the source says it is a good size for an Instant Pot
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (177°C) for the crust and the cheesecake; 450°F (232°C) for the topping
- ⏲️ **Bake:** crust 8-10 minutes; cheesecake 20-25 minutes; topping 5 minutes
- 🛠️ **Pan:** 8-inch spring-form pan for the Instant Pot method

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🍬 1/3 cup sugar
- 🌾 1 tsp flour
- 🧈 1/3 cup margarine or butter, softened
- 🍪 12-14 (about 1 1/2 cups) graham crackers, crumbled, or 1 1/2 cups chocolate wafers, crushed

### Cake

- 🧀 4 3-oz packages cream cheese (the author uses 5 packages)
- 🍬 3/4 cup sugar
- 🧂 dash of salt
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🌼 1/2 tsp vanilla

### Topping

- 🥣 1 cup sour cream
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla
- 🍬 2 Tbsp sugar

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥧 Crust: cream together the sugar and butter. Add the flour and graham cracker crumbs. Press into a large pie plate. Bake in a 350°F (177°C) oven for 8 to 10 minutes. Fill.
2. 🥣 Cake: blend the softened cream cheese and sugar; beat in the eggs one at a time, beating until fluffy. Add the vanilla; pour into the graham cracker pie crust.
3. 🔥 Bake at 350°F (177°C) for 20 to 25 minutes.
4. ❄️ Remove from the oven and let cool 15 minutes.
5. 🥣 For the topping, mix the sour cream, vanilla, and sugar together and spread on top of the cheesecake.
6. 🔥 Bake at 450°F (232°C) for 5 minutes.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍲 Instant Pot instructions (used instead of baking in the oven): pour 1 1/2 cups water into a 6- or 8-quart cooker. Set a heat- and pressure-safe trivet in the cooker. Make an aluminum foil sling, set the filled 8-inch spring-form pan on it (you can cover the pan with foil), and use the sling to lower the pan into the pot. Fold down the ends of the sling so they do not touch the batter in the pan. Lock the lid onto the pot. Press Pressure cook (Manual) on High pressure for 25 minutes with the Keep Warm setting off. When the machine has finished cooking, turn it off and let the pressure return to normal naturally, about 20 minutes. Unlatch the lid and open the cooker. Use the sling to transfer the hot spring-form pan to a wire rack. Cool for 15 minutes, then refrigerate for 1 hour. Cover and continue refrigerating for at least 1 more hour, or up to 2 days.
