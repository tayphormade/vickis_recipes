# Chocolate Peanut Butter Cheesecake

🔗 **Source:** [The Girl Who Ate Everything](http://www.the-girl-who-ate-everything.com/2013/02/chocolate-peanut-butter-cheesecake.html)

- 🛠️ **Pan:** 10-inch spring-form pan
- 🔥 **Crust:** 350°F (177°C), 5-6 minutes
- 🔥 **Cheesecake:** 300°F (149°C), 50-60 minutes
- 🧊 **Cool:** about 1 hour on a cooling rack
- ❄️ **Chill:** overnight

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🍪 1 1/2 cups crushed Oreo cookies (about 15 cookies)

### Cheesecake

- 🧀 4 (8 ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🥚 4 eggs
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 🍫 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 🥜 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter

### Chocolate ganache

- 🥛 1/2 cup whipping cream
- 🍫 1 cup milk chocolate chips

### Garnish

- 🥜 Reese Peanut Butter cups, optional

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C).
2. 🍪 Press the crushed Oreo cookies into the bottom of a 10-inch spring-form pan. Bake 5-6 minutes or until set. Do not burn! Cool on a wire rack while preparing the cake. Reduce the oven heat to 300°F (149°C).
3. 🥣 Beat the softened cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla until smooth and creamy; add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Divide the batter equally between two bowls.
4. 🍫 Melt 1 cup of semi-sweet chocolate chips and stir into one bowl of cheesecake batter; pour that batter into the crust-prepared pan. Microwave the peanut butter until soft, about 20-25 seconds; stir it into the other bowl of cheesecake batter and pour on top of the chocolate layer.
5. ⏲️ Bake the cheesecake at 300°F (149°C) for 50-60 minutes, or until the center is almost set (not jiggly). Do not overbake. Don't worry if there are a couple of cracks. They will come together when refrigerated, and you are covering it in chocolate anyway.
6. ❄️ Place the pan on a cooling rack and cool for about 1 hour. Carefully run a knife around the edge of the pan and remove the sides. Place the cream in a microwave-safe bowl and heat for one minute, or until boiling. Pour the chocolate chips into the cream and let sit until soft. Stir until the chips are melted and the mixture is smooth and creamy. Pour the chocolate over the top and sides of the cheesecake. You may want to place the cheesecake on a cookie sheet to catch any run-off. Refrigerate overnight. Cut the optional Reese Peanut Butter cups into quarters and garnish the top of the cheesecake. Serve with a dollop of whipped cream. It's great to cut the richness. Refrigerate leftovers.
