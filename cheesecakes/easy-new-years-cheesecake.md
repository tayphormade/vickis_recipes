# Easy New Year's Cheesecake

- 🛠️ **Pan:** 7-inch (18 cm) spring-form pan
- ⏲️ **Active time:** about 5 minutes (not including chill time)
- 🔥 **Oven:** none (no-bake)
- ❄️ **Chill:** until firm and cold (can be placed in the freezer)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🍪 200g biscuits (chocolate cookies)
- 🌰 100g nuts (walnuts, to taste)
- 🧈 80g butter, melted
- 🧂 a pinch of salt

### Cake

- 🍫 200g Nutella chocolate
- 🧀 400g cream cheese (2 parts, 200g each)
- 🍬 50g powdered sugar
- 🥛 200 ml cream 33%
- 🌰 Nuts and chocolate, for decorating

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Crush and grind the cookies to crumbs. Add the finely chopped nuts and blend. Add the melted butter and a pinch of salt. Mix together and press into a 7" (18 cm) spring-form pan, going up the sides. Set aside.
2. 🥄 In a bowl, mix 200g of softened cream cheese and the Nutella chocolate. Spread into the spring-form pan.
3. 🥛 In another bowl, whip the cream with the powdered sugar until soft peaks form. Separately, whip the other 200g of cream cheese, then fold the whipped cream into the cream cheese and spread over the chocolate layer in the pan.
4. 🌰 Garnish the top with finely chopped nuts, and drizzle tempered (melted) chocolate in a grid pattern over the cake.
5. ❄️ Place in the refrigerator until firm and cold (it can also be placed in the freezer).
