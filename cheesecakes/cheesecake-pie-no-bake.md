# Cheesecake Pie (No Baking)

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 1 pie
- ❄️ **Chill:** at least 3 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥧 1 (6 oz) ready-crust graham cracker pie crust
- 🧀 1 (8 oz) package cream cheese, softened
- 🥛 1 (14 oz) can Eagle Brand sweetened condensed milk (not evaporated milk)
- 🍋 ⅓ cup lemon juice from concentrate
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🍒 1 (21 oz) can cherry pie filling, chilled, or any fruit in season

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🧀 Beat the cream cheese with a mixer until fluffy.
2. 🥛 Gradually beat in the sweetened condensed milk until smooth.
3. 🍋 Stir in the lemon juice and vanilla.
4. 🥧 Pour into the crust.
5. ❄️ Chill for at least 3 hours.
6. 🍒 Top with the pie filling before serving. Refrigerate leftovers.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Add whipped cream or another garnish, if desired.
