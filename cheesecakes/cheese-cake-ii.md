# Cheese Cake II

- 🍽️ **Size:** a much smaller cheesecake
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C) for the cake, 450°F (230°C) for the topping
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 20 to 25 minutes, then 5 minutes after the topping is added

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crust

- 🍪 1 graham cracker pie crust (see the crust recipe below)

### Cake

- 🧀 4 3-oz packages cream cheese (I use 5 packages)
- 🍬 ¾ cup sugar
- 🧂 Dash of salt
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🌼 ½ tsp vanilla

### Topping

- 🍦 1 cup sour cream
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla
- 🍬 2 Tbsp sugar

### Graham cracker or chocolate wafer crust

- 🍬 ⅓ cup sugar
- 🌾 1 tsp flour
- 🧈 ⅓ cup margarine or butter, softened
- 🍪 12–14 (about 1½ cups) graham crackers, crumbled, or 1½ cups chocolate wafers, crushed

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 To make the crust, cream together the sugar and butter. Add the flour and graham cracker crumbs. Press into a large pie plate.
2. 🔥 Bake the crust in a 350°F (175°C) oven for 8 to 10 minutes. Fill.
3. 🥣 Blend the softened cream cheese and sugar. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, beating until fluffy. Add the vanilla.
4. 🥧 Pour into the graham cracker pie crust. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 20 to 25 minutes.
5. ⏳ Remove from the oven and let cool 15 minutes.
6. 🍦 For the topping, mix the sour cream, vanilla, and sugar together and spread it on top of the cheesecake.
7. 🔥 Bake at 450°F (230°C) for 5 minutes.
