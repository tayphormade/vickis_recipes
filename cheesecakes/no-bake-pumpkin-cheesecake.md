# No-Bake Pumpkin Cheesecake

🔗 **Source:** [H-E-B](https://www.heb.com/recipe/print/1392679743596)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 1 hour 15 minutes
- 🍳 **Cook:** N/A
- ⏳ **Total:** 1 hour 15 minutes
- ❄️ **Chill:** 1 hour or more, until firm
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 360 calories (serving size 139 g); fat 19g; saturated fat 10g; trans fat 0g; cholesterol 30mg; sodium 270mg; carbohydrates 44g; dietary fiber 3g; sugars 26g; protein 10g

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧀 8 oz H-E-B Cream Cheese, softened
- 🎃 15 oz Hill Country Fare Solid Pack Pumpkin
- 🍬 1/2 cup H-E-B Cane Sugar
- 🍂 1/2 tsp McCormick Pumpkin Pie Spice
- ☁️ 1 1/2 cups Hill Country Fare Whipped Topping, thawed
- 🥧 1 Keebler Graham Cracker Crust

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine the softened cream cheese, pumpkin, sugar, and pumpkin pie spice in a large mixing bowl.
2. 🥄 Mix with an electric mixer on high speed for 3 minutes, or until the mixture is smooth and creamy.
3. 🥄 Stir in the thawed whipped topping using a large spoon, until the mixture is a pumpkin gold color.
4. 🥧 Pour the pumpkin mixture into the prepared pie crust.
5. ❄️ Refrigerate for 1 hour or more, until the pie is firm.
