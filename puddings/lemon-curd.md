# Lemon Curd

- ⏱️ **Cook:** about 12 minutes, over medium heat
- 🧊 **Chill:** at least 5 hours
- 🗓️ **Storage:** can be made 1 week ahead; cover and keep refrigerated

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍬 2⅓ cups sugar
- 🌽 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 🍋 1 cup fresh lemon juice
- 🥚 4 large eggs
- 🥚 4 large egg yolks
- 🧈 ¾ cup (1½ sticks) unsalted butter, cut into ½-inch pieces

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine the 2⅓ cups sugar and 2 teaspoons cornstarch in a heavy medium saucepan. Gradually whisk in the fresh lemon juice.
2. 🥚 Whisk in the eggs and yolks, then add the butter.
3. 🔥 Whisk over medium heat until the curd thickens and boils, about 12 minutes.
4. 🥣 Pour into a medium bowl. Refrigerate until cold, at least 5 hours.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Variation: Lime Curd. Substitute fresh lime juice for the lemon juice.
