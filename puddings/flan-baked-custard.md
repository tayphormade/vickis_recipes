# Flan/Baked Custard

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 6 custard cups (6-ounce), or 1 large custard
- 🔥 **Oven:** 325°F (163°C), oven method
- ⏱️ **Bake (oven, 6 cups):** 40 to 45 minutes
- ⏱️ **Bake (oven, 1 large custard in a 1-quart pie plate):** about 60 minutes
- ⏲️ **Pressure cooker:** 6 to 8 minutes of cooking time at pressure
- 🧊 **Cool:** 1 hour, then refrigerate at least 3 hours if you want them chilled (especially for flan)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Caramel (for flan)

- 🍬 1 cup sugar, OR ½ cup sugar + 2 tablespoons water

### Custard

- 🥚 4 slightly beaten eggs
- 🍬 ¼ cup sugar
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🥛 2 cups milk, scalded
- 🌼 ½ teaspoon vanilla
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon cinnamon (for flan, optional)
- 🍂 Dash of nutmeg (for custard, optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍬 Caramel, dry method: Place 1 cup sugar in a wide sauté or fry pan over high heat and wait. DO NOT STIR. Pick up the pan and swish it back and forth. When the sugar is almost all melted, turn off the heat; the sugar will finish melting while the pan is swished.
2. 🍬 Caramel, water method (alternative): Place ½ cup sugar and 2 tablespoons water in a saucepan over medium heat, and stir until golden brown.
3. 🍯 Pour the caramel into 6 custard cups evenly, and turn the cups until the caramel coats the bottom and sides. Let cool.
4. 🥣 Custard: Combine the eggs, sugar, and salt. Slowly stir in the lightly cooled milk and vanilla (adding cinnamon for flan). Fill six 6-ounce custard cups. Sprinkle with nutmeg for custard, if desired.
5. 🔥 Oven method: Set the cups in a shallow pan on an oven rack. Pour hot water into the pan, 1 inch deep. Bake at 325°F (163°C) for 40 to 45 minutes, or until a knife inserted off-center comes out clean. Serve warm or chilled. For one large custard, bake in a 1-quart pie plate for about 60 minutes.
6. 🍲 Pressure cooker method: Cover the custard cups tightly with foil. (If you prefer a large custard, pour it into a container that will fit into the cooker.) Add 2 cups of water to the pressure cooker, with the steamer basket or rack. Place the custard cups on the rack in the cooker. Close and lock the pressure cooker top, turn the heat to high, and when it reaches pressure, turn the flame down to minimum. Count 6 to 8 minutes of cooking time. When time is up, turn off the heat and allow a natural release: do not do anything; wait for the pressure to come down naturally. If after 10 minutes all of the pressure has not released, release the rest with the pressure valve. For electric pressure cookers, disengage the Keep Warm setting when the cooking time is up, then turn off or unplug the cooker. When the 10 minutes have passed, open the top, relieving any excess pressure.
7. 🔍 Check whether the custards are done by sticking a toothpick in the middle of one of the custards. If it is not clean, lock the top back on and wait another 5 minutes; the residual heat from the pressure cooker will finish cooking them. If the custard is still liquid, cook under pressure for another 5 minutes.
8. 🧊 Remove and let the custards cool for one hour. If you wish them chilled (especially flan), refrigerate for at least 3 hours. To unmold chilled custard, first loosen the edge; then slip the point of a knife down the side to let air in. Invert onto plate(s).
