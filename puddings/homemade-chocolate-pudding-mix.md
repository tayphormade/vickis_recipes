# Homemade Chocolate Pudding Mix

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 5 batches of pudding; 2 servings per batch
- 🗄️ **Storage:** cool, dry place, airtight, up to 6 months

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Dry pudding mix

- 🍫 3 cups cocoa powder
- 🥛 3 cups dry milk powder
- 🌽 3 cups cornstarch
- 🍬 1 cup granulated sugar
- 🧂 1 tsp salt
- 🌿 2 whole vanilla beans

### To make pudding

- 🥣 1 cup dry pudding mix
- 🥛 2 cups milk
- 🧈 1 Tbsp butter

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 In a bowl, mix together the cocoa powder, dry milk powder, cornstarch, granulated sugar, and salt.
2. 🔪 Open the vanilla beans with a knife, scrape out the seeds, and add them to the mixture. Cut the beans into 2-3 pieces and add them to the mixture, too.
3. 🫙 Put the mix in an airtight container and store in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.
4. 🥄 To make pudding: in a small bowl, mix 1 cup dry pudding mix (take out the vanilla beans) with 1 cup cold milk until there are no lumps.
5. 🔥 Scald the remaining milk. Add the pudding mixture while whisking continually and cook until the mixture thickens, about 2-3 minutes.
6. 🧈 Turn off the heat and add the butter. Serve warm or cold.
