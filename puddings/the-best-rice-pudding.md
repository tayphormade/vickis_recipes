# The Best Rice Pudding

🔗 **Source:** [Sip and Feast](https://www.sipandfeast.com/wprm_print/the-best-rice-pudding)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- 🔥 **Cook:** 1 hour (total 1 hour 5 minutes)
- ❄️ **Chill:** at least 2 hours, if served cold
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 260 kcal; carbohydrates 44.2g; protein 7g; fat 6.4g; saturated fat 3.3g; cholesterol 61mg; sodium 63mg; sugar 24.5g; fiber 0.7g

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥛 3 1/2 cups whole milk, divided
- 💧 5 cups water
- 🍚 1 cup medium-grain rice, uncooked
- 🍬 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 🧂 1 pinch fine sea salt
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🍇 1/2 cup raisins
- 🧈 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 🍂 Ground cinnamon, plus more for garnish
- 🍒 Maraschino cherries (optional, for garnish)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍲 In a large pot over a touch less than medium heat, add 3 cups of the milk, the water, rice, sugar, and salt. Bring to a simmer while stirring frequently to prevent the milk from burning or scalding.
2. ⏳ Once it begins to bubble (should take about 15 to 25 minutes), reduce the heat to low and cook for another 25 to 35 minutes, or until the rice is soft and the mixture has thickened. The liquid will have reduced by about half. Stir frequently to prevent the rice from sticking or burning.
3. 🥚 In a separate bowl, beat the egg and the remaining 1/2 cup of milk. Temper the mixture by gradually adding about 1 cup of the hot rice mixture to the egg mixture while stirring. Then add the tempered egg mixture back to the pot while stirring.
4. 🍇 Add the raisins and vanilla and cook for an additional 3 minutes or longer, depending on the thickness. The rice pudding will continue to thicken as it cools.
5. 🧈 Remove from the heat and add the butter. You can mix in cinnamon if you like, but it will turn the rice pudding brown.
6. ❄️ Eat warm, or let cool and then refrigerate to chill for at least 2 hours. Serve and garnish with cinnamon and maraschino cherries, if desired.

---

## 📝 Notes

- ⏱️ The exact cooking time will vary. It takes about 50 to 75 minutes for the liquid to reduce enough and for the rice to become tender and creamy, depending on your stove top and the type of rice you use.
- 🌡️ Rice pudding can be served warm or cold. It will continue to thicken as it cools, and even more once refrigerated for a few hours.
- 🍚 Medium-grain rice works best for texture and creaminess, but any type of rice can be used.
- 🟤 Cinnamon can be mixed in toward the end of cooking but will turn the pudding brown. The source recommends sprinkling it on top of each bowl when serving.
- 🍇 If you love raisins, you can increase to 3/4 cup or more.
- 🗄️ Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
- 📖 Source: Sip and Feast (James Delmage and Tara).
