# Vanilla Pudding Mix Recipe

🔗 **Source:** [My Frugal Home](https://www.myfrugalhome.com/vanilla-pudding-mix-recipe/)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** enough mix for 6 batches of pudding; each batch makes about 4 servings
- ⏲️ **Prep (mix):** 2 minutes
- 🔥 **Cook (each batch):** about 12 minutes on medium heat

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### To make vanilla pudding mix

- 🍬 3 1/4 cups + 2 Tbsp granulated sugar
- 🌽 1 cup + 2 Tbsp cornstarch (or your preferred thickener)

### To make vanilla pudding

- 🥣 3/4 cup vanilla pudding mix
- 🥛 2 cups milk
- 🌼 2 Tbsp vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Stir the sugar and cornstarch together to make the mix.
2. 🫙 Store the vanilla pudding mix in an air-tight container. It is sized to fit a quart jar.
3. 🥣 To make pudding, combine all the wet and dry ingredients (3/4 cup mix, 2 cups milk, and 2 Tbsp vanilla extract) in a saucepan.
4. 🔥 Stir continuously over medium heat. It will take a little while for the dry ingredients to stir in completely.
5. ⏲️ When the pudding thickens and begins to boil, it is done. This should take around 12 minutes.
6. 🥄 The pudding will continue to thicken as it cools.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🥛 To avoid adding fresh milk when you make the pudding, add 4 cups of powdered milk to the mix, and measure out an extra 2/3 cup of mix for each batch.
