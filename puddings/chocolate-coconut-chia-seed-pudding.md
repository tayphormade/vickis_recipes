# Chocolate Coconut Chia Seed Pudding

No-bake and vegan. Prepare it the night before and leave it in the refrigerator to thicken.

- ⏳ **Chill:** minimum 5 hours (overnight is best)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌱 ⅓ cup chia seeds
- 🍫 ¼ cup cocoa powder
- 🍂 ½ tablespoon chai powder (you can use cinnamon or pumpkin pie spice too)
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon sea salt
- 🌼 ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥥 ½ cup coconut milk (not coconut cream)
- 🥛 1 cup soy milk
- 🍯 2 tablespoons honey (add more or less to taste)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Food processor method: Put all the ingredients (except for the honey) into a food processor.
2. 🌀 Food processor method: Blend on medium until everything is mixed together. Add the honey while the processor is blending.
3. ❄️ Food processor method: Divide the pudding into serving containers and refrigerate for a minimum of 5 hours. Overnight is best.
4. 🫙 Jar method: Get a jar with a sealable, waterproof lid. Mix all the dry ingredients together in the jar.
5. 🥛 Jar method: Add all the liquids (the milks, vanilla extract, and honey), seal the jar, and shake it vigorously until everything is mixed.
6. ❄️ Jar method: Put the jar in the refrigerator and wait a minimum of 5 hours, but overnight is best.
7. 🍓 Serve with your choice of toppings: fresh fruit, granola, coconut whipping cream, fresh cream, custard, or a dash more coconut milk.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The source describes the food processor method and the jar method as two ways to make the pudding. If you make it in a jar, the jar becomes the serving container. To share, mix it and divide it into serving containers before refrigerating.
- 🌟 Sweetener: any liquid sweetener works. The source suggests agave or maple syrup as substitutes for the honey.
- 🌟 Milk: the source uses coconut milk and soy milk, but says you can use all milk, or all soy milk or almond milk.
- 🌟 Fresh fruit: the source says fresh fruit became mushy and grey when mixed in, so add fresh fruit only when serving. Dried fruit can be mixed in.
- 🌟 Variations listed in the source: coconut milk replaced with cold coffee, or with cold Earl Grey tea (the source says "also removed the chia powder" in this version); soy milk replaced with chocolate milk; both milks replaced with strawberry milk or a pre-made latte drink (with the honey removed).
- 🌟 The author's morning serving, from the source: sprinkle with shredded coconut and cocoa nibs, pour a little soy milk or coconut milk over, and eat it as is; or add chopped banana or strawberries.
- 🌟 The source's post is dated December 12, 2015. The source does not name an author.
