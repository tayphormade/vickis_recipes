# Creamy Rice Pudding

A quick stove-top rice pudding. Cooked rice is combined with milk, sugar, egg, and golden raisins.

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 4
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 25 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cook:** 20 minutes
- ⌛ **Ready in:** 45 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍚 ¾ cup uncooked white rice
- 💧 1½ cups water, for cooking the rice (listed in the directions, not in the ingredient list)
- 🥛 2 cups milk, divided
- 🍬 ⅓ cup white sugar
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🥚 1 egg, beaten
- 🍇 ⅔ cup golden raisins
- 🧈 1 tablespoon butter
- 🌼 ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 Bring 1½ cups water to a boil in a saucepan. Stir the rice into the boiling water. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes.
2. 🥣 In a clean saucepan, combine 1½ cups of the cooked rice, 1½ cups of the milk, the sugar, and the salt. Cook over medium heat until thick and creamy, 15 to 20 minutes.
3. 🥚 Stir in the remaining ½ cup of milk, the beaten egg, and the raisins. Cook 2 minutes more, stirring constantly.
4. 🧈 Remove from the heat and stir in the butter and vanilla.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Coconut rice pudding: substitute 1 (13-ounce) can coconut milk plus ½ cup milk for the 2 cups of milk.
- 🌟 Coconut rice pudding: substitute 1 tablespoon coconut oil for the 1 tablespoon butter, or use ½ tablespoon coconut oil and ½ tablespoon butter.
- 🌟 Coconut rice pudding: add ½ teaspoon coconut flavoring in addition to the vanilla extract (per the second copy of this source).
