# Easy Microwave Lemon Curd

🔗 **Source:** [King Arthur Flour](http://www.kingarthurflour.com/recipes/PrintRecipe?RID=4059&radio=1)

Creamy, tangy lemon curd made in the microwave in about 8 minutes, with no stirring over the stove.

- 🍽️ **Yield:** a scant 2 cups
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 10 to 15 minutes
- ⌛ **Total:** 10 to 15 minutes
- 🧊 **Storage:** refrigerated up to 3 weeks; freeze for longer storage

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍋 1 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🧈 ½ cup (8 tablespoons) butter, melted
- 🥚 2 large eggs

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine all of the ingredients in a large microwave-safe bowl. To avoid bubble-overs, the contents should fill no more than ¼ of the bowl; an 8-cup measure works well here.
2. ⏲️ Microwave the ingredients in 1-minute increments, removing the bowl and stirring to combine after each minute.
3. 🌡️ The curd is done when it starts to thicken, coats the back of a spoon, and starts to mound a bit as you stir. This will take anywhere from 4 to 10 minutes, depending on the power of your microwave. The curd's temperature should have reached at least 185°F.
4. 🥄 Stir the curd one more time, spoon it into a storage container, and refrigerate until firm.
5. 🧊 Keep the curd refrigerated for up to 3 weeks; freeze for longer storage.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Tip: Four large, juicy lemons should be enough to yield 1 cup of juice.
- 🌟 Tip: For a more intense flavor, grate the rind of 2 to 4 of the lemons, combine it with the sugar in a food processor, and process until the rind is finely ground. Proceed with the recipe as directed.
- 🌟 Tip: For a passion fruit version, use passion fruit puree or concentrate in place of the lemon juice.
