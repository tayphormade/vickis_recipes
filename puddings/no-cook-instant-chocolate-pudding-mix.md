# No-Cook Instant Chocolate Pudding Mix

🔗 **Source:** [MyFrugalHome](https://www.myfrugalhome.com/no-cook-instant-chocolate-pudding-mix/)

This no-cook chocolate pudding mix is the replacement for store-bought instant pudding mix.

- 🍽️ **Yield:** equivalent to four 3.9-ounce boxes of instant chocolate pudding mix (the source's "1x" yield)
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 2 minutes
- ⌛ **Total:** 2 minutes
- 🗂️ **Category:** Desserts
- 🍳 **Method:** Mix
- 🌎 **Cuisine:** American
- ✍️ **Author:** Erin Huffstetler, MyFrugalHome.com

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Pudding mix

- 🍬 2 cups granulated sugar [400 g]
- 🍫 1 cup cocoa powder (natural or Dutch-processed) [96 g]
- 🌽 1 cup Instant Clear Jel [153 g]

### Small-batch mix (replaces one 3.9-ounce box)

- 🍬 ½ cup granulated sugar [100 g]
- 🍫 ¼ cup cocoa (natural or Dutch-processed) [28 g]
- 🌽 ¼ cup Instant Clear Jel [38 g]

### To use the mix

- 🥛 2 cups cold milk
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🥄 1 cup instant chocolate pudding mix

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Measure out the ingredients. Spoon the cocoa and Instant Clear Jel into your measuring cups so you don't end up with extra dry ingredients in your recipe (scooping tends to do that).
2. 🥣 Stir everything together until well combined. Store the instant chocolate pudding mix in an air-tight container. This fits perfectly in a quart jar.
3. 🥛 To use: In a large bowl, combine 2 cups cold milk, 2 teaspoons vanilla extract, and 1 cup instant chocolate pudding mix.
4. 🌀 Beat with a mixer or whisk until everything comes together and the pudding begins to thicken, approximately 2 minutes. An immersion blender is great for this task.
5. ⏳ Allow the pudding to rest at least 5 minutes before serving. Like store-bought pudding mix, it'll be thicker and tastier if you wait 30 minutes (or longer) before you eat it.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Using 1 cup of mix makes the same amount of pudding as one 3.9-ounce box of instant chocolate pudding mix.
- 🌟 Recipe variation: To make dairy-free or vegan chocolate pudding with this mix, use soy, almond, coconut, or rice milk instead.
- 🌟 Pie filling: Decrease the milk to 1¾ cups, and cut the beating time to 1 minute. Replace the store-bought pudding mix called for in a pie recipe with this homemade mix. One 3.9-ounce box contains ¾ cup of mix.
- 🌟 Ice cream: use this pudding mix to make chocolate ice cream.
- 🌟 The source says its nutrition facts are for ½ cup of prepared pudding made from the mix, using 2% milk, but the nutrition values are not included in the source text.
