# Chocolate Instant No-Cook Dry Pudding Mix (Add Water)

There's no need to buy little boxes of chocolate no-cook pudding mix. You can easily make your own convenient mix in minutes. It's perfect for a nice cool dessert on a hot summer day. Mix 1 part homemade pudding mix with 2 parts milk.

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- 🍽️ **Servings:** 16
- ✍️ **Author:** www.TexasHomesteader.com

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥛 ¾ cup dry milk powder
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🌽 ½ cup Instant No-Cook Clear Jel corn starch
- 🍫 ⅓ cup unsweetened cocoa

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine all the ingredients in a bowl and stir until they are completely combined. Store in a cool, dry place in a tightly sealed container.
2. 🧁 To use the dry mix in baking, ½ cup of the mixture will replace a 4-ounce package of instant pudding mix.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 To use this mix as an instant pudding dessert, add 1 part pudding mix to 2 parts cold milk. You can substitute 1 part of the milk with cold coffee if desired. Beat vigorously with a whisk until all lumps are removed and the pudding is thickened, about 2 minutes.
- 🌟 The source suggests adding a couple of drops of vanilla extract for extra flavor.
- 🌟 This makes a very thick pudding. Add a small amount of milk (or cold coffee) if you want a thinner consistency. Pudding thickens more upon chilling.
- 🌟 The source's title line says "(Add Water)," but its pudding directions use cold milk or cold coffee.
