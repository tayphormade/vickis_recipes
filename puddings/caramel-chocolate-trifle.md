# Caramel Chocolate Trifle

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 16
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 20 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** 20 minutes, plus cooling
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 349 calories; fat 11g (saturated fat 7g); cholesterol 21mg; sodium 533mg; carbohydrates 61g (sugars 29g; fiber 1g); protein 3g

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Cake

- 🍫 1 package (9 ounces) devil's food cake mix

### Chocolate pudding

- 🍫 2 packages (3.9 ounces each) instant chocolate pudding mix (see the small-batch mix below)

### Topping and layers

- ☁️ 1 carton (12 ounces) frozen whipped topping, thawed
- 🍯 1 jar (12-1/4 ounces) caramel ice cream topping (see the caramel sauce below)
- 🍬 1 package (7-1/2 or 8 ounces) English toffee bits or almond brickle chips

### Small batch instant chocolate pudding mix (replaces one 3.9-ounce box)

- 🍬 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 🟤 1/4 cup cocoa (natural or Dutch-processed)
- 🌾 1/4 cup Instant Clear Jel (modified corn starch; regular cornstarch would require cooking the pudding)

### Pudding (made with the small-batch mix)

- 🥛 2 cups cold milk
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🍫 1 cup instant chocolate pudding mix

### Caramel sauce (yield 1 cup)

- 🍬 1 cup granulated sugar
- 🧈 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into 6-8 pieces
- 🥛 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 🧂 1/8 teaspoon kosher salt (or use salted butter and taste)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Prepare and bake the cake according to package directions for an 8-inch square baking pan. Cool on a wire rack. Prepare the chocolate pudding according to package directions (see step 3).
2. 🥣 Cut the cake into 1 1/2-inch cubes. Place half the cubes in a 3-quart trifle bowl or large glass serving bowl; lightly press down to fill in gaps. Top the cake with half each of the whipped topping, pudding, caramel topping, and toffee bits; repeat the layers. Cover and refrigerate until serving.
3. 🥄 Small-batch pudding: in a large bowl, combine 2 cups cold milk, 2 teaspoons vanilla extract, and 1 cup instant chocolate pudding mix. Beat with a mixer or whisk until everything comes together and the pudding begins to thicken, approximately 2 minutes. An immersion blender is great for this task. Allow the pudding to rest at least 5 minutes before serving. Like store-bought pudding mix, it will be thicker and tastier if you wait 30 minutes (or longer) before eating. This makes the same amount of pudding as one 3.9-ounce box of instant chocolate pudding mix.
4. 🍯 Caramel sauce: place the sugar in a large saucepan over medium heat. Stir constantly with a rubber spatula and let the sugar heat; it will clump together as it starts to melt and then start turning to liquid. Keep stirring so it doesn't burn, until it is completely melted into an amber-colored liquid. Once the sugar is melted, immediately add the pieces of butter. The liquid will bubble up when you add the butter. Stir the caramel until the butter is completely melted, about 2 minutes. Slowly drizzle in the heavy cream while still stirring. Be careful, as the caramel will bubble up a lot when you add the cream. Allow the mixture to boil for 1 minute, stirring constantly, then remove from the heat. Stir in the salt and allow it to cool down a bit before using it, or pouring it into another container, as it will be extremely hot. Cover it tightly and store it in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks. You can warm it up in the microwave before using it. Scoop the amount you are going to use into another small bowl to heat, because microwaving it multiple times will cause it to not melt quite as nicely.

## 📝 Notes

- 🍰 Author's note: the author used King Arthur GF Chocolate Cake Mix in place of the devil's food cake mix.
- 🥥 Author's note: the author used canned coconut milk (Thai Kitchen Organic) instead of cold milk in the pudding, which gave it a more mousse-like consistency.
- 🥥 Author's note: the author used the Coconut Whip for the whipped topping.
- 🍬 Author's note: the author used Enstrom's English toffee.
- 🔗 The source notes that Instant Clear Jel can be found on Amazon.
- 🧂 The original caramel recipe calls for 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt for a salted caramel. The author used 1/8 teaspoon.
- 🔗 The author's note links a salted caramel sauce recipe at https://bake-eat-repeat.com/salted-caramel-sauce-recipe/ and says the author took out the salt.
