# Almond Croissants Recipe (French Bakery Style)

🔗 **Source:** [Natasha's Kitchen](https://natashaskitchen.com/almond-croissants-french-bakery-style/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 12 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cook:** 18 minutes
- ⌛ **Total:** 30 minutes
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏱️ **Bake:** 15 to 18 minutes, or until the cream is golden

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Croissants

- 🥐 8 one-day-old medium/large croissants, left at room temperature uncovered overnight
- 🌰 3 tablespoons sliced almonds
- ❄️ Powdered confectioners' sugar, for dusting

### Syrup

- 💧 1 cup water
- 🍬 2 tablespoons sugar
- 🥃 4 tablespoons rum (optional, but highly recommended; you can use 1 teaspoon vanilla extract instead)

### Almond filling

- 🍬 ½ cup granulated sugar
- 🌰 1 cup almond meal or almond flour, or ⅔ cup whole blanched almonds (you'll need a good food processor to grind whole almonds into a meal)
- 🧂 ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 1 stick (8 tablespoons) unsalted butter, room temperature, sliced
- 🥚 2 large eggs

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 Syrup: In a small saucepan, combine 1 cup water, 2 tablespoons sugar, and 4 tablespoons rum. Bring to a simmer for a minute and stir until the sugar dissolves. Remove from heat and let cool to room temperature.
2. 🌰 Almond filling, using whole almonds: Combine ½ cup sugar, ⅔ cup almonds, and ⅛ teaspoon salt in the bowl of a food processor, and pulse until finely ground. Add the butter and mix again until well blended. Add the eggs one by one, and process on high speed until creamy and fluffy.
3. 🥣 Almond filling, using almond meal or almond flour: In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment, combine ½ cup sugar, 1 cup almond meal, and ⅛ teaspoon salt. Mix until well incorporated, then blend in the butter. Finally, add the eggs one at a time and mix on medium/high speed until the whole mixture is creamy and fluffy with a frosting-like consistency.
4. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper.
5. 🥐 After your croissants have been on the counter drying out overnight, and you're ready to use them, slice each croissant horizontally like you would for a sandwich.
6. 💧 Work with each croissant one by one: dip it into the syrup, coating both sides and the ends well. The croissant should be quite moist, but not soaking wet. Just dip it once on each side.
7. 🥄 Arrange the croissants on a baking dish, cut side up. Spread about 2 tablespoons of almond filling on the bottom half of each croissant.
8. 🌰 Place the top halves on and spread about 1 tablespoon of almond filling over the top. Sprinkle with sliced almonds.
9. 🔥 Bake on the center rack for 15 to 18 minutes, or until the cream is golden. Transfer to a wire rack and cool to room temperature or just warm. Dust with powdered sugar before serving. These are best served the same day they are made.
