# Better Than Croissants

- 🔥 **Oven:** 356°F (180°C), preheated half an hour before baking
- ⏱️ **Bake:** 25 to 30 minutes
- 🍽️ **Servings:** not stated in the source

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Dough

- 🥛 1½ glasses (300 ml) milk
- 💧 1 glass (200 ml) water (100 ml if using starter)
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🦠 1 pack (10 g) dry yeast (or 200 g sourdough starter)
- 🍬 2 tablespoons sugar
- 🧂 1 teaspoon salt
- 🌾 700 g wheat flour (600 g if using starter), 1 part all-purpose and 2 parts pastry flour according to Julia Child

### Butter

- 🧈 50 g butter, melted

### Egg wash

- 🥚 1 egg yolk
- 🥛 1 tablespoon milk

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine the milk, water, sugar, yeast, and egg. Pour in the flour slowly and carefully.
2. 🧂 Add the salt. The dough is quite sticky. Sprinkle flour on top to remove the stickiness of the dough, then knead until the dough is smooth and soft.
3. ⚪ Form the dough into a ball. Grease the bowl with a little vegetable oil, cover the dough, and let it rise in a warm place for 40 minutes.
4. 👊 After 40 minutes, the dough is nicely risen. Remove the air from the fermented dough. Divide the dough into 2 equal parts and form a ball out of each part.
5. 🪵 Roll each piece of dough to a 40 x 40 cm (16 x 16 inch) square.
6. 🧈 Melt the 50 g of butter and pour or brush it over the dough.
7. 🌾 Sift a small amount of flour onto the dough. Fold one end of the dough to the thickness of 4 fingers, continuing to fold until you reach the end.
8. 🪵 Prepare the other half of the dough in the same way.
9. 🔪 Put the two pieces of dough side by side, touching on the long side. Cut the dough into pieces of equal thickness, about 3 cm (1¼ inches) wide.
10. 🥢 Place two pieces of dough on top of each other and press them together with 2 wooden skewers through the middle, lengthwise.
11. 🌀 Stretch each piece and wrap it around your finger.
12. 🧻 Place on a baking tray lined with baking paper. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
13. 🥚 Mix 1 egg yolk and 1 tablespoon milk in a small bowl. Brush the rolls with the mixture.
14. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 180°C (356°F) half an hour beforehand. Bake in the preheated oven at 180°C (356°F) for 25 to 30 minutes.
