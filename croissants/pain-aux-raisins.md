# Pain aux Raisins

- 🍽️ **Yield:** about 20 pieces, 3 ounces (90 g) each
- 📅 **Timing:** make a day ahead
- 🔥 **Bake:** 400°F (200°C) for about 20 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Raisins

- 🍇 8 ounces (250 g) mixed raisins, soaked in water overnight

### Croissant dough

- 💧 1 cup (250 ml) water at 82°F (28°C)
- 🦠 2 packets (14 g) RapidRise yeast, instant yeast, or (30 g) fresh yeast
- 🍬 1 Tbsp (10 g) granulated sugar
- 🌾 1.7 cups (250 g) unbleached all-purpose flour
- 🌾 1.7 cups (250 g) unbleached bread flour
- 🧂 2.7 tsp (12 g) salt
- 🍬 ¼ cup (40 g) granulated sugar
- 🧈 6.5 Tbsp (100 g) unsalted butter, at room temperature

### Butter slab for tourage (folding and turning)

- 🧈 16.5 Tbsp (250 g) European-style unsalted butter, at room temperature

### Egg wash

- 🥚 1 egg, beaten with a pinch of salt

### Pastry cream (crème pâtissière)

- 🥛 1.2 cups (300 ml) whole milk
- 🌼 ½ Tbsp (7.5 ml) vanilla extract or paste
- 🍬 ¼ cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 🥚 3 each (60 g) egg yolks
- 🌽 2 Tbsp (25 g) cornstarch or potato starch
- 🧈 2 Tbsp (30 g) butter

### Orange marmalade simple syrup

- 🍊 2 Tbsp (30 g) orange marmalade or apricot preserve
- 💧 2 Tbsp (30 ml) water
- 🍬 3 Tbsp (30 g) sugar

### Finish (choose one option)

- ⚪ Option 1: pearl sugar
- 🥮 Option 2: extra softened butter for English muffin rings, brown sugar, and orange simple syrup

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 In a large bowl, combine the water, yeast, and 1 tablespoon (10 g) of sugar and let sit for 5 minutes. Add the remaining dough ingredients and mix until it comes together. Transfer the mixture onto the work surface and knead for about 8 minutes, until smooth (no extra flour needed). The temperature of the dough should be around 73 to 80°F (23 to 27°C).
2. ⚪ Form the dough into a tight ball and place it in a lightly greased container. Wrap it up loosely or cover with a wet kitchen towel and let rise for about an hour, or until it has tripled in size. Flip the dough over on the work surface and deflate it (no extra flour needed). Fold up the corners and shape it into a tight rectangle. Place the dough on a previously frozen baking tray lined with a silicone mat. Freeze each side for 30 minutes to ease the lamination process.
3. 🧈 Meanwhile, make a slab of butter using parchment paper folded into an 8 x 8-inch (20 x 20 cm) square, and chill.
4. ❄️ Tourage (one double turn and one single turn, double turn first): Do your preparation in a cool room. Most important, the dough and butter must be the same consistency. The butter should remain cool but malleable. If it's too soft and greasy, it will ooze out of the dough; if it's too firm, it might break through the dough. As you roll it out, work quickly and handle the dough as little as possible, so the heat of your hands doesn't melt the butter. Let the butter warm up for about 5 minutes before folding it into the dough. Flour the work surface and roll out the dough into a rectangle twice the size of the butter slab. Place the butter slab on the dough and fold over. Roll out the dough into a 28-inch (72 cm) rectangle. Brush off any excess flour and fold in four (double turn). Wrap up and refrigerate for an hour to rest.
5. 🧈 Single turn (tour simple): Flour the work surface lightly and roll the dough into a 34 x 7-inch (85 x 18 cm) rectangle. Brush off any excess flour and fold into thirds (single turn). Chill for an hour or overnight to rest.
6. 🥛 Pastry cream: Bring the milk and vanilla to a boil. Meanwhile, beat the sugar, starch, and yolks together. Gradually pour the hot milk into the yolk mixture and mix well. Transfer the mixture back into the saucepan. Bring to a boil and cook for 2 minutes. Turn off the heat and mix in the butter. Transfer the pastry cream onto a baking tray lined with plastic wrap. Wrap up in contact with the cream and let cool to room temperature, then chill completely. Beat to smooth out before using. Pastry cream can be refrigerated up to 3 days.
7. 🍇 Pain aux raisins log: Drain the plumped raisins and set aside. Beat the chilled pastry cream to smooth it out and set aside. Dust the work surface and dough with flour. Roll out the dough into a 24 x 13-inch (60 x 33 cm) rectangle. During this process, the dough sheet can be placed in the freezer for about 30 minutes to relax. Using an offset spatula, spread the softened pastry cream evenly all over the croissant dough. Sprinkle evenly with the reserved raisins. Roll tightly into a 20-inch (51 cm) log and place the log seam side up to ease the cutting process. Cut the log into 20 portions, 1 inch (2.5 cm) thick. Seal the seam underneath each roll. Brush with egg wash, wrap up, and freeze overnight or up to a month.
8. 🥄 Arrange 6 frozen pain aux raisins per tray, lined with a silicone mat or parchment. If using English muffin rings, butter the rings generously and coat them with brown sugar. Let proof on the countertop overnight. Brush with egg wash, sprinkle with pearl sugar if using option 1, and bake.
9. 🍊 Orange marmalade simple syrup: Bring all the ingredients to a boil and set aside.
10. 🔥 Bake the pain aux raisins in a preheated 400°F (200°C) oven for about 20 minutes. For option 2, using English muffin rings, glaze the just-baked pain aux raisins with the orange marmalade simple syrup. Transfer onto a wire rack and let cool. Best at room temperature and eaten within 12 hours.

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Source: Bruno Albouze, "Pain Aux Raisins / 2 ways," © 2016. The source includes both a pearl sugar option and an English muffin ring option.
- 🌟 Equipment: English muffin rings (optional, for option 2).
- 🌟 The source lists the vanilla as "vanilla extra or paste." It is written here as "vanilla extract or paste."
- 🌟 The source's pastry cream can be refrigerated for up to 3 days.
- 🌟 The source's yield line says "Make a day ahead."
- ⚠️ The source heads the single-turn step "Single Turn w/ Sugar (Tour Simple)," but it does not say how much sugar to add or when.
