# Pumpkin Pie Leather

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- 🔥 **Dehydrate:** 6 to 8 hours at 125°F (52°C), until leathery

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🎃 2 cups pureed pumpkin
- 🍎 2 cups unsweetened applesauce
- 🥥 2 cups canned coconut milk
- 🌴 4 Medjool dates
- 🍯 ¼ cup honey
- 🍂 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground allspice

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌀 Blend all ingredients in a high-speed blender until smooth.
2. 🥄 Spread the mixture onto parchment-lined dehydrator trays, about ¼ inch thick.
3. 🔥 Dry at 125°F (52°C) for 6 to 8 hours, until leathery.
4. 🔪 Let cool, then cut into strips.
