# Pumpkin Pecan Cobbler

- 🍽️ **Makes:** one 9x13-inch pan
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 5 minutes
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 40 to 50 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🎃 2½ cups pumpkin puree
- 🥛 1½ cups evaporated milk
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🍂 1 tablespoon pumpkin spice (cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, allspice, and cloves)
- 🍰 4 cups yellow or spice cake mix, homemade or 1 box store-bought
- 🌰 1 cup chopped pecans
- 🍬 ½ cup brown sugar
- 💧 3 cups hot water

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Combine the pumpkin puree, evaporated milk, eggs, and pumpkin spice.
2. 🍰 Add the cake mix and blend together.
3. 🧴 Pour into an oiled 9x13-inch baking dish.
4. 🌰 Sprinkle the pecans and brown sugar over the top.
5. 💧 Pour the hot water over the mixture. Do not stir.
6. 🔥 Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 40 to 50 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the middle comes out clean.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Half batch for an 8x8-inch dish: 1¼ cups pumpkin, ¾ cup evaporated milk, 1 large egg, ½ tablespoon pumpkin spice, 2 cups cake mix, ½ cup pecans, ¼ cup brown sugar, and 1½ cups hot water.
- 🌟 Quarter batch for a 7-inch round dish: ½ cup pumpkin, ⅓ cup evaporated milk, 1 small egg, 1 teaspoon pumpkin spice, 1 cup cake mix, ¼ cup pecans, 2 tablespoons brown sugar, and ¾ cup hot water.
