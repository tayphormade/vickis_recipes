# Concord Grape Pie

🔗 **Source:** [Mama Gourmand](https://www.mamagourmand.com/concord-grape-pie/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 50 minutes
- 🕑 **Total time:** 1 hour 10 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 400°F (200°C)
- 📊 **Per serving:** 483 calories (15 g fat, 89 g carbohydrate, 65 g sugar, 4 g protein, 3 g fiber, 239 mg sodium)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Filling

- 🍇 6 cups washed, de-stemmed Concord grapes (about 2½ lbs. with stems on)
- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🌾 ⅓ cup all-purpose flour or gluten-free flour (the source recommends Cup 4 Cup gluten-free flour)
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 🍋 1 tablespoon lemon juice

### Streusel topping

- 🌾 ½ cup all-purpose flour or gluten-free flour (the source recommends Cup 4 Cup gluten-free flour)
- 🍬 ½ cup sugar
- 🧈 ¼ cup unsalted butter, softened

### Crust

- 🥧 1 pie crust shell (unbaked), store-bought or homemade

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. 🍇 Gently squeeze the grapes so the pulp slips out of the skin into a medium saucepan. Separate the skins into a small bowl and set aside to use in the pie filling.
3. 🔥 Bring the pulp of the grapes to a boil over medium heat. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. 🥣 Remove from heat and immediately pour through a mesh strainer fitted over a bowl to separate the seeds from the pulp. Press and stir so ALL the pulp is extracted into the bowl. Scrape the bottom of the strainer occasionally to get the pulp from there too. When you are done, you should be left with only the seeds, and you won't be able to get any more pulp out. Discard the seeds.
5. 🧈 Place the butter into the bowl of hot pulp and let it sit for a few minutes until it melts. When the butter is melted, whisk in the lemon juice, sugar, flour, and salt until smooth. Stir the reserved skins into the pulp mixture.
6. 🥧 Pour the grape mixture into the unbaked pie shell. Prepare the streusel topping: with a fork, mix all of its ingredients together until the butter is well incorporated. Sprinkle the topping over the pie filling.
7. ⏲️ Bake for 40–50 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the grape filling is bubbly throughout. Cool for at least an hour before serving. Serve warm with ice cream or at room temperature. Store leftovers on the counter, uncovered.

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Mama Gourmand, "Concord Grape Pie" by Melissa Erdelac. Printed 10/25/2019.
- 🍇 A comment added to the source next to the grapes says: "about 1 quart (4 cups)." The source does not say whether this refers to the grapes or the pulp.
- 🧊 Before cutting, let the pie cool 1–2 hours so the filling sets.
- 🥧 Storage: do not refrigerate leftovers. The pie can sit on the counter up to 2 days. Leave it uncovered to keep the crust crisp; if you need to cover it, turn a large bowl over the pie.
- ❄️ Freezing: the pie can be frozen fully assembled. Prepare it without baking, freeze on a baking sheet until solid, then wrap securely in plastic wrap and cover with a double layer of foil. Freeze up to 6 months. Bake from frozen; it will take up to twice as long, so protect the crust with a silicone pie shield or aluminum foil.
- 🍨 Concord Grape Crisp (variation from the same source): forgo the pie crust. Pour the grape filling into an 8x8 baking dish and preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Prepare the streusel topping, adding ¼ cup brown sugar, 1 cup old-fashioned oats, and an extra 4 tablespoons of softened butter. Sprinkle over the filling and bake 30–35 minutes, or until the fruit is bubbling all over. Let cool 20 minutes and serve warm with ice cream.
