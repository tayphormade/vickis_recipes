# Harvest Pear Crisp

Cinnamon-spiced pears bake under a crunchy streusel topping. Assemble the dessert ahead of time, and put it in the oven when guests arrive. Or bake it earlier in the day and serve it at room temperature. The source suggests serving it with low-fat vanilla ice cream.

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 8 servings
- 🔥 **Oven:** 375°F (190°C)
- ⏱️ **Bake:** 40 minutes, or until the pears are tender and the topping is golden brown
- 🧊 **Cool:** 20 minutes on a wire rack

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍐 6 cups Anjou or Bartlett pears, cored and cut lengthwise into ½-inch-thick slices (about 3 pounds)
- 🍋 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 🍬 ⅓ cup granulated sugar
- 🌽 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 🍂 1½ teaspoons ground cinnamon, divided
- 🌾 ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- 🍬 ½ cup packed brown sugar
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🧈 3 tablespoons chilled butter, cut into small pieces
- 🌾 ⅓ cup regular oats
- 🌰 ¼ cup coarsely chopped walnuts

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. 🍐 Combine the pears and lemon juice in a 2-quart baking dish, and toss gently to coat. Combine the granulated sugar, cornstarch, and 1 teaspoon of the cinnamon, and stir with a whisk. Add the cornstarch mixture to the pear mixture, and toss well to coat.
3. 🌾 Lightly spoon the flour into a dry measuring cup, and level it with a knife. Place the flour, ½ teaspoon of the cinnamon, the brown sugar, and the salt in a food processor, and pulse 2 times or until combined. Add the chilled butter and pulse 6 times, or until the mixture resembles coarse meal. Add the oats and walnuts and pulse 2 times. Sprinkle the flour mixture evenly over the pear mixture.
4. ⏱️ Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 40 minutes, or until the pears are tender and the topping is golden brown. Cool 20 minutes on a wire rack. Serve warm or at room temperature.
