# Boston Market Cinnamon Apples Fat-Free

By Todd Wilbur. The cinnamon apple side dish has been on the Boston Market menu since the company opened its first outlet. This version uses Butter Buds to cut the fat while keeping the flavor.

- 🍽️ **Serves:** 4
- 📏 **Serving size:** ½ cup
- 📊 **Per serving:** 177 calories (original 250); 0 g fat (original 4.5 g)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏱️ **Bake:** 40 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍏 3 Golden Delicious apples
- 💧 ⅔ cup water
- 🌾 ½ tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 🌽 1 teaspoon cornstarch
- 🧈 2 teaspoons Butter Buds Sprinkles
- 🍬 ½ cup light brown sugar
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🧂 Pinch of salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. 🍎 Peel and core the apples. Cut each one into 16 slices and arrange the slices in an 8 x 8-inch baking dish.
3. 🥣 In a small bowl, combine the water with the flour, cornstarch, and Butter Buds, and stir until the dry ingredients are dissolved and not lumpy. Add the brown sugar and cinnamon, and stir until smooth.
4. 🥄 Pour the cinnamon mixture over the apple slices, cover the dish with foil, and bake for 40 minutes. Stir the apples every 10 minutes.
