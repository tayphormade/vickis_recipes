# Keto Shortbread Cookies

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 33 (coconut flour version), 54 (almond flour version), 45 (walnut meal version)
- 📊 **Per cookie (coconut flour):** 83 calories, 1.5 g net carbs

- ⏲️ **Bake:** 10 to 12 minutes (up to 15 for crispier cookies)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🌾 Coconut flour version

- 🥥 90 g (¾ cup) coconut flour
- 🧈 250 g (1 cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 🍬 50 g (½ cup) Lakanto powdered sweetener, or any keto-friendly sweetener
- 🧂 ½ to ¾ teaspoon salt
- 🍒 50 g (½ cup) unsweetened dried cranberries
- 🌰 100 g (1 cup) toasted chopped pecans
- 🌼 Vanilla extract (for the dough)

### 🌰 Almond flour version

- 🌰 360 g (3 cups) almond flour
- 🧈 250 g (1 cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 🍬 80 g (¾ cup) Lakanto powdered sweetener, or any keto-friendly sweetener
- 🧂 1 to 1½ teaspoons salt
- 🍒 65 g (½ cup) unsweetened dried cranberries
- 🌰 120 g (1 cup) toasted chopped pecans

### 🥜 Walnut meal version

- 🥜 240 g (2 cups) walnut meal
- 🥥 60 g (½ cup) coconut flour (absorbs the extra fat from the walnuts)
- 🧈 188 g (¾ cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 🍬 80 g (¾ cup) Lakanto powdered sweetener, or any keto-friendly sweetener
- 🧂 1 to 1½ teaspoons salt
- 🍒 65 g (½ cup) unsweetened dried cranberries
- 🌰 120 g (1 cup) toasted chopped pecans

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Beat the butter and powdered sweetener until light and fluffy.
2. 🌼 Add the vanilla and whisk to combine.
3. 🌾 Add the flour and salt, and whisk to combine.
4. 🥄 Mix the dough well with a spatula. Add the cranberries and pecans, and mix until combined.
5. 🫐 Blend or finely chop the cranberries first, so the pieces are very small. Large pieces make the cookies chewy.
6. ⚪ Shape the dough into small balls and flatten them, or press it into a parchment-lined square mold.
7. 🪵 If using a mold, divide the dough into 2 portions and shape each into a log. Press the dough evenly into the mold, fold the paper over, and remove the mold.
8. ❄️ Freeze the wrapped dough for 1 hour, until firm enough to slice.
9. 🔪 Slice into ¼-inch pieces and place them on a parchment-lined tray. Chill any pieces you're not baking yet.
10. 🔥 Bake at 320°F (160°C) for 10 to 12 minutes. For crispier cookies, bake up to 15 minutes, but they will look darker.
11. 🧊 Let the cookies cool on the tray for 15 to 20 minutes, until firm enough to move to a wire rack. Let them cool completely. They firm up more the next day.
12. 🥡 Store in an airtight container for up to 2 weeks, in the refrigerator, or in the freezer.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 For a vegan version, use vegan butter.
- 🌟 The cookies taste very good chilled.
- 🌟 Freeze the cookies and eat them straight from the freezer.
- 🌟 Use a non-stick U-shaped mold for square pieces.
- 🌟 The walnut meal version needs a video to learn how to grind the walnuts (not included here).
