# Almond Macaroons

- 🍽️ **Makes:** about 3 dozen (small batch) or 60 or more (big batch)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌰 7 oz (210 g) almond paste
- 🍬 ½ cup (3.5 oz / 60 g) sugar
- 🥥 ⅔ cup (2 oz / 40 g) coconut
- 🥚 2 egg whites (2 oz / 40 g)

### 📦 Big batch

- 🌰 7 pounds almond paste
- 🍬 3½ pounds sugar
- 🥥 2 pounds coconut
- 🥚 32 ounces egg whites

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Mix all the ingredients together.
2. 🥄 Scoop the dough onto a cookie sheet.
3. 🔥 Bake at 325°F (163°C) for 20 minutes, until lightly browned.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Odense brand almond paste comes in 7 oz tubes.
