# Snickerdoodles (Gluten Free)

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 48 cookies
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 10 to 12 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🧈 Dough

- 🧈 ¾ cup butter or vegetable shortening
- 🍬 1½ cups sugar
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla

### 🌾 Dry ingredients

- 🌾 2 cups sorghum flour
- 🥔 ½ cup tapioca flour
- 🌽 ¼ cup arrowroot starch
- 🥄 2 teaspoons cream of tartar
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt

### 🍂 Coating

- 🍬 2 tablespoons sugar
- 🍂 1 teaspoon cinnamon

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. 🥄 Cream the shortening and sugar in a large bowl.
3. 🥚 Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
4. 🌾 In a separate bowl, combine the sorghum flour, tapioca flour, arrowroot, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt.
5. 🥄 Pour the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients and beat until well mixed.
6. ⚪ Roll the dough into balls about the size of a large walnut.
7. 🍂 Mix the 2 tablespoons sugar and the cinnamon in a small bowl.
8. 🍂 Roll each dough ball in the cinnamon sugar.
9. 🍪 Place the balls on a greased cookie sheet.
10. 🔥 Bake for 10 to 12 minutes.
