# Peppermint Meringue Cookie Recipe

🔗 **Source:** [The Salted Pepper](https://thesaltedpepper.com/peppermint-meringue-cookies/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 40 cookies (about 40 per batch, depending on size)
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 4 hours
- 🕑 **Total time:** 4 hours 15 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 200°F (90°C); **dehydrator:** 175°F (80°C)
- 📊 **Per cookie:** 16 calories; 4 g carbohydrate; 4 g sugar; 0.3 g protein; 0.02 g fat; 5 mg sodium

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥚 ½ cup egg whites (about 5 large eggs, separated)
- 🍬 ¾ cup granulated sugar
- 🌿 1 teaspoon peppermint bakery emulsion, or ¾ teaspoon peppermint extract
- 🔴 Red gel food coloring (optional)

### Equipment

- 🧰 Dehydrator or oven

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Add the room temperature egg whites to a clean bowl of your stand mixer, or a large bowl if using an electric hand mixer. Whisk on medium speed for about 3 to 5 minutes, until the egg whites become frothy.
2. 🍬 With the mixer running, stream in the granulated sugar. Once it's incorporated, add the flavoring. If using peppermint extract, use about ¾ teaspoon.
3. 🥣 Increase the speed to high and whisk until stiff peaks form. This means that when you lift the whisk attachment, the egg whites stand up and don't droop over.
4. 🔴 Add color: after stiff peaks form, stick a toothpick into the red (or whatever color you want) gel food coloring and make strokes through the meringue. Do not mix the meringue unless you want solid-color meringues. The source says to dip the toothpick into the gel food coloring 6–8 times. Fill the piping bag with the meringue (do not mix the food coloring in unless you want solid-color meringues), and pipe swirls onto prepared trays. This swirl of color only works with a piping bag.
5. 🧻 Line your baking trays or dehydrator racks with parchment paper. Fill a pastry bag with the meringue and, using a large closed star tip, pipe swirls about 2–3 inches in diameter and 1 inch high. Or simply scoop out about 1 Tablespoon of meringue onto the parchment and make little swirls in the top with a fork.
6. 🔥 Dehydrator: place the trays into the dehydrator, set the temperature to 175°F (80°C), and set the time for 4 hours. When the time is up, leave the cookies in the dehydrator for another 4–8 hours.
7. 🔥 Oven: preheat the oven to 200°F (90°C). Place the trays of meringues on the center rack, or as close to the middle of the oven as you can. Bake for 90 minutes, then turn the oven off. Leave the meringues in the oven for at least 4 hours, but overnight is better.
8. 🛍️ Storage: once the meringues have sat in the dehydrator or oven and are completely cool, place them in a freezer bag and squeeze out as much air as possible. Store at room temperature. The source says they stayed fresh at room temperature for a month, and recommends freezing them for longer storage.

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: The Salted Pepper, "Peppermint Meringue Cookie Recipe" (author: Louise). Printed 12/4/2023.
- 💡 Tips from the source: don't make them on a humid day, since humidity can keep them from drying and leave them chewy and sticky. Make sure the mixing bowl and utensils are clean, dry, and free of oil, since oil keeps egg whites from whipping. Line the sheet pan or dehydrator tray with parchment, or the cookies will stick. Making them in the evening lets them dry overnight.
- 🛍️ Storage per the source: store cooled cookies in plastic freezer bags at room temperature for 2–3 days, or in the freezer for longer storage.
