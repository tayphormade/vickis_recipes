# Flourless Chocolate Cookies

🔗 **Source:** [Sugar Spun Run](https://sugarspunrun.com/flourless-chocolate-cookies/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 24 cookies
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 25 minutes
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 9 minutes
- 📊 **Per cookie:** 130 calories

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍫 6 oz (170 g) 60% cacao chocolate bars, chopped
- 🧈 ½ cup (113 g) unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 🥚 2 large eggs, room temperature preferred
- 🍬 ½ cup (100 g) granulated sugar
- 🍬 ¼ cup (50 g) light brown sugar, firmly packed
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🍫 ¼ cup (25 g) natural cocoa powder
- 🌽 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 🥄 ¼ teaspoon baking powder
- 🍫 ½ cup (85 g) mini chocolate chips

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. 🍫 In a microwave-safe bowl, heat the chopped chocolate and butter in 25-second increments, stirring between each, until smooth.
3. 🥚 In a separate bowl, beat the eggs, both sugars, vanilla, and salt on high speed for about 2 minutes, until thick, pale, and increased in volume.
4. 🥄 Gently fold the chocolate mixture into the egg mixture with a spatula.
5. 🌽 Sift the cocoa powder, cornstarch, and baking powder over the batter. Gently stir until about 50% combined.
6. 🍫 Add the mini chocolate chips, and gently stir until completely combined.
7. ⚪ Scoop the dough by 1½ tablespoons onto the baking sheet, spaced at least 2 inches apart. The dough thickens as it sits. That's fine.
8. 🔥 Bake for 9 to 10 minutes. Cool completely on the baking sheet before removing.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
