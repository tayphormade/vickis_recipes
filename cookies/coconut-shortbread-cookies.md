# Coconut Shortbread Cookies

- ⏲️ **Chill:** 5 hours or more
- 🍪 **Bake:** 30 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 ¾ cup butter, softened
- 🍬 ¾ cup sugar
- 🌾 2 cups flour
- 🥥 1 cup sweetened flaked coconut

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Cream the butter and sugar until fluffy.
2. 🥄 Stir in the flour and coconut.
3. 🪵 Shape the dough into a log and wrap it in plastic.
4. ❄️ Chill the log for 5 hours or more.
5. 🔪 Slice the log ⅛ to ¼ inch thick.
6. 🍪 Place the slices close together on a cookie sheet.
7. 🔥 Bake at 300°F (150°C) for 30 minutes.
8. 🧊 Cool completely on a wire rack.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The original recipe is an approximation of the cookies from Mrs. Barry's of Kona.
- 🌟 A double Spam musubi mold works well for shaping the log.
