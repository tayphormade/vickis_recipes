# Green Mint Cookies

- ⏲️ **Bake:** 15 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌿 20 grams mint leaves
- 🍬 100 grams sugar
- 🥚 1 egg
- 🧈 100 grams butter
- 🌾 200 grams flour
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking soda

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌿 Blend the mint leaves and sugar with an immersion blender into a paste.
2. 🥚 Add the egg and butter, and blend until well mixed.
3. 🌾 Mix in the flour and baking soda by hand until incorporated.
4. ⚪ Form the dough into flat golf ball-size balls.
5. 🍪 Place the balls on an ungreased cookie sheet.
6. 🔥 Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 15 minutes.
7. 🧊 Let the cookies cool.
