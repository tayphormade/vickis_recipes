# Chocolate Brownie Cookies

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 2 dozen
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 14 to 16 minutes
- 📊 **Per serving:** 60 calories, 2 g fat, 13 g carbohydrates, 1 g fiber, 1 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍚 3 cups gluten-free powdered sugar
- 🍫 ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 🧂 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 🥚 2 large egg whites
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🍫 4 oz bittersweet chocolate, chopped
- 🫘 3 tablespoons cacao nibs

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Place racks in the lower and upper thirds of the oven. Preheat to 350°F (175°C).
2. 🥄 In a large bowl, whisk the powdered sugar, cocoa powder, and salt.
3. 🥚 Whisk in the egg whites and egg.
4. 🍫 Fold in the chopped chocolate and cacao nibs.
5. 🥄 Spoon the batter by the tablespoonful onto 2 parchment-lined baking sheets, spaced 2 inches apart.
6. 🔥 Bake for 14 to 16 minutes, rotating the sheets once, until the cookies are puffed, cracked, and set around the edges.
7. 🧊 Transfer the sheets to wire racks and let the cookies cool on the pan. They firm up as they cool.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The cookies can be baked up to 3 days ahead. Store them airtight at room temperature.
