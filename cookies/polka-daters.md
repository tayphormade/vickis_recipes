# Polka Daters

- 🌡️ **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** about 30 minutes
- 🍽️ **Yield:** about 30 bars (in a 15x10x1-inch pan)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌴 1¼ cup (8-oz pkg) chopped dates
- 💧 1 cup hot water
- 🧈 1 cup butter, softened
- 🍬 1¼ cup sugar
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🌾 1¾ cup sifted flour
- 🧪 ½ tsp baking soda
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla
- 🍫 1 cup (6 oz pkg) chocolate chips
- 🌰 ½ cup nuts, chopped

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 💧 Mix together the dates and hot water. Cool.
2. 🥣 Beat the butter, sugar, and eggs until creamy.
3. 🌾 Sift and stir in the flour and baking soda gradually.
4. 🥣 Stir in the date mixture.
5. 🌼 Stir the vanilla and half of the chocolate chips into the date mixture.
6. 🧈 Spread in a greased 15x10x1-inch pan.
7. 🍫 Top with the remaining chocolate chips and nuts.
8. 🔥 Bake in a 350°F (175°C) oven for about 30 minutes.
9. 🧊 Cool.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: family recipe from an annual holiday cooking pamphlet from a Madison, Wisconsin power company, from the late 1960s. The family makes it every Christmas.
- 🔁 The family has always used a 13x9-inch pan instead of the 15x10x1-inch pan. The source does not give a bake time for that pan.
- 💬 The source says this recipe is "all over the internet almost word for word."
