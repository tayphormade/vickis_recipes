# Valentine's Day Meringue Kisses

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 50 meringue kisses
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 20 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 2 hours
- 🕑 **Total time:** 2 hours 20 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 200°F (93°C)
- 📊 **Per meringue kiss:** 17 calories; 0 g fat; 0 g fiber; 3.7 g sugar; 0.2 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥚 3 large egg whites, room temperature
- 🍬 180 g superfine sugar
- 🫙 Pinch of cream of tartar
- 🍇 2 Tbsp organic pomegranate powder

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 200°F (93°C).
2. 🥚 Place the egg whites in a large, clean mixing bowl, and add the cream of tartar.
3. 🥣 Whip the whites on medium speed until foamy and just beginning to turn white.
4. 🍬 While continuing to whip, add the sugar very slowly (about 1 tsp at a time).
5. 🍇 Stir in the pomegranate powder.
6. 🥣 Turn the mixer up to high speed and whip until the meringue is glossy and very stiff.
7. 🧁 Transfer the meringue to a clean piping bag fitted with a star tip, and pipe 1-inch-diameter kisses onto a parchment-lined baking sheet.
8. ⏲️ Bake the meringues in the warm oven for 2 hours, or until light and crisp (do not brown).
9. 🧊 Cool, then store in an airtight container.
