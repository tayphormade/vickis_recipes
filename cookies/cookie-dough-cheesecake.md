# Cookie Dough Cheesecake

- 🛠️ **Pan:** 10-inch spring-form pan (per the plain cheesecake recipe in the source)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 325°F (163°C) for the cheesecake, bake 50 minutes or until a knife comes out fairly clean (from the plain cheesecake recipe included in the source)
- ❄️ **Freeze:** cookie dough balls 45 minutes, or until completely frozen
- ❄️ **Chill:** cheesecake at least 3 hours

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Cookie dough

- 🧈 1/2 cup butter
- 🟤 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla
- 🌾 2 cups flour
- 🥛 1 (14 oz) can sweetened condensed milk
- 🍫 1 cup chocolate chips

### Plain cheesecake

- 🥚 5 eggs
- 🍬 1 cup sugar
- 🧀 3 8-oz cream cheese, softened
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla
- 🥣 1/2 cup sour cream

### Crust

- 🍬 1/3 cup sugar
- 🌾 1 tsp flour
- 🧈 1/3 cup butter, softened
- 🍪 12-14 (about 1 1/2 cups) graham crackers, crumbled, or 1 1/2 cups chocolate wafers, crushed

### To finish

- 🌰 Chopped walnuts
- 🍫 More chocolate chips
- 🥣 Whipped cream, for the edge

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Prepare the cookie dough: in an electric mixer, beat together the butter and sugar. Add the vanilla. With the mixer on low, slowly add the flour until completely incorporated. It will be very dry. Beat in the milk until well combined. Stir in the chocolate chips. Roll into small 1-inch balls. Place in the freezer for 45 minutes, or until completely frozen.
2. 🍰 Use your favorite plain cheesecake recipe with a chocolate cookie crumb (the source's plain cheesecake and crust are listed above). Mix half of the cookie dough balls into the cheesecake batter, saving the rest for another cheesecake. Bake as directed, with chopped walnuts and more chocolate chips on top.
3. ❄️ When cool, refrigerate at least 3 hours, then edge the cake with swirled dollops of whipped cream and serve.
