# Vanilla Meringue Cookies

- 🍽️ **Yield:** about 30
- 🕑 **Total time:** 2 hours 30 minutes (plus cooling)
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 225°F (107°C)
- 🧰 **Special equipment:** pastry bag and an Ateco #848 or other large star tip

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥚 5 large egg whites, room temperature
- 🫙 ¼ tsp. cream of tartar
- 🧂 ¼ tsp. Diamond Crystal or Morton kosher salt
- 🍬 ¾ cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- ❄️ ¾ cup (83 g) powdered sugar
- 🌼 1 tsp. vanilla bean paste or vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Place racks in the upper and lower thirds of the oven, and preheat to 225°F (107°C). Place 5 large egg whites, room temperature, in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment. Sprinkle ¼ tsp. cream of tartar and ¼ tsp. Diamond Crystal or Morton kosher salt over them and let sit 5 minutes.
2. 🥣 Meanwhile, sift ¾ cup (150 g) granulated sugar and ¾ cup (83 g) powdered sugar into a medium bowl and whisk to combine.
3. 🥚 Beat the egg white mixture on medium speed until foamy, 30–60 seconds. With the motor running, add the sugar mixture 1 Tbsp. at a time, incorporating each addition completely before adding more and scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed, 6–8 minutes total. Increase speed to high and beat until glossy stiff peaks form (the meringue should look billowy), about 2 minutes. Stiff peaks are important here, as the meringue will deflate slightly when piped; the cream of tartar will prevent overbeating. Reduce speed to medium, add 1 tsp. vanilla bean paste or vanilla extract, and beat until just incorporated.
4. 🧁 Place the pastry bag fitted with the tip into a tall glass or vase, and fold the top of the bag over the rim of the glass to hold it in place. Fill the bag with meringue, then bring the sides together and twist to close. Dab a little meringue in each corner of a baking sheet and line it with parchment paper; lightly press the meringue dabs to adhere the parchment to the sheet. Holding the pastry bag vertically about 1 inch above the baking sheet, work in an upward motion and twist at the base to create a swirled shape, piping the meringue into a 2-inch-wide star. Repeat to fill the baking sheet, spacing the meringues about 1 inch apart. Repeat with another baking sheet, refilling the bag as needed.
5. ⏲️ Bake the meringues until crisp on the outside and shiny (but not sticky or tacky), 2 hours to 2 hours and 45 minutes. To check if they're ready, lift a meringue off the baking sheet: it should be light, lift off the sheet cleanly, and sound hollow when tapped on the bottom. If the meringues are not done after 2 hours, keep baking, checking every 20 minutes.
6. 🧊 Turn off the oven and leave the door slightly ajar (prop it open with a wooden spoon if needed). Let the meringues cool in the oven for at least 1 hour and up to 12 hours.
