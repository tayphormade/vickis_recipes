# DoubleTree Chocolate Chip Cookies

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 26 cookies (about 60 g each)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 20 to 23 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🧈 Dough

- 🧈 ½ pound (2 sticks) butter, softened
- 🍬 ¾ cup + 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 🍬 ¾ cup packed light brown sugar
- 🥚 2 large eggs
- 🌼 1¼ teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🍋 ¼ teaspoon freshly squeezed lemon juice

### 🌾 Dry ingredients

- 🌾 2¼ cups flour
- 🥣 ½ cup rolled oats
- 🥄 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🧂 1 teaspoon salt
- 🍂 Pinch of cinnamon

### 🍫 Mix-ins

- 🌰 1¾ cups chopped walnuts
- 🍫 2⅔ cups Nestlé Toll House semi-sweet chocolate chips

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Cream the butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar in a stand mixer on medium speed for about 2 minutes.
2. 🥚 Add the eggs, vanilla, and lemon juice. Blend on low for 30 seconds, then on medium for about 2 minutes, until light and fluffy. Scrape down the bowl.
3. 🌾 On low speed, add the flour, oats, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon. Blend for about 45 seconds. Don't overmix.
4. 🍫 Remove the bowl from the mixer. Stir in the chocolate chips and walnuts.
5. ⚪ Portion the dough with a scoop (about 3 tablespoons, 60 g each) onto a parchment-lined baking sheet, about 2 inches apart.
6. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 300°F (150°C). Bake for 20 to 23 minutes, until the edges are golden brown and the centers are still soft.
7. 🧊 Cool on the baking sheet for about 1 hour.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 You can freeze the unbaked cookies. Don't thaw them. Bake them at 300°F, until the edges are golden brown and the centers are still soft.
