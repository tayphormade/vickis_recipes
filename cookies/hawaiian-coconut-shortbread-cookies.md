# Hawaiian Coconut Shortbread Cookies

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 36 cookies
- 🕑 **Ready in:** 1 hour 5 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 300°F (150°C)
- 📊 **Per cookie** (23 g): 115 calories; 7 g fat (4.9 g saturated); 13.6 mg cholesterol; 74.9 mg sodium; 12.5 g carbohydrate (0.4 g fiber, 6.9 g sugars); 0.9 g protein

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧈 1 cup butter, softened (8 ounces)
- 🍬 ¼ cup granulated sugar
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour (measured after sifting)
- 🧂 ¼ teaspoon salt
- 🥥 2 cups sweetened flaked coconut
- ❄️ 1 cup powdered sugar, sifted (or more as needed, for coating after baking)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Cream the butter until smooth. Add the granulated sugar and vanilla, and cream again until smooth and slightly fluffy.
2. 🌾 Sift the flour, then measure out 2 cups. Add the salt to the flour and sift again.
3. 🥣 Gradually add the flour to the creamed butter mixture until all the flour is added and the mixture is smooth. Mix the coconut into the cookie dough.
4. 🧊 Shape the dough into a roll about 1½ inches in diameter and wrap it in plastic wrap. Refrigerate until the dough is firm, about 2 to 4 hours, or for several days until ready to use. It may be frozen for later baking.
5. 🔪 Using a sharp knife, cut ¼-inch slices from the dough roll and place them on a very lightly greased cookie sheet, about 1 inch apart.
6. 🔥 Bake at 300°F (150°C) for about 20 minutes, until light golden brown at the edges but not browned entirely across the top.
7. ⏳ Cool the cookies on the sheet just until they are set, usually about 2 to 3 minutes. Remove them with a spatula and place them on the 1 cup of sifted powdered sugar, spread on wax paper or a similar surface.
8. ❄️ Cover the cookies with powdered sugar on all sides, by sifting more sugar over them or "rolling" them in the sugar. After they have cooled and the powdered sugar has set, you can coat them with more powdered sugar if you like. Cool completely, then store in an airtight container for however long you can keep them from being eaten!

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📚 Source: Genius Kitchen, "Hawaiian Coconut Shortbread Cookies," recipe by Ismy Echo. Printed 4/1/2019.
- 📖 Chef's note: the recipe is from an old Sunset Desserts Cookbook, likely from the 1970s. The author lost the book in a move and found a handwritten copy of the recipe in their mother's cookbook.
- 🎁 The source says the cookies are good for make-ahead baking and care packages, if cushioned and packed securely, and that they improve in flavor as they mature.
- 🔁 Variations suggested in the source: toast some of the coconut before adding it to the dough, or add finely chopped macadamia nuts, ginger, cardamom, or any citrus zest. The source suggests key lime zest and macadamias as a combination.
