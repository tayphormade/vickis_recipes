# Cake Pops

🔗 **Source:** [Preppy Kitchen](https://preppykitchen.com/cake-pops/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 38 (one pop each)
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- 🔥 **Cook:** 25 minutes
- ❄️ **Chill:** 4 hours
- ⏱️ **Total:** 4 hours 55 minutes
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 137 kcal; carbohydrates 20g; protein 1g; fat 6g; saturated fat 2g; sugar 14g; sodium 115mg

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🎂 1 box vanilla cake mix (15.25-ounce)
- 💧 1 cup water
- 🛢️ 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 🥚 3 eggs
- 🥣 1 cup frosting
- 🍫 12 ounces candy melts or chocolate melting wafers
- 🥢 38 lollipop sticks
- 🎉 Sprinkles (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C). Butter a 13x9-inch cake pan.
2. 🥣 In a large bowl, whisk together the cake mix, water, oil, and eggs until combined. Pour into the cake pan.
3. 🎂 Bake according to package directions. Cool completely.
4. 📄 Line a small baking sheet or plate with wax or parchment paper.
5. 🥄 Crumble the cooled cake into a large bowl. Add the frosting and stir with a wooden spoon, or beat on low speed, until the mixture resembles wet sand but is not smooth (about 30 seconds if using an electric mixer). Scoop 1 tablespoon of the cake mixture and roll it into 1-inch balls. Place on the lined plate. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or freeze for 30 minutes.
6. 🍫 Melt the candy or chocolate wafers according to the package instructions, and place in a 2-cup liquid measuring cup or tall drinking glass. Pull a few cake balls out, leaving the others in the fridge as you work so they stay cold (keeping them cold helps the coating set quickly). Dip the tip of a lollipop stick in the melted chocolate and insert it about halfway into a cake ball. Dip the cake ball into the chocolate, making sure to cover the base where the stick and cake ball meet, and turn to coat. Remove and gently tap on the side of the cup to let excess coating drip off.
7. ✨ Decorate with sprinkles if desired. Place upright in a styrofoam block or cardboard box. Let stand until the coating is set. Cake pops should be stored in the fridge for up to 1 week.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🎨 To make them more colorful, add gel food coloring to the candy melts. Gel is more concentrated, so add less; liquid food coloring may thin the frosting if you add too much.
- ❄️ Let the cake cool completely before crumbling it. If the cake is too warm, it keeps the frosting that binds it warm, making the pops difficult to hold together. If you have time, make the cake a day ahead so it has time to cool.
- 💡 A moist cake and frosting are hard to roll perfectly round. Allow the mixture time to chill before rolling.
- 🍰 The finer you make the cake crumbs, the more evenly they roll and hold their shape.
- 🧊 To hold the pops upright, poke tiny holes in a styrofoam block or the bottom of a 4-5 inch deep cardboard box. Insert the end of the lollipop stick once the pop is coated and decorated. Let stand until set. They can then go in plastic wrappers or jars to stay upright. Without styrofoam, a jar filled with dried beans works.
- 📄 Alternatively, lay the cake balls cake-ball side down on a wax- or parchment-lined baking tray to dry. This gives them a flat side but makes them easier to transport.
- 🎂 This recipe works with almost any classic cake mix flavor and complementary frosting. Bake your favorite flavor in a 13x9-inch sheet and proceed as instructed. The source mentions a separate Pumpkin Cake Pops variation.
