# Texas Sheet Cake

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 20
- 🔥 **Oven:** 375°F (191°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 22 minutes, or until a wooden toothpick comes out clean
- 🛠️ **Pan:** 13 x 9-inch

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Cake

- 🧴 Cooking spray
- 🌾 2 teaspoons all-purpose flour (for dusting the pan)
- 🌾 2 cups all-purpose flour (about 9 ounces; high altitude: 10 ounces, 272 g)
- 🍬 2 cups granulated sugar
- 🫧 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🍂 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🧂 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 💧 3/4 cup water
- 🧈 1/2 cup butter
- 🍫 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa
- 🥛 1/2 cup low-fat buttermilk
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥚 2 large eggs

### Icing

- 🧈 6 tablespoons butter
- 🥛 1/3 cup fat-free milk
- 🍫 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa
- ✨ 3 cups powdered sugar
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 🌰 1/4 cup chopped pecans, toasted

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 375°F (191°C).
2. 🧴 Coat a 13 x 9-inch pan with cooking spray or butter, and dust with 2 teaspoons of flour. Set aside.
3. 🥄 Lightly spoon 2 cups of flour into dry measuring cups; level with a knife. Combine the 2 cups of flour and the next 4 ingredients (through the salt) in a large bowl, stirring well with a whisk.
4. 💧 Combine the 3/4 cup water, 1/2 cup butter, and 1/4 cup of cocoa in a small saucepan; bring to a boil, stirring frequently. Add to the flour mixture. Beat at medium speed with an electric mixer until well blended.
5. 🥚 Add the buttermilk, 1 teaspoon vanilla, and eggs; beat well. Pour the batter into the prepared pan.
6. ⏲️ Bake at 375°F (191°C) for 22 minutes or until a wooden toothpick comes out clean. Place on a wire rack.
7. 🍫 Combine the 6 tablespoons butter, milk, and remaining 1/4 cup cocoa in a saucepan; bring to a boil, stirring constantly. Remove from heat. Gradually stir in the powdered sugar and the remaining 2 teaspoons vanilla.
8. 🌰 Spread the icing over the hot cake and sprinkle with pecans. Cool completely on a wire rack.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🏔️ High altitude: use 10 ounces (272 g) of flour instead of 9 ounces.
