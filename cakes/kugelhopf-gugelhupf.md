# Kugelhopf (Gugelhupf)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 1 Gugelhupf (22 cm mold)
- 🕒 **Rise:** pre-ferment 30 minutes and main dough 60 minutes (fresh yeast; the pre-ferment is skipped with dry yeast); filled dough until double, about 45 minutes
- 🔥 **Oven:** 160°C (320°F) fan (Umluft), 60 minutes. The source also gives 180°C (356°F) conventional heat, about 55 minutes.

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 550g flour
- 🥛 250ml warm milk
- 🍞 1 pkg. dry yeast (Trockenhefe)
- 🧈 250g butter, room temperature
- 🍬 50g sugar
- 🥚 3 eggs
- 🧂 Pinch of salt
- 🍇 150g rum-soaked raisins (candied lemon and/or orange may be included)
- 🌰 100g slivered almonds (Mandelstifte)
- ✨ Powdered sugar, for dusting

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥛 Mix the yeast into the warm milk to proof. Mix in the eggs one at a time.
2. 🥣 In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, and salt. Mix in the room-temperature butter until a fine crumb forms.
3. 🥄 Mix in the milk mixture until it forms a soft dough that just doesn't stick to your fingers (add a couple of tablespoons of flour if too sticky).
4. ⏳ Sprinkle the top of the dough with flour and let rise until double.
5. 🌾 On a lightly floured surface, roll out the dough to a 30 cm x 40 cm rectangle.
6. 🍇 Sprinkle with the rum-soaked raisins and candied fruit, then the slivered almonds.
7. 🧻 Roll the dough up along the long side. Place it in a well-buttered Gugelhupf mold (or a bundt cake mold if you must), meeting the ends to make a circle.
8. ⏳ Let rise until double, about 45 minutes.
9. 🔥 Bake at 160°C (320°F) fan (Umluft) for 60 minutes.
10. ❄️ Let the cake cool about 10 minutes, then turn it out to cool. When cool, sprinkle with powdered sugar.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍇 Soak the raisins overnight in a rum, water, and sugar mixture (75 ml rum, 75 ml water, 50g sugar, 150g raisins; the source lists this as the filling).
- 🧈 The second version of the recipe in the source (in German) uses 500g flour, 250 ml warm milk, 1 cube fresh yeast (or 7g dry yeast), 250g soft butter, 50g sugar, 3 eggs, and a pinch of salt for the yeast dough, plus 100g almonds (Mandelstifte) in the filling.
- ⏳ Keep the rising times: 30 minutes for the pre-ferment, 60 minutes for the main dough, and 45 minutes for the filled dough. Pre-ferment is skipped with dry yeast.
- 🧂 If the dough is too sticky while kneading, add flour until it can be rolled out without problems.
- 📏 Roll the dough to about 40 x 30 cm.
- 🍽️ Best served the same day. Dust with powdered sugar.
- 🛠️ Equipment: 22 cm Gugelhupf mold, greased.
