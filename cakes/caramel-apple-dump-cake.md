# Caramel Apple Dump Cake

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- 🥧 **Yield:** 1 apple dump cake
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 10 minutes
- 🔥 **Cook:** 50 minutes at 350°F (175°C)
- ⏳ **Additional time:** 15 minutes
- ⏱️ **Total:** 1 hour 15 minutes
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 486 calories; fat 23g; saturated fat 11g; cholesterol 41mg; sodium 532mg; carbohydrates 69g; fiber 2g; sugars 47g; protein 4g

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🧴 Cooking spray
- 🍎 2 (21 ounce) cans apple pie filling
- 🍯 1/2 cup caramel sauce
- 🎂 1 (15.25 ounce) package spice cake mix
- 🧈 1 cup salted butter, melted
- 🌰 1 cup coarsely chopped walnuts

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking pan with cooking spray.
2. 🥄 Spread the apple pie filling in an even layer on the bottom of the pan. Drizzle the caramel sauce evenly over the apples. Sprinkle the cake mix evenly over the apples and caramel. Drizzle the melted butter evenly over the cake mix. Sprinkle evenly with the chopped walnuts.
3. ⏲️ Bake in the preheated oven until the cake is golden brown and the filling is bubbling through to the top, 50 to 55 minutes. Let cool at least 15 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍦 The source suggests serving it with vanilla ice cream, topped with more of the caramel sauce.
