# Peachberry Buckle

🔗 **Source:** [King Arthur Flour](http://www.kingarthurflour.com/recipes/PrintRecipe?RID=4957&radio=1)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 9 to 12 servings
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 to 25 minutes
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏱️ **Bake:** 50 to 60 minutes
- ⌛ **Total:** 1 hour 5 minutes to 1 hour 25 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Cake

- 🧈 ¼ cup (4 tablespoons) butter
- 🍬 ¾ cup sugar
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥄 1½ teaspoons baking powder
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🍂 ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
- 🌾 2 cups King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 🥛 ½ cup milk

### Filling

- 🍓 1 cup berries, chopped if large (e.g., strawberries)
- 🍑 1 cup sliced peaches, peeled or not, your choice

### Topping

- 🧈 ¼ cup (4 tablespoons) butter
- 🍬 ¼ cup brown sugar
- 🌾 ½ cup King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 🍂 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🌰 ½ teaspoon almond extract
- 🧂 Pinch of salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a 9-inch round cake pan.
2. 🥣 To make the cake: In a medium-sized mixing bowl, beat together the butter and sugar.
3. 🥚 Beat in the egg and vanilla.
4. 🥄 Stir in the baking powder, salt, and nutmeg.
5. 🌾 Add the flour in three additions, stirring it in alternately with the milk, beginning and ending with the flour. Set the batter aside while you make the topping.
6. 🍂 To make the topping: Mix all of the ingredients until crumbly. Don't overmix, or you'll get a smooth paste rather than crumbs.
7. 🍑 To assemble the buckle: Spread half the batter (about 10 ounces) in the prepared pan. Layer with the peach slices.
8. 🫐 Fold the blueberries into the remaining batter, and dollop it on top; a tablespoon cookie scoop works well here.
9. 🥄 Sprinkle the topping over the batter.
10. ⏱️ Bake the buckle for 50 minutes to 1 hour, or until it's a deep golden brown and a cake tester or toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
11. 🧊 Remove the buckle from the oven and cool it on a rack. Serve warm, or at room temperature. Store at room temperature, wrapped in plastic, for several days; freeze for longer storage.
