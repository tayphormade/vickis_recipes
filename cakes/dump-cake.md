# Dump Cake

- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (177°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 45 minutes, or until done
- 🛠️ **Pan:** lightly greased 13 x 9-inch pan

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🥫 1 can fruit pie filling (21 oz. usually)
- 🎂 1 box of cake mix (usually anywhere from 18.5 to 15.25 oz)
- 🧈 12 tbsp (1 1/2 sticks) butter, cut in small pieces or melted

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C).
2. 🥄 In a lightly greased 13 x 9-inch pan, spread the fruit pie filling on the bottom evenly.
3. 🎂 Sprinkle the cake mix over the fruit, covering it completely and evenly.
4. 🧈 Sprinkle pieces of butter over the cake mix (or pour the melted butter over it).
5. ⏲️ Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes, or until done.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍰 **Combinations**
  - 🍒 Yellow or chocolate cake mix with cherry pie filling (optional: add crushed pineapple on top of the cherries, even coconut flakes on top)
  - 🍏 Yellow cake mix with apple pie filling (add nuts, cinnamon, etc.)
  - 🍑 Yellow cake mix with peach pie filling, blueberry pie filling, raspberry, blackberry, or grape pie filling
  - 🍓 White cake mix with strawberry or strawberry/rhubarb
