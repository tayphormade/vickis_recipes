# New York Crumb Cake

🔗 **Source:** [Sip and Feast](https://www.sipandfeast.com/wprm_print/new-york-crumb-cake)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 15 large pieces
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 20 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** 15 minutes (cake base), then 25-30 minutes (with crumb topping) at 350°F (177°C)
- 🧊 **Cooling:** 10 minutes before topping
- ⏱️ **Total:** 1 hour 10 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crumb topping

- 🧈 1 1/2 cups (339g) unsalted butter, melted
- 🌾 4 1/2 cups (585g) all-purpose flour
- 🧂 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 🟤 1 packed cup (200g) dark brown sugar
- 🍬 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 🍂 1 1/2 tablespoons (12g) cinnamon

### Cake

- 🌾 2 1/2 cups (325g) cake flour, spooned and leveled (can sub all-purpose flour)
- 🫧 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 🫧 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 🧂 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 🍬 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 🧈 11 tablespoons (156g) unsalted butter, room temperature
- 🥛 3/4 cup (168g) buttermilk, room temperature
- 🥚 3 large eggs, room temperature
- 🥚 1 egg yolk, room temperature
- 🌼 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- ✨ Powdered sugar, for dusting

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Crumb topping: in a large bowl, combine the flour, dark brown sugar, granulated sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
2. 🧈 Pour the melted butter into the bowl and toss with your hands to make large crumbs. Do not overmix! If the crumbs are too small, make larger crumbs by squeezing some of the smaller ones together. If the crumbs are too wet, add 1 tablespoon of flour at a time and gently mix with your hands to form larger crumbs.
3. 🥄 Set the crumb mixture aside.
4. 🔥 Cake: preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and set the rack to the middle level. Line a 9x13 inch cake pan with parchment paper, or grease it, and set aside.
5. 🥣 In a large bowl, whisk together the cake flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
6. 🥚 In a separate bowl, or in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, beat together the sugar and butter until smooth, then add the eggs and extra yolk, one at a time, and beat until combined.
7. 🥛 Add the buttermilk and vanilla, and mix until smooth. Do not worry if the mixture looks curdled.
8. 🌾 Add the flour mixture to the egg mixture and mix for 30 seconds, or until just combined. Scrape down the sides with a spatula as needed. Avoid overmixing.
9. 🍰 Pour the cake batter into the prepared pan and bake for 15 minutes, or until the corners are set. The cake will not be fully cooked.
10. ❄️ Allow the cake to cool for 10-15 minutes before topping.
11. 🥄 Top the cake with the crumb mixture. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean after inserting it into the center of the cake.
12. 🧊 Place the cake on a wire rack to cool, and once cool, dust with powdered sugar.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍰 Makes 15 large pieces, but can be cut into much smaller pieces if you like.
- 🔥 The recipe was written for a conventional oven. For convection ovens, reduce the temperature by 25°F (about 14°C) and begin checking for doneness at the 75% mark.
- 🥧 The recipe is written for a 9x13 cake pan. For a "New York bakery style" cake, bake it in a half sheet pan (13x18) by doubling the recipe.
- 🗄️ Leftover crumb cake can be stored covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
- 📖 Source: Sip and Feast (James Delmage and Tara).
