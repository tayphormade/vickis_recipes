# Fresh Strawberry Cake

🔗 **Source:** [Divas Can Cook](https://divascancook.com/fresh-strawberry-cake-recipe/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 40 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** 20-22 minutes at 350°F (177°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Strawberry cake

- 🍓 1 pound fresh strawberries, stemmed
- 🌾 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (measured correctly; see notes)
- 🫧 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 🧂 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 🧈 1/2 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 🛢️ 1/2 cup butter-flavored shortening
- 🍬 2 cups granulated sugar
- 🥚 3 large eggs, room temperature
- 🍓 1 tablespoon pure strawberry extract
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥣 1/2 cup sour cream
- 🔴 6-9 drops red food coloring
- 🥛 3/4 cup whole milk

### Strawberries and cream frosting

- 🍓 1 pound fresh strawberries, stemmed
- 🧈 1/2 cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 🧀 2 oz cream cheese, room temperature
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🍓 1/2 teaspoon pure strawberry extract
- 🧂 1 pinch salt
- 🍬 4 cups powdered sugar (sifted and measured correctly; see notes)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Cake: preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C). Grease and flour 3 (9-inch) round cake pans. Set aside.
2. 🍓 Make the strawberry reduction: place the strawberries in a blender and blend until pureed. Pour the puree into a saucepan and simmer for about 20 minutes, until most of the juice evaporates and you have about 1/2 cup of strawberries. Set aside to cool.
3. 🥣 In a bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
4. 🧈 In a large bowl, cream together the butter, shortening, and sugar. Mix in the eggs one at a time until combined. Mix in the strawberry extract and vanilla extract. Stir in the sour cream. (The batter may look spotty, and that is fine.)
5. 🔴 Add the food coloring. (The cake will be a VERY pale pink if you do not use food coloring. Start with 5-6 drops and increase after you add the puree in the next step if you want it darker.)
6. 🥛 Add the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients, alternating with the milk, until the mixture is well combined. (Be careful not to overmix, or the cake will be crumbly.)
7. 🍓 Fold in the strawberry reduction. Mix thoroughly so there are no streaks in the batter. (Add more food coloring if you want it darker. In the video, the author used 8 drops of McCormick liquid food coloring. Some brands are not as concentrated, so you may need more or less.)
8. 🥄 Pour the batter evenly into the prepared pans.
9. ⏲️ Bake for 20-22 minutes (may need longer) or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with moist crumbs clinging to it.
10. 🧊 Place the cakes on a cooling rack. When the pans are cool enough to touch, remove the cakes and allow them to finish cooling on the wire rack.
11. 🍓 Frosting: follow step 2 above to make the strawberry reduction, reducing it down to 1/4 cup this time. Set aside.
12. 🧈 In a large bowl, cream together the butter, cream cheese, vanilla extract, and strawberry extract until combined. (Use a stand mixer if desired.)
13. 🍬 Mix in the salt and powdered sugar a little at a time until creamy.
14. 🍓 Add the strawberry puree by the tablespoon until the desired firmness is reached. (The author uses all 1/4 cup.)
15. ❄️ Chill the frosting, then mix until fluffy before frosting the cakes. (The frosting should be extra airy and fluffy. See notes.)
16. 🔪 Frost the cake layers using an offset spatula. (This frosting is rich with bold strawberry flavor. A little goes a long way, so use thin layers of frosting between the cakes.)
17. 🍰 Slice with a cake knife and serve. (Chill the cake if you wish to firm up the frosting before slicing.)
18. 🫙 Store the finished cake in the refrigerator, and let it come to room temperature before serving, for best results. (The strawberry flavor increases the next day, so the author usually makes this cake the day before. It is still great the day it is made.)

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📏 Measuring flour and powdered sugar: first fluff with a spoon or sift. Spoon into the measuring cup until overflowing. Tap the rim of the measuring cup to knock out any air bubbles. Level off the top with the back of a knife.
- 🥄 Frosting: mix on high until super fluffy and airy. If the frosting is thick and heavy, it has not been mixed enough. It will not look like enough frosting to frost the cakes, but it will be.
