# Super Easy King Cake (WOW GOOD!)

🔗 **Source:** [Southern Plate](http://www.southernplate.com/2011/02/super-easy-king-cake-wow-good.html)

- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (177°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 30-35 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cook time / total time:** 35 minutes each (per source)
- 🕒 **Rise:** 45 minutes to 1 hour, in a warm place

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### King cake

- 🍞 1 roll frozen bread dough, thawed
- 🧀 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
- ✨ 1 cup powdered sugar
- 🍋 1 teaspoon lemon extract
- 🧴 Cooking spray

### Icing

- ✨ 1 1/2 cups confectioner's sugar
- 🥛 3 tablespoons milk (more if needed, but stir this amount first)
- 🌼 1 tsp clear butter or vanilla flavoring

### Topping

- 🎉 Colored sprinkles (optional)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🌾 On a floured surface, roll out the dough to a rectangle of about 10 x 18 inches. It doesn't have to be exact; close enough works!
2. 🥣 In a mixing bowl, combine the cream cheese, powdered sugar, and extract, and mix until creamy and well blended. Spread over the dough to within an inch of each side. Roll up lengthwise to make a long log, and brush the final edge with water before pressing it into the log to seal.
3. ⭕ Take the log and form it into a circle, pressing the ends into each other and brushing with water if needed to seal together. Spray the top lightly with cooking spray and cover with plastic wrap. Set in a warm place to rise for 45 minutes to an hour.
4. 🔥 Remove the plastic wrap and bake in a 350°F (177°C) oven for 30-35 minutes. Allow to cool.
5. 🍬 Mix the confectioner's glaze and drizzle it over the top of the cake with a spoon. Immediately sprinkle colored sprinkles over the glaze if desired. Allow to sit until the glaze hardens.
