# Peach Cobbler Cupcakes

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 12 cupcakes (cupcake liners or tins, half full)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- ⏲️ **Bake:** about 25 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 1½ cups flour
- 🥄 1¼ teaspoons baking powder
- 🥄 ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- 🧈 ½ cup unsalted butter, room temperature
- 🍬 ¾ cup brown sugar
- 🍬 ¼ cup white sugar
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🥛 ½ cup sour cream
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 🍑 1¼ cups ripe peaches, peeled and roughly chopped (about 2 medium to large)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Whisk the flour, baking powder, and baking soda together in a bowl with a fork.
2. 🧈 Beat the butter and sugars in a large bowl for 1 minute, until light and fluffy.
3. 🥚 Beat in the eggs, sour cream, and vanilla until blended.
4. 🌾 Mix in the flour mixture.
5. 🍑 Fold in the peaches.
6. ⚪ Fill the cupcake liners or tins half full.
7. 🔥 Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 25 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Optional: frost lightly with cream cheese icing.
