# Discover the French Kings' Cake

🔗 **Source:** [Denver Cooking Examiner](http://www.examiner.com/cooking-in-denver/discover-the-french-kings-cake?render=print)

- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (177°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 40 minutes, or until golden brown
- ❄️ **Chill:** pastry cream at least 2 hours; assembled cake 30 minutes before baking
- 🧊 **Cool:** 20 minutes before eating

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Pastry cream

- 🥛 1 cup milk
- 🥚 2 eggs
- 🌾 1/4 cup flour
- 🍬 1/3 cup sugar

### Almond paste

- 🧈 4 Tbsp melted butter
- 🌰 1 1/2 cups ground almond
- 🥚 4 egg yolks
- 🍬 1/2 cup sugar

### Assembly

- 🥐 2 puff pastry sheets, defrosted and rolled out
- 🥚 1 egg yolk and an ounce of water, for egg wash

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥛 Pastry cream: bring the milk to a boil. In a medium bowl, whip the eggs with the 1/3 cup sugar, then add the flour and the boiling milk. Return to the pan and cook for three minutes on low heat, mixing constantly until very thick. Pour into a dish lined with plastic wrap. Wrap the cream, let it set for 15 minutes, then refrigerate for at least two hours.
2. 🌰 Almond paste: whip the egg yolks with the sugar, add the almond powder and the melted butter, and mix well. When the cream is cold, mix it up with the almond paste and fill a pastry bag with the mix.
3. 🔥 Building the pie: preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C) and place the first layer of pastry on a baking sheet.
4. 🥚 Whip the egg yolk with an ounce of water. Brush the egg on the edge of the first layer of pastry. Fill with the cream, starting in the center and working in a circular motion, until the cream is an inch from the edge. Place the second layer on top of the filling and press the edges firmly together so no filling escapes. With a knife, mark the edges of the pastry with little dents, going one direction and then the other (to form little "X" shapes all around the edge). Brush the top with egg mixture and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
5. 🖌️ Brush the top a second time and decorate with a knife.
6. ⏲️ Bake for 40 minutes or until golden brown.
7. ❄️ Cool for 20 minutes before eating.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 💡 Let the cream rest a long time in the refrigerator. The more solid the cream, the better the filling will be (and less runny).
- ⚠️ Depending on how large the pie is, there may be too much filling. Do not overfill, or the filling will overflow in the oven and the pastry will not rise.
- 🌰 You can use store-bought almond powder, or blanch the almonds and process them in a grinder.
- 📦 Wrap leftovers in foil and leave them at room temperature, because refrigeration dries out the crust.
- 📖 The source article says the recipe comes from "L'atelier des chef," a French cooking school. The author, Annabelle Denmark (Denver Cooking Examiner), translated, adapted, and tested it using metric measurements from the original.
