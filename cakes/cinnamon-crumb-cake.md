# Cinnamon Crumb Cake

- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** 30 minutes at 350°F (177°C)
- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 1 1/4 cups (6 1/4 oz./170g) all-purpose flour
- 🍬 2/3 cup packed brown sugar
- 🍂 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🧂 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 🧈 1/4 cup chilled stick butter, cut into small pieces
- 🫧 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 🫧 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 🥛 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🥚 1 large egg

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C).
2. 🥣 Combine the flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt in a bowl; cut in the butter until the mixture resembles coarse meal. Reserve 1/2 cup of the flour mixture for the topping; set aside.
3. 🥄 Combine the remaining flour mixture, baking powder, and baking soda; add the buttermilk, vanilla, and egg to the flour mixture. Beat at medium speed of a mixer until blended.
4. 🍰 Spoon the batter into a greased 8-inch round cake pan. Sharply tap the pan once on the counter to remove air bubbles. Sprinkle the reserved 1/2 cup flour mixture over the batter.
5. ⏲️ Bake at 350°F (177°C) for 30 minutes or until the cake springs back when touched lightly in the center. Cool on a wire rack.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍰 Bake in one greased 8-inch round cake pan.
