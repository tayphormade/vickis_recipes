# Chocolate Cake in a Mug

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 1 serving
- 🔥 **Cook:** microwave on high for 90 seconds
- ⏲️ **Active:** 10 minutes
- ⏱️ **Total:** 15 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 🍬 3 tablespoons sugar
- 🟤 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
- 🫧 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 🧂 Pinch of salt, optional
- 🥛 3 tablespoons milk
- 🛢️ 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 🌼 Splash vanilla extract, optional
- 🍫 3 tablespoons chocolate chips

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🛠️ Special equipment: a 12-ounce microwave-safe ceramic mug and a microwave oven.
2. 🥄 Put the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt (if using) in a 12-ounce microwave-safe ceramic mug. Blend thoroughly with a fork. Add the milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla (if using) and blend until smooth. Stir in the chocolate chips.
3. ⏲️ Microwave on high for 90 seconds. Do not overcook, or the cake will be dry. Let cool for 2 to 3 minutes before eating.
