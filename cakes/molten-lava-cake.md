# Molten Lava Cake

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 4 individual cakes
- 🔥 **Oven:** 450°F (232°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 8-10 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🍫 5 oz. dark chocolate
- 🧈 1 stick (1/2 cup) butter
- 🍬 3/4 cup sugar
- 🥚 4 whole eggs
- 🌾 1/2 cup flour
- 🌼 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 🟤 Cocoa, for coating the ramekins (amount not given)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 450°F (232°C).
2. 🧈 Butter four ramekins and coat them with cocoa, tapping out any excess cocoa.
3. 🫕 Over hot but not boiling water, melt the chocolate and butter until just melted. Remove from heat.
4. 🥣 Mix the eggs with the sugar until light. Add the vanilla. Add the chocolate mixture to the egg mixture slowly, so as not to cook the eggs. Add the flour and mix.
5. 🥄 Pour equally into the ramekins.
6. ⏲️ Place in the oven for 8-10 minutes.
7. 🍽️ When done, turn out onto serving plates (placing a plate over the ramekin and flipping over may help). Sprinkle with powdered sugar or cocoa, and add whipped cream or vanilla ice cream on the side.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🍦 Serve sprinkled with powdered sugar or cocoa, with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream on the side.
