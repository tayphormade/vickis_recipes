# Wild Blueberry Lemon Crumb Cakes

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** about 25 minutes at 375°F (190°C)
- ❄️ **Chill:** 30 minutes (crumb topping)
- ⏳ **Stand:** 20 minutes (lemon zest and sugar)
- 🧊 **Cool:** 30 minutes (in pan, then on wire rack; cool completely before serving)
- ⏱️ **Total:** 2 hours
- 🔢 **Per serving:** 242 calories; fat 13g; saturated fat 7g; cholesterol 62mg; sodium 211mg; carbohydrates 30g; fiber 1g; sugars 16g; protein 3g

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Crumb topping

- 🧈 1/4 cup unsalted butter, softened [59g]
- 🧂 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 🌾 1/3 cup all-purpose flour [45g]
- 🍬 1/4 cup white sugar
- 🟤 1 tablespoon brown sugar

### Batter

- 🌾 1 1/3 cups all-purpose flour [181g]
- 🧂 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 🫧 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 🫧 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 🍬 1/2 cup white sugar
- 🟤 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 🍋 2 generous tablespoons grated lemon zest
- 🧈 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened [117g]
- 🥚 2 large eggs
- 🌼 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🫙 2 tablespoons plain yogurt or milk
- 🍋 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 🫐 1/2 cup wild blueberries, thawed and drained [70g]

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Combine 1/4 cup butter, 1/8 teaspoon salt, 1/3 cup flour, 1/4 cup white sugar, and 1 tablespoon brown sugar in a small bowl. Mash together with the back of a spoon until uniformly combined.
2. ❄️ Chill in the refrigerator until firm, about 30 minutes. Once cold, crumble into pea-sized crumbs, and return to the refrigerator until needed.
3. 🥣 Whisk together 1 1/3 cups flour, 1 teaspoon salt, baking powder, and baking soda in a bowl; set aside.
4. 🍋 Add 1/2 cup white sugar, 2 tablespoons brown sugar, and lemon zest to a large bowl; stir and let sit for 20 to 30 minutes.
5. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 12-cup muffin tin or line the cups with paper liners.
6. 🥄 Add the butter to the bowl with the lemon zest. Beat with an electric hand mixer or whisk until light and fluffy. Beat in the eggs one at a time. Add the vanilla extract, yogurt, and lemon juice, and mix briefly to just combine. If the mixture separates, it will come back together when you add the dry ingredients.
7. 🌾 Add the flour mixture, and blend just until the flour disappears.
8. 🥄 Divide the batter equally between the prepared muffin cups. Top each cake with a heaping teaspoon of blueberries, and lightly press the berries down in the center. Divide the crumb mixture evenly over the cakes.
9. ⏲️ Bake until golden brown and a skewer inserted near the center comes out clean, about 25 minutes. Cool in the pan for 10 to 15 minutes before removing to a wire rack. Cool the crumb cakes completely before serving.
