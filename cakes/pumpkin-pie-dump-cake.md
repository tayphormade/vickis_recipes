# Pumpkin Pie Dump Cake

- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 45 to 50 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### 🎃 Pumpkin layer

- 🎃 1 can pumpkin
- 🥛 1 can evaporated milk
- 🍬 1 cup brown sugar
- 🥚 3 eggs
- 🍂 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
- 🌼 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

### 🍰 Top layer

- 🍰 1 spice cake box mix
- 🧈 2 sticks butter, melted
- 🌰 1 cup chopped pecans

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥄 Mix the pumpkin, evaporated milk, brown sugar, eggs, pumpkin pie spice, and vanilla until well mixed. Set aside.
2. 🧴 Spray a 9x13-inch baking pan with cooking spray, or coat it with butter.
3. 🎃 Pour the pumpkin mixture into the pan.
4. 🍰 Spread the dry spice cake mix evenly over the top.
5. 🧈 Pour the melted butter over the cake mix.
6. 🌰 Sprinkle the pecans over the top.
7. 🔥 Bake in a preheated 350°F (175°C) oven for 45 to 50 minutes.
