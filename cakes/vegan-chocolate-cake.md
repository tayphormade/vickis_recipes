# Vegan Chocolate Cake

🔗 **Source:** [Pick Up Limes](https://www.pickuplimes.com/recipe/easy-vegan-chocolate-cake-74/print)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- 🔥 **Bake:** 35 minutes at 350°F (180°C)
- ❄️ **Cool:** 30 minutes
- ⏱️ **Total:** 1 hour + 20 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Vegan buttermilk

- 🥛 1 cup (240 mL) unsweetened soy milk (see Notes)
- 🥄 1 Tbsp (15 mL) white vinegar

### Dry ingredients

- 🌾 2 cups (276 g) all-purpose flour
- 🍬 1 1/2 cups (336 g) granulated sugar
- 🟤 1 cup (106 g) Dutch processed cocoa powder
- 🫧 2 tsp (7 g) baking powder
- 🫧 1 1/2 tsp (8 g) baking soda
- 🧂 1/2 tsp salt

### Wet ingredients

- 🍎 3/4 cup (180 mL) unsweetened applesauce
- ☕ 1/2 cup (120 mL) brewed espresso
- 🥥 1/2 cup (109 g) refined coconut oil, melted (see Notes)
- 🌼 1/2 Tbsp (7 mL) pure vanilla extract

### Frosting

- 🧈 7.9 oz (224 g) unsalted vegan butter, at room temperature
- 🍬 2 1/4 cups (302 g) powdered sugar
- 🟤 3/4 cup (80 g) Dutch processed cocoa powder
- 🥛 1/3 cup (80 mL) unsweetened soy milk
- 🌼 1 tsp (5 mL) pure vanilla extract
- 🍫 1/3 cup (93 g) melted dairy-free dark chocolate

### Decoration ideas (optional)

- 🍫 Chocolate shavings
- 🌿 Fresh mint
- 🫐 Fresh mixed berries

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Lightly grease two 8-inch (20 cm) round cake pans with oil, and line the bottom of each with cut-out round pieces of parchment paper the size of the base of the pans.
2. 🥛 In a medium bowl, add the milk and vinegar and mix. Set aside. You will see it starts to curdle. This creates vegan buttermilk.
3. 🥣 Place a sieve over a large bowl, and sift all the dry ingredients through it. Mix together, and then create a shallow well in the middle. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
4. 🥄 Return to the medium bowl containing the buttermilk, and add all of the remaining wet ingredients. Mix thoroughly.
5. 🥣 Add all the wet ingredients to the shallow well in the dry ingredients and mix until everything is just combined, being careful not to overmix.
6. 🍰 Divide the cake batter between the two cake pans. Gently tap the pans on the counter a couple of times to level the batter and remove any air bubbles. Bake in the center rack of the preheated oven for 35 minutes, or until a thin wooden skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
7. 🧈 Meanwhile, make the frosting. In a large bowl, add the butter and use an electric hand mixer to beat it until fluffy.
8. 🥄 Add the powdered sugar, cocoa powder, milk, and vanilla. Start mixing at a low speed and then gradually increase until smooth. Once the melted chocolate has cooled off a bit, mix it in as well.
9. ❄️ When the cakes are done baking, let them cool for 10 minutes in the cake pan before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely (see Notes). If desired, the cakes can be leveled with a serrated knife to create a flat surface for stacking.
10. 🧻 Rip a piece of parchment paper into 6 pieces and place them in a circular shape on the cake stand, just wider than the size of your cake. These papers help keep the cake stand clean and will be removed later. Place one of the cakes on the cake stand.
11. 🥄 Spread a layer of frosting on top of the first cake. Place the second cake on top, upside down, and frost the top and sides with the leftover frosting.
12. 🧻 Remove the parchment paper squares from under your cake.
13. ✨ Decorate the chocolate cake with desired toppings.

## 📝 Notes

- 🥛 Milk: the source recommends soy or pea milk, because the high protein content of these milks lets them curdle to create vegan buttermilk. The source cannot guarantee other plant-based milks.
- 🥥 Coconut oil: the source does not recommend swapping the coconut oil for other vegetable oils, since other oils affect the texture. Refined coconut oil is recommended because it does not impart a coconut taste or scent. Unrefined coconut oil can be used if you don't mind the coconut taste.
- ⏳ Cooling: be patient; a warm cake will cause the frosting to melt when applied.
- 🗄️ Storage: best enjoyed immediately. The cake can be stored in the fridge for up to 3 days. The frosting can be made ahead and stored in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.
