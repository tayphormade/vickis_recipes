# Gluten, Egg, and Dairy-Free Chocolate Cake

🔗 **Source:** [MOMables](https://www.momables.com/gluten-egg-dairy-free-chocolate-cake-recipe/)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 2-layer 9-inch cake, 12 slices
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 15 minutes
- 🔥 **Cook:** 25-30 minutes at 350°F (177°C)
- ⏱️ **Total:** 45-50 minutes
- 🔢 **Per slice:** 337 calories; fat 10g; carbohydrates 61.3g; protein 2.8g; sugar 29.2g; fiber 2g; sodium 434.5mg (frosting not included)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 3 cups gluten-free all-purpose flour
- 🍬 1 3/4 cups granulated sugar
- 🟤 6 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 🫧 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 🧪 2 teaspoons xanthan gum, if using
- 🧂 1 teaspoon salt
- 🥛 2 cups chocolate non-dairy milk
- 🛢️ 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 🍎 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (177°C). Coat 2 9-inch cake pans with cooking spray and set aside.
2. 🥣 In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, xanthan gum (if using), and salt.
3. 🥛 In a medium bowl, combine the milk, oil, vinegar, and vanilla.
4. 🥄 Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir until smooth, scraping the bottom and sides of the bowl.
5. 🍰 Pour into the greased cake pans and place in the preheated oven.
6. ⏲️ Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
7. ❄️ Remove from the oven and transfer the cakes to a wire rack to cool completely.
8. 🍫 While the cakes are cooling, make the frosting.
9. 🧁 Frost the cake by placing one base layer on your plate or cake stand, spreading about a cup of frosting on top, topping it with the other cake layer, and spreading the remaining chocolate frosting over the top and sides.

---
