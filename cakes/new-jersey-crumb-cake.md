# New Jersey Crumb Cake

🔗 **Source:** [Epicurious](https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/new-jersey-crumb-cake)

- 🍽️ **Yield:** 12-16 servings
- ⏱️ **Total time:** 1 hour 10 minutes (plus cooling)
- 🔥 **Bake:** 36-42 minutes at 350°F (177°C)
- 🧊 **Cool:** about 2 hours in the pan

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Topping

- 🌾 3 cups plus 2 Tbsp. (391 g) all-purpose flour
- 🟤 1 cup (packed; 200 g) dark brown sugar
- 🍬 1/4 cup (50 g) granulated sugar
- 🧂 2 tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1 1/4 tsp. Morton kosher salt
- 🍂 2 tsp. ground cinnamon
- 🧈 1 1/4 cups (2 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter, melted, slightly cooled

### Cake

- 🛢️ Nonstick vegetable oil spray (for pan)
- 🌾 1 cup (125 g) all-purpose flour
- 🫧 1 tsp. baking powder
- 🧂 1 tsp. Diamond Crystal or 1/2 tsp. Morton kosher salt
- 🥚 1 large egg, room temperature
- 🍬 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- 🥣 1/2 cup sour cream, room temperature
- 🛢️ 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 🌼 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- ✨ Powdered sugar (for serving)

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Topping: using a fork or your fingers, mix the flour, dark brown sugar, granulated sugar, salt, and cinnamon in a medium bowl to combine. Add the melted butter and mix until large crumbs form and no dry streaks of flour remain. Let the crumb topping sit while you make the cake batter, at least 10 minutes.
2. 🛢️ Cake: place a rack in the middle of the oven and preheat to 350°F (177°C). Lightly coat a 9x9" metal cake pan with nonstick vegetable oil spray and line the pan with parchment paper, leaving at least a 2" overhang on 2 sides. Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together in a medium bowl. In a second medium bowl, whisk together the egg, sugar, sour cream, oil, and vanilla until smooth. Add to the dry ingredients and gently whisk until almost completely combined. Using a rubber spatula, fold the batter a few times to combine completely (it's okay if there are a few lumps). Scrape the batter into the prepared pan and smooth into an even layer.
3. 🍂 Using your hand, squeeze a palm-size piece of crumb topping together. Starting from the sides of the pan, crumble the mixture in your hand over the batter, aiming to create about 1/4" pieces. Repeat the process, working your way toward the center, until you've used about two-thirds of the topping. (The surface of the cake should be completely covered with crumbs.) Crumble the remaining topping over the entire cake, aiming for larger, about 1/2" pieces. (It will seem like too much crumb, but that's the point.)
4. ⏲️ Bake until the cake starts to pull away from the edges of the pan, a tester inserted into the center comes out clean, and the topping is set a little but still looks moist in places, 36-42 minutes. Transfer the pan to a wire rack and let the cake cool in the pan, about 2 hours.
5. 🔪 Using the parchment overhang, remove the cake from the pan and transfer it to a cutting board. Dust generously with powdered sugar, then slice into a 4x3 grid for the classic rectangular slices you'd find in a Jersey bakery or deli, or a 4x4 grid for more modest squares. (If you'd like your topping to be extra-tender, wrap the cake in plastic wrap and let it sit 8 hours before serving.)

---

## 📝 Notes

- 📅 Do ahead: the cake can be baked 4 days ahead. Store tightly wrapped at room temperature.
