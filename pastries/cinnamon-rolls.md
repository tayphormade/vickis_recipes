# Cinnamon Rolls

- 🔥 **Oven:** 400°F (204°C)
- ⏱️ **Bake:** 10 to 12 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Rolls

- 🥖 ⅓ recipe of Refrigerator Rolls dough (the Refrigerator Rolls recipe is not included in the source)
- 🧈 3 to 4 tablespoons melted butter
- 🍬 ¼ to ½ cup brown sugar
- 🍂 1 to 2 tablespoons cinnamon

### Icing

- 🧈 4 tablespoons butter, softened
- 🧀 ⅛ cup (1 ounce) cream cheese
- ❄️ ¾ cup powdered sugar
- 🌼 ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 🧂 Dash of salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 400°F (204°C).
2. 🥖 Roll out the dough to a rectangle about 15 x 20 x ¼ inches.
3. 🧈 Brush the melted butter over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border on three sides and brushing to the edge on a short side.
4. 🍬 Sprinkle the brown sugar and cinnamon over the dough.
5. 🌀 Starting at the short end that has the butter brushed to the edge, roll the dough into a log.
6. 🔪 Cut the log into 1½-inch pieces and place them in a baking pan.
7. ⏲️ Let the rolls rise.
8. 🔥 Bake for 10 to 12 minutes.
9. 🥄 While the rolls are baking, make the icing. Cream the butter and cream cheese together. Add powdered sugar until it reaches a frosting-like consistency. Add the vanilla and a dash of salt.
10. 🍯 Spread the icing on the rolls after they are done baking.
