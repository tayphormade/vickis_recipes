# The Ultimate Gluten Free Cinnamon Rolls

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8 large rolls or 16 to 18 smaller rolls
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 25 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cook:** 25 minutes
- ⏳ **Proofing and chilling:** 8 hours
- ⌛ **Total:** 8 hours 50 minutes
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)
- ⭐ **Rating:** 4.42 from 468 votes (as shown in the source)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Dough

- 🌾 3½ cups (490 g) Kim's gluten free bread flour blend
- 🍬 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
- 🥄 1¾ teaspoons baking powder
- 🦠 2 tablespoons plus 1½ teaspoons (24 g) instant yeast
- 🌿 2 tablespoons whole psyllium husks (or 1½ tablespoons psyllium husk powder), optional (see notes)
- 🧂 1 teaspoon salt
- 🥛 1½ cups (360 ml) milk (or dairy-free alternative) (see notes)
- 🧈 ¾ cup (1½ sticks or 169 g) butter, very soft or melted (or dairy-free butter alternative)
- 🥚 2 large eggs, lightly beaten

### Cinnamon sugar filling

- 🧈 ½ cup (1 stick, 8 tablespoons, or 113 g) butter, softened
- 🍬 1 cup (220 g) brown sugar
- 🍂 4 teaspoons cinnamon

### Cream cheese frosting

- 🧀 4 ounces (113 g) cream cheese, softened
- 🧈 4 tablespoons (½ stick or 57 g) butter, softened
- ❄️ 2 cups (250 g) powdered sugar
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract or vanilla bean paste

### Glaze (instead of frosting)

- ❄️ 2 cups (250 g) powdered sugar
- 🥛 3 to 4 tablespoons milk, or more as needed for desired consistency
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥣 Blend the dry ingredients in the bowl of a stand mixer using the paddle or dough hook attachment. With the mixer running on low, slowly add the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Once completely combined, turn the mixer up to about medium or medium high and beat for 5 minutes. The dough should be smooth and somewhat stretchy, but still pretty fluid and not at all like bread dough should be.
2. 🌡️ Remove the dough hook or paddle attachment. Scrape the dough together into a rough ball or mound using a bowl scraper or spatula. Cover the bowl and place it in a warm, draft-free area for about 2 hours, or until at least doubled in volume. Turning your oven on to the warm function (about 160 to 170°F, 71 to 77°C) and then turning it off before placing the dough inside is a great area for dough proofing. The dough should be fluffy looking and almost look like buttercream frosting. Chill for at least 4 to 6 hours.
3. 🍂 Mix the filling ingredients with a stand mixer or handheld mixer until fluffy, 3 to 4 minutes. Cover and set aside.
4. 🌾 On baking day (or the night before baking), remove the dough from the fridge. On a well-floured surface (using extra gf bread flour blend), knead the dough into a smooth ball, adding extra flour as needed until the dough is smooth. Roll out to a rectangle that's about ¼ inch thick.
5. 🍂 Spread the filling over the surface of the dough, leaving a ½-inch border at the far edge to seal. Roll up jelly roll style and place seam side down. Using a piece of dental floss, position it underneath the roll and pull it up on both sides. Crisscross it and pull down to cut each roll. You can also use a serrated knife if you don't have dental floss, but first place the entire roll in the freezer for 15 minutes. This will allow you to cut it cleanly.
6. 🥖 Place the rolls into a greased baking dish (depending on the size of your rolls, you'll need either a square 8 by 8 inch or a rectangular 9 by 13 inch pan or similar). Cover with plastic wrap and proof again until noticeably larger, but not quite doubled in size. If baking the rolls the next morning, place them in the refrigerator overnight at this point.
7. ⏲️ If the rolls have been refrigerated overnight, remove them from the fridge about an hour before you plan to bake them, to allow them to come to room temperature and finish their second rise. They should rise until puffed and noticeably larger, but not quite double in size.
8. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). When the rolls have risen sufficiently (they will rise more in the oven), remove the plastic wrap and bake for 20 to 25 minutes (larger rolls may take 5 minutes longer), or until golden brown. Frost as desired.
9. 🥄 For the cream cheese frosting, beat together the frosting ingredients until smooth.
10. 🍯 Or for the glaze, use it instead of the cream cheese frosting for an easy, quick, and pourable topping. Whisk all glaze ingredients in a small bowl and drizzle over warm rolls.

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Recipe by Kim at Let Them Eat Gluten Free Cake.
- 🌟 Keywords in the source: cinnamon rolls, gluten free.
- 🌟 Psyllium husks give the dough more stretch and a slightly softer texture. Without them, reduce the milk to 1¼ cups (300 ml).
- 🌟 If you don't have instant yeast, use active dry yeast by letting it bloom first in the milk. Heat the milk to about 100 to 110°F and add about 1 teaspoon of the sugar to the bowl, along with the same amount of yeast. Stir together and let sit until foamy on top. Proceed with the recipe as instructed.
- 🌟 Without a stand mixer, the dough may be hard to get to the proper consistency. The source suggests a handheld mixer (beat for about 5 more minutes, for a total of 10 minutes), mixing by hand with a dough whisk, or using a food processor. The source says the author has not tried a handheld mixer or hand mixing.
- 🌟 Rolls can be frozen up to the point right before baking. Freeze them in the pan, wrapped in a layer or two of plastic wrap and then foil. Thaw in the fridge, still wrapped, overnight. Proceed as above.
- 🌟 The source notes that the recipe was developed with the author's own gluten free bread flour blend, and results may differ with other flour blends.
