# Chinese Moon Cakes

- 🎑 **For:** Harvest Moon Festival
- 🍽️ **Yield:** 7 cakes per pastry recipe (the pastry can be multiplied)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 375°F (191°C)
- ⏲️ **Bake:** 15 minutes, then spray with water and bake 7 minutes, then brush and bake 8 more minutes (about 30 minutes total)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Syrup (makes 600 ml)

- 🍬 1/2 kg sugar
- 💧 350 ml water
- 🍋 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 🧪 1 tbsp lye water (substitute milk)

### Sweet bean paste (makes far more than needed; store for later use)

- 🫘 1 pound red adzuki beans
- 🧈 3/4 cup lard or oil
- 🍬 1 3/4 cups sugar (add more or less according to taste; makes a middle-sweet paste)

### Pastry (makes 7 cakes)

- 🍯 200 ml syrup (from the recipe above)
- 🌾 200 gm wheat flour
- 🌱 1 tsp malt (barley malt, like beer malt; the author substituted malted milk powder)
- 🛢️ 50 ml cooking oil

### Cake finishing

- 🔴 1 tsp annato oil (optional, for red color), or red food coloring mixed in 1 tablespoon water
- 🥚 1 egg yolk mixed in 1 tablespoon water, for wash

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍯 Syrup: bring the sugar and water to a boil and cook for 20 minutes. Add the lemon juice and continue to boil for 15 minutes; add the lye water. It should make 600 ml; add additional water to bring it up to 600 ml. Can be made ahead and refrigerated.
2. 🫘 Sweet bean paste: soak the beans in water to cover for 2 hours. Drain and discard the water. Cover with 8 cups water and cook for 1 1/2 hours or until the skins split. Strain the beans and discard the skins (or food processor until smooth). Place in a saucepan with the lard or oil and sugar. Cook, stirring constantly, until thick enough to form balls. Let cool.
3. 🥣 Pastry: mix the syrup, flour, malt, and cooking oil together to make a dough, adding more flour if necessary.
4. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 375°F (191°C).
5. ⚪ Roll the sweet bean paste into golf-size balls. Roll the pastry into golf-size balls (7 balls per pastry recipe). Using flour to keep it from sticking, roll each pastry ball into a 3-4 inch circle. Place a bean paste ball in the center and enclose it.
6. 🧁 Place in a mold or muffin pan with the bottom facing up. Remove from the mold and place on a greased cookie sheet. Bake for 15 minutes.
7. 💦 Spray the cakes with water and bake 7 minutes.
8. 🖌️ Brush with annato oil or red food coloring, and brush with egg wash. Bake 8 more minutes or until done.
9. ❄️ Let cool. They will soften overnight in a container. Serve.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🏺 Special molds for moon cakes can be bought. The author used a bear cast-iron muffin pan for panda bears (not traditional).
- 🌾 The author substituted malted milk powder for barley malt.
