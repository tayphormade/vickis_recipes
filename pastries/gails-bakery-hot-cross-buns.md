# Gail's Bakery Hot Cross Buns

🔗 **Source:** [Red Online](http://www.redonline.co.uk/food/recipes/gails-bakery-hot-cross-buns)

- 🍽️ **Makes:** 20 buns
- ⏲️ **Preparation:** 30 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cooking:** 20 minutes
- 🔥 **Oven:** 392°F (200°C), gas mark 6, then reduced to 356°F (180°C), gas mark 4

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### For the buns

- 🦠 20 g fresh yeast
- 🥛 1 tablespoon whole milk
- 🍬 1 teaspoon caster sugar
- 🌾 400 g plain flour
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🍂 ½ teaspoon freshly ground nutmeg
- 🍂 1 teaspoon mixed spice
- 🥛 200 ml whole milk
- 🍬 70 g caster sugar
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🧈 70 g soft unsalted butter (cut the cold butter into 2 cm cubes and leave at room temperature for an hour before using)
- 🍇 40 g sultanas
- 🍇 40 g dry currants
- 🍒 40 g dried unsweetened cranberries
- 🍊 70 g mixed peel
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon fine sea salt

### For the cross topping

- 🌾 200 g plain flour
- ❄️ 230 g icing sugar
- 🫒 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 🥛 3 tablespoons milk

### For the syrup

- 🍬 300 g caster sugar
- 💧 200 ml water
- 🌰 1 whole nutmeg
- ⭐ 1 whole star anise
- 🌸 1 whole clove
- 🌼 ½ vanilla pod, split in half lengthwise

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🦠 In a large mixing bowl, melt the yeast with a tablespoon of whole milk and a teaspoon of caster sugar. Make sure the yeast has completely dissolved and leave for 15 minutes.
2. 🫧 After 15 minutes, you should notice mini bubbles on top of your yeast liquid. That means your yeast is alive.
3. 🌾 In a separate bowl, sieve the flour with the spices.
4. 🥣 Add the spiced flour to the yeast mixture. Keep adding the rest of the milk, the sugar, and the egg.
5. 🤲 Begin to bind the mixture together using your hands, for 4 to 5 minutes until it forms a rough dough, and then add the butter a chunk at a time while kneading the dough.
6. 🧈 After adding all the butter slowly, it should take around 2 to 3 minutes to have a nice sticky dough. Continue kneading for 5 minutes more until you get a smooth, shiny dough.
7. 🍇 Add the salt and fruits and continue kneading for 3 minutes more.
8. ⏳ Cover the bowl with a tea towel and leave at room temperature for 1.5 hours, until it grows to almost double in size.
9. ⚪ Pour your dough onto a lightly floured surface and divide it into 20 pieces at 50 g each. Roll the dough pieces into shape by rolling them on a surface against your palms to create smooth buns.
10. 🧻 Arrange the buns on a baking tray lined with baking paper (all the buns onto one tray) and make sure you keep an even gap between them all. Cover the tray with a clean tea towel and leave to rise at room temperature until almost double in size.
11. 🥚 When the buns are ready (the sides of each bun should be touching and you almost shouldn't be able to see the tray), make an eggwash by whisking 1 whole egg, 1 egg yolk, and a teaspoon of milk. Brush the buns carefully with this eggwash and leave to dry out for 10 minutes. Brush again.
12. 🔥 Preheat the oven to 200°C (392°F), gas mark 6.
13. 🥄 Using a hand whisk, gently combine all the cross topping ingredients into a smooth paste.
14. ✂️ Fill a plastic piping bag with the topping mixture and cut a small hole using scissors. Always cut a smaller hole than you think you might need, as you can cut it again to make a bigger hole, but you can't make a big hole smaller!
15. ✖️ Pipe thin crosses on top of the buns on the tray and pop it into the hot oven.
16. 🔥 Immediately reduce the oven temperature to 180°C (356°F), gas mark 4, until thoroughly baked and golden (around 20 to 22 minutes).
17. 🍬 Meanwhile, put all the syrup ingredients in a small saucepan and, over a low heat, bring to a boil, stirring occasionally. Once the sugar has dissolved completely, increase the heat and simmer for a couple of minutes until you have a syrupy consistency. Remove from the heat and fish the spices out.
18. 🍯 Once the buns are ready, take them out of the oven and carefully brush them with the hot syrup.
19. ❄️ Leave to cool for 15 minutes before serving.

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The source is Red magazine's Gail's Bakery Hot Cross Buns recipe, posted March 19, 2018.
- 🌟 The source describes these as "quite possibly the best hot cross buns of all time."
- 🌟 The source lists "3 tbsp tbsp milk" for the cross topping; it is written here as 3 tablespoons milk.
- 🌟 The egg wash (1 whole egg, 1 egg yolk, and a teaspoon of milk) appears in the method, not in the ingredient list.
- 🌟 The printed recipe gives oven temperatures in Celsius and gas marks; the Fahrenheit values (392°F and 356°F) come from handwritten comments added to the source PDF.
- 🌟 A comment added to the source PDF says: "At 50g each, I had 24 rolls." It also says to make half of the cross topping, since the full amount is enough to do it 4 times.
