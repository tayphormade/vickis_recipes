# Hot Cross Buns

🔗 **Source:** [RecipeTin Eats](https://www.recipetineats.com/hot-cross-buns-recipe/)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 12 buns
- ⏲️ **Prep:** 25 minutes
- ⏱️ **Cook:** 20 minutes
- ⏳ **Total:** 45 minutes (inactive time: 1 to 2 hours)
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (180°C) for all oven types
- 📊 **Calories:** 217 kcal per serving (as listed in the source)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Buns

- 🦠 1 tablespoon dry yeast (see notes)
- 🍬 110 g / ½ cup caster sugar (superfine sugar)
- 🥛 1½ cups / 375 ml milk, whole or low fat, lukewarm
- 🌾 637.5 g / 4¼ cups bread flour, plus ¼ cup extra
- 🍂 2 teaspoons cinnamon powder
- 🍂 2 teaspoons allspice
- 🧂 ½ teaspoon salt
- 🍇 1½ cups / 210 g sultanas
- 🍊 1 to 2 oranges, zest only
- 🧈 50 g / 3½ tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 🥚 1 egg, at room temperature

### Crosses

- 🌾 ½ cup flour (any white flour)
- 💧 5 tablespoons water

### Glaze

- 🍯 1 tablespoon apricot jam
- 💧 2 teaspoons water

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🦠 Place the yeast and 2 teaspoons of the sugar in a large bowl, then pour in the milk. Leave for 5 minutes until it froths.
2. 🥣 Add the remaining buns ingredients, starting with 4¼ cups flour, including the remaining sugar.
3. 🥖 Use a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook to mix until a smooth elastic dough forms. Add extra flour if required. See the video for how the dough should look.
4. 🤲 Alternatively, dust a work surface with flour and knead by hand for 10 minutes, or until smooth.
5. ⏳ Rise #1: Leave the dough in the bowl, cover with a wet (clean) tea towel, and place in a warm place to rise until doubled in size. This will take anywhere from 30 minutes to 1½ hours.
6. 🧻 Line a 31.5 x 23.5 cm / 9 x 13-inch tray with baking paper with overhang.
7. 👊 Remove the tea towel and punch the dough to deflate.
8. 🪵 Dust the work surface with flour, place the dough on it, knead lightly (to deflate air), and shape into a log. Cut into 12 equal pieces.
9. ⚪ Take one piece and press it down with your palm, then use your fingers to gather it into a ball, then roll the dough briefly to form a ball. This stretches the dough on one side, which gives a nice smooth surface.
10. 🧻 Place the ball smooth side up on the tray. Repeat with the remaining dough. Line them up 3 x 4.
11. ⏳ Rise #2: Spray a piece of cling wrap lightly with oil, then place it over the tray. Return the tray to a warm place and leave for 30 to 45 minutes, until the dough has risen by about 75% (less than double in size).
12. 🔥 Partway through Rise #2, preheat the oven to 180°C/350°F (all oven types).
13. 🥄 Crosses: Mix flour and water until a runny paste forms. See the video for the thickness required.
14. 🛍️ Spoon the paste into a round 3 mm piping bag or small ziplock bag, then snip the corner.
15. ✖️ Remove the cling wrap and pipe crosses onto the buns. Go slowly so the paste hugs the curves.
16. 🔥 Bake for 20 minutes (fan/convection) or 23 minutes (standard), or until the surface is a deep golden brown. The surface colour is the best test for this recipe.
17. 🍯 Meanwhile, place the jam and water in a bowl and microwave for 30 seconds. Mix to combine.
18. 🖌️ Remove the buns from the oven. Brush with the jam mixture while warm.
19. 🧊 Use the overhang to lift the buns onto a cooling rack. Allow to cool to warm before serving.

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Yeast: 1 tablespoon dry yeast = 9 grams. The author uses Lowan Dried Instant Yeast (red tube, sold in the baking aisle), which technically doesn't need to be frothed, but the author froths it anyway. If using packets, 2 x 7 g sachets (4½ teaspoons) is slightly more than 1 tablespoon and works fine.
- 🌟 Fresh yeast: use 27 g (0.9 oz), crumbled in and left to stand until it foams.
- 🌟 If the yeast does not froth, the source says it is not active and the buns will not rise.
- 🌟 Milk: scalding hot milk kills the yeast. The author heats it for 45 seconds on high in the microwave, and tests that it is comfortably warm to a finger.
- 🌟 Bread flour makes the buns fluffier and more tender, but the source says normal white flour also works.
- 🌟 Sultanas: the source adds them before kneading so they are evenly dispersed.
- 🌟 Orange zest: the author dislikes bitter pieces of citrus and uses orange zest instead of candied peel, which is not sold at supermarkets. Use 2 oranges for a more prominent orange flavour.
- 🌟 Dough: about 5 minutes on speed 2 with a KitchenAid. Add extra flour only as needed so the dough comes away from the bowl.
- 🌟 Warm place: the author runs an empty dryer for 1 to 2 minutes and then places the bowl inside, which the source says gives a 40-minute rise.
- 🌟 Storage: best served on the day it is made. Reheat the day after for 15 seconds in the microwave. Buns freeze well and can be defrosted. To reheat batches, cover with foil and heat at 160°C/320°F for about 8 minutes.
