# Puff Pastry in 5 Minutes

- 🍽️ **Makes:** about 500 g (1.1 lb)
- ⏲️ **Chill:** 30 minutes

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

- 🌾 200 g flour
- 💧 100 g cold water
- 🧈 200 g frozen butter, in chunks
- 🧂 Pinch of salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🔪 Put the butter, flour, water, and salt in a food processor. Blend 3 times, for 10 seconds each.
2. ✋ Turn the dough onto a work surface and gather it into a rough square.
3. 🥖 Sprinkle with flour and roll into a rectangle.
4. 🌀 Roll one end of the dough like a jelly roll. Flatten the roll with the rolling pin.
5. 🥖 Roll the dough lengthwise into a long strip, then roll the strip into a large coil.
6. 🥡 Wrap the roll in a plastic bag and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
7. 🥖 Remove from the refrigerator. Place the dough on its side with the ends facing you, and flatten with the rolling pin.
8. 📏 Roll into a rectangle about ⅛ to ¼ inch thick.

---

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 Use right away, or wrap in parchment and then a plastic bag. Refrigerate for up to a week, or freeze longer.
