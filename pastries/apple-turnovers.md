# Apple Turnovers

🔗 **Source:** [Allrecipes](http://allrecipes.com/Recipe-Tools/Print/Recipe.aspx?recipeID=59124&origin=detail&servings=8&metric=false)

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8
- ⏱️ **Prep:** 30 minutes
- ⏲️ **Cook:** 25 minutes
- 🕑 **Ready in:** 55 minutes
- 🌡️ **Oven:** 400°F (200°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Filling

- 🍋 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 💧 4 cups water
- 🍎 4 Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, and sliced
- 🧈 2 tablespoons butter
- 🍬 1 cup brown sugar
- 🌿 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 🌽 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 💧 1 tablespoon water

### Pastry

- 🥐 1 (17.25 ounce) package frozen puff pastry sheets, thawed

### Glaze

- 🍬 1 cup confectioners' sugar
- 🥛 1 tablespoon milk
- 🌼 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🍋 Combine the lemon juice and 4 cups water in a large bowl. Place the sliced apples in the water to keep them from browning.
2. 🔥 Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Drain the water from the apples and place them into the hot skillet. Cook and stir for about 2 minutes.
3. 🍬 Add the brown sugar and cinnamon, and cook, stirring, for 2 more minutes.
4. 🥣 Stir together the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon water. Pour into the skillet and mix well. Cook for another minute, or until the sauce has thickened. Remove from heat to cool slightly.
5. 🌡️ Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
6. 🥐 Unfold the puff pastry sheets, and repair any cracks by pressing them back together. Trim each sheet into a square, then cut each larger square into 4 smaller squares.
7. 🥟 Spoon apples onto the center of each square. Fold over from corner to corner into a triangle, and press the edges together to seal. Place turnovers on a baking sheet, leaving about 1 inch between them.
8. ⏲️ Bake for 25 minutes in the preheated oven, until puffed and lightly browned. Cool completely before glazing.
9. 🥣 To make the glaze, mix together the confectioners' sugar, milk, and vanilla in a small bowl. Adjust the thickness by adding more sugar or milk if necessary.
10. 🍯 Drizzle the glaze over the cooled turnovers.
