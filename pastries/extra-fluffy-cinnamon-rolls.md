# Extra-Fluffy Cinnamon Rolls

With Cream Cheese Frosting

- 🍽️ **Servings:** 8 to 12
- ⏲️ **Active time:** 1¼ hours
- ⏳ **Total time:** 3½ hours
- 🔥 **Oven:** 350°F (175°C)

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### Dough

- 🥛 2¼ cups whole milk, divided
- 🌾 5¾ cups (719 g) all-purpose flour, divided
- 🥚 1 large egg
- 🦠 1 ¼-oz. envelope instant yeast (about 2¼ tsp.)
- 🍬 ⅓ cup (67 g) granulated sugar
- 🧂 1 Tbsp. Diamond Crystal or 1¾ tsp. Morton kosher salt
- 🧈 ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, room temperature, cut into pieces

### Filling

- 🧈 ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 🍬 1 cup (packed; 200 g) dark brown sugar
- 🍂 2 Tbsp. ground cinnamon
- 🥛 2 Tbsp. heavy cream
- 🧂 ½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¼ tsp. Morton kosher salt

### Frosting and assembly

- 🧈 4 Tbsp. unsalted butter, room temperature, plus more for pans
- 🌾 All-purpose flour (for surface)
- 🧀 8 oz. cream cheese, room temperature
- ❄️ 1¼ cups (138 g; or more) powdered sugar
- 🥛 1 Tbsp. (or more) heavy cream
- 🍋 1 tsp. lemon zest (optional)
- 🧂 ¼ tsp. Diamond Crystal or Morton kosher salt

---

## 👩‍🍳 Steps

1. 🥛 Whisk ¾ cup whole milk and ¼ cup (31 g) all-purpose flour in a medium saucepan until smooth. Place the saucepan over medium heat and cook, whisking constantly, until the mixture is thickened and whisk lines are visible on the bottom of the pan, about 2 minutes. Scrape the tangzhong into the bowl of a stand mixer and let cool.
2. 🥚 Add 1 large egg, one ¼-oz. envelope instant yeast (about 2¼ tsp.), ⅓ cup (67 g) granulated sugar, 1 Tbsp. Diamond Crystal or 1¾ tsp. Morton kosher salt, and the remaining 1½ cups whole milk to the tangzhong and whisk to combine. Add the remaining 5½ cups (688 g) all-purpose flour. Fit the bowl onto the stand mixer and fit the mixer with the dough hook attachment. Mix on low speed until combined, about 1 minute. Increase the speed to medium-low and mix, scraping down the sides of the bowl occasionally, until the dough is smooth and elastic, 18 to 24 minutes. Pinch off a golf-ball-size piece of dough and stretch it between your fingers as thin as possible without breaking. If you can see light through the dough, it's ready. If not, continue mixing a few more minutes, then try again.
3. 🧈 Add ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, room temperature, cut into pieces, to the dough and mix on medium speed until incorporated, about 2 minutes. Continue mixing until the dough pulls away cleanly from the sides of the bowl and is no longer tacky to the touch, 5 to 7 minutes longer. Remove the bowl from the mixer and cover with a damp kitchen towel. Let it sit in a warm spot until doubled in volume, 60 to 90 minutes.
4. 🍂 While the dough is rising, cook ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the butter foams and the milk solids turn dark amber, about 4 minutes. Transfer to a heatproof bowl and let cool slightly. Add 1 cup (packed; 200 g) dark brown sugar, 2 Tbsp. ground cinnamon, 2 Tbsp. heavy cream, and ½ tsp. Diamond Crystal or ¼ tsp. Morton kosher salt to the butter and mix to combine.
5. 🧈 Butter two 9-inch-diameter cake pans. Punch down the dough and transfer it to a lightly floured surface. Stretch the dough into a rough rectangle; if the dough feels stiff, let it sit 5 to 10 minutes to allow the gluten to relax. Arrange the dough with a short side facing you and roll it out to a 24 x 13-inch rectangle. Spread the filling over the dough in an even layer.
6. 🌀 Starting at the edge closest to you, tightly roll up the dough into a log; roll the log seam side down. Using a sharp serrated knife and long sawing motions, trim the ends of the log; discard. Using a serrated knife or, preferably, unflavored dental floss, cut the log in half crosswise, then cut each half into 6 equal rounds. (If using dental floss, slide the floss underneath the dough, then cross over the top and pull the ends through.) Nestle 6 rounds, cut side down, in each prepared cake pan. Cover with damp kitchen towels and let sit in a warm spot until doubled in volume, about 30 to 50 minutes.
7. 🔥 Place a rack in the middle of the oven and preheat to 350°F (175°C). Bake the rolls until golden on top and puffed (don't be alarmed if they rise dramatically in the center), 25 to 30 minutes.
8. 🧀 Meanwhile, using an electric mixer on medium speed or a whisk, beat 4 Tbsp. unsalted butter and 8 oz. cream cheese, both at room temperature, in a large bowl, scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed, until combined. Add 1¼ cups (138 g) powdered sugar, 1 Tbsp. heavy cream, 1 tsp. lemon zest (if using), and ¼ tsp. Diamond Crystal or Morton kosher salt and beat until the mixture is smooth and the sugar is incorporated. Taste and beat in more powdered sugar (up to ¾ cup) if desired. Thin with more cream as needed, a teaspoonful at a time, until the frosting is thick but spreadable.
9. 🍯 Let the cinnamon rolls cool in the pan for 5 minutes; spread the frosting over.

## 📝 Notes

- 🌟 The dough uses a tangzhong (a cooked flour and milk paste), as described in Step 1.
- 🌟 The source lists both Diamond Crystal and Morton kosher salt amounts. Use the one you have, following the source's measurements for each brand.
- 🌟 The source's frosting and assembly section calls for butter for the pans.
- 🌟 The source gives "8–12 servings"; the log is cut into 12 rounds, 6 per cake pan.
- 🌟 The source does not name the publication in the text, but the file is titled as a Bon Appétit recipe. The source is dated December 22, 2022.
